# T34 search speed: reuse futures; do not budget cold search as live latency

2026-10-19 · game-AI search advice · main snapshot `0916fa649aaa`; T33 tree `eeb46b72fc0d`

**There are worthwhile tricks, especially exact continuation reuse. They can remove most repeated verification work, but cold H300 search with the current CNN is not demonstrated within either live deadline. Teacher throughput has credible incremental upside; Path B needs a different scheduling strategy, not merely faster simulation.** The ratified [SUCCESS_CONTRACT](../final_run_20261019/SUCCESS_CONTRACT.md) permits assistance and supersedes older bare-network restrictions. This is advice, not a registration, launch or budget amendment.

The measurements need three corrections:

- In T33’s `ROUND2_MAC_MPS_LOCKSTEP.json`, 179,500 active steps take 42.641 seconds: **4.21k/s end-to-end**. The ~175 µs host plus 54 µs hero figures are amortized elapsed costs with **three Numba threads**, huge batches, H32/N4 and repeated L800 roots—not one-thread H300 timings. Host-only throughput is ~5.7k/s. Multiplying those amortized costs gives 69 ms/verify and 412 ms/six full branches, not live latency. See [T33 optimization log](https://github.com/josenunezwork/snake-dqn/blob/eeb46b72fc0d507703bbdf8feee42ff4a9c1bc2d/research/t33_batched_teacher/OPTIMIZATION_LOG.md), final-source MPS section, receipt implementation pin `7a5d208dea25`.
- [T33 rejects frozen opponents carrying serving vetoes](https://github.com/josenunezwork/snake-dqn/blob/eeb46b72fc0d507703bbdf8feee42ff4a9c1bc2d/research/t33_batched_teacher/teacher.py#L359-L362). Its timing does **not** establish mixed-Watch6 FRP3+v8 cost. Six actions are three relative directions × normal/boost. [T3](../../../research/t3_rollout_labels_20261015/ceiling.py) searched advisory-safe roots exhaustively; T34 specifies legal roots and verify-first. These distinctions change work and potentially decisions.
- The raw T3 dense receipt confirms **38/64 → 54/64**, mass **608.05 → 700.01**, pooled B8-s0/s3 against scripted opponents. Its 55,002,897 rollout frames / 16,684.83 wall seconds yield **3.30k/s aggregate**. Calibration confirms 31–53% agreement over 300 decisions, but the two full references themselves agree only 98/300: this does not isolate shallow-horizon error. Sources: `snake-dqn-artifacts/{t3-rollout-labels-20261015/dense/ceiling-result.json,t33-batched-teacher/calibration-v1.json}`.

I ran only a sub-second forward-only probe: actual B8, `Ego2sNet`, CPU fp32, Torch 2.9.1, intra/inter-op and BLAS threads=1, five warmups, 64 synthetic-input calls, concurrent host. Batch-one median/p95 were **0.414/0.529 ms**; batch-six **1.927/2.688 ms**. Thus 300 serial forwards alone extrapolate to **124 ms**, or **578 ms** for six branches batched together. These are inference-only estimates, not gameplay timings; checkpoint `3e649783…`, shapes `(7,31,31)/(4,37,37)/60`, 1,638,263 parameters.

One root is only six candidate continuations, not an exponential game tree. Its expensive requirement is recomputing six-snake dynamics, observations and policies sequentially for 300 steps. T33’s simulator accounts for ~49% of stage time, hero inference ~23%; halving the simulator alone yields only ~1.33× overall. The supplied 68k/s sim-only number was not independently recovered and cannot price CNN-driven long-body continuations. [T10](../t10_certificate_horizon_2026-10-17.md) supports weak long-horizon prediction (~0.74–0.77 AUC); it does not prove an immutable prediction ceiling or justify truncating H300.

Treat “50–80 steps” as shorthand: [T1’s observed late last-avoidable-decision quartiles](../t1_commitment_study_2026-10-15.md) were **16.5/88.5/248.5**. A fixed short horizon misses substantial decision history.

My ranked dependency queue:

1. **Measure the actual contract first.** Freeze a small long-body fixture spanning three mixes, with genuine veto opponents and legal-action branching. Measure one-thread cold verify, forced expansion and complete handoff p50/p95/p99; count active/resident steps, CPU inference, copies and queue age separately. First compare complete action/state/RNG traces with the reference. Use bounded local probes; retain the existing A5 budget and <3k/s STOP rule. No extra paid experiment is proposed.

2. **Then reuse exact futures.** Store the deterministic B8 continuation. If the current root matches its predicted prepared state, retain its suffix and append only the missing steps. Every-eighth-decision verification becomes **8 steps instead of 300** on a hit. At hit probability p, expected work is `8p + 300(1−p)`: p=90%/99% gives **8.1×/27.5× confirmation-work savings**. This adapts established [search reuse](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.03049), not a measured Snake speedup.

   Equality must include ordered bodies/food, RNG, all opponents’ carry, frame/life, checkpoint, and live food maintenance. Publish a newly validated current-frame result; never relabel a stale action as current. Re-root after interventions and discard mismatches. After warmup, one appended step per tick could cost milliseconds; cold starts and failed continuations remain expensive. Speculatively prepare alternatives at predicted future roots, accepting them only if those roots materialize exactly. Measure hit rate **near interventions**, not just easy straight paths.

3. **After identity, reduce decision work.** For serving, visit alternatives in descending root Q; the first H300 survivor is the specified winner. If none survives, complete the death-time comparison. The teacher still needs all per-action outcomes/F, so this stopping rule cannot erase its label work. Skip CNN/raster construction when the actual rollout rule permits exactly one action. Measure that fraction; advisory masks are not universal death proofs.

4. **Optimize the remaining host path.** Compact active rows through opponents/observations, reuse prepared buffers, and batch corpse/food events. Investigate incremental occupancy updates and body rings before rebuilding whole grids. Preserve collision, boost, food-order and RNG semantics. T33 already implements substantial assembly, food-staging and v8 improvements; do not count them twice. Hypothesis: **1.3–2×** further end-to-end improvement, conditional on matched profiling and trace identity.

For teacher placement, apply the plan’s own assumptions consistently: `5.7k × (6/3 cores) × 0.7 / (2.4–3.7 slowdown)` gives **2.2–3.3k host steps/s/L4**, before missing-veto cost—not assured 3.5–5.5k. A measured 1.5–2× host improvement would imply approximately **3.2–6.7k/s**. Mac’s existing three-thread/MPS batch gives 4.2k/s; a 1.3–2× improvement implies **5.5–8.4k/s**, not single-thread serving speed. All are projections for a different H300 workload.

At the plan’s 0.4M steps/world, 2.2–3.3k/s means **20–30 worlds/hour/lane**. Across three lanes, 800 worlds take ~9–13.5 hours, **54–81 units** at the plan’s 2 u/lane-hour, before setup/export. Removing all 130k confirmation steps from a 400k-step world saves at most **1.48× overall**; death-window expansions dominate.

I would defer MCTS, generic transpositions and coarse opponent models. Fixed action tapes after divergence, shorter horizons, macro-actions and surrogate rollout policies change the teacher. Cheap topology may order search, but require new efficacy evidence before pruning. A cold H300 needs ≤140/277 µs per complete step at 42/83 ms; the measured CNN alone exceeds both at its median. **Withdraw the unmeasured 12–30 ms Path B expectation.** Keep its real-deadline efficacy test first: verification without an on-time replacement changes nothing, and misses concentrated on rescue decisions can erase the entire gain.
