# CPU, Simulator & Learner Performance Optimization (`perf-20261013` / `simd-vector61` / `perf-sim` / `S0`–`S2`)

## Question & Goal

Can bit-exact simulator geometry optimizations (`perf-sim`), Numba-vectorized `vector61`/`ego2s` feature and opponent-veto kernels (`simd-vector61`, `actor_perf_20261017`, `t34_speed_s2`), and learner batch-scaling/compilation (`perf_*_20261013`) deliver `1.5–5.5x` wall-clock speedups across live `GameState` evaluation, `GridBatchSim` actor collection, `bv8` learner updates, and T34 teacher rollouts without altering policy decisions or evaluation parity?

## Final Verdict & Key Numbers

### 1. Batched `vector61` Featurizer & Policy (`simd-vector61`, 2026-09-26 to 2026-10-03)
- **Parity & Speedup:** `Vector61Featurizer` (`src/simd_env/vector61_featurizer.py`) and `Vector61SimdPolicy` (`src/simd_env/vector61_policy.py`) matched live `GameState.get_state` and `tournament_eval.rollout` bit-for-bit across `~66,000` vector decisions (`28,000` in `tests/test_vector61_featurizer_parity.py` and `tests/test_vector61_simd_rollout_parity.py` + `38,000` in standalone probes, `0` divergences) and **`48/48` `H5000` evaluation records** (`3.65x` aggregate wall speedup: `367.5 s` SIMD vs `1,341.1 s` live) ([`docs/research/simd_vector61_plan_2026-09-26.md`](../simd_vector61_plan_2026-09-26.md)).
- **Veto Parity:** `v5` boost-aware veto matched `12/12` `H5000` records (`~2.0x` speedup: `126.3 s` vs `251.3 s`), and `v7`/`v8` row-view hooks (`_RowSnakeView` / `_RowEnvView`) passed `H5000` parity (`research/simd_parity_v7v8_h5000_20261003/h5000_check.py`, [`docs/research/simd_vector61_plan_2026-09-26.md`](../simd_vector61_plan_2026-09-26.md)).

### 2. Live Simulator Geometry Optimization (`perf-sim`, 2026-10-06 to 2026-10-07)
- **Strict Gate & Actor Throughput (`git 68c0efef`):** On the 18-core M5 Pro Mac, `SnakeGeometryCache` (`src/game/snake_geometry.py`), integer squared-radius checks, and flood-fill target memoization increased strict-world `H5000` evaluation (`frp3-s12 + v8`) from `111 -> 233` episodes/h/core (**`2.10x`**, `32.5 -> 15.5 s/ep`; `2.42x` from earlier `92 -> 224` eps/h/core), FRP-v3 gate-world actor throughput from `145 -> 317` rows/s (**`2.19x`**, `6.91 -> 3.16 ms/frame`), and default Apex arena throughput from `2,031 -> 2,634` rows/s (`1.30x`) ([`docs/notes/gpu-engine-and-speed.md`](../../notes/gpu-engine-and-speed.md), `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/perf-sim-20261007/`).
- **Bitwise Identity Proof:** Verified **`100%` bitwise identity** across `42` strict-world `H5000` episodes (`210,000` frames, `730,308` policy forward rows), `15` gate-world actor episodes (`55,896` buffer rows), and `23` default-arena episodes (`9,932` rows) (`/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/perf-sim-20261007/`).

### 3. Quiet-Host Learner, Numba Actor & Batch-Scale Pass (`perf_*_20261013`, 2026-10-13 to 2026-10-14)
- **M5 Pro Learner (`bench_learner`, `bv8`):** Baseline `35.0 ms/update` (`3,658 tr/s`) improved to **`30.4 ms/update`** (**`1.15x`**, `4,212 tr/s`) with `--learner-fast` (`t1`: pinned buffer + fused target/online forward + `foreach` Polyak), and to **`44.7 ms / 256 tr`** (**`1.57x`**, `5,730 tr/s`) with `t1 + --batch-scale 2` (`batch=256`, `update_every=64`). `torch.compile` on MPS was net-negative (`0.72–0.99x` steady-state plus `8–21 s` warm-up) ([`research/perf_quiet_20261013/RESULTS.md`](../../../research/perf_quiet_20261013/RESULTS.md)).
- **M5 Pro Actor (`bench_actor`, B8 flags):** Numba `vector61` (`--v61-backend numba`) sped up short-body actors **`1.9–2.1x`** (`4,210 -> 7,845 steps/s` at `E=64`, `5,337 -> 11,300 steps/s` at `E=256`) and long-body actors (`--min-init-len 400 --max-init-len 1200`) **`5.3–5.5x`** (`1,184 -> 6,264 steps/s` at `E=64`, `1,336 -> 7,324 steps/s` at `E=128`) ([`research/perf_quiet_20261013/RESULTS.md`](../../../research/perf_quiet_20261013/RESULTS.md)).
- **M5 Pro End-to-End Trainer (`bench_e2e` & 3M-Frame A/B):** Learner-bound at `3,726–3,986 tr/s` across `64x1`..`128x4`, rising to **`5,364 tr/s` (`1.44x`)** at `128x2 + --batch-scale 2`. In a 2-seed 3M-frame B8 A/B at `H5000x32` (`/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/perf-ab-k2-20261014/`), `K2` (`--batch-scale 2`) achieved **`537.5` mean mass** (`+102.6` vs `frp3-s12+v8`, `75.0%` survival) in **`1.49 h`** vs `K1`'s `527.3` mass (`+92.4`, `68.8%` survival) in `2.10 h` (`+10.2` mass, **`1.41x` faster wall**) ([`research/perf_quiet_20261013/RESULTS.md`](../../../research/perf_quiet_20261013/RESULTS.md)).
- **RunPod RTX 4090 (x86 EPYC, Torch 2.5.1+cu124):** Learner baseline `14.9 ms/update` (`8,603 tr/s`) reached **`8.4 ms/update`** (**`1.74x`**, **`15,170 tr/s`**; **`22,212 tr/s`** with `k2`) under `t1 + --compile-learner reduce-overhead`, while EPYC actor cores were `~2.4x` slower per actor than M5 Pro (`3,401 vs 7,845 steps/s` short, `2,469 vs 6,264 steps/s` long) ([`research/perf_quiet_20261013/RESULTS.md`](../../../research/perf_quiet_20261013/RESULTS.md), `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/perf-cuda-compile-20261014-try1/out/`).
- **Multi-Seed Grouped-Conv Learner (`perf_multiseed_20261013`):** Stacking `K in {2,3,5}` independent `Ego2sNet` models via `groups=K` Conv2d and `bmm` Linear layers yielded only `0.96–1.05x` per-seed throughput on MPS (`35.2 ms` at `K=1` vs `33.6 ms/seed` at `K=2`, `36.9 ms/seed` at `K=3`), failing the `>= 1.5x` gate (**`NO-GO`**) ([`research/perf_multiseed_20261013/DESIGN.md`](../../../research/perf_multiseed_20261013/DESIGN.md)).

### 4. Fused Numba Actor Kernel (`actor_perf_20261017`, 2026-10-17)
- **Step Timing (`E=64`, mixed lengths up to `750`):** Fusing `ego_crops_and_food_features_numba` with `v8_features_for_env_numba` (`src/simd_env/ego_raster.py`) reduced total actor step time from **`5.042 ms` (`198.3 steps/s`) to `3.047 ms` (`328.2 steps/s`, `1.65x` speedup)** and feature extraction (`extract_ego2s`) from **`3.829 ms` to `1.839 ms` (`2.08x` speedup)**, with `11/11` bitwise parity tests passing in `tests/test_ego2s_featurizer.py` ([`research/actor_perf_20261017/README.md`](../../../research/actor_perf_20261017/README.md)).

### 5. T34 Speed `S0` Baseline & `S2` Native Numba `v8` Opponent Veto (2026-10-20 to 2026-10-21)
- **`S0` Contended M5 Pro Baseline (`REFERENCE_MAC.json` sha `2561df19...`):** `103.1 s` per 6-world `H300` round (`209` worlds/h, `155` active row-steps/s, `16,021` row-steps/round); pooled worker shares were **`v8` `56.8%`**, inference `13.5%`, digest `11.9%`, step `8.4%`, hero `7.4%` (`long-frozen` `v8` `56.5%`, `long-mixed` `v8` `67.0%`) -> **`D1 PASS`** ([`research/speed_s0_baseline/RESULT_MAC_CONTENDED_01.md`](../../../research/speed_s0_baseline/RESULT_MAC_CONTENDED_01.md)).
- **`S2` Native Numba `v8` Hook (`fcb0b16a`, `s2-matched-mac-contended-01/matched-s0.json` sha `cf8320ec...`):** Replacing Python `_v8_opponent_hook` with `v8_override_mask_for_env_numba` (`T34_S2_NATIVE_V8=1`) cut median 6-world round wall time from **`98.534 s` to `51.878 s`** (**`1.8994x` throughput, `-47.35%` wall**; `long-mixed` `70.810 -> 27.615 s` = `2.56x`; `long-frozen` `11.476 -> 7.719 s` = `1.49x`), with **`144/144` retained files byte-identical** to `REFERENCE_MAC.json`, `2,995/2,995` stock-audited native rows exact (`s2-respawn-audit-01/respawn-audit.json` sha `2ac1eee5...`), and `33/33` tests passing (`18.78 s`) -> **`D3 PASS`** ([`research/t34_speed_s2/RESULT_D3_MAC_CONTENDED_01.md`](../../../research/t34_speed_s2/RESULT_D3_MAC_CONTENDED_01.md)).
- **Mac vs L4 `S0` Export Comparison (`L4_MISMATCH.md` sha `4870327f...`):** Saved Mac (`mac-01`) and Colab L4 (`l4-01`) `S0` exports had identical root actions (`1,800/1,800`), lengths, rewards, and terminal states across all 6 worlds, differing only in `4,443 / 10,800` stored `float32` Q scalars (`max |ΔQ| = 7.629e-6`, `min top1-top2 margin = 3.219e-4`) ([`research/t34_speed_s2/RESULT_D3_MAC_CONTENDED_01.md`](../../../research/t34_speed_s2/RESULT_D3_MAC_CONTENDED_01.md)).

## What Was Learned & Current Status

1. **Long-body flood-fill and Python `v8` veto dominated CPU wall time:** Both in `GridBatchSim` actor collection (`perf_actor_20261013`) and T34 `H300` teacher rollouts (`S0`), Python flood-fill / `v8` safety-veto evaluation consumed `56–67%` of long-body wall time. Moving `vector61`, `ego2s`, and `v8` into nogil Numba kernels (`--v61-backend numba`, `ego_crops_and_food_features_numba`, `T34_S2_NATIVE_V8=1`) delivered **`1.9–5.5x`** speedups while preserving 100% bitwise action/mask parity.
2. **MPS is launch-bound rather than FLOP-bound at `batch=128`:** On Apple M5 Pro MPS, `torch.compile` and grouped-conv multi-seed stacking (`perf_multiseed_20261013`) failed to help because Metal command-buffer dispatch and `channels_last` layout copies dominated, whereas doubling batch size (`--batch-scale 2` + `--learner-fast`) raised throughput **`1.57x`** (`3,658 -> 5,730 tr/s`) and improved 3M-frame B8 mass (`+10.2`). On CUDA (`RTX 4090`), `torch.compile(mode="reduce-overhead")` delivered **`1.74x`** (`15,170 tr/s`).
3. **Status:** **CLOSED (adopted in production).** All winning optimizations (`perf-sim`, `--learner-fast`, `--batch-scale 2`, `--v61-backend numba`, `--obs-pack`, fused `ego2s` Numba featurizer, and `S2` native `v8` opponent veto) are merged on `main`.
