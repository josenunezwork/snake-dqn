## Exact definition and accounting

cell in the actual ego2s-bv8 local raster. The 31 by 31 crop puts the head at
row 23, column 15: `ahead in [-7, 23]`, `right in [-15, 15]`, inclusive and
rotated with heading. The featurizer's own offset table and owner grid determine
membership. Hero-owned cells and walls do not count as other snakes. Crowded
remains global `alive >= 5`; these strata can overlap. Geometry fixes the
definition; the measured distance distribution does not select a cutoff.
