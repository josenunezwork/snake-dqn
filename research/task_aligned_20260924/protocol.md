# Task-aligned CZ continuation: implementation draft

Status: **IMPLEMENTATION DRAFT — NOT ADMITTED BY THIS DOCUMENT.** This is a new package-utility study. No numerical work may run until the complete implementation and this protocol are independently reviewed, exact bindings are frozen, and a new prospective deadline is recorded.

## Decision

Can a materially larger native stream mixing solo retention with sustained two- and six-snake encounters improve long-game living mass over each frozen parent? All three CZ enemy-visible mark-4608 lineages are retained. Their choice is outcome-informed for an existing enemy-input contract and demonstrated absolute competence; they are not previously designated winners. CW measured earlier CV models and does not establish long-horizon CZ competence.

The unchanged S2 dose line stays closed. This package changes the experience distribution and collection horizon as well as dose. It cannot isolate the cause of a gain. Failed or provisional counterfactual results supply no training evidence. Rejected banks, held trajectories, and selected failure records never enter optimization.

One model and one optimizer serve persistent E16 native collection runtimes with physical S1/S2/S6 rosters. New hero-terminal S6 collection is an explicit artifact-local contract; stock S2 validation must not be bypassed or relabelled. Death is terminal. Live frame-limit truncation bootstraps. Opponents use the frozen scripted pool, not training snapshots. Exact collector, mask, reward and input bindings remain mandatory before admission.

## Fixed dose, selected only by hardware qualification

Rollout length 16; one exact-coverage SGD epoch; minibatch ceiling 256. Scheduled runtime cycle is `[solo, S2, S2, S6, S6]`. This is 20/40/40 percent of scheduled rollouts, not a guarantee of episode or valid-row fractions. All actual valid rows, simulator frames, opponent actions, network calls, state evaluations, optimizer steps, and reset counts are reported. The checkpoint_files_hashed counter covers input-loader verification only; output sealing and harness authentication reads are separate and are not claimed as total physical file I/O.

Maximum final dose is 39,060 completed rollouts per lineage, equivalent to at most 9,999,360 scheduled hero slots. Qualification measures 30 complete cycles (150 discarded rollouts, at most 38,400 hero rows and 150 Adam updates) on a disposable restoration of source 6101. Hardware-only timing, synchronized on MPS, selects the largest multiple of five rollouts no greater than 39,060 whose twice-conservative forecast fits the 25,200-second per-lineage cap. The same selected dose applies to all three lineages and is sealed before the first science update. If fewer than 7,815 rollouts fit, stop for resource infeasibility; no smaller opportunistic science run. This stopping dose is not selected using loss or gameplay. Qualification data is discarded. New serving qualification uses at most 12,288 learned batch frames, 6,144 anchor frames and 128 batch/scalar parity frames; the new lifecycle fixture uses 17 frames. Combined with 38,400 training slots, the maximum is 56,977 completed native frames under a 60,000-frame cap. Qualification permits at most four checkpoint loads, 6,000 model calls, 150,000 evaluated model rows, 150 discarded optimizer updates, 38,400 SGD samples, 4,096 episode starts and 256 preview calls. Every extra operation is explicitly counted. The six-snake masked collector and saved-record fixtures are new integration checks; no old qualification is replayed.

Final endpoints alone decide behavior. Fixed intermediate checkpoint marks may record training telemetry but are not evaluated or selected. No partial arm restart is available. Shared full-state continuation with fresh procedural worlds is not a replay/RNG resume.

## One final comparison

Freeze a new seed namespace before any interaction. After all final endpoints and fixed marks are sealed, materialize 32 procedural native worlds per profile. One initial world per ID, no teacher-solvability selection or resampling. All roles share the same ex-ante seed mapping; subsequent random consumption may diverge by policy. Verify initial descriptor identity within world pairs. Training and evaluation namespaces and generated seed IDs must be disjoint. This is a bounded provenance boundary, with no all-history initial-geometry novelty claim.

Profiles: solo; `food_pressure` with four greedy-food and one random-safe opponent; `mixed` with two greedy-food and three random-safe opponents. Horizon 3,000, fixed CPU inference with E32 world batching for every role. Evaluate each of three parents and three final candidates exactly once per world/profile: 576 hero episodes, at most 1,728,000 simulator lane frames. Evaluate greedy-food and random-safe hero anchors once per world/profile: 192 episodes, at most 576,000 more frames. Total evaluation maximum 768 episodes and 2,304,000 lane frames. Qualification has separately enumerated reset/fixture counts and is not hidden inside this evaluation budget.

Primary metric is total-horizon living mass, including zero mass after death. Report every world, lineage and profile; food, alive-time fraction, endpoints, boost use and encounter exposure remain visible. Pair by world within each lineage. Three lineages remain three training replications; 32 world pairs do not create 32 learners. All intervals are screening intervals without a familywise or powered population claim.

Practical gain proposal: each lineage achieves a mean mass increase of at least max(10 percent of parent mass, 1 mass unit) in both opponent profiles and a positive paired 95 percent lower bound. Solo paired 95 percent lower bounds must be no lower than minus 5 percent of parent mean mass/food and minus 0.02 for alive-time fraction. The absolute gain floor avoids calling a negligible gain over a near-zero parent useful. These are prospective product tolerances, not retroactive changes to old gates or power-derived margins. At least eight worlds in each opponent profile must expose every parent and the greedy-food anchor to a live enemy body within 16 cells after frame 16; random and candidate exposure remain descriptive. Clear interval-demonstrated harm takes precedence, then inadequate exposure is inconclusive, then all gain/retention atoms advance the package, then all six gain-interval upper bounds below their practical margins select futility; other results are inconclusive. See protocol-evaluation.md for every exact atom. Retention failures take headline precedence. Passing only permits considering the separate strict tournament gate, never automatic promotion.

## Finite resource envelope

Proposed stage wall caps: qualification 1,800 seconds; three training jobs at 25,200 each; six model-evaluation jobs at 900 each; anchors 900; saved-output analysis 600; independent audit 900. Total 85,200 guarded seconds. A new 86,400-second elapsed window includes all orchestration and a separate 120-second handoff reserve, leaving 1,080 seconds of scheduling headroom. Before each stage and again after shared-lock acquisition, every remaining cap plus handoff must fit. A failed or ambiguous child stops. Recovery budget is zero; no quiet retry.

Two intraop and one interop CPU threads, RSS at most 8 GiB, MPS driver at most 24 GiB, and at least 9.6 GiB available host RAM. Existing shared locks and Mac monitor serialize numerical children. Per-stage physical caps, complete source closure, exact source/checkpoint metadata, new collector qualification fixtures, and the independent implementation review remain admission blockers until bound in intent.
