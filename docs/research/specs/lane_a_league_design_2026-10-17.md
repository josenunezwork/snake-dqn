# Lane A: a bounded league to beat released B8 — 2026-10-17

**Status: design proposal, not an executed experiment or a ratified launch.** This change writes only this document. Code, checkpoints, serving defaults and running jobs are outside this task. All numerical rules below labelled **proposed** are fixed design choices for a future registration, not findings. Implement, test, resolve the stated launch prerequisites, then commit a hash-bound registration before scientific gameplay. No guarantee of improvement is implied.

**Recommendation:** one main learner, one main exploiter and one league exploiter per independent replica, scheduled serially on one device; five replicas for inference. Start with three learner slots and three frozen-pool slots per world. Preserve the current ego2s-bv8 network, reward/TD/anchor recipe and **256 ingested replay transitions per optimizer update**. Use a small immutable checkpoint pool with PFSP inside protected opponent families. Screen at 8M main transitions; only a GO permits preparation of a 20M final. Do not start with 8–16 concurrent learners or mutate the recipe to meet a throughput target.

## 2. League population, curriculum and frozen pool

### 2.1 Roles and independent replicas — proposed

Run five independent replicas, IDs `r0..r4`, each warm-started **weights-only from released B8-s3**. Replicas share immutable initial anchors but no adaptive checkpoints, payoff data, replay, PBT donors or training RNG. Replicate IDs are not the old B8 training-seed IDs. Resolve actual fresh seed identities through the seed audit; do not assume any numeric range is unused. This estimates fine-tuning variability conditional on the one released initializer. It is not five independent B8 pretraining runs. T7's uncertainty pooled five training-seed effects, with df=4 (`research/redesign_t7_phase_r_20261016/PREREGISTRATION_T7.md:39-44,94-100`); six slots or related population descendants cannot substitute for that replication.

| Role within each replica | Objective and assignment | Budget and reset rule |
|---|---|---|
| Main M | Robust candidate; N=3 learner-controlled seats, three frozen seats; ordinary individual reward, no team reward or shared kill credit. | 8M initial learner decisions; after GO, continue to 20M total. Only its registered final is eligible for strict evaluation. |
| Main exploiter ME | Find a weakness of frozen M: N=1; 80% of worlds have all five opponents on the latest M snapshot, 20% use fixed anchors. Same reward and 256 cadence. | 2M total before initial screen, 4M total by final. Reset weights to B8 at each scheduled 1M burst, with fresh optimizer/target initialization and empty replay/labels. No unbounded pursuit of an unbeatable main. |
| League exploiter LE | Find a specialist against historical main/ME/LE support: N=1; five opponents drawn from historical-family PFSP, with 20% anchor worlds. | Same 2M/4M cap. Alternate B8 and the oldest retained main as weights-only initializer on successive 1M bursts; record lineage. |
| Matched static-pool control C | N=3 and identical recipe/initializer, but no admission, PFSP adaptation or trained exploiters. It receives the fixed initial family fallback mixture below. | 8M per replica, once. This tests the complete adaptive-league intervention against equally long static mixed-slot training. Exploiter compute is reported separately, so this is not an equal-total-compute comparison. |

At M=2M and 6M, pause the main at a registered boundary, freeze M, run ME then LE for 1M each, assess admissions, and resume M. For a GO continuation, repeat at M=10M and 16M. Maintain the same main optimizer/replay when continuing in the same process, or require a validated full-state resume before leaving that process. Freeze pool versions for each 2M main interval; admissions become visible only at the next interval and actor segment boundary. A changed pool never rewrites old replay provenance. Internal 2M checkpoints train the pool; the 8M and 20M finals alone decide the respective scientific stages.

This serial schedule limits GPU contention and makes interaction reproducible at declared barriers. It is a measured design alternative to concurrent population training, not a claim of equivalent wall-clock behavior. The advisor explicitly warned that actor CPU capacity constrains multiple trainers (`docs/research/advice/2026-10-17_a100_vs_h100.md:61-64`), and newer smoke-03 evidence already saturates one learner.

### 2.2 Immutable identities and capacity — proposed

Six permanent identities: B8 bare; frp3-s12 bare; frp3-s12+v8; champion_a5+v8; `scripted:greedy_food`; `scripted:random_safe`. A policy ID binds **checkpoint SHA + architecture/obs spec + action-mask rules + wrapper variant/source SHA + configuration**, not a display name. Bare and wrapped FRP are distinct policies. Pin registry bytes for B8/FRP and resolve champion from its verified registry; never accept a silent fallback. Release identities/wrappers are documented in `docs/research/b8_s3_release_2026-10-17.md:148-176`; current opponent construction is vector61-specific (`research/redesign_m3c_20261008/train_m3c.py:1419-1438`). Frozen vector61 opponents do not reopen vector61 training (`docs/STATUS.md:47`).

Active capacity: **16 identities per replica**, six permanent plus ten dynamic. Protect the newest two main snapshots, the oldest main snapshot, and up to two witnesses that most strongly expose a current-main weakness. No immutable artifact is deleted when retired: retirement removes sampling eligibility, preserves records, and leaves an archive for the ladder. Limit each actor's resident dynamic BV8 models to four. Sample the full eligible roster first, then process policy groups in deterministic microbatches, loading/evicting immutable weights as needed; residency never restricts a draw or renormalizes PFSP. Publish marginal exposures, draw probabilities and cache misses. CPU/GPU eviction must never silently substitute another opponent. This may be slower than a small-pool smoke, so include cache costs in the matched league timing.

Admission is evaluated on training-only payoff worlds, separate from development and gate worlds:

1. Snapshot every 2M M decisions; make the latest M mandatory at the next interval even if it is weaker. “Admitted” means useful training support, not promoted.
2. For ME/LE, after each 1M burst, require valid bytes/finite actions and 64 paired comparison worlds per context. Admit if its contextual mass-preference score against current M is at least 0.60 in either fixed background context below, with the corresponding 90% lower bound above 0.50; alternatively use a protected-witness result only if both entrant and current-main comparisons already exist with the exact same immutable identities/context under the panel budget below, and the entrant meets the same threshold while M does not. No extra unbudgeted witness games are launched. Both pathways and their evidence are logged. These adaptive tests select training opponents only.
3. When full, retire the oldest unprotected dynamic entry whose response vector differs from a retained entry by <=0.05 in every adequately sampled panel cell (at least 64 worlds/cell). If none qualifies, retire the oldest unprotected entry; log loss of diversity and retain archive access. Deterministic ties: policy SHA. Never retire the six anchors or witnesses to make a chart look stronger.
4. An entrant with missing statistics remains probationary: two bounded panel assessments maximum, then archive without admission. Missing/corrupt policy bytes halt dispatch; they do not earn zero probability and disappear unnoticed.

### 2.3 PBT boundary — proposed

**A0 uses no hyperparameter PBT.** Main, exploiters and control retain the fixed training recipe, including 256 transitions/update; exploiter reinitialization is a diversity mechanism, not a claim that full PBT has been tested. The broad advice suggested mutating learning rate, n-step, replay ratio and boost costs (`docs/research/advice/2026-10-17_new_lane.md:39-44`), but smoke-02 rejects changing update frequency as a speed claim (`docs/research/t16_colab_smoke02_results_2026-10-17.md:23-24,46-47`) and the retrospective closes several reward/loss routes.

If A0 passes, a **separately registered A1** may add a second main per replica and limited PBT: assess every 4M decisions on training-only fixed panels; replace the weaker main only when its panel mass is >=10% below the donor and neither product survival guard improves; copy donor weights, reset optimizer/replay/target and explicitly start a new lineage. Mutate only learning rate by a preregistered draw from `{0.8,1.2}`, bounded to `[0.5,2] × original`; keep 256 cadence, reward, boost cost, n-step, precision and architecture fixed. Never copy across independent replicas, rank on gate worlds, or call a weights-only reset an exact resume. Predeclare per-replica final selection before A1; the A0 budget below does not authorize A1 or 40M runs.

Main/control schedule time is cumulative learner decisions since fine-tune start; it does not reset at 2M pauses or the 8M screen. Each explicitly weights-reset exploiter burst starts its own schedule at zero; record this because a 1M exploiter never reaches the main's later schedule regime. Expand every default into launch inputs rather than relying on future parser defaults. Replace only initialization/seed identity, bounded transitions, roster flags and measured device/performance settings; do not carry the smoke's billion-transition placeholder or training namespace into a scientific run. The original schedule definitions are `research/redesign_m3c_20261008/train_m3c.py:183-212`; any different fine-tuning schedule is a new recipe arm.

Add an opt-in league configuration with `learner_slots=N` (1..6; N=0 only in evaluation fixtures), pool manifest, role and replica identity. For every world, construct a seeded permutation of six seats at segment creation; exactly N are learner seats. Assignment persists through deaths/respawns to the end of the segment. Current code's binary choice and stream indexing occur at `research/redesign_m3c_20261008/train_m3c.py:2804,2933-2936`; these are explicit edit sites.

Only learner-driven, actually alive pre-action rows enter replay, the on-policy label ring, epsilon/anchor schedules, transition budgets or training diagnostics. Pool decisions count separately. Death contributes the existing terminal return exactly once; respawn cannot inherit a previous life's n-step queue. Flush segment-end tails using existing bootstrap semantics before reassignment. Pool and frozen-main versions remain stable within a segment; learner versions may refresh through the existing publication path. Preserve the advisory normal-action fallback and existing BV8 features; those features are not a serving veto (`research/redesign_m3c_20261008/train_m3c.py:2811-2867`).

### 3.2 CPU/GPU policy batching — proposed implementation

Build shared prepared game state once per frame on CPU. Partition live rows by `(obs spec, exact policy ID, wrapper, device)` and restore actions through a stable inverse `(world,slot)` index. Build BV8 rasters/scalars only for BV8-driven rows; vector61 features for FRP/champion; scripted actions directly. One network with different weights cannot consume a concatenation of opponents as if it were one model.

Start with the existing actor-owned model architecture: one batched main forward per actor, plus one `no_grad` forward per resident frozen BV8 policy across all its live rows. Frozen modules are eval-only and excluded from optimizers. On CUDA, keep frozen weights resident; use bounded batches and transfer actions back once per group/frame. On Mac, measure CPU frozen BV8 versus MPS frozen BV8 under the same main learner load before pinning one. For A0 explicitly configure scripts on CPU and vector61 on CPU with `--v61-backend numba`, using the existing merged-vector61 option only where validated. This is a proposed configuration: the current backend fallback is Python (`research/redesign_m3c_20261008/train_m3c.py:1412`). `MergedVector61Opponents` already groups compatible vector policies, but its device path changes math (`research/redesign_m3c_20261008/train_m3c.py:68-74,2725-2741,2779-2793`); it is not a ready BV8 league server.

A central GPU inference broker or stacked-parameter/vmap implementation is a later, separately verified performance change, only if profiling shows actor inference binds. Do not add it to A0's first correctness slice. Report separate learner-update GPU time, learner-action inference, frozen inference, raster/v8/vector features, sim, transfers, queue wait and policy lag. The reported 84% GPU busy cannot be assigned automatically to opponent inference; frozen inference competes with the already busy learner. Cap actor queues at the tested 2–4 frames and compare lag to the same-N control; adding actors is not a default cure (`docs/research/advice/2026-10-17_t17_plan_review.md:57-69,106-113`).

Strict evaluation retains rowwise Mac forwarding and pinned profiles. No claim of bitwise CPU/GPU action identity follows from training batch equivalence; exact float32 tie differences are documented (`docs/STATUS.md:70-76`). Training batching must pass legal-action/order/feature tests; strict parity uses the serving-compatible path.

## 4. Sampling: PFSP without losing anchors

All rules in this section are **proposed** implementations of the advisory PFSP direction (`docs/research/advice/2026-10-17_new_lane.md:39-57`); none exists in the binary roster path cited above.

Main frozen-seat marginals:

| Family | Probability | Within-family rule |
|---|---:|---|
| Permanent anchors | 40% | B8 15 percentage points; FRP+v8 10; FRP bare 5; champion+v8 5; each scripted bot 2.5. |
| Main history | 40% | PFSP among admitted main snapshots, with latest/oldest protected. |
| Exploiters | 20% | 10% ME, 10% LE, PFSP within each. |

Each slot draws its family then policy, with replacement; identical policies can legitimately fill several seats. Sample and freeze at segment start. Empty history falls back to B8; empty ME falls back to FRP+v8; empty LE falls back equally to the two scripts. Record the effective distribution explicitly. The static control uses these initial fallback probabilities throughout. For N=3, a frozen seat is not guaranteed in every family within each individual world; quotas are marginal across declared roster blocks. Use deterministic stratified scheduling across blocks to approximate these marginals without enforcing an unregistered per-world curriculum.

**Define the score before weighting.** The game has no native two-player win label matching the mass objective. For ordered policies (i,j), run 64 training-only paired H5000 worlds in each of two backgrounds: four fixed B8 opponents, or four fixed FRP+v8 opponents. Seat i and j in the other two slots, then swap their seats on a matched copy of each world. A pair scores 1 if i's average full-horizon mass integral exceeds j's, 0 if lower and 0.5 on an exact tie. Treat the seed plus swap as one statistical unit. Record both contexts separately and death/kill/length metrics alongside. This measures contextual mass preference, not a zero-sum game value or tournament winning probability; frozen rosters and full-horizon mass follow the evidence distinction in `docs/research/t13b_six_snake_roster_2026-10-17_results.md:25,53-55`.

For each context, use Beta(1,1) smoothing: p=(wins+0.5×ties+1)/(n+2). Main PFSP uses p equal to the smaller of the two context estimates; without 64 worlds/context use p=0.5 and mark unmeasured. Within eligible family F:

`q(j | i,F) = 0.10 / |F| + 0.90 × (1-p_ij)^2 / sum_k (1-p_ik)^2`.

If the denominator is zero, choose uniform. This retains easy opponents and prioritizes weaknesses; do not use p(1-p) as the default, which would downweight near-certain losses. Cap a single dynamic policy at 50% of its family's probability when at least two are eligible, redistributing excess proportionally. Permanent family masses never change with PFSP. ME concentrates on the frozen current main by the role rule; LE applies the same PFSP to its historical target family.

Payoff updates happen only at declared 2M barriers. Policy versions are immutable: a new main has a new row, no inherited evidence masquerading as observations. Cache earlier immutable pair results, retain age/count/context, and allow at most six comparison jobs per barrier, including every admission, probation and refresh assessment: two current-main/anchor jobs (B8 and FRP+v8); two current-main/ME-or-LE jobs (new entrant first, then its one allowed probation retry, otherwise the strongest retained exploiter); one current-main/history job alternating latest and oldest; and one round-robin current-main/dynamic job. A job includes both contexts and both seat orders; reuse its reciprocal score for admission. Deduplicate jobs, fill vacancies from the rotation, and never exceed six. Extra admission/witness requests wait for a free job or remain unassessed; a candidate unassessed at two successive barriers is archived. New main rows reset counts, so some entries will remain uniform probation until sampled; this is disclosed sparse PFSP, not a fully refreshed payoff matrix. Deduplicate equal IDs and never feed development, extension, confirmation or strict results back into training. Adaptive data are deliberately confined to this training ledger.

## 6. Predeclared decisions, compute and stopping limits

### 6.1 A0 screen rules — proposed, before any scientific run

At 8M per main/control, compute per-replica paired effects, average replicas equally, and report a hierarchical paired bootstrap (10,000 draws; fixed analysis RNG in the registration): resample replica clusters then matched world clusters, preserving all policy comparisons. For medians recompute the pre-decision length distribution in each resample. Report per-replica results; do not treat frames, slots or shared-replica descendants as independent n. This extends T13b's paired-world bootstrap (`docs/research/t13b_six_snake_roster_2026-10-17_results.md:53-55`); it is a development decision, not strict inference.

Define D_P and D_W as M−B8 hero deaths/1k on P/W (negative is better); G_P/G_W as relative median-length gains (divide by paired B8 median, fail analysis if zero/missing). Define D_PC as M−C deaths/1k on P. These metrics jointly target the advisor's crowded-play premise (`docs/research/advice/2026-10-17_new_lane.md:49-57`; `docs/research/advice/2026-10-17_after_t13.md:65-72`).

Apply the following in order:

1. **INVALID/INCOMPLETE stop:** missing checkpoint/records, failed hash/seed audit, nonzero operational exit, exhausted wall/credit cap, restore failure or corrupted replay. Preserve the failed attempt; no scientific null conclusion, automatic retry or substitute replica. An actual numerical/behavioral tripwire below is instead KILL.
2. **KILL:** any main/control/exploiter numerical or action/boost tripwire; or at complete L2 any Tier-1-style screen guard fails vs B8 (−0.03 pooled alive per mix, −0.15 per-replica scripted floor, 0.50 absolute episode floor); or point D_P>=0; or D_W>+0.02; or either G<−0.05. Also KILL the adaptive-league mechanism if D_PC>=0: more total league compute has not beaten the matched static training on its primary.
3. **GO** only if all remaining conditions hold: D_P<=−0.05 deaths/1k and its one-sided 90% upper bound <0; D_W<=0; G_W>=+0.05 and G_P>=0; D_PC<0 with its 90% upper bound <0; at least 4/5 replicas improve D_P. Tier-1-style screen guards must pass; report Tier 2 but do not claim strict qualification. The 0.05 effect target is proposed, motivated by but not estimated from T13b's 0.096 roster gap; it is not a promise of power.
4. **EXTEND** exactly once for any complete, valid result satisfying neither KILL nor GO: add 64 new product worlds per replica per roster and 96/32/32 historical mix worlds, for all M/C/B8 comparisons, with frozen checkpoints. Recompute on the combined data. No added training and no changed thresholds. Apply KILL then GO again; otherwise record **INCONCLUSIVE, close A0**. Exploratory intervals after the adaptive extension do not confer strict confidence. A later independent strict test handles selection.

GO authorizes planning the fixed continuation to 20M, not a changed recipe or automatic launch/promotion. At 20M evaluate a fresh L2-shaped screen versus B8, with the same product/guard thresholds (control comparison remains an 8M mechanism result, not a matched 20M causal contrast). No extension at 20M. Only a pass advances to L3/L4 preparation. If planned counts lack precision, record that; do not enlarge them after seeing results.

**Behavior tripwires, proposed:** preserve the trainer's >90% single-action collapse over the full 100k action window (`research/redesign_m3c_20261008/train_m3c.py:3414-3417`). Preserve greedy boost ceilings >2% overall and >1% at lengths <700 per 100k telemetry window, including exploiters; these match the explicit LOCAL30 bounds (`docs/research/specs/t11_prereg_2026-10-17.md:61,73-76`). Add a low-boost style guard: after 2M, three consecutive windows with greedy boost <0.05% is KILL; if there are <1,000 boost-eligible decisions/window, classify the window as insufficient support and use the next complete window, not zero. Report eligible and all-decision denominators separately; ceiling retains the existing all-decision definition. This floor is a new proposal below B8's descriptive 0.15% executed boost, not a claim that those metrics are identical. No reward for crossing a boost quota. Emit non-finite loss/Q/gradient and existing numerical halt reasons immediately; do not relax tripwires for an exploiter that appears useful.
