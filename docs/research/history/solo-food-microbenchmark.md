# Solo-Food Microbenchmark Series (Studies P–W, 2026-09-13)

## Question & Goal

On a controlled 16-frame, length-1 solo food-seeking microbenchmark (`BatchSim` Watch stepping, `raster31v3` observations, `RasterDuelingNetwork`, `144` train tasks and two `288`-task held-out splits: new worlds and new placements at distances 3, 5, and 7; frozen code pin `56e0e92434ae510a84ebaf6f58cf41c3e4421b04`), can `RasterDuelingNetwork` learn reliable greedy food collection (`>= 95%` train, `>= 90%` per held-out split, `>= 75%` per heading $\times$ placement cell, `<= 5 pp` midpoint-to-final drop across `3/3` seeds), why does online PQN fail where supervised behavior cloning (BC) succeeds, and do models trained on 16-frame single-pellet tasks transfer zero-shot to 64-frame replenishing native solo play?

## What Was Learned

1. **Unconstrained Unchosen Q-Values Cause Policy Collapse Under Scalar Return Fitting:** Study U vs Study V isolated the core failure mode of value-only learning on sparse trajectories: regressing $Q(s, a_{\text{chosen}})$ toward scalar Monte Carlo return $G_t$ drives MAE below `0.01`, yet greedy play fails (`21%–47%`) because unvisited actions $a \neq a_{\text{chosen}}$ receive zero gradient and sit above $Q(s, a_{\text{chosen}})$. Adding a `0.1` cross-entropy action-ranking term immediately restored `100% / 100% / 100%` greedy success across all seeds and checkpoints while preserving `100%` within-`0.05` return calibration at update 125.
2. **Teacher-State Accuracy and Closed-Loop Gameplay Efficiency Are Distinct:** A policy can achieve `100%` closed-loop food collection with `13.89%–59.38%` teacher-state agreement by taking longer multi-step detours (`0.50–0.72` distance efficiency in Studies P, Q, R), or achieve `83.33%` teacher-state accuracy on straight segments while failing `67%` of episodes due to missed first turns (Study R seed 2303).
3. **16-Frame Length-1 Microbenchmarks Do Not Transfer to Growing Native Play:** Training exclusively on length-1, 16-frame single-pellet tasks leaves the network OOD once food replenishes, frame progress saturates (`> 16`), and body growth unlocks boost (`L >= 4`), motivating continuous native-trajectory imitation and RL (`TASK_ALIGNED_AND_APEX_SERIES.md`).

## Why It Closed / Current Status

Closed on 2026-09-13 after Study W (`transfer_not_reliable`) proved that 16-frame microbenchmark training must be replaced by native multi-pellet H64/H256 training and evaluation.
