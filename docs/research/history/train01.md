# Resident A100 ExIt / PPO Fine-Tuning (`train01`, Attempts 01–16)

## Question and Goal

Can an Expert Iteration (`ExIt`) / PPO-style resident A100 training loop (`research/sandbox_a_resident/`) warm-started from bare `b8-s3` (`Ego2sNet`, with pre-warmed value critic `E360` and online search-teacher targets) complete a 384-update (`200k`+ frame) run under the Resume-by-Default snapshot contract and clear the `+25` equal-mix H5000 mass nomination bar against bare `b8-s3`?

## Final Verdict and Key Numbers

1. **Final Scientific Outcome (`train01-15` / `TRAIN01_16.md` — `BELOW_NOMINATION_BAR`):**
   - After resolving all memory, staging, numerical, and snapshot-export blockers across attempts `06`–`14`, `train01-15` completed all **`384` updates** cleanly on Colab A100 ([`research/sandbox_a_resident/TRAIN01_15.md`](../../../research/sandbox_a_resident/TRAIN01_15.md), [`TRAIN01_16.md`](../../../research/sandbox_a_resident/TRAIN01_16.md)).
   - On the 160-world paired H5000 evaluation bank, the final checkpoint (`update 384`) achieved **`+8.09` equal-mix mass vs `b8-s3`** (`95% CI [-18.66, +34.85]`), and the best intermediate checkpoint (`update 320`) achieved **`+14.23` equal-mix mass vs `b8-s3`** (`95% CI [-13.42, +41.88]`). Both were positive directionally—making `train01-15` the first online post-B8 fine-tune to finish above `b8-s3` on the 160-world screen—but fell short of the pre-registered **`+25.0` mass nomination bar** ([`research/sandbox_a_resident/TRAIN01_16.md`](../../../research/sandbox_a_resident/TRAIN01_16.md), [`docs/notes/big-lever-experiments.md`](../../notes/big-lever-experiments.md)).
2. **Engineering Facts Preserved from Consolidated `TRAIN01_06`, `TRAIN01_07`, and `TRAIN01_10`:**
   - **Observation-view memory leak fix (`TRAIN01_06`, commit `b4225e25`):** In `_extract_obs_single(batch_obs, idx)`, numpy array views (`tactical[idx]`, `strategic[idx]`, `scalars[idx]`, `12,443 B` combined) kept alive the entire 24-row `E24` batch (`298,632 B`, `24x` amplification = `47.78 GB` at `160,000` rows). Adding `.copy()` on each slice reduced the `160,000`-row buffer upper bound to **`1.99 GB`** ([`research/sandbox_a_resident/TRAIN01.md`](../../../research/sandbox_a_resident/TRAIN01.md), [`research/sandbox_a_resident/VERIFICATION_TRAIN01_06.json`](../../../research/sandbox_a_resident/VERIFICATION_TRAIN01_06.json)).
   - **Startup 256-frame distribution screen & terminal support rule (`TRAIN01_06`, `TRAIN01_07`, commit `152af938`):** Replaced the `H5000` (`30,000`-step) startup distribution screen with a `256`-frame (`1,536`-step, `19.53x` smaller) prefix screen and required `>= 16` pooled terminal events (`MIN_TERMINAL_SUPPORT_EVENTS = 16`) before enforcing the `15 pp` collision/starvation cause-share check, because over `256` frames a 6-episode sample observes only `~1`–`2` deaths (`1` death shifts cause share by `50`–`100 pp`). On the 1-thread CPU fallback in `train01-07`, `scripted` passed in `1,411.95 s` (`mean_length = 123.51`, `alive_ratio = 0.9961`, `1` terminal event -> `SKIPPED_LOW_SUPPORT`), while `frozen` (`201/256` frames at `2,700.01 s`) and `mixed` (`188/256` frames at `1,092.01 s`) hit `INCOMPLETE_TIMEOUT` ([`research/sandbox_a_resident/VERIFICATION_TRAIN01_07.json`](../../../research/sandbox_a_resident/VERIFICATION_TRAIN01_07.json)).
   - **Background sparse source delivery (`TRAIN01_10`, `background-sparse-v1`):** Because the repository had grown to `12,943` tracked files (`571.1 MB`), running `git fetch --depth 1` synchronously inside `deliver_source('fetch')` exceeded the 30-s `colab exec` RPC cap (`30.014 s` in `train01-08`, `30.009 s` in `train01-09`; locally measured at `32.379 s`–`>35 s`). `background-sparse-v1` packed only the `191` tracked runtime files (`src/`, `configs/`, `research/sandbox_a_resident/`, `research/t34_*/`, `research/t15_*/`, `925,402 B` compressed) and installed them in **`0.025`–`0.035 s`** (`>850x` faster; [`research/sandbox_a_resident/VERIFICATION_TRAIN01_10.json`](../../../research/sandbox_a_resident/VERIFICATION_TRAIN01_10.json)).

## What Was Learned

- **Pre-warming the value critic (`E360`, `EV = 0.4404`) prevents the catastrophic value-bootstrap collapse seen in un-warmed RL fine-tunes:** Unlike T23 (`-80.8`) and S1 (`-12.5` / `-14.9`), `train01-15` maintained positive mass deltas (`+8.09` to `+14.23`) over `b8-s3`.
- **Low-temperature teacher distillation (`T ≈ 0.0084`) overflows `float32` `log_softmax`:** Scaling `Q` values (`~100`) by `1 / T` (`~118.5`) yields logits near `12,000`, where `float32` precision loss corrupts gradients unless `log_softmax` and KL/cross-entropy are computed in `float64` ([`TRAIN01_12.md`](../../../research/sandbox_a_resident/TRAIN01_12.md)).
- **NumPy slice views inside experience buffers silently pin parent batch arrays:** Always `.copy()` individual observation slices when extracting rows from multi-world batch tensors into long-lived replay buffers.

## Why It Closed / Current Status

**Closed (`COMPLETE` / `BELOW_NOMINATION_BAR`, 2026-10-24).** `train01` demonstrated that critic-warmed ExIt fine-tuning preserves `b8-s3` competence (`+8.09` to `+14.23` mass), but its gain was too small relative to the `+25` bar and the much larger gains from test-time survival-gated search (`exact-stop-l2-prime`, `+73.17` confirmed mass).
