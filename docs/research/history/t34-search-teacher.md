# T34 ExIt-B8 Search Teacher, Shadow Design, and Claim A Distillation (`t34-search-teacher`)

## Question and Goal

Could an exact-simulator lookahead search teacher (`GridBatchSim`) beat the bare `b8-s3` (`ego2s-bv8`) checkpoint (sha256 `3e64978337b67b7acaf4daa93bdefa827d8296bcb0f63413886a1aaad12eb4f8`) on H5000 worlds across all three strict opponent mixes (`scripted`, `frozen`, `mixed`) and be distilled via supervised DAgger projection (`KL` anchor + decisive hinge, no TD term) into a single bare `Ego2sNet` student (**Claim A**), under the owner-ratified T34 Campaign Contract ([`SUCCESS_CONTRACT.md`](../final_run_20261019/SUCCESS_CONTRACT.md), [`FINAL_PLAN.md`](../final_run_20261019/FINAL_PLAN.md), [`DESIGN_V2_SHADOW.md`](../final_run_20261019/DESIGN_V2_SHADOW.md), [`P7_STATISTICS.md`](../final_run_20261019/P7_STATISTICS.md))? This lineage tracks the overnight Q0/Q1 host checks, the Phase 0 stochastic `N8/H128` premise test, the T33 many-worlds batched continuation teacher, the M1–M4 rolling-shadow and LAD measurements, the T34 ExIt-B8 teacher engine (`teacher_v2`, shadow batching, `K=12` fan-out, and A5 L4 gate), the Round 0 / Round 0-D teacher premise tests, the student fit cache and Claim A `D0` distillation diagnostic, the `S0–S2` speed program, and the `O1/O2` offense-harvest follow-ons.

## What Was Learned

1. **Stochastic rollouts (`eps > 0`) are too noisy for small-`N` action ranking:** Two independent `N8/H128` draws (`eps=0.02`) agreed on only `32.7%` (`98/300`) of `L >= 600` calibration decisions, whereas deterministic `eps=0` continuation along a rolling shadow trajectory (`D=300` or `D=500`) turns every surviving greedy decision into a zero-branch-cost confirmation and concentrates search on actual predicted deaths.
2. **Distinguish true horizon rescues from doomed death delays:** On the 64-world T3 ceiling bank, `33 / 75` dense-search overrides had no `H=300` survivor anywhere in the `D=300` window (`1/2/4`-frame delays before unavoidable death). Filtering to `horizon_rescue` (`39/42 = 92.9%` recovered by bisection+radius-3 at `23.08×` less work) prevents poisoning teacher labels with doomed stalls.
3. **Single-step behavioral cloning / hinge distillation cannot absorb multi-step lookahead rescues without breaking anchor behaviour:** Because `horizon_rescue` states represent `~0.12%` (`225 / 190,779`) of trajectory frames where `b8-s3`'s local features strongly prefer the greedy trap action, any hinge weight/temperature strong enough to flip `>= 70%` of held-out decisive actions destroys `b8-s3` anchor agreement within `< 512` updates (`D0` `KILL`).
4. **Sequential rollouts on L4 are host-bound (`v8` + digest), not GPU-bound:** Batch-1 CUDA inference (`4.35 ms`) is slower than 1-thread CPU PyTorch (`0.93 ms`) or ONNX (`0.138 ms`). Moving to `K=12` single-thread `W=1` CPU processes per L4 lane, contiguous numpy digests (`1.71×`), and `S2` numba `v8` (`1.90×`) yielded an order-of-magnitude throughput gain.
