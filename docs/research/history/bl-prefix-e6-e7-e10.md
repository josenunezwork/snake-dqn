# Big-Lever Prefix Escape Dividend (E6), Frontier-Existence (E7), and Population Bridge (E10)

## Question and Goal

Within the October 21, 2026 Big-Lever program (`research/bl_prefix/`), three linked pre-data registrations tested whether short prefix interventions before late `b8-s3` deaths (`frame >= 2000`, `L >= 700`) could unlock at least `+40` H5000 mass-integral units at the population level: **E6** (`REGISTRATION_E6.md`) tested whether substituting `64` candidate (`R` or `F`) decisions starting `100` decisions before death (before `b8-s3` resumes) improves remaining H5000 mass across `128` death worlds (`44` scripted, `42` frozen, `42` mixed) without harming `128` length-matched (`±100` cells) surviving ordinary worlds; **E7** (`REGISTRATION_E7.md`) tested whether `8` stochastic `b8-s3` prefixes (`5%` non-greedy softmax temperature) rolled out from `100`, `150`, and `200` decisions before death yield a confirmed `>= +20` mass-integral rescue on `>= 20%` of death worlds across `2` independent future RNG streams (`TRANSFER_ADMISSIBLE` vs `FRONTIER_SCARCE`); and **E10** (`REGISTRATION_E10.md`) registered a two-phase population bridge (`θ = mean_m(q_m · μ_m)`) testing whether the one-sided 90% upper bound `UB90(U)` reaches `+40` (`FINITE_MENU_INSUFFICIENT` if `UB90(U) < 40`) ([`research/bl_prefix/README.md`](../../../research/bl_prefix/README.md)).

## Final Verdict and Key Numbers

- **Verdict:** **Built, Mac-preflighted, and held pre-dispatch (`NOT_DISPATCHABLE` / `NOT_DISPATCHED`, `0` scientific Colab units spent).** E6/E7/E10 were resource-ready but scientifically blocked on E2's R/F endpoints, and E9 blocked admission of E2 as designed (E2 was not dispatched), so scientific E6/E7/E10 runs were never launched ([`docs/notes/big-lever-experiments.md`](../../notes/big-lever-experiments.md), `research/bl_ppo/E9_RULE.md`, [`research/bl_prefix/CAP_NOTE.md`](../../../research/bl_prefix/CAP_NOTE.md)). The full-H5000 work bounds were `37.76M` (E6) and `24.96M` (E7) world-steps.
- **Key Numbers and Primary Sources:**
  - **High-range `2**40` seed audit:** `11,224` audited seeds (`source=4096`, `future=3000`, `exploration=4000`, `preflight=128`) in domain `bl-prefix-e6e7-development/20261021/v2` with `0` collisions; `3,998` unburned exploration seeds after retiring `1102882751176` and `1100083338059` (`research/bl_prefix/SEED_AUDIT.json`, SHA-256 `69d6a44a982e3884f431ccd14d2137bf62e610fa52b30b9d13798bf70f9d1a31`; [`research/bl_prefix/OPERATIONAL_BURN_ADDENDUM.md`](../../../research/bl_prefix/OPERATIONAL_BURN_ADDENDUM.md)).
  - **Full-H5000 per-worker step rates and L4 forecasts (`3×` L4 CPU slowdown, `12` workers, `+15 min` setup/export):**
    - **E6:** **`0.0032107433 s/step`** (`47,946` steps in `76.971149 s` across 2 workers) × **`37.76M` maximum world-steps** (`4,096` screened sources, `256` selected replays, `128` terminal-sealer replays, all primary/secondary controls and continuations) = **`520.156944 min`** (`17.338565 u` expected at `2 u/h`; proposed `2.5×` cap **`43.4 u`**, `8.68 u` reserve, `1,041.6 min` timeout vs `4 u` original cap) ([`research/bl_prefix/CAP_NOTE.md`](../../../research/bl_prefix/CAP_NOTE.md), `snake-dqn-artifacts/big-lever/exp/bl-e6e7/production-preflight-v2-e8-check/`).
    - **E7:** **`0.0031417799 s/step`** (`199,034` steps in `312.660509 s` across 2 workers) × **`24.96M` maximum world-steps** (`3` ancestors × `128` death worlds, `8` training prefixes, paired training B8, and `2` confirmation streams) = **`341.745108 min`** (`11.391504 u` expected at `2 u/h`; proposed `2.5×` cap **`28.5 u`**, `5.7 u` reserve, `684.0 min` timeout vs `20 u` original cap) ([`research/bl_prefix/CAP_NOTE.md`](../../../research/bl_prefix/CAP_NOTE.md), `snake-dqn-artifacts/big-lever/exp/bl-e6e7/production-preflight-v2/`).
  - **E10 population bridge constants:** Universal H5000 score ceiling **`B = 2501.5`** (`sum_{t=1..5000}(1+t)/5000`), minimum productive rescue fraction **`r >= 0.163`**, and required target ratio **`τ = (40 + C) / gross <= 1`** (`FINITE_MENU_INSUFFICIENT` iff `UB90(U) < 40`, where `v_m = (|μ|·sqrt(q(1-q)/(N-1)) + q·sqrt(s_U^2/k))^2` under the Cauchy overlap-aware variance bound) ([`research/bl_prefix/REGISTRATION_E10.md`](../../../research/bl_prefix/REGISTRATION_E10.md)).

## What Was Learned

1. **Cost of Full-H5000 Post-Prefix Continuations:** Rolling every prefix intervention out to `H = 5,000` across `8` prefixes and `2` confirmation streams requires `25M–38M` simulator world-steps (forecast `341.7–520.2` min, i.e. `5.7–8.7` L4 hours under the assumed `3×` slowdown), far above the original `4u`/`20u` caps ([`research/bl_prefix/CAP_NOTE.md`](../../../research/bl_prefix/CAP_NOTE.md)).
2. **Two-Phase Population Accounting (`E10`):** Conditioning only on rare late-death roots (`q_m = e_m / N_m`) inflates apparent rescue gains unless multiplied by natural encounter prevalence `q_m`, evaluated against the full dead-tail horizon `(5000 - death_frame + 1)`, and debited by ordinary-world collateral `C` (`τ = (40 + C) / gross <= 1`) ([`research/bl_prefix/REGISTRATION_E10.md`](../../../research/bl_prefix/REGISTRATION_E10.md)).
3. **High-Range `2**40` Seed Namespace & Roster Adapter:** Shifting derived seeds by `+2**40` avoids the crowded 32-bit historical seed space (`11,224` seeds audited with `0` collisions), while wrapping `strict_promotion.materialize_rosters` with ordinal indices preserves exact opponent-roster rotation without modifying protected evaluation code (`research/bl_prefix/SEED_AUDIT.json`, [`research/bl_prefix/ALLOCATION_ADDENDUM.md`](../../../research/bl_prefix/ALLOCATION_ADDENDUM.md)).
