## Frozen configurations and policy

Diagnostic 0 runs first: cosine retrieval in frozen 256-D `phi` and concatenated `[phi,q_base]`, separately for matching served base actions, with k = 5, 15, 50. Only training neighbors vote, and only their exact legal selectable action labels enter survival probabilities. Reports include decisive-vs-ordinary AUROC, decisive TPR strictly above the maximum ordinary risk score (empirical FPR=0), and ungated action-ranking micro/life retention. These are empirical retrieval ceilings on this dataset and split, not mathematical bounds on all classifiers. Query outcomes enter scoring only after neighbor predictions exist.

Six neural configurations use `phi`, `phi+q/context`, or `phi+q/context+geometry`, each for 500 and 1500 updates. A shared per-action 256/256 LayerNorm+SiLU classifier receives action identity, context, and optional geometry, predicting six direct survival logits. Context for the latter two modes includes q, base one-hot, selectable mask, stored length and log1p base margin. Train-only means/stds are frozen in checkpoints. Adam 0.003 with cosine decay, batch 512, seed 3403, row-uniform sampling. Loss is row-normalized BCE on exact legal actions across all admitted rows, with unknown/illegal actions omitted. No additive residual, Q-margin competition, or validation-based training selection.

Every configuration is evaluated at `tau_veto = [0, .001, .005, .01, .02, .05, .1, .2, .3, .5, .7, 1]`, crossed with survival advantage `[null, .1, .25]`: 12 immutable configurations, 432 development points, 6,000 neural updates. The primary null-advantage rule keeps B8 unless `p_surv(base) < tau_veto`, then selects the maximum-probability selectable alternative, independent of q. The optional advantage variants additionally require `p_surv(alternative)-p_surv(base) > advantage`. Threshold zero is the exact B8 no-override control. No validation label determines a selected action or a threshold.

A point is **promising iff micro rescue retention >= 0.70, ordinary flip_rate <= 0.01, and ordinary harm count <= 1**, with nonempty decisive and ordinary denominators. Every report also includes life-macro retention, decisive/life/ordinary counts, ordinary harm rate/count, and life-macro ordinary harm. Ordinary flip_rate is changed actions divided by ordinary rows, a closed-loop proxy, not a rollout measurement. The Pareto frontier maximizes micro and life retention and minimizes both ordinary flip_rate and harm count. All configurations and all threshold points are retained, including failures of the promising criterion.

## Stored geometry and B8's existing inputs

The admitted NPZ contains local/global B8 rasters and 60 scalars, not full simulator snapshots. B8 already receives 30 per-action v8 safety scalars in that 60-D vector (`src/simd_env/ego_raster_bv8.py`), alongside its spatial rasters. B-3 exposes local action-conditioned geometry directly to the survival classifier, bypassing the frozen phi bottleneck; it does not claim these observations are wholly new to B8. The action input has 12 values: seven new crop-derived values (immediate legality, reachable area/length, local reachable fraction, visible TTL-one reach, landing degree, local occupancy, effective boost) and five existing v8 scalars (area_g, head_risky, landing_ok, v5_eligible, spacious). Geometry uses only local/scalar inputs, never exact branch outcomes. See `geometry_b3.py` for the precise feature names, TTL interpretation, boost handling, and visibility limitations. Full-board physical tail reachability cannot be recovered from this crop; it must not be claimed by these diagnostics.

## F4 metric/split note (source metadata only)

F4's micro retention uses the same decisive-row denominator as B-1/B-2: exact/legal base death with at least one exact/legal/selectable survivor, retained only when the selected action is exact and survives (`research/bl_residual/fit.py`, `heldout_metrics`). Thus base-already-surviving rows do **not** dilute its micro denominator. F4 splits by ultimate parent using `bl-residual-f3-split-v1/<parent>` SHA256 modulo five, not by random rows (`data.world_fold`, `F4_REGISTRATION.md`); fold zero supplies its 64 reserve rescue lives and a distinct natural ordinary archive. Sandbox B uses the different `a100-dev/sandbox-b/split-v1` parent salt, and its registry excludes all F4 fold-zero parents. F4 life macro averages all explicit reserve lives and becomes undefined if any has no decisive rows (`fit.heldout_metrics`, `heldout.py`); sandbox life macro averages lives containing decisive rows. F4 harm's natural archive denominator also differs from sandbox ordinary validation rows. These source differences establish non-comparability of the splits/archives; they do not establish a cause for F4's historical performance. No F4 heldout outcomes or protected data were opened.

## Operations and exports

The finite endpoint is all 12 configurations; base runaway timeout 60 minutes, effective 120 minutes (2x), including setup/export. It is a generous operational guard rather than a forecast from CPU smoke. Each config publishes an immutable tar with checkpoint, report, internal hashes, SHA sidecar, and identity completion marker last. Resume skips exactly Mac-verified units and rejects foreign namespace/allocation/recipe, duplicates, or corrupt bundles. The initial ledger is empty. One original plus at most two outcome-blind mechanical completion attempts, fresh packet/output/receipt per attempt, only after verified mechanical failure/stop and missing units; never automatic scientific retries. A completed unit is never recomputed into the result set.

Telemetry starts before feature extraction, emits approximately every two minutes within long stages, and after each complete config. Final exports: per-config units/checkpoints plus `results.json`, `results.csv`, `pareto.json`, `verified-ledger.json`, `timing.json`, and `checkpoints.tar`. The source closure and data hashes are pinned in `b3-packet-01/dispatch-manifest.json`; its committed ten-line sandbox record binds that packet. Preflight and `dispatch.sh --dry-run` perform no allocation. Crossing remains limited to one frozen checkpoint and requires a separate judged registration, fresh paired bank, Mac strict/serving checks and owner release authority.
