## What Was Learned and Why It Closed

- **Lookahead (`L2'`) solves self-trapping (`+83.0` mass), not 1-step hazards (`L1'`)**: `b8-s3`'s `v8` scalar inputs already handle 1-step collisions (`exact_stop` saves `3/4` branches on `98.8%` of dense roots); the real gain comes from `H=64` continuation rollouts on the `~0.1–0.5%` of decisions where bare `B8` enters a multi-turn self-trap (`85%` `DEATH_SELF`).
- **Live Python co-resident search violates the Mac frame-pacing contract, but `L2'` is ideal for offline replay**: Even after `K-1`'s `20.13×` kernel speedup and `M0`'s `72`-step lead window, CPython's frame-loop object churn triggers `10–23 ms` pauses that fail the `0`-overrun / `11.688 ms` p95 serving bar (`M1`, `GC A1`). Consequently, live search (`C15`) was closed, `L2'` (`exact-stop-l2-prime`) was promoted via a pre-computed `132`-session Watch replay bank, and live-inference work focused on 1-forward-pass `Ego2sNet` distillation (`Claim A`) and hindsight label factories (`R24/R27`).
- **Process gates and Resume-by-Default eliminated repeat operational waste**: Round 12's 8 gates (`G1`–`G8`: ruler before compute, power before launch, two-cutoff clock seam, upload-cap preflight) and the standing **Resume-by-Default** contract (`JUDGMENT_RESUME_POLICY.md`) prevented repeat Colab upload-cap and timeout losses across all subsequent packages.
