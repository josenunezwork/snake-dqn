# Learning review: capability exists; reliable control does not yet follow

The evidence supports a gap between demonstrated benchmark competence and established product competence, not a general inability to learn. Fresh canonical Apex learned every short food case; the 20k-update study learned substantial fresh H64 food seeking. More strongly, all three fixed BO shared-head policies passed fresh H512 solo criteria, and all three CW native-PQN length-access policies passed fresh H768 food/survival criteria. These positive results must survive the strategy reset. CW's mass integrals nevertheless varied 40.32/60.97/12.52 and boost fractions 18.88%/0.15%/48.35%: food/survival success does not establish a common growth strategy or opponent competence. Sources: [BO](../experiments/solo_food_h512_confirmation_2026-09-20/README.md), lines 63–80; [CW](../experiments/solo_food_pqn_h768_length_confirmation_2026-09-21/README.md), lines 3–15, 29–47, 58–75. Apex remains the **operational incumbent**, not a demonstrated algorithmic winner. This review reads reports and source only; no numerical work or provisional counterfactual supplies new training evidence.

## 1. The learning task needs sustained food and anticipatory safety together

**Observed blocker.** The smallest scaling arm collected 292/301/293 foods against teacher 304 on fresh H64, while random already survived all 96 games. At H256, no scaling endpoint passed the full food/survival package. Food learning is real; short-horizon survival supplies little evidence of learned safety. The continuation's 15 recorded deaths all followed empty safe masks, locating the eventual failure after immediate safe choices had disappeared. This does not identify the earlier cause or establish an avoidable action. Sources: [width/breadth](../experiments/apex_scaling_width_breadth_2026-09-23/README.md), lines 50–65; [H256](../experiments/apex_scaling_h256_2026-09-24/README.md), lines 23–49; [terminal masks](../experiments/apex_terminal_mask_2026-09-23/README.md), lines 3–19.

**Hypothesis.** Training insufficiently distinguishes food progress that preserves future escape routes from food progress that creates traps. Reward, exposure, representation, and temporal credit could each explain this; none is identified independently.

**Counterevidence.** The saved finite-return screen found no strong predeclared reward discordance; adding ambient-food reward in controlled PQN lowered food point estimates in every lineage and failed retention. Simply increasing food incentive has no supporting result. Sources: [finite return](../experiments/apex_saved_finite_return_2026-09-23/README.md), lines 3–9; [ambient objective](../experiments/controlled_encounter_ambient_objective_2026-09-22/README.md), lines 16–54.

**Discriminator.** On a new training distribution with independently validated delayed trap/escape alternatives, compare ordinary collection with fixed-quota exposure to those alternatives, holding reward, representation, accepted rows, and updates constant. Decide using both sustained food and survival on an untouched long-horizon distribution. Improvement would support the exposure package, not prove reward or representation optimality. Do not train on selected held failures or use the unaudited native counterfactual to define labels.

## 2. TD adaptation can destroy competence, but the cause is unresolved

**Observed blocker.** Apex imitation parents fell from 96/96 food to held totals 9/15/62 after canonical TD, while all three fresh controls reached 96/96. Controlled PQN completed 6,144 roots and 1,024 extra native updates per lineage without satisfying competence/retention; H16 food fell in every lineage. More updates therefore cannot be presumed to preserve useful behavior. Sources: [canonical retention](../experiments/apex_canonical_retention_2026-09-22/README.md), lines 3–24; [native full cycle](../experiments/controlled_encounter_native_full_cycle_2026-09-22/README.md), lines 3–38.

**Hypotheses and limits.** Uncalibrated imitation Q values, bootstrap feedback, conflicting objectives, and distribution drift remain candidates. Higher initial imitation Q scale is descriptive, not a cause. PQN's audited target replay was exact; rare saturated terminal residuals were output-derivative proxies, not parameter-gradient attribution. Longer traces and half-MSE did not establish their required paired benefit. Sources: canonical retention, line 43; [terminal signal](../experiments/solo_food_pqn_terminal_signal_2026-09-20/README.md), lines 19–54; [loss shape](../experiments/solo_food_pqn_loss_shape_2026-09-20/README.md), lines 96–126.

**Discriminator.** If imitation transfer remains a strategic requirement, compare an action-preserving Q-scale calibration against untouched imitation initialization, with the same frozen transition corpus, sampling schedule, optimizer initialization, and TD dose. Measure action retention and fresh gameplay. This isolates the initial scale treatment under fixed data; it does not establish online stability. Otherwise stop rescuing imitation parents and use fresh Apex as the simpler baseline already supported by behavior.

## 3. “More data” has been conflated with roots, transitions, and repeated updates

**Observed blocker.** Each scaling cell used 79,872 accepted rows, 20,000 updates, and 5.12 million replay draws—approximately 64 draws per accepted row in aggregate, not uniform coverage. Width rose from 429,831 to 1,646,087 parameters without a reliable factor benefit. Broader roots at fixed row count redistribute experience; they do not add transition volume. Sources: [width/breadth](../experiments/apex_scaling_width_breadth_2026-09-23/README.md), lines 23–36, 69–81; [H256](../experiments/apex_scaling_h256_2026-09-24/README.md), lines 7–13.

**Counterevidence and uncertainty.** These are bounded three-seed screens, not proof scaling never works. The 40k continuation reset replay and changed collection exposure; its fresh branch stopped on overlap, so it neither isolates updates nor supplies fresh negative evidence. PQN first-cycle completion likewise is not state/action coverage. Sources: [horizon continuation](../experiments/apex_horizon_continuation_2026-09-23/README.md), lines 35–53, 103–123; native full cycle, lines 9–14.

**Discriminator.** If scaling is revisited, use one fresh-start factorial separating accepted transition volume from replay draws, with width fixed, matched acquisition protocol, explicit wall-time accounting, and a single independent evaluation distribution. A learning curve against both quantities would distinguish useful new experience from repeated optimization more directly than another width/root sweep. Broad production scaling is not warranted yet; a bounded information-gathering budget is.

## 4. Representation and teacher agreement remain underidentified

Most Apex exact teacher disagreements were equal-distance safe alternatives; local equivalence exceeded 95% in five of six cells. Exact replay absence also occurred throughout successful gameplay, so neither metric identifies deficient representation or causal coverage. Exposing PQN body information improved survival in two seeds and reversed it in the third; it did not reliably solve the problem. Sources: [teacher geometry](../experiments/apex_teacher_geometry_2026-09-23/README.md), lines 3–9; [coverage specificity](../experiments/apex_coverage_specificity_2026-09-22/README.md), lines 17–39; [body access](../experiments/solo_food_pqn_body_access_2026-09-20/README.md), lines 58–77.

**Discriminator.** Test vector61 against a parameter-matched representation containing explicit local body topology on the same independently generated delayed-safety task and supervision, then compare greedy behavior. A supervised success followed by TD failure would shift attention toward learning dynamics; failure under one representation would motivate information/inductive-bias work. This is prospective, not evidence that CNNs or recurrence are required.

## Strategy decision

The primary recommendation is a deployment-gap milestone using **all three fixed competent CW/CV policies**, alongside compatible incumbent/reference controls, before another training campaign. Preserve each policy's exact mechanics/observation contract; a cross-stack comparison requires separately established compatibility. Freeze desired sustained growth, survival, and opponent behavior before evaluation. This recommendation is falsified as a missing-competence diagnosis if existing policies already meet that contract; then integration, rather than more learning research, becomes the work.

Stop serially patching teacher disagreement, missing exact-state matches, reward coefficients, or inherited checkpoints. The four discriminators above are conditional alternatives, not an authorized queue. Observed deployment failure strata should select one. Bigger models and longer unchanged continuations lack a reliable benefit; more informative experience and lower replay reuse remain hypotheses. Changing replay reuse changes the recipe and needs a control. Preserve prior failures and distinguish audit success from policy success.
