## Final Verdict and Key Numbers

1. **Offline search ceiling (`L2'`)**:
   - On `160` fresh paired H5000 worlds (`64` frozen / `64` mixed / `32` scripted; `8/8` chunks TERMINAL_VALID, `5.2` Mac wall-hours), the unchanged T3 dense-search rule beat bare `b8-s3` by **`+83.0` pooled mass** (`LB90 +64.1`), **`+56.1` frozen** (`LB90 +34.8`), **`+27.6` mixed** (`LB90 +12.1`), **`+165.3` scripted**, **`+19.8 pp` no-death**, with **`28` rescues** (deaths `23 → 10` frozen, `11 → 6` mixed, `17 → 7` scripted) and **`0/160` negative worlds**. Ruling: **FUND_SYSTEMS** for the offline ceiling (Source: [`docs/notes/big-lever-experiments.md`](../../notes/big-lever-experiments.md); [`docs/research/advice/2026-10-22_L2prime_fable_advice.md`](../advice/2026-10-22_L2prime_fable_advice.md); `snake-dqn-artifacts/big-lever/LIVE_FACTS.md`).
   - The intervention changed `0.07–0.52` actions per `1,000`; deaths were `85%` `DEATH_SELF`; gain was heavy-tailed (`47/64` frozen and `57/64` mixed zero-gain worlds). Fable advised quoting frozen/mixed `+28` to `+56` × delivered fraction instead of the pooled `+83` headline (`≈50%` delivery needed for a `+40` point, `≈70%` for `LB90 > 0`) (Source: [`docs/research/advice/2026-10-22_L2prime_fable_advice.md`](../advice/2026-10-22_L2prime_fable_advice.md)).
2. **Kernel speedup (`K-1`) vs live co-resident serving (`M0` / `M1` / `GC A1`)**:
   - `K-1` passed identity on **`294/294`** comparisons and achieved **`20.13×`** on `opponent_v8` (`>= 5×` bar), cutting frozen whole-batch p50 from `19.5–21.4 s` to `2.3–2.4 s` and mixed from `7.2–10.5 s` to `1.2–1.5 s` (Source: [`docs/notes/big-lever-experiments.md`](../../notes/big-lever-experiments.md)).
   - `M0` lead census: `18` frozen/mixed rescues, `F(L) >= 0.50` through `L = 230` frames; `L* = 72`, `F = 0.944` (not closed) (Source: [`docs/notes/big-lever-experiments.md`](../../notes/big-lever-experiments.md)).
   - `M1` was BLOCKED by `5–26` overruns per cell, then printed **`FINAL FAIL`** ⇒ `KEEP_B8` for serving; the bare served game alone overran `43×` with update p95 `13.2 ms`. Round 23 attributed the overruns to CPython gen-2 GC pauses (`86/89` overrun ticks). `GC A1` also **FAIL**ed: four times as many gen-2 collections and update p95 `13.8–23.3 ms` under both policies (Source: [`docs/notes/big-lever-experiments.md`](../../notes/big-lever-experiments.md); `docs/notes/_facts/big-lever-program.md` (removed in consolidation; see git c4c4ab41)).

## What Was Learned and Why It Closed

- **Exact search has a real offline ceiling, concentrated in rare survival rescues**: `L2'`'s `+83.0` pooled gain came from survival, touched only `0.07–0.52` actions per `1,000`, and was heavy-tailed across worlds; it established an offline ceiling, not delivered serving value.
- **Live co-resident search failed the Mac frame-pacing contract**: even after `K-1`'s `20.13×` kernel speedup and `M0`'s `L* = 72` lead window, `M1` failed on overruns that also afflict the bare served game (CPython gen-2 GC pauses), and `GC A1` did not fix it. Serving stayed `KEEP_B8`, and `C15` was not built.
- **Process gates and resume-by-default came out of wave-1 waste**: Round 12 adopted eight gates (target-platform canary, measure before planning, execution attestation, shared-schema validation, error classes, power sheet before build, Mac lease, pre-claim ledger check), and the DENSITY-2 resume ruling made idempotent completed units plus verified-ledger resume-skip a standing requirement ([`JUDGMENT_RESUME_POLICY.md`](../advice/2026-10-21_big_lever/JUDGMENT_RESUME_POLICY.md)).
