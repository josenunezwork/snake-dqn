# S1 Mature-Schedule League vs Hero Fine-Tuning (`s1-mature`)

## Question and Goal

Did T23's `-80.8` mass regression against bare `b8-s3` happen because T23 restarted B8's training schedule (`gamma = 0.99 → 0.998`, `eps = 0.05 → 0.01`, `anchor = 1.0`) from `t = 0`, and can holding B8's terminal mature schedule constant (`gamma = 0.998`, `eps = 0.01`, `anchor = 0.10` throughout `8,000,000` learner decisions across `5` paired seeds on Colab L4) under either `N3` (3 learner seats + 3 static-pool seats) or `N1` (1 hero learner seat vs historical gate mixes) beat released `b8-s3` on paired H5000 full-horizon mass?

## Final Verdict and Key Numbers

1. **Scientific Verdict (`KILL` for both `N3` and `N1`):**
   - Holding B8's terminal mature schedule (`gamma = 0.998`, `eps = 0.01`, `anchor = 0.10`) reduced the catastrophic `-80.8` mass loss seen in T23's restarted schedule by ~85%, proving that schedule restart had been the main driver of T23's collapse ([`docs/notes/redesign-and-b8.md`](../../notes/redesign-and-b8.md)).
   - However, neither mature-schedule arm improved upon bare `b8-s3`: **`N3` scored `-12.5` paired equal-mix H5000 mass vs `b8-s3`** and **`N1` scored `-14.9` paired equal-mix H5000 mass vs `b8-s3`**, failing the pre-registered `LB90(ΔM) > 0` and `mean >= +20.0` GO bar (`KILL`; [`research/s1_mature_20261018/README.md`](../../../research/s1_mature_20261018/README.md), [`docs/notes/redesign-and-b8.md`](../../notes/redesign-and-b8.md)).
2. **Exact Barrier Accounting & Preflight Measurements (`research/s1_mature_20261018/*.json`):**
   - **Barrier overshoot root cause and fix (`TRAIN_PREFLIGHT.json` → `TRAIN_PREFLIGHT_BARRIER_FIX.json`):** In the initial implementation (`9a18e661`), `train_m3c.py` continued polling `out_qs` while `pending_barrier_target` was set (`4,000` / `8,000` / `12,000`), causing `N3` to reach `12,048` decisions at the `12,000` barrier (`overshoot = 48 > max_overshoot = 47`) ([`research/s1_mature_20261018/TRAIN_PREFLIGHT.json`](../../../research/s1_mature_20261018/TRAIN_PREFLIGHT.json)). Adding per-actor `pending_actor_msgs` FIFOs and halting queue reads while `pending_barrier_target is not None` produced **`0` overshoot** (`decisions = 12,000`, `replay_rows = 12,000`, `updates = 46`, `debt_rows = 224` across both `N3` and `N1`; [`research/s1_mature_20261018/TRAIN_PREFLIGHT_BARRIER_FIX.json`](../../../research/s1_mature_20261018/TRAIN_PREFLIGHT_BARRIER_FIX.json)).
   - **Full-state replay checkpoint size (`CHECKPOINT_SIZE_MEASUREMENT.json`):** At full capacity (`500,000` replay rows + `200,000` label rows, `12,443 B`/row), raw array footprints are **`10,103,294,896 B` (`N3`)** and **`9,481,840,816 B` (`N1`)**, `torch.save` files are **`11,178,149,683 B`** and **`10,490,632,471 B`**, per-snapshot staging + `result.tar.gz` bounds are **`22,680,004,768 B` (`21.12 GiB`)** and **`20,268,551,032 B` (`18.88 GiB`)**, and `10` jobs x `4` snapshots would require **`867,602,770,220 B` (`~867.6 GB`)** ([`research/s1_mature_20261018/CHECKPOINT_SIZE_MEASUREMENT.json`](../../../research/s1_mature_20261018/CHECKPOINT_SIZE_MEASUREMENT.json)).
   - **Final-only export footprint (`FINAL_ARTIFACT_SIZE_FIXTURE.json`, `TRAIN_PREFLIGHT_FINAL_ONLY.json`):** Exporting only final weights (`final.pth = 6,566,741 B`, `target_final.pth = 6,562,677 B`) reduced the compressed `result.tar.gz` to **`12,180,957 B` (`11.62 MiB`)** per job (`<= 64 MiB` enforced limit in `run_s1_job.py`; [`research/s1_mature_20261018/FINAL_ARTIFACT_SIZE_FIXTURE.json`](../../../research/s1_mature_20261018/FINAL_ARTIFACT_SIZE_FIXTURE.json)).
   - **Mac evaluation timing (`EVAL_PREFLIGHT_FINAL.json`):** Evaluating `10` H5000 worlds (`20` episodes: `N3` vs `b8-s3`) took **`67.21 s`** with 2 CPU workers and **`63.68 s`** serially with identical `summary_sha256` (`2cc65c1cf0529e3535bd379a96f6b6c2f5b7ba7f9cb13f22b73cfcf019d7fb5a`) ([`research/s1_mature_20261018/EVAL_PREFLIGHT_FINAL.json`](../../../research/s1_mature_20261018/EVAL_PREFLIGHT_FINAL.json)).

## What Was Learned

- **Restarting `gamma` / `eps` / `anchor` caused most of T23's `-80.8` regression, but online TD + `v8` margin fine-tuning from `b8-s3` is already at its ceiling:** Even with B8's mature schedule (`-12.5` and `-14.9` mass vs `-80.8`), online M3C fine-tuning with fresh Adam and empty replay degrades `b8-s3` slightly rather than discovering a stronger policy.
- **Uncompressed `500k`-row `ego2s-bv8` replay buffers (`~10.5–11.2 GB` per snapshot) cannot be exported repeatedly to Drive:** Uninterrupted 1-hour L4 runs should export only terminal model/target weights (`~12.2 MB` compressed), or compress reduced replay buffers (`xz -6`) when full-state snapshots are required.
