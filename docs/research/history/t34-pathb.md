# T34 Path B Paced 24 fps Background Search, Retention Repairs, and Unpaced Colab Arm

## Question and Goal

Under the owner-ratified T34 Campaign Contract ([`SUCCESS_CONTRACT.md`](../final_run_20261019/SUCCESS_CONTRACT.md) §4) and [`DESIGN_V2_SHADOW.md`](../final_run_20261019/DESIGN_V2_SHADOW.md) (plus Amendment 2), **Path B (Claim B)** asked whether an asynchronous, visibly labelled online lookahead controller running on a background CPU worker over the released bare `b8-s3` policy could rescue natural deaths and beat `b8-s3` on both H5000 terminal skill and continuous-respawn mixed-Watch6 persistence **while meeting the real-time 24 fps serving timing contract** (`mixed-Watch6` update `p95 <= 11.688 ms`, zero tick overruns at 24 and 12 fps, `<= 5%` cadence loss, no tick blocking, `<= 1` outstanding worker request, and immediate fallback to bare `b8-s3` when results arrive late or stale). This lineage covers:
1. **Path B v1 (`research/t34_pathb` v1, 2026-10-19):** The initial periodic `H=40` (`L >= 600`, every 5th frame) asynchronous background verifier/expander and default-OFF `SNAKE_SERVE_PATHB=1` serving hook, retired pre-data once T34 M1–M3 shadow assays showed `H=40` was far shorter than the `255–357` frame warning horizon needed before traps become unrecoverable.
2. **Path B v2 (`research/t34_pathb` v2, 2026-10-20):** The Amendment 2 continuous `D=500` exact shadow trajectory + earliest-first forward search controller (`engine_v2.py`, `controller_v2.py`, `adapter_v2.py`), its pre-registered paced retention study (`PREREGISTRATION_RETENTION_V2.md`), six iterative pre-data `FIX-FIRST` governance/engineering hardenings, the `N=32 -> 48` terminal / `16 -> 32` Watch6 power resize, the pre-data Colab offload of the descriptive unpaced arm, and the bare-unpaced + relative-boost-floor amendment (`t34-retention-v2-operator-02`).
3. **Path B v2 Deliverability Smoke (`research/t34_pathb_smoke`, 2026-10-21) and S4 Multi-Session Design:** The pre-committed development deliverability smoke on the 75 historical T3 rescue/delay changes and 5 M3 doom intervals at real 24 fps (`N=1` and `N=6` worker threads), which delivered **`0 / 34` horizon-surviving rescues** under real 24 fps wall-clock deadlines and triggered `STOP_OPERATOR02_DO_NOT_RUN_PACED_STUDY` before the 18–19 h `operator-02` Mac retention chain was ever launched.

## Final Verdict and Key Numbers

- **Lineage Verdict:** **`STOP_OPERATOR02_DO_NOT_RUN_PACED_STUDY` — Asynchronous CPU background search over `D=500` (`Path B v2`) cannot deliver rescues in real time at 24 fps (`0 / 34` rescues delivered at both `N=1` and `N=6`), closing the live paced CPU lookahead line before `operator-02` was ever launched** ([`research/t34_pathb_smoke/RESULT.md`](../../../research/t34_pathb_smoke/RESULT.md); primary artifact `snake-dqn-artifacts/t34-pathb-smoke-20261021/receipt.json`, sha256 `d493fe5824f44db138e92fac282cd1d6141cb0432613d5a1b8982fef5994e336`).

    - **Delivered any improving action:** **`7 / 75 = 9.33%`** (vs `>= 50%` stopping-rule bar) — all 7 (`b8_s3_6-c04`, `b8_s3_6-c08`, `b8_s3_6-c09`, `b8_s3_8-c01`, `b8_s3_8-c03`, `b8_s3_8-c07`, `b8_s3_9-c04`) were **delay-only (`D`)**.

## What Was Learned

1. **Earliest-first search from `t_detect` has a zero-margin deadline paradox at 24 fps:** When the `D=500` shadow detects doom at live frame `t_live`, the earliest branch points `t_b` inside `[t_live + 1, t_doom]` are only `1..8` frames (`41.7–333 ms`) in the future, whereas rolling out a 40-state window of 8 branch points x 5 alternatives to `D=500` on CPU takes **several seconds** (`2.7–17.6 s` in `RESULT.md`). By the time the first window finishes evaluating, the live game has already advanced dozens of frames past those branch points, rendering every earliest-first candidate stale (`475` late evaluations at `N=1`, `0 / 34` rescues delivered).
2. **Multi-threading Python/PyTorch rollouts inside one process degrades real-time pacing:** Splitting branch windows across 6 Python threads (`N=6`) did not speed up search (`15.5–30.0 s` vs `10.8–17.6 s` at `N=1`) because GIL and CPU contention stalled both the worker threads and the foreground 24 fps tick loop (`21,077` tick overruns, achieved fps dropping to `5.0–15.9 fps`). True concurrency on the Mac requires separate `spawn` processes (`2026-10-21_paced_multisession_design.md`).
3. **Cheap pre-committed deliverability smokes save days of ceremonial runtime:** Running the 57-minute, 80-doom `t34_pathb_smoke` before launching `operator-02` saved `~18–19` Mac hours and `~42.5` Colab units on a study whose candidate controller was mechanically incapable of delivering a single rescue on time.
4. **Why Watch later succeeded via short-horizon closed-loop search + pre-recorded replay bank:** Because deep (`D=500`) asynchronous CPU lookahead cannot finish before 24 fps deadlines, the campaign pivoted to shallow closed-loop 2-step / exact-stop controllers (`exact-stop-l2-prime`) and ultimately served Watch from a confirmed 132-session replay bank (`watch-replay-bank-live-132`) with live `b8-s3` fallback.
