# July–September 2026 PQN Redesign Blueprint, State-System Audit, and RL Portfolio Reviews

## Question and Goal

Following the June 2026 promotion of the `vector61` Apex free-space champion (`champion_a5_freespace_20260621.pth`), the project asked whether the existing 61-dimensional hand-crafted state vector and distributed Apex DQN pipeline could reach competitive multi-agent tactics, or whether a spatial dual-scale raster representation (`raster31v2` / `raster31v3`) paired with synchronous Parallelized Q-Network (`PQN` $Q(\lambda)$) training on a vectorized `BatchSim` simulator should replace it. Between July and September 2026, this lineage drafted the PQN redesign blueprint, conducted a full state-representation and system audit (`state_system_audit_2026-09-06`), executed a 14-milestone RL contract repair (`rl_repair_execution_2026-09-08`), diagnosed PQN rollout sampler coverage (`pqn_sampler_2026-09-06`), specified and benchmarked per-environment autoreset (`B0–B5`), tested whether `raster31v3` peripheral 1-segment enemy heading aliasing was decision-relevant (`H1-A`), and resolved the Watch/trainer pre-move vs. post-move decision-phase contract (`watch_pre_move_v1`).

## Final Verdict and Key Numbers

1. **Historical CNN Retraction and Blueprint Baseline (`2026-07`):**
   - Git archaeology in [`docs/ml_redesign_blueprint_2026-07.md`](../../ml_redesign_blueprint_2026-07.md) (Appendix B) proved that the June 2026 `CNNNetwork` (`6bc8677` to `34d399c`) was never trained and produced `0` checkpoints; all 10 arms of the June 20 overnight benchmark were feedforward `vector58`/`vector61` MLPs evaluated on a survivorship-biased gate (mean mass over alive frames only).
   - The blueprint established the `raster31v2` dual-scale spatial contract (`(9, 31, 31)` tactical at cell size `10 px`, `(3, 25, 25)` strategic at `3×3` pooled `30 px` cells, and `(26,)` scalars) and `RasterDuelingNetwork` (`829,895` parameters).

2. **September 6 State-System Audit and PQN Sampler Diagnostic (`S1`):**
   - The audit across [`docs/state_and_system_review_2026-09-06.md`](../../state_and_system_review_2026-09-06.md) and [`docs/experiments/state_system_audit_2026-09-06/README.md`](../../experiments/state_system_audit_2026-09-06/README.md) identified `5` observation blind spots in `vector61`/`raster31v2` (`310 px` tactical horizon vs. `2,000 × 2,000` arena, `750 px` strategic horizon, `0.0` food-urgency signal until `201/240` frames without food, unoriented enemy body segments, and missing self-tail relative coordinates) plus `4` evaluation/training contract bugs (terminal frame `3000` truncation bootstrap, `BatchSim` batch-barrier reset censoring when `alive_count <= 1`, `Apex` actor `get_observation()` omitting enemy `all_snakes` during training rollouts — see [`docs/experiments/state_system_audit_2026-09-06/vector/actor_vs_full_obs.md`](../../experiments/state_system_audit_2026-09-06/vector/actor_vs_full_obs.md) — and tournament gate ranking without multi-comparison correction).
   - In the 5-seed × 12-world `S1` PQN sampler experiment ([`docs/pqn_sampler_results_2026-09-06.md`](../../pqn_sampler_results_2026-09-06.md); artifacts in `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/rl-repair-20260908/`), `epoch` sampling achieved `100.0%` unique transition coverage per update (`2,503,528` total draws across `11,237` steps) vs. `68.132%` for `legacy` i.i.d. sampling (`2,903,004` draws across `12,024` steps). Paired per-seed `epoch - legacy` final mass deltas were `-3.3868` (seed `401`), `-4.7433` (seed `502`), `-0.9776` (seed `603`), `+2.0003` (seed `704`), and `+0.2602` (seed `805`), yielding a mean delta of `-1.3694` (`SD = 2.7192`, `SE = 1.2161`, `95%` Student-$t$ CI `[-4.7480, +2.0092]`, $df = 4$). Because neither arm cleared `+0` nor significantly beat the untrained initialization (`legacy - initial = +1.0225`, `95%` CI `[-1.9453, +3.9903]`; `epoch - initial = -0.3470`, `95%` CI `[-1.6352, +0.9413]`), `legacy` remained the default.

3. **RL Repair Execution (`C0`–`H0`, `G0-R3`, `X0`, `2026-09-08`):**
   - Upgraded the raster observation contract to `raster31v3` (`10` tactical channels adding head-direction cos/sin, `5` strategic channels adding coarse arena coverage, and `30` scalars adding `arena_type`, normalized `starvation_age`, `food_urgency`, and relative self-tail coordinates; `RasterDuelingNetwork` parameter count `831,911`), repaired off-policy $n$-step timeout bootstrap (`OFF`), unified `PQN` $\lambda$-return masking (`P1`), added exact `mpmath` two-sided Student-$t$ + Holm-Bonferroni gate statistics (`E2`), and passed full qualification `G0-R3` (`2,519` non-slow + `3` slow + `167` frontend tests + `100,901`-step MPS shakedown on commit `7cb66a94`; artifacts in `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/rl-repair-20260908/manifest.json`).
   - The 5-arm `500,000`-step screen `X0` (`x0_control`, `x0_obs_v3`, `x0_pqn_lambda`, `x0_epoch_sampler`, `x0_combined_v3` on seed `20260908`) ended `EXECUTED_INCONCLUSIVE`: all 5 arms hit the `435.61 s` wall-clock cap at `416,687`, `404,516`, `431,780`, `424,379`, and `455,227` steps (`2,132,589` total steps, `0` final checkpoints) because early all-hero calibration (`1,513.5` steps/s) overestimated later sparse-tail and frozen-opponent rollout rates (`859.7` steps/s).

4. **Per-Env Autoreset (`B0–B5`) and Throughput Calibration (`A2/A3`, `2026-09-09` to `2026-09-12`):**
   - `A2/A3` (`a3_calibration_v2` on `7cb66a94`, `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/pqn-followup-20260909/a3_calibration_v2/summary.json`) measured full `500,000`-step wall times of `587.78 s` (`all-hero`, `850.7` steps/s) and `603.40 s` (`frozen-opponent`, `828.6` steps/s), establishing an `822 s` per-child timeout for `500k`-step runs.
   - `B0–B4` (`9245c003`) implemented `BatchSim.reset_envs(env_mask, *, seeds=None)` and `src/training/pqn_lifecycle.py` (`pqn-episode-lifecycle/v1` and `pqn-rollout-policy-source/v1`), eliminating whole-world batch barrier censoring while preserving episode-boundary return masking.
   - In `B5 v2` (`9265eb59`, `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/pqn-followup-20260909/b5_screen_v2/summary.json`), Pair 1 (`seed 20260909`) completed cleanly inside both workers: `batch_barrier_v1` reached `200,115` useful steps (`197` updates, `221.62 s`, `54,177.10` steps/min, `3.06 GB` RSS) and `per_env_autoreset_v1` reached `200,376` useful steps (`173` updates, `226.95 s`, `52,580.11` steps/min, `-2.948%` throughput delta, `2.71 GB` RSS, `0` batch barrier resets, `166` per-env resets). A parent `psutil.NoSuchProcess` race during child teardown triggered `child_exit_unconfirmed` (exit `2`), halting Pairs 2–3; the monitor fix landed at `b51b1588` (`43` unit tests).

5. **Observation-Value Gate (`H1-A`) and Decision-Phase Alignment (`watch_pre_move_v1`, `2026-09-12`):**
   - `H1-A` (`e174fe48`, `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/observation-value-20260912/h1a/summary.json`) generated `36` matched state pairs across `22/24` worlds (`9` dev in `7/8` worlds, `27` holdout in `15/16` worlds) where rotating a peripheral 1-segment enemy's heading by `+90°` left `raster31v3` observations and legal action masks byte-identical. Across `28,800` simulator steps (`26.07 s` wall time, `243.05 MiB` RSS), `51/113` next-step observations diverged across `20/36` pairs, confirming genuine state aliasing, but discounted action-value regret at horizons `H1, H8, H16, H32` ($\gamma=0.99$) was `0.0` across all `36` pairs (`0` disjoint near-best action sets, `holdout_lcb = 0.0`), closing the peripheral enemy-heading channel hypothesis (`INCONCLUSIVE_NOT_ADVANCED`).
   - `LP1` phase witness (`v1–v4`, `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/research-portfolio-20260912/phase-witness/runs/phase-witness-v4.json`) isolated a decision-phase mismatch between `BatchSim` (`simd_post_move_v0`, observing after food spawn and step counters) and `GameState` (`watch_pre_move_v1`, observing before food/movement). Commits `ec39ece` and `b760b40` ([`docs/pqn_decision_phase_2026-09-12.md`](../../pqn_decision_phase_2026-09-12.md)) moved `BatchSim`'s step-start food spawn to the end of reset/step and aligned step-start observations to `watch_pre_move_v1`.
   - The `R1–R12` portfolio audit also discovered that `train_pqn.py --no-self-play` co-adapted `6` live learner heroes per arena rather than running `1` hero against `5` fixed `random_safe` opponents, and `VC1` (`0aae2e89`, `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/research-portfolio-20260912/vc1-value-conditioning/summary.json`) showed that an auxiliary 3-way return probe on `215` `H1-A` states (`161` train / `54` holdout) memorized train states (`1.000` accuracy at update `200`) while stalling at `0.5926` held-out accuracy (`0.5299` macro-F1) before hitting its `28 s` cap (`28.425 s`).

## What Was Learned and Why It Closed

- **Why it closed:** The July–September 2026 PQN audit and repair phase succeeded in building and qualifying the `raster31v3` observation contract, `per_env_autoreset_v1` episode lifecycle, `watch_pre_move_v1` pre-move phase alignment, and exact Student-$t$/Holm tournament gating (`E2`), while ruling out PQN minibatch permutation sampling (`S1`) and peripheral 1-segment enemy-heading channels (`H1-A`) as primary bottlenecks. The unanswered learning question — why short PQN runs still failed to beat `random_safe` — was handed off directly to the September 13 four-hour diagnostic campaign ([`docs/research/history/four-hour-2026-09-13.md`](four-hour-2026-09-13.md)), which traced the remaining gap to terminal death penalty scaling, exploration floors, and learning rate.
- **Current status:** Closed historical lineage. The `BatchSim` / `raster31v2`–`v3` / `PQN` stack remains in the repository as an experimental baseline (`src/scripts/train_pqn.py`), while the October 2026 campaign shifted the main redesign line to `GridBatchSim` + `ego2s-bv8` distilled DQN / ExIt (`B8`).
