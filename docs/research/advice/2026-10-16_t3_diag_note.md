# Note on the T3 diagnostics and the TWO_SIDED readout (advice, 2026-10-16)

Advisory note from the cloud session on `docs/research/t3_diagnostics_2026-10-16.md`. Not gate
evidence. Nothing in the running TWO_SIDED arm needs to change. Two points for the readout.

## 1. Reconcile the boost numbers before reading the disagreement map

On CTRL's round-62 states, LABELS disagrees on 67% of decisions and 75% of those are boost-mode
changes, so LABELS's argmax is a boost on roughly half of CTRL's states. Yet LABELS's own greedy
boost rate in training telemetry is 3.7-7.5%, and the LO arm's is 0.2-0.6% against a 51%
disagreement rate with 85% boost changes. The masks match, so the gap is either a definition
difference (which action set the telemetry counts) or a real state-distribution split: the
student prefers to boost in CTRL's large crowded states but, on its own trajectory, stays small
enough never to reach them. The second reading is the caution mechanism itself, in behaviour
space, and it would mean the 450-unit growth loss is "avoid the states where I would want to
boost". One cheap check settles it: LABELS's boost-argmax rate on its own probe trajectories
versus on CTRL's, at matched length buckets. Do this before interpreting TWO_SIDED's map.

## 2. The third readout: behavioural discriminability, not just length buckets

Disagreement by length bucket is already in the report and cannot separate targeted from global
on its own, because the bank is also concentrated at large lengths. The readout that can is the
argmax-change rate of TWO_SIDED versus CTRL on three state sets at matched length and -max Q
decile:

| State set | Targeted change | Global change |
|---|---|---|
| Decisive bank rows | high | high |
| Counter bank rows | near zero | high |
| CTRL-trajectory states outside the bank, same length and decile | low | high |

The third row is the one that matters: it measures spillover to look-alike states directly, which
the two-sided bank_agree cannot see because those states are not in the bank. Report it per
decile, with the cross-boost share separately. Add the growth curve as the second diagnostic:
mean alive length at frames 1000, 2000, 3000, 4000, 5000 against CTRL on shared survivors, since
55 of the 75 lost mass units were growth, not deaths. Both are post hoc and diagnostic only, as
the request says; neither changes the pre-registered rule.

## 3. One expectation to write down now

If TWO_SIDED's counter agree holds above 0.9 and the third row above stays low while decisive
agree also exceeds 0.9, the inputs carry the distinction and expert iteration is next. If
decisive agree is high but the third row is also high, the loss is still generalising and the
margin or counter matching needs tightening before return regression. If decisive agree cannot
rise above about 0.8 while counter agree holds, the states are ambiguous at the input and the
campaign moves to inputs.
