# Big-Lever E4/E2 PPO Pilot, WP-3 P1 100M PPO Endpoint, Y3 Critic Qualification, and A3 Learner Ceiling

## Question and Goal

Within the October 2026 Big-Lever campaign, this lineage asked whether a PPO actor-critic built on the released bare `b8-s3` `ego2s-bv8` checkpoint (SHA-256 beginning `3e649783`) — copying the B8 trunk, keeping frozen calibrated B8 logits as the base, training a zero-initialized residual actor head, and using a PopArt critic with a critic-only remaining-horizon input — could raise terminal-hero `H5000` full-horizon mass integral by at least `+40` versus bare B8 on fresh paired worlds (`research/bl_ppo/README.md`, `research/bl_ppo/REGISTRATION_P1.md`). The lineage moved through:

1. **E4 collector ruler and E2 two-arm pilot (2026-10-20 to 10-21):** E4 on one L4 (`128` worlds, `6` vs `12` single-thread collector processes, at most `30` allocation minutes / `2 u`), then E2 arms `R` (`GRU256`) and `F` (parameter-matched feedforward) under a combined first-cap-wins `20 u` / `10` L4 allocation-hours / `48` elapsed hours (`research/bl_ppo/INTENT.md`), gated by the E9 operating-characteristic rule (full funding ≥ `.60` at `+40`, family null pass ≤ `.10`).
2. **WP-3 P1 single 100M endpoint and Y3 / E8 critic qualification (2026-10-21 to 10-23):** one feedforward `F` artifact, seed `20261021`, `100,000,000` actor hero transitions, `128` worlds, `12` collector processes, rollout `128`; actor unfreeze requires the held-out late-body (`L >= 800`) critic gate `EV >= .1 AND MSE < fit-world time/length baseline` (`REGISTRATION_P1.md`).
3. **A3 actor-enabled P1 learner ceiling (2026-10-22):** can the P1 learner deliver `10,000` hero transitions/s (≤ `1.6384 s` per `128 × 128` rollout), and does A100-40GB beat L4 (`research/a3_p1_ceiling/PROTOCOL.md`)?

## Final Verdict and Key Numbers

1. **E9 blocks two-arm E2.** With T23 paired SDs (`364.683` / `262.732` / `212.360`) the minimum full funding probability at `+40` is `.27045` (`96/32/32`, MC95 lower `.26434`) and `.30700` (`128/48/48`, MC95 lower `.30065`); null pass `.05050` / `.06160`. The frozen options stop at `128/48/48`, so `selection.dispatch_admitted = false` (`E9_RULE.md`; report SHA-256 `9b8ae3eb…`). None of the three Z-4 replacement designs reaches `.60` (best `.36216`) (`E9_REPLACEMENT.md`). The lineage then moved to the single pre-registered P1 endpoint (`REGISTRATION_P1.md`).
2. **Z-9 upload.** Direct per-file upload could not fit the six-checkpoint roster in the `300 s` shared cap (`~690.6 s`); the `2,409 B` public-input catalog with HTTPS fetch fits a `150 s` forecast budget (`65.41 s`; `242.11 s` worst case with fallback and fetch bound).
3. **E8 late-body critic.** Source-02's online refit failed the gate with its metrics lost. The D1 offline diagnostic showed the registered `[z,h]` head can reach EV `0.5867` / MSE `307,567.7` vs baseline `561,561.6` on `162` natural held-out late rows when fit to convergence. Amendments 03/04 replaced the Adam pass with a float64 least-squares solve on a Helmert zero-sum basis (rank `257`), and the L4 `source-latebody-06` run registered a critic PASS. That PASS "certifies value-head initialization only" (`P1_Y3_SOURCE_REPAIR_AMENDMENT_06.md`).
4. **A3.** Baseline learners exceeded the `1.6384 s` kill bar on both platforms; the optimized learner passed numerically only on L4 (`0.98 s`). Host-CPU collection (`~9.3–9.6 s`) dominated either way, so A100 gave no end-to-end gain.

## What Was Learned and Current Status

- **What was learned:**
  1. At T23-sized paired variance, 96–192-world-per-mix screens give only `.27–.36` joint funding power at `+40`, far below `.60`.
  2. Frozen B8 trunk features plus the clock already explain a substantial share of held-out late-body return variance (D1 EV `0.5867`); the earlier one-pass Adam recipe did not converge to that solution.
  3. The registered `128 × 128` P1 rollout is collection-bound on the Colab host CPU; GPU learner speed is not the bottleneck.
- **Why it closed / current status:** the critic PASS stands and P1 stays on L4, but Y3 is **not PASS**: the source finalisation lacked an actor operational snapshot (`HOLD_MISSING_OPERATIONAL_PROOF`), and the final repair `-05` is prepared but held (`P1_E8_04_SOURCE_REPAIR_HANDOFF_14.md`). B8 s3 remains served.
