**There is useful headroom, but fresh H300 search on one CPU thread is substantially slower than the plan suggests. Prioritize exact trajectory reuse for Path B, and active batching plus host fusion for the teacher. Do not budget a 12–30 ms cold search.** The ratified [SUCCESS_CONTRACT](../final_run_20261019/SUCCESS_CONTRACT.md) authorizes the assisted-hero objective and supersedes older bare-only advice. This document changes no registration, budget or launch authority.

The evidence needs three corrections:

- `ROUND2_MAC_MPS_LOCKSTEP.json` measures **N4/H32**, three Numba threads, MPS, repeated L800 snapshots. Its 1,024-source cell executes 179,500 active row-steps in 42.641 s: **4,210/s end-to-end**, versus **5,647/s host-only** after subtracting hero/vector forwards. The quoted 175 µs is amortized elapsed host cost, **not single-core cost**. Exclusive stages are approximately 113 µs simulation, 27 observations, 12 opponent dispatch, 10 carry, 12 assembly, 1 prepare and 54 hero forward; residual overhead remains. Neither 50 ms verification nor 260 ms expansion follows as one-thread latency.
- T33 `teacher.py:359–362` explicitly rejects opponents with `veto_slots`; its dispatcher performs masked argmax. It does **not** establish costs for Watch6's FRP3+v8 opponents. Preserve that policy's actual veto and carry when measuring T34.
- Six actions mean **three relative directions × two speeds**. T3 dense's 1,126 continuation env-frames/worker-second implies approximately 3.38k across three ideal workers, not a measured 4k aggregate. Its 38/64→54/64 survival and +91.951 mass are verified development results ([T3 ceiling](../t3_ceiling_test_2026-10-15.md)); they do not establish deadline-limited efficacy.

**A tiny new probe strengthens the latency concern.** I loaded released B8 `3e649783…` (1,638,263 parameters), using PyTorch 2.9.1 CPU, intra/inter-op and BLAS threads=1, `inference_mode`, synthetic uint8 raster inputs, 10 warmups and 60 calls per shape. Batch-1 median/p95 was **307/533 µs**; batch-6 **1,997/2,860 µs per call**. This <1-second process ran alongside existing jobs; it is a forward-only diagnostic, not serving qualification. Reproduction: `Ego2sNet(7,60)` via `load_ego2s_checkpoint`, local `(B,7,31,31)`, global `(B,4,37,37)`, scalars `(B,60)`; model source SHA `06b262b7…`.

Thus 300×median is **92 ms of hero inference alone**; 1,500 equivalents cost approximately 0.46–0.50 s. Multiplying call percentiles would not estimate search percentiles. Physics-only 68k rows/s is irrelevant to these serial CNN dependencies. Even an optimistic **2× CPU-forward improvement plus 20–50 µs host work/step** gives 52–61 ms verification and 0.26–0.31 s for 1,500 steps. Those are engineering targets, not measurements: cold full correction remains implausible at 42/83 ms.

**My ranked implementation queue is dependency-driven:**

1. **Measure the correct boundary first.** On saved deployment-available Watch6 snapshots, time actual FRP3+v8 H300 verify and expansion separately, short/coiled/long bodies, one total worker thread. Record clone, CNN, masks/v8, observation, lifecycle and event costs, active rows and p50/p95/p99. Include cold fill and contention. Cap exploratory local cells at 60 seconds; no new cloud allocation. The existing A5 envelope remains the cloud gate. Evaluate interventions completed before their deadline, not just fast confirmations; retain the contract's 11.688 ms update-p95 and cadence bars.

2. **Then reuse deterministic futures across roots.** If the actual state at frame t+1 exactly matches the previous prediction for that frame, H300 verification reuses 299 steps and extends one. Every-eighth-decision confirmations ideally reuse 292: **37.5× less verification work**, excluding fill/invalidation. Cache all six bodies, ordered food/RNG, carry, lifecycle, frame/life and checkpoint identity. Predict and expand a *future* root while time remains; accept only when that root arrives and fully matches. An answer for an expired frame remains stale. Keep one outstanding request, bounded storage and bare fallback. This offers sub-deadline steady-state assistance despite slow cold fill; future-root hit rate and useful corrections are unknown. Under the plan's 0.13M confirmation +0.27M death-window work/world, eliminating confirmations alone saves only approximately **1.48× overall**. Teacher labels still require every registered branch outcome. For live selection alone, testing alternatives in descending Q order can stop at the first H300 survivor; if none survive, all must be resolved. Freeze that rule and its tie handling prospectively.

3. **Then specialize execution, preserving the reference.** Rings, owner/slot grids, coarse counts, stamped BFS, membership bitsets, corpse acceleration, ordered-food mirrors and persistent scratch already exist (`grid_sim.py`, `v8_nb.py`, T33 `OPTIMIZATION_LOG.md`). Fuse the small-row lifecycle/move/mask path into compiled loops with preallocated storage; remove resident-sized NumPy intermediates and Python event crossings. Preserve ordered food, RNG draw order, tail release and respawn-before-policy semantics. Whole-board component sizes are B8 inputs: replacing them with local/capped floods changes decisions. Native loops must release the GIL to protect the serving thread ([Numba documentation](https://numba.readthedocs.io/en/stable/user/jit.html#nogil)); asynchronous Python alone provides no such guarantee.

4. **For the teacher, batch independent roots and compact active branches.** T33 forwards 4.38 resident rows per active row on this fixture; eliminate padding with bounded batch buckets, then overlap independent host/GPU work. N1 verify has no stochastic followers, so that 4.38× opportunity does not transfer wholesale. Treat **2–3× host-cost reduction** as a target. Keep GPU inference fp32 and prove action identity when shapes change; tiny Q differences can change trajectories. Full GPU simulation, reduced precision, shorter horizons, cheaper rollout policies and frozen opponent actions require separate semantic/quality evidence.

For capacity planning, the corrected host-only rate gives `5,647 × (6/3) × 0.7 / (2.4…3.7)` = **2.1–3.3k active steps/s per L4 host**, before transfer/inference, assuming perfect host scaling. Even this conditional model can miss A5's 3k floor; the plan's 3.5–5.5k is not established. After successful fusion/compaction, **4–10k/L4 lane and 6–12k/three Mac host threads plus MPS** are plausible targets, not promised rates or v8-inclusive receipts. At 4–10k, 800×0.4M steps require **8.9–22.2 lane-hours**, or **18–44 units** at the plan's 2 u/h, excluding setup/export/fits; retain reserves. Report avoided work separately from executed steps/s.

The immutable `snake-dqn-artifacts/t33-batched-teacher/calibration-v1.json` confirms 31–53% short-search agreement; this does not validate approximation. T9/T10 support developing enclosures and horizon-dependent AUC around .74–.77, not a universal 50–80-step warning guarantee. Require frozen-oracle state/observation/action/RNG identity, including ties, deaths and stale-cache cases, before timing optimized code. **Path B's decisive test remains efficacy with real misses and fallbacks; faster offline labels cannot substitute for it.**
