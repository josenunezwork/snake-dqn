## F2: preserve productive B8 behavior while allowing local improvement

This is conditional on a valid F1 with **no efficacy GO**, and D0/F1 showing at least a **10 percentage-point drop** in B8 greedy-action agreement on the fresh corpus by 2M in at least 4/5 seeds of the chosen control, while v8-label agreement declines by less than 2 points. This is an operational retention trigger, not proof that every disagreement is harmful. Require a negative 8M mass effect in that control as well. Fix the control before F2: use D if its mean exceeds C, otherwise C; ties choose C. No N=3 or intermediate-checkpoint substitution.

Compare five **fresh** paired 8M seeds of that control against one protected-update package:

- LR **1e−5** instead of 1e−4; other schedules and replay cadence unchanged.
- Add a frozen B8 action-preference loss on **256 extra states per update**, drawn from the D0 training corpus after reserving entire worlds for validation. Existing v8 anchor rows remain unchanged. This adds compute, not incoming transitions or a new TD replay ratio.
- Define `a_B=argmax_legal Q_B`; teacher margin `m=min(1, Q_B(a_B)−max_other Q_B)`. Loss is the mean `max(0, m + max_other Q_theta − Q_theta(a_B))`, multiplied by **0.1**. Single-legal-action rows contribute zero; exact teacher ties give margin zero. Stop gradients through B8. Use the existing legal/advisory mask for both networks. This preserves action preferences without forcing the absolute pretrained Q scale onto new targets.

All constants are fixed proposals; no coefficient sweep. The held-out corpus is never sampled for the loss. Require finite gradients and test that the loss is zero for B8 itself and positive after a deliberate teacher-action inversion. Here and in P0/P1, “legal” means the existing resolved advisory action mask, including its empty-mask fallback enabling normal actions 0–2; it is not a privileged simulator collision filter. Test empty masks and trapped states explicitly. Fresh on-policy samples still provide TD learning; no claim of offline-policy improvement is made. Both the explicit LR and frozen-B8 loss need implementation: the existing none/linear/sqrt LR scaling cannot express 1e−5 at bs2. Bind the effective LR, teacher/corpus hashes, loss coefficient and sampling state into recipe/resume compatibility; do not present this as an existing CLI command.

This tests a **retention package**, not separate causal effects of LR and imitation. An eventual successful package can be ablated later. Its 8M candidate must pass the common B8 GO and have mean protected−control mass ≥20 with one-sided 90% seed-level lower bound >0. Better agreement or a recovery from −80 to −5 is not stronger-than-B8 evidence. A failed package closes this attempt; no further anchor-weight grid. Knowledge retention motivates the test, not a guarantee of success. [Wołczyk et al., 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02868).

## P0/P1: spend simulator compute on better decisions, then test whether a network can learn them

My alternative bet is **mass-advantage expert iteration during training**, using the exact simulator. It supplies a policy-improvement operator distinct from more bootstrapping on the same labels. Expert Iteration separates search from network generalization; success in Hex does not establish transfer to Snake. [Anthony et al., 2017](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.08439).

This is not another death-within-30 classifier. Restrict the first experiment to **length 50–400**, sample ordinary decision states rather than death windows, score **accumulated mass**, and preserve existing boost tripwires. Do not reuse T3/T11 death banks. Planning is permitted only as a training teacher; the candidate assessed for promotion is a single forward network with no teacher, search, veto, or teacher-only input at serving.

**P0 fixed teacher:** at a queried state branch over every legal first action, then roll forward B8 for **128 decisions** against the pinned fixed opponents. Score `sum(length_after * alive_after)/128`, dead frames zero, with no survival bonus, no kill bonus and no Q bootstrap. Use **four common-random-number continuations** per action, each with B8 epsilon .01 and independent rollout RNG; the actual teacher's executed action is the argmax estimated mass, with exact ties reverting to B8. If opponents/simulator are deterministic, repeated identical branches are not four independent samples. Restore complete world, opponent carry state and RNG before branching; branching must not mutate the real continuation. The truncated score is a deliberately imperfect mass surrogate, so require closed-loop H5000 validation.

Collect **512 states from at least 128 fresh training-only worlds**, maximum four states/world, balanced by the three fixed mixes as closely as integer counts permit. Maximum labeling cost is `512×6×4×128 = 1,572,864` branched world frames, plus collection. Partition 96/32 worlds for fit/holdout before labeling. Fit a B8-initialized probe with the preference loss below, **2,000 updates, batch 256, Adam 1e−5**, no RL. World-level holdout prevents near-duplicate frames from masquerading as generalization.

The preference loss uses each action's estimated mass divided by 100, subtracts the B8-action value, and sets a pairwise target margin `clip(positive advantage, 0, 1)`. Apply a hinge between the best branch and B8 only on positive-advantage rows; mix 50% unchanged B8 preference rows from the training portion of D0. Label all legal boost actions too. Do not train only the selected positive rows and hide the prevalence of useful decisions. Fix sampling, tie order and loss weights in the executable registration.

Separately evaluate the **teacher**, not the probe, against B8 on **48 fresh H5000 worlds, 16/mix**. Invoke search at every 64th live hero decision with length 50–400, capped at 32 queries/episode; otherwise execute B8. This bounds search at **4,718,592 branch frames**, plus at most 480,000 real candidate/reference frames. Record the fixed search cadence; this is not the ceiling of an unrestricted planner. Mac parity is a prerequisite for this development adapter.

**P0 GO to P1 requires all of:** equal-mix teacher mass gain ≥20, one-sided 90% stratified paired-world bootstrap lower bound >0, scripted mass point ≥0, common pooled survival guards passed, and probe held-out action agreement with the teacher ≥80% on teacher/B8-disagreement states, at least 10 points above B8. Require ≥64 disagreement states spanning ≥16 held-out worlds; insufficient support means INCONCLUSIVE, not a successful tiny denominator. Bootstrap 10,000 whole-world samples with a pinned RNG. Report changes by mix, action and length. With 48 worlds this is a deliberately coarse spend gate, not a strict claim.

Teacher failure closes this horizon/score experiment. Teacher success but probe failure points to an information/generalization issue; do not spend a large distillation budget. The P0 limits may prove too small to establish support or fit the runtime cap; accept that outcome rather than silently growing the bank. A genuinely different observation could then earn its own future proposal, but is not admitted here.

**P1, only after P0 GO:** freeze the P0 teacher and collect a new **8,192-state** bank, half B8 states and half states visited by the fixed P0 probe, with whole-world train/validation separation and the same length/query rules. This is one predetermined data-aggregation round, not adaptive iteration until a good checkpoint appears. Labeling cap is **25,165,824 branch frames**, in addition to collection. Five student seeds start from B8, each trains the same preference/rehearsal loss for **20,000 updates, batch 256, Adam 1e−5**. Freeze the final update count; no best-validation-checkpoint selection. A B8-only preference loss is zero at B8 initialization, so use that as a bounded identity/optimizer-drift fixture, not five redundant “trained controls.” Pinned B8 is the paired gameplay control. ## What I would defer

- **PFSP/exploiters/PBT:** T23 did not implement adaptive PFSP, so it cannot disprove that mechanism. It does show that the proposed initializer/update/distribution package is destructive. First demonstrate useful fixed-opponent improvement; then a bounded adaptive-versus-static comparison can earn a league. The old Lane A panel budget can exceed training cost by orders of magnitude.
- **Large teacher plus high replay ratio:** plausible after a learning signal, but not a single interpretable intervention now. Replay ratio changes the algorithm and may amplify bias or forgetting. Keep separate counts for updates, TD samples and fresh transitions. [Fedus et al., 2020](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/fedus20a.html).
- **R2D2/recurrence:** first establish a useful history-dependent prediction or action advantage on held-out worlds beyond a same-capacity feedforward control. T23 does not establish missing memory. Sequence replay, burn-in and recurrent-state staleness would add several new failure modes.
- **MuZero-style learned dynamics:** start with the available exact simulator for the teacher. A learned model is justified only if measured exact-rollout cost prevents a demonstrated useful teacher from scaling; otherwise it adds model error before policy improvement is established. No planning at serving is proposed.
- **Pure mass reward / gamma≈1 / longer n-step sweep:** audit the actual reward algebra first. Exact finite-horizon mass optimization may eventually need time-to-go and different terminal handling, which are substantive changes. Do not assume a stronger death penalty, more survival labels or a higher discount aligns the current objective for free.
- **More precision/performance tuning:** pin fp32 bs2 for these scientific comparisons. T22/T24 can be adopted after their relevant parity/throughput qualification, but projected speed is not a measured result and faster failed R0 is not progress toward B8 superiority.

Give each `(study,arm,seed,stage)` one lease with heartbeat and fencing token. Run hot writes on local scratch, publish hash-verified artifacts under an attempt ID, and commit a completion receipt only after complete coverage. An expired lease cannot let an old worker overwrite a replacement attempt. This is a requirement for the queue being built, not a claim that it already implements these semantics.

Evaluation shards may reuse completed immutable world rows after validating hashes. Interrupted learning requires validated optimizer, target, replay, schedules, actor/opponent/RNG and update-debt state to resume. Otherwise retain INCOMPLETE and require an explicit registered retry decision; do not quietly warm-start from the last weights. Avoid automatic promotion, arm creation or failure retries. Ready jobs can use spare runtimes; no ready scientific work means release the runtime. The objective is stronger evidence per hour and unit, not maximum idle-GPU avoidance.

The first concrete deliverables should therefore be a D0 diagnostic packet, a tested N=1 same-pool adapter with the four-cell F1 intent, and the small P0 teacher packet. Those can distinguish recoverable fine-tuning damage from the need for a new improvement operator without spending the owner's compute balance on an unsupported long league.
