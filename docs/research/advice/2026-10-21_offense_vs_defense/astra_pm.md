The objective is a materially stronger mixed-Watch6 hero than **bare B8 s3**, with skill, survival, product and timing bars. The contract requires pooled H5000 mass gain ≥40 with LB90 >0, scripted no-death gain ≥0.03, and Watch deaths/1k improvement with paired 95% evidence while preserving mass. The relative boost amendment supersedes the printed absolute floor for the affected registrations (`docs/research/final_run_20261019/CAMPAIGN_LOG.md:75-82`).

**My belief:** Path B is still the shortest route to a credible served improvement because its remaining question is whether an already demonstrated offline mechanism can deliver on time. Offense deserves an option now because the current teacher leaves some healthy-state action choices unexplored. Neither belief establishes which has the larger eventual gain. A full pivot would exchange an imminent feasibility/replication answer for a new instrumentation, policy-design and qualification chain. Staying exclusively defensive would leave a cheap, distinct hypothesis untested.

## 4. What the offense lane actually buys

**O1 hypothesis:** B8 leaves material net mass on the table at decisions where its normal continuation survives; an opportunity controller can capture some without changing mechanics or learning a new reward function. Start with corpse harvesting and near-term cut-offs, not encirclement curricula. Short one-action branches may miss multi-action encirclements; a negative O1 closes this inexpensive design, not all offense.

Implementation prerequisites are real work, **not existing qualified capability**: add a diagnostic event ledger and a separate opportunity selector/entrypoint. Keep them out of the frozen T34 import closure. The grid food map distinguishes ambient/corpse cells, but boost trails also enter as corpse-class food (`src/simd_env/grid_sim.py:13,411-436,517`). Therefore `corpse_ate` alone cannot identify “mass from kills.” Track food creation cause, victim/creator and consuming snake, including deduplication/eviction; report boost-trail food separately from death-corpse food and ambient food. Check per-life length changes against food/growth, boost expenditure and other dynamics; initial length and respawn length are not acquired food. Kill credit is a separate counter, not a second mass credit.

Before any performance claims: instrumented versus uninstrumented actions/state/RNG must agree on deterministic fixtures including simultaneous deaths, contested food, corpse-cap eviction and boost trails; a tiny live/Grid accounting fixture must agree too. Clone opponents' mutable state and recompute their responses on each branch. “Frozen opponent policy” does not mean its realized actions remain unchanged after the hero intervenes. Preserve the exact deployment-available state/RNG contract; an oracle branch using unavailable future randomness is only a ceiling diagnostic.

**Frozen O1 design proposal:**

- One fresh, collision-audited namespace, **96 B8 s3 H5000 worlds, 32 per mix**, mechanics/profile/opponents matching the campaign. Record net mass ledger, kills, corpse capture fraction, deaths and boost by length band. This bank is development-only and burned for subsequent efficacy testing.
- At decisions **200,400,…,4800** (24 roots/world maximum), if B8 is alive and its H300 continuation survives, test all advisory-legal first actions with greedy B8 continuation for min(300, remaining horizon). Rank survivors by full-horizon branch mass, dead frames zero, ties to B8 then lowest action index. No kill bonus, reward shaping, repeated macro, or threshold sweep.
- Count at most one “opportunity world” per world: a non-B8 action improves H300 mean mass by **≥20**, survives the same horizon, and actually adds death-corpse intake or a credited kill relative to the bare branch. Also report food-only wins; they may be useful foraging but do not validate the offensive premise. **Advance to O2 only with ≥12/96 opportunity worlds, including ≥4 frozen and ≥4 mixed**, valid accounting and a complete cost-qualified packet. These are proposed resource-allocation thresholds, not significance or promotion bars. Do not sum overlapping counterfactual gains into an H5000 estimate.
- Order: **INVALID/INCOMPLETE → KILL for faults or an unaffordable completed-work forecast → GO on the frozen opportunity rule → NO-GO otherwise**. No extra roots or worlds after the look. A failed provenance implementation is not evidence that offensive opportunities are absent.

**Cost admission:** maximum branch work =96×24×6×300 = **4,147,200 active branch frames**, plus base trajectories, clone/feature work and exports. A5 observed 377/432 row-steps/s per L4 lane and 54.4/57.2 teacher worlds/h (`docs/research/final_run_20261019/CAMPAIGN_LOG.md:59`). Using the slower 377 only as a sensitivity gives **3.06 lane-h /6.11 u** for branch stepping. It is **not a measured offense rate**. The ≤2-u disjoint admission fixture must measure the complete workload, including census/base cost, and project completion within the remaining **16-u/8-h total**. Otherwise do not launch the fixed bank; revise a future design before collecting it. Do not price this in RL transitions/s or count GPU idle time as free.

**O2 hypothesis:** the opportunity rule improves the actual trajectory rather than cherry-picked branch scores. Freeze one controller before the fresh bank: use the O1 fixed schedule and survival filter, take the top-ranked non-B8 action only when its predicted gain is ≥20 **and** its simulated continuation predicts additional death-corpse intake or a credited kill relative to the bare branch; otherwise use bare B8. This event predicate uses forward simulations from the current deployment-available state, never observed future events. No death rewind or known-future action injection. Its baseline is bare B8. This tests an offense-targeted selector; retain the event ledger to check whether realized gains actually came from corpse acquisition rather than incidental ambient foraging, and require that attribution before arguing for an offense pivot. Combining it with the rescue controller would require another frozen candidate and another bank, outside this envelope.

- Compare on **96 new paired H5000 worlds, 32/mix**, equal-weighted mix means. Unit = paired world; one final look; paired, mix-stratified uncertainty. A single controller has no five-training-seed claim. P7 makes these distinctions explicit (`docs/research/final_run_20261019/P7_STATISTICS.md:10-24,38-39`).
- Proposed decision order: invalid/incomplete first; then unconditional faults or survival harm (pooled alive-frame point delta <−0.05, any mix <−0.075, any absolute alive fraction <0.30) → stop; **advance** only if mass point ≥40 and one-sided LB90>0, scripted no-death point gain ≥0.03, scripted mass point ≥0, all above survival guards, and delivered boost share between half paired B8 and 10%. Mass UB90≤0 → scientific no-go; otherwise inconclusive, with no extension. These point survival screens are triage, not a substitute for the registered strict guard tests.
- Run **12 fresh paired continuous-respawn mixed-Watch6 H5000 sessions** as a development product check; aggregate by complete session. Use the same 200-frame schedule in session time, never resetting it on hero respawn: at most 24 opportunity roots per session. Branch survival is for the current hero life, with no respawn credit. Require directionally lower deaths/1k and mass relative point delta ≥−5% to buy the next experiment; report wide intervals. This deliberately does **not** claim the contract's paired 95% product pass. A development skill success with unresolved product behavior is not release-ready.
- The admission fixture must forecast all 192 skill trajectories plus 24 product trajectories, repeated branch work and export within **32 u/16 lane-h**; otherwise O2 is not admitted. A Colab unpaced winner subsequently needs a new Mac deliverability/paced identity test, fresh independent confirmation/strict banks, serving qualification and owner OK.

**What changes my mind:** a complete O2 success buys a larger offense share in the next tranche, especially if Path B cannot deliver at either tested N. A matched fresh **offense versus defensive finalist** comparison, both meeting product/timing bars, can justify a campaign pivot; cross-study point estimates cannot. If O1 has few safe opportunities or O2 gains kills but loses mass/survival, close this lane and keep defense. If both defense premises fail and offense is positive, it becomes the research lead; if all are negative, stop spending on these mechanisms and formulate a genuinely new question.
