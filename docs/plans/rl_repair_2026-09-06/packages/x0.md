## Implementation / execution steps

1. Freeze five fresh training seeds, 500k hero transitions each, initial checkpoints, explicit epsilon horizon, the legacy sampler, unchanged Adam/Huber/network and the versioned corrected profile. Freeze 12 development evaluation worlds per chosen mix before training.
2. Run one learner at a time. Per-arm wall cap comes from G0 shakedown projection plus 25% headroom, with a 30-minute absolute ceiling; exceeding it is an incident, not permission to extend. Resource caps remain in force.
3. Evaluate final and same-seed initial networks on paired worlds/mixes plus scripted anchors. Average repeated worlds/mixes within each trained seed; report a five-seed interval and raw results.
4. Keep no-learning or inconclusive results. They do not justify silent tuning or block a separately sound PPO control. Do not pool these scores with the older SIMD campaign.
