## Disclosures and limits

- B8 was trained with v8 labels and consumes live v8-derived input features. It is served
  **without a veto wrapper**; the v8 inspector feature group describes inputs.
- Tier 2 passed by **0.034 above the threshold at SE 0.023** (unrounded margin 0.034375).
  This is a narrow margin and does not establish a safety advantage.
- Watch drives an all-B8 roster; Play drives B8 opponents. Those self-play rosters were
  **not gated** by T7's scripted/frozen/mixed comparisons. T12's 25 Watch episodes recorded
  53 deaths / 125,000 stepped hero decisions = **0.424 deaths per 1,000 decisions**.
  Logical length: min 1, p05 14, p25 75, median 172, p75 304, p95 528, max 867,
  mean 208.219784. These are monitoring baselines, not additional skill claims.
- The existing ego2s speed ceilings remain: at most six driven AI, **24 fps**;
  seven through twelve, **12 fps**; larger rosters, `max(1, floor(144/n))`.
  The Play human is excluded; respawnable AI are included. T12B qualified only
  Watch6/Watch12/Play6/Play12, with 6/12/5/11 driven AI respectively. These are
  processing-budget ceilings, not promises of achieved cadence. The sleep-after-work
  loop, network and client rendering limits are unchanged.
- Play opponents are heavier than before. Leaderboard rows before and after this release
  are **not comparable**. Scored runs already record the served checkpoint name.
- Train is not qualified: ego2s Train requests are refused and return to Watch with a
  forward-only message. No frontend changes are needed for this release; T12/T12B retain
  the inspector, narrator, steering-wheel and speed-control qualification evidence.

## Monitoring and owner check-in

Monitor through the first week **or** the first **100,000 Watch hero decisions**,
**whichever ends later**. The owner checks the counters at seven days (**2026-10-24**),
then continues the release window if 100,000 decisions have not yet accrued.

Roll back on any of these three conditions:

1. Any served fallback reason or any exception in the ego2s path.
2. Watch hero deaths per 1,000 decisions **>0.848**, twice the 0.424 qualification baseline.
3. Default-speed frame/update p95 **>1.5×** the matched qualification measurement.

Review late-death complaints without automatic rollback: that behavior was at parity
with the previous served agent and remains a disclosed research problem.

The server emits `b8-release-monitor:` JSON records to stderr. Retain the complete log
across restarts; explicit rollback resolutions remain visible but do not contribute
B8 gameplay samples. To capture a server operated outside this verification task:

```bash
mkdir -p logs
./venv/bin/python web/serve.py 2>> logs/b8-release.stderr.log
```

Read that retained log with:

```bash
./venv/bin/python scripts/monitor_b8_release.py logs/b8-release.stderr.log
```

The script prints each trigger against its threshold, window progress and the day-seven
check-in. It does not restart or stop the server. Exit 0 means the available measured
metrics are within bounds; exit 2 means a rollback trigger or incomplete/invalid telemetry.
The observation window is reported separately and cannot finish until both seven days
and 100,000 decisions have accrued. Missing data is never reported as healthy.

Watch decisions count actual hero `update` dispatches, including dispatch after a
start-of-frame respawn; deaths use `frame_death_causes`. Deaths/decisions aggregate across
speeds. Latency measures `GameState.update`, excluding the first 200 updates after each
construction/rebuild/reset and using only default-speed (12 fps) samples. This follows
the T12B update measurement rather than socket, browser, or full tick latency. Warm-up
frames still contribute hero decisions/deaths. Mode and driven-AI roster groups remain
separate; unsupported roster sizes are reported as lacking a qualified baseline.
