# Big-Lever E4/E2 PPO Pilot, WP-3 P1 100M PPO Endpoint, Y3 Critic Qualification, and A3 Learner Ceiling

## Question and Goal

Within the October 2026 Big-Lever campaign, this lineage asked whether a Proximal Policy Optimization (`PPO`) actor-critic initialized from the released `b8-s3` `ego2s-bv8` checkpoint (`saved_snakes/b8_s3_t7_20261016.pth`, sha256 `3e649783…`) — keeping `b8-s3`'s encoder and calibrated base logits while training a zero-initialized residual actor head and a PopArt value head augmented with a critic-only normalized remaining-horizon clock $h = (5000 - \text{frame}) / 5000$ — could overcome `b8-s3`'s late-body (`L >= 800`) self-enclosure ceiling and beat bare `b8-s3` by $\ge +40$ `H5000` mass-integral points. The lineage progressed through four coupled stages:
1. **E4 Collector Ruler & E2 Two-Arm PPO Pilot (`2026-10-20` to `2026-10-21`):** Measuring Colab L4 `GridBatchSim` collection throughput (`E4`), qualifying HTTPS checkpoint staging (`Z-9`), and testing whether a two-arm (`R` `GRU256` vs. `F` feedforward) pilot screen (`E2`) could clear the `E9` operating-characteristic gate ($\ge 0.60$ joint funding probability at $+40$ mass and $\le 0.10$ family-wise null pass rate).
2. **WP-3 P1 Single-Endpoint 100M Registration & Y3 Late-Body Critic Qualification (`2026-10-21` to `2026-10-23`):** Pivoting after `E9` blocked two-arm screening to a single pre-registered `100,000,000`-transition feedforward endpoint (`arm F`, seed `20261021`, `128` worlds / `12` workers), gated by cross-L4 pause/restore durability (`Y3`) and natural held-out late-body (`L >= 800`) critic qualification (`E8`: $\text{EV} \ge 0.10$ and $\text{MSE} < (1, h, L)$ baseline).
3. **A3 Actor-Enabled P1 Learner Ceiling Assay (`2026-10-22`):** Measuring whether the actor-enabled P1 learner (`128 × 128 = 16,384` transitions, 2 PPO epochs) could clear `10,000` hero transitions/s ($\le 1.6384\text{ s}$/rollout) when collection is free and whether Colab `A100-40GB` beat `L4` end-to-end.

## Final Verdict and Key Numbers

1. **`E9` Operating-Characteristic Gate Blocks Two-Arm `E2` Screening (`2026-10-21`):**
   - Using measured `T23` paired `H5000` mass-integral standard deviations (`364.683` scripted, `262.732` frozen, `212.360` mixed), the 20,000-replicate Monte Carlo evaluation ([`research/bl_ppo/E9_RULE.md`](../../../research/bl_ppo/E9_RULE.md); `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/big-lever/exp/bl-e2/e9-oc-04/report.json`, sha256 `9b8ae3eb…`) showed that at a true $+40$ mass-integral effect and non-negative survival delta, minimum joint funding probability across correlations $\rho \in \{0, 0.5, 0.9\}$ was `0.27045` (`MC95` lower `0.264338`, family null pass `0.05050`) on `96/32/32` worlds and `0.30700` (`MC95` lower `0.300645`, family null pass `0.06160`) on `128/48/48` worlds — well below the registered `0.60` bar.
   - The 100,000-replicate `Z-4` follow-up ([`research/bl_ppo/E9_REPLACEMENT.md`](../../../research/bl_ppo/E9_REPLACEMENT.md); `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/big-lever/exp/bl-e2/e9-z4-01/report.json`, sha256 `3597a19f…`) tested three replacement designs: (a) `192/64/64` single-stage (`0.36216` min funding power, null `0.06593`), (b) `128/48/48 + E6` late-prefix gate (`0.08648` min funding power, null `0.03342`), and (c) two-stage `96/32/32 + 96/32/32` (`0.35954` min funding power, null `0.06393`). Reaching $\ge 0.60$ joint power at $+40$ required $n \ge 530$ worlds/mix (`~1,590` worlds total, `~53.9 u`/arm), motivating the pivot to a single pre-registered feedforward endpoint in `WP-3 P1` ([`research/bl_ppo/REGISTRATION_P1.md`](../../../research/bl_ppo/REGISTRATION_P1.md)).

2. **`Z-9` Upload Qualification and Snapshot Byte-Bound Sizing (`2026-10-21` to `2026-10-22`):**
   - Direct `colab-cli` upload of `b8-s3` (`6,562,005 B` in `147.51 s` = `44,484.79 B/s` in `big-lever/exp/bl-e2/e4-e8-output/receipt.json`) meant uploading the 6-checkpoint opponent roster (`30,720,004 B`) would require `~690.6 s`, exceeding the supervisor's `300 s` total upload deadline. Staging checkpoints via `PUBLIC_INPUTS.json` (`2,409 B`, sha256 `736f7b4e…`, [`research/bl_upload/README.md`](../../../research/bl_upload/README.md)) cut normal upload forecasts to `65.41–69.47 s` (`122.11–148.31 s` with sparse-source archive fallback).
   - Full `128`-world `GridBatchSim` pause closures (`128 × 6 × 10,400 × 2 × 8 = 127,795,200 B` raw body arrays plus `10,000`-row ordinary and `20,000`-row refit reservoirs) measured `427.9 MB` for the primary `.pt` snapshot and `595,248,269 B` across the 9-file origin closure (`427.9 MB` `.pt` + `184.5 MB` `.sqlite` banks), requiring `byte_bounds.py` effective (`2×`) ceilings of `1,399,502,416 B` (`1.40 GB`) per primary snapshot and `2,345,171,152 B` (`2.35 GB`) per complete closure.

3. **Natural Late-Body (`L >= 800`) `E8` Critic Qualification via Rank-257 Helmert Zero-Sum SVD (`2026-10-22` to `2026-10-23`):**
   - Across `2,523,136` total refit transitions (`2,010,624` fit-world transitions across `102` fit worlds and `26` held-out worlds), natural `b8-s3` rollouts yielded `62` completed held-out late-body lives (`162` rows with $L \ge 800$, mix breakdown `11 / 25 / 26 >= 8`) and `286` fit late-body lives (`796` rows), clearing the [`research/bl_ppo/E8_LATE_BODY_AMENDMENT_02.md`](../../../research/bl_ppo/E8_LATE_BODY_AMENDMENT_02.md) support floors (`>= 40` held-out lives / `160` rows; `>= 160` fit lives / `640` rows).
   - Online 1-pass Adam over all transition lengths failed the held-out $L \ge 800$ gate (`EV = -0.0561`, `MSE = 874,775.40` vs. `(1, h, L)` baseline `MSE = 561,561.60`), whereas the `D1` offline least-squares diagnostic (`/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/big-lever/exp/bl-p1/y3/source-latebody-05-snapshot-bounds-staging-repin-20261022/offline-critic-diagnostic-01/diagnostic.json`, sha256 `cd282a6d…`) achieved `EV = 0.586714 >= 0.10` and `MSE = 307,567.74 < 561,561.60` (`bias = -105.1`), proving `D1 OPTIMISATION_SHORTFALL` ([`research/bl_ppo/E8_LATE_BODY_AMENDMENT_03.md`](../../../research/bl_ppo/E8_LATE_BODY_AMENDMENT_03.md)).
   - Because `LayerNorm` without elementwise affine parameters enforces $1^T z = 0$ (making raw `[1, z, h]` rank `256` with condition number `4.30e9`), [`research/bl_ppo/E8_LATE_BODY_AMENDMENT_04.md`](../../../research/bl_ppo/E8_LATE_BODY_AMENDMENT_04.md) projected $z$ onto a `256×255` Helmert zero-sum orthonormal basis $Q$ (`helmert-zero-sum-256-v1`), restoring full column rank `257`, reducing condition number to `95.30`, matching float64 fit `MSE = 896,460.1646` within fp32 relative error `1.04e-8 <= 1e-6`, bounding 3-step actor PopArt drift to `2.0773 <= sigma 4.9825` (`FIRST_STEP_FIXTURE_04`), and passing live L4 `E8` qualification in `source-latebody-06-e8-04-review-20261022-08/final` (`49cb3c93…`, [`research/bl_ppo/P1_E8_04_FINALISATION_HANDOFF_09.md`](../../../research/bl_ppo/P1_E8_04_FINALISATION_HANDOFF_09.md)).

4. **`A3` Actor-Enabled P1 Learner Ceiling Assay (`2026-10-22`):**
   - On `128 worlds × 128 steps = 16,384` hero transitions (2 PPO epochs, fp32), 1,024-row chunked forward evaluation matched unchunked gradients within `3.0640512704849243e-7` (`atol=2e-5, rtol=2e-4`) and achieved cached learner times of `0.41 s` on Colab `A100-40GB` (`~39,960` transitions/s) and `0.98 s` on Colab `L4` (`~16,718` transitions/s) — both well below the `1.6384 s` (`10,000` transitions/s) kill threshold ([`research/a3_p1_ceiling/README.md`](../../../research/a3_p1_ceiling/README.md); [`research/a3_p1_ceiling/PROTOCOL.md`](../../../research/a3_p1_ceiling/PROTOCOL.md)). Because 12-worker host-CPU rollout collection dominated at `~9.56 s` per rollout on both runtimes, `A100` provided no end-to-end wall-clock advantage over `L4`.

## What Was Learned and Current Status

- **What was learned:**
  1. High per-world `H5000` variance (`SD ~ 212–365`) makes multi-arm 96–192-world screening underpowered (`~27%–36%` joint funding power at $+40$ mass), requiring either single-endpoint pre-registration or $\ge 530$ paired worlds per mix.
  2. `b8-s3`'s frozen 256-D trunk (`z`) plus normalized remaining clock (`h`) already linearly explains `58.67%` of held-out late-body ($L \ge 800$) return variance (`MSE = 307,567.74` vs. `561,561.60` baseline), but online 1-pass Adam over all transition lengths overwrites the scarce late-body tail (`~0.04%` of frames) and unprojected zero-sum `LayerNorm` features create an ill-conditioned (`4.30e9`) collinear intercept; solving in float64 SVD on the `256×255` Helmert basis `Q` (`helmert-zero-sum-256-v1`) fixes both.
  3. On `128 × 128` PPO rollouts, the 1,024-row chunked learner takes `< 1 s` on L4 (`0.98 s`), whereas CPU `GridBatchSim` collection takes `~9.56 s`, making CPU collection rather than GPU learner compute the binding throughput bottleneck.
- **Why it closed / current status:** `E8` late-body critic qualification passed on L4 (`source-latebody-06`, exporting the qualified `427.9 MB` origin snapshot `dbdc10c6…`) and `A3` completed with `KEEP P1 ON L4`. The `Y3` origin-import durability repair (`source-repair-05`) was placed on operational **HOLD** (`10.51 u` spent of `34.50 u` envelope) on `2026-10-23` as the campaign prioritized the serving-time `exact-stop-l2-prime` Watch controller (`T34 Claim B`) and faster GPU-assisted training lanes.
