## What Was Learned

1. **Why M4-B (`B8`) Succeeded Where M3 / M3-C Failed:**
   - Feeding `v8`'s `30` per-action spatial/safety features directly into `Ego2sNet`'s scalar fusion trunk (`ego2s-bv8`) while training with a survival-aligned mass reward (`kappa=1.0`, zero kill bonus), a long horizon (`gamma=0.998`), `25%` mixed opponents, and on-policy `v8` margin labels let the bare CNN internalize short-horizon collision avoidance (`+138.7` H5000 mass over `frp3-s12 + v8` without a runtime veto).
2. **Why Every Single-Pass Late-Death Fine-Tune (`M6`, `T2–T6`, `T11`) Either Lost Mass or Collapsed into Boost:**
   - **The Margin Spillover / Caution Trap (`T2`, `T3`, `T4a`, `T4c`):** In `B8`, Q-values at `L >= 700` sit on a flat plateau where the fatal-vs-surviving action gap at the Last Avoidable Decision (LAD) is tiny (median `0.45 Q`), while `D=300` rollout survival labels heavily favor speed boost (`55.86%` in v1) or caution. Unconstrained margin losses (`T3`) or large Huber deltas (`T2`/`T2b`) shift action preferences across `50–90%` of ordinary `L >= 700` states, cutting late-game foraging growth by `135–460` length units. When spillover is reduced via `prefer-nonboost` relabeling + distance weighting + `10,885` matched outside-counter rows (`T4c TWO_SIDED_T`, consequential drift `3.81%` vs `0.99%` baseline), held-out episode survival still does not improve (`-0.014`).
   - **The Truncated-Horizon Boost Illusion (`T4e`, `T4f`, `T11`):** In `D=300` rollout return regression, boosting at a decisive state buys immediate 300-step survival (`+32 Q` target advantage over normal actions) while pushing the `gamma=0.998` mass-loss penalty of burned segments into the bootstrapped terminal value `V(s_T)`, causing rapid policy collapse into boost (`34%` in `RETURN`, `>2%` tripwire in `RETURN_C` and `LOCAL30`).
   - **Multi-Agent Non-Determinism at Long Horizons (`T5`, `T8`, `T9`, `T10`, `t4_gate1`):** Single-snapshot features (60 scalars, 84 scalars, raw rasters, 108 arrival-race features, and even a privileged full-arena critic in `t4_gate1`) achieve `~0.91–0.93` death AUC at `30` steps but decay to `~0.56–0.75` at `300` steps because neck-sealing pockets emerge from `50–100` steps of interactive multi-snake trajectories rather than static geometry at `t=0`. Closing late deaths therefore required decision-time tree search / online rollout controllers (later realized in T34/T35 `exact-stop-l2-prime`), not static single-pass weight nudges.
