## Scientific decision

The fixed-cohort CZ-visible mark-4608 continuation is coherent as a single new package:
longer native collection plus a substantially larger fresh stream allocated across
solo, S2 and S6 tasks. It is not the unchanged closed S2 dose continuation. CZ had enabled
enemy inputs; the new package preserves those weights and all three lineages. Cohort
selection was outcome-informed, and the result estimates package utility rather than an
isolated cause or a training-seed population effect. CW's H768 result belongs to earlier
CV parents and cannot be inherited as tested CZ performance.

The study answers a concrete question: does the complete package improve total-horizon
living mass in both declared opponent profiles while retaining solo competence? It
uses each lineage's own frozen parent, all fixed world IDs, a practical gain floor,
paired screening intervals and explicit exposure checks. No favorable seed, world,
checkpoint mark or shortened horizon may replace the fixed endpoint. A pass is not
promotion; the shared tournament remains separate.
