## Implementation / execution steps

1. Check actor/buffer liveness and heartbeat age during warmup and training. Use bounded join and termination cleanup; never rely on Queue.qsize() for correctness.
2. Propagate learner/actor/buffer failure to nonzero exit. Never emit a success-named final checkpoint after failure; label optional recovery artifacts failed.
3. Add explicit learner-update, environment-transition and wall-time budgets. Preserve --total-steps as a documented/deprecated learner-update alias; stop at the first declared limit.
4. Report actor frames/transitions, emitted replay rows, learner samples/updates, policy version and heartbeat ages. Bound overshoot by the documented in-flight batch allowance.

## Acceptance evidence

- Subprocess fixtures cover actor exit before warmup, buffer exit before/after ready, learner exception, ignored shutdown and timeout. Each fixture has its own short hard timeout.
- No child remains after every outcome; failure never reports success. Counts reconcile on a controlled tiny run.
- The existing --total-steps command still resolves to learner updates; new limits and their exit causes appear in receipts.
