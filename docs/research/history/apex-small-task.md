# Apex Small-Task Mechanism & Terminal-Mask Diagnostic Series (2026-09-22 to 2026-09-24)

**Question & Goal:** After the September 2026 PQN controlled-encounter series failed its all-seed competence and retention bars (`solo-food-microbenchmark`), this 25-study program transferred the controlled micro-benchmark protocol to the `vector61` `ApexNetwork(61, 512, 6)` and canonical `ApexLearner` (PER + 3-step returns + target network) across three fixed seed lineages (`2026099201`–`2026099203`, and `2026101001`–`2026101003` for the scaling/H256 series). It asked: (1) whether fresh Apex networks learn single-food seeking (`H=8`, `H=16`) via supervised imitation or from-scratch TD updates; (2) whether canonical TD updates retain supervised warm starts or extend competence to multi-food replenishing growth (`H=64`, `H=256`) under longer collection, lower exploration (`epsilon=0.1`), H16/H64 replay mixing, uniform replay (`alpha=0`), or 2x2 width/breadth scaling (`512` vs `1,024` hidden units, `96` vs `384` roots); and (3) what causes late-horizon growth failures and terminal self-collisions.

## Final Verdict & Key Numbers

- **Short-Horizon Learning vs Warm-Start Retention (`H=8`, `H=16`)**: Fresh `ApexNetwork(61, 512, 6)` models achieved **`96/96` held food and `96/96` survival** in all `3` seeds both under supervised cross-entropy (`512` updates, `apex-solo-imitation/closeout.json` SHA-256 `59907bee…`) and from-scratch canonical PER/3-step TD (`5,000` updates, `apex-canonical-retention/closeout.json` SHA-256 `6c5245d4…`), transferring zero-shot to the fresh `H=16` grid with **`96/96` food and `100%` teacher agreement** across all `3` seeds (`apex-fresh-h16/closeout.json` SHA-256 `cde752c9…`). By contrast, running canonical TD updates from supervised `BC512` parents failed retention (`26/9, 17/15, 64/62` train/held food); the parents' initial raw Q scale was much larger (`max Q` `11.66`–`14.67` vs `0.247`–`0.426` for `BC0`), which the study reports as descriptive scale evidence, not a demonstrated cause.

## What Was Learned

1. **Canonical Apex TD learns solo food seeking rapidly from scratch, unlike the tested PQN recipe:** Whereas the tested PQN recipe got worse with more training in the four-hour window and the PQN controlled-encounter studies failed, canonical `ApexLearner` (`vector61` + PER + 3-step TD + target network) learned `96/96` single-food collection from scratch in `5,000` updates and generalized zero-shot to `H=16` (`96/96`) and strongly to `H=64` (`122`–`136` foods at `5,000` updates; `289`–`303` foods vs `304` teacher at `20,000` updates).
2. **Naive supervised warm-starting into TD did not retain:** Starting canonical TD from cross-entropy imitation weights (initial `max Q` `11.66`–`14.67`) failed the retention rule at 5,000 updates, whereas `BC0` controls (`max Q` `0.247`–`0.426`) learned `96/96`. The Q-scale gap is descriptive; the study did not establish it as the cause.
3. **Late-horizon (`H=256`) self-collision deaths followed a low-free-space choice one step before the trap:** At mark `40,000`, `15/15` deaths came on the first empty-safe-mask frame, and in `14/15` the predecessor chose a normal action with recorded free-space feature `0.03125` while another safe normal action scored `1.0`. This is a saved-record taxonomy; the native rescue probe is unaudited/provisional. The series overview records that H5000 self-collision deaths motivated the later serving-time free-space veto (09-27 to 10-03; see `apex-veto.md`).
