## Shared bank and statistical protocol

Both probes retain T5's exact **19,976 states, 103,264 legal branches, 19,094 deaths and
342 worlds**. The target is the recorded branch dying within 300 transitions; death at
transition 300 is positive, survival through 300 is negative for this horizon. Illegal
or missing branches do not enter either classifier. These outcomes belong to the
source continuation policies, not to new counterfactual rollouts.

The canonical T5 dataset SHA256 is
`e4ecbe991061c90bb2ae3cdb4b236d0025f1219d61fa806b7e96f43962d9e02a`.
Both probes load T5's saved five world-grouped folds, validate state/world identity,
and retain its original historical holdout worlds inside the pooled out-of-fold
analysis. No state or action branch crosses its world's fold. All AUC intervals
are percentile 95% intervals from the same 2,000 whole-world bootstrap draws as T5
(seed 20261017). Bootstrap multiplicity applies to every branch from each sampled
world; intervals condition on fitted held-out predictions, without model refitting.
AUC deltas use paired world draws.

As in T5/T5b, the outside-state sample is concentrated in source final s0. The
results describe this reused bank, not fresh gameplay or a population-wide bound.

## Probe 1: CNN trained from raw raster

The new diagnostic network has **294,353 parameters**. Two independent three-layer
convolution towers process the unpacked ego2s-b local **7×31×31** and global
**4×37×37** planes. Each tower uses 3×3 stride-2 convolutions with 16, 32 and 32
channels and ReLU. Their flattened outputs are concatenated with all 60 original
scalars and an explicit six-way one-hot action. A 192-unit ReLU head, dropout 0.1,
and one binary logit predict D300 death. Convolution outputs are shared across the
six action inputs only to avoid redundant computation; the loss includes exactly
the recorded legal branches.

Raw byte planes are divided by 255, with the local reach-time channel scaled by
31 instead. Scalars use the inner training states' mean and standard deviation.
The network starts from scratch for every fold. Fixed settings: AdamW, learning
rate 0.001, weight decay 0.0001, 128 states per batch, unweighted legal-branch BCE,
gradient norm cap 10, maximum 80 epochs. Label-independent 20% of the outer training
worlds form the inner validation split. Early stopping selects the minimum inner
validation BCE with improvement 0.0001 and patience eight; outer worlds never
participate in normalization, fitting or epoch selection. No hyperparameter sweep
or result-driven refit was performed. Selected epochs were 3, 5, 3, 8 and 6;
the five runs stopped after 11, 13, 11, 16 and 14 epochs, respectively.

The full end-to-end timing benchmark measured CPU 0.04236 s/batch and MPS 0.00878
s/batch, so MPS was used under the spec's faster-device exception. The full diagnostic
run took **115.16 seconds**. No policy checkpoint was initialized or updated by this
classifier. Saved fold models carry an explicit diagnostic-only artifact tag.

**Advisor reading:** the ≥0.85 signal-recovery criterion is not met. The CNN is

## Probe 3: arrival-race inputs

Each of six actions adds three values, flattened action-major into 18 features.
The one-frame hypothetical hero move, boost fallback/burn, completed post-frame
body collision check, pending growth and slack-one own-tail release map are
inherited from T5. Food growth during the hypothetical move remains unmodeled.
Opponent occupied cells retain T5's static-obstacle convention; their current
heads seed a simultaneous multi-source BFS, allowed to leave their source cells.
This is a unit-cell-per-frame arrival approximation, not predicted opponent
policy actions or simulated opponent body trajectories. Both floods use the same
post-move hero-tail release schedule and T5 no-wait first-arrival traversal.

- **Voronoi area:** hero-reachable cells with strictly earlier hero arrival than
  every opponent; ties are excluded. The flood is uncapped; the reported feature
  is exactly `log2(min(area,4096)+1)/12`.
- **Contested fraction:** cells in the entire action's tail-aware hero region
  with an opponent arriving earlier or at the same time, divided by that region's
  size.
- **Margin:** minimum hero BFS arrival distance to a contested cell, i.e. the
  nearest contested frontier at unit speed. A valid uncontested region uses
  `width*height+1`; immediate contest uses zero. Invalid/dead action feature rows
  are zero.

The Python reference flood and compiled CPU flood agreed exactly on 25 randomized
grids, all 276 actions from the 46 saved T1 states, and 9,090 normalized feature
values from 505 restored bank states. The compiled kernel changes execution speed
only. Two disjoint CPU extraction processes restored all 19,976 bank states in
342 worlds, byte-checking every packed observation and all 60 scalar values.
Both receipts rechecked source, plan, manifest and checkpoint hashes at completion.
The partitions took 1,126.75 and 1,075.75 seconds; feature calculation itself took
24.18 and 28.28 seconds. No additional process was launched while both extractions
were active.

The classifiers receive 60+24+18 features plus the same six action indicators
as T5: **108 columns**. GBM and logistic-regression settings are unchanged from
T5, including fold-local LR standardization, no GBM early stopping and fatal
convergence warnings. The 60+24 baseline is refitted on the same folds and must
reproduce every saved T5 prediction within 1e-10 before results are accepted.

**Advisor reading:** arrival features contain modest incremental signal: both
paired CIs exclude zero. However, GBM AUC is **0.749106**, still approximately
0.75, rather than the requested jump above the existing static-feature level.
It is not the strong positive result envisaged for a subsequent training arm.
The gain must not be reported as zero, but it also does not establish a new
high-separability representation or authorize policy training.
