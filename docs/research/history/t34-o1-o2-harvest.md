# T34 Offense-vs-Defense Audit, O1 Offensive Harvest, and O2 Bank (prepared, never run)

## Question and Goal

Initiated on 2026-10-20/21 during the T34 ExIt-B8 campaign, this lineage tested the owner's premise that the largest remaining mass upside over bare `b8-s3` lay in offensive play (killing opponents and harvesting corpse drops) rather than defensive rescue search (`Path B` / `teacher_v2`). It proceeded in three stages: (1) a five-persona Astra audit and Fable judgment (`docs/research/advice/2026-10-21_offense_vs_defense/JUDGMENT.md`) verifying the simulator's mechanics-v2 corpse economy and measuring `b8-s3`'s actual kill and food-source shares; (2) **O1** (`research/t34_o1_harvest/`), a 48-world L4 counterfactual branch census testing whether single-action H500 deviations capture `>= +5` death-drop-attributed branch mass; and (3) **O2** (`research/t34_o2_harvest/`), a prepared one-look H5000 terminal-hero comparison of bare `b8-s3`, rescue-only search (`D`), and rescue plus harvesting (`D+O`). O2 was resized to `N = 1,260` paired worlds (`420` per mix) but was **parked and never produced a study outcome**.

## Final Verdict and Key Numbers

1. **Simulator Mechanics v2 & B8 Food Economy (`docs/research/advice/2026-10-21_offense_vs_defense/JUDGMENT.md`, `astra_measure.md` removed in consolidation; see git c4c4ab41; `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/astra-offense/measure/run02/receipt.json`):**
   - Across 36 terminal-hero H5000 worlds (`12` per mix, `164,514` acted frames), bare `b8-s3` recorded **`332` credited kills** (`18.083/game` scripted, `4.083` frozen, `5.500` mixed) and **`206` boosted frames (`0.125%`)**.
   - Gross mass intake was **`20,952` ambient (`50.53%`), `16,467` death drops (`39.71%`), and `4,049` boost trails** (death-drop share `59.84%` scripted, `29.63%` frozen, `26.76%` mixed).
   - Under mechanics v2 (`src/core/mechanics_constants.py`), corpse and trail pellets share a **board-wide FIFO cap of `max(10, floor(0.5 × max_food))` = `150` pellets** and pickup is one pellet per tick; the 9 deaths at length `>= 800` (mean `1,018`; largest dying length `1,454`) each retained exactly **`150`** newly inserted pellets.
2. **O1 Counterfactual Harvest Census (`GO`, `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/t34-o1-20261021/census-02-extracted/decision.json`):**
   - Across `48` fresh H5000 worlds (`16` per mix), **`35/48` worlds** (scripted `13`, frozen `9`, mixed `13`; frozen+mixed `22`) contained at least one surviving single-action H500 branch with `delta_A500 >= +5` from death-drop intake. The decision file records `240` roots and `1,306` evaluated branches; `71` roots had at least one qualifying branch. Scope: "single-action H500 harvesting mechanism only; GO to separately registered O2; no release".
3. **O2 (`UNUSED`):** No O2 study world was ever run; no O2 mass, survival, or contrast estimate exists. The registered requirement was that O2 show `(D+O − D)` `LB90 > 0` in addition to `+40` / `LB90 > 0` vs B8 (`CAMPAIGN_LOG.md`, 2026-10-21 ~02:55 PT).

## What Was Learned

1. **The offense premise did not survive measurement:** mechanics v2 was already implemented and served, `b8-s3` already kills and boosts rarely, and the 150-pellet corpse cap plus one-pickup-per-tick bound what harvesting can deliver; the endpoint is a mass integral, so keeping a long body alive (defense) dominates (`JUDGMENT.md`).
2. **A harvesting mechanism exists locally (O1 GO, 35/48 worlds) but its closed-loop value was never measured:** O2 was underpowered at `N = 96`, required `5,097` worlds for adequate power, and at the `1,260`-world fallback had only `.339888` capped power; it was parked on its L4 timing forecast after mechanical transport failures.

## Why It Closed and Current Status

**CLOSED.** O1 is `RESULT-RECORDED` (`GO`). O2 is `UNUSED`: `PARKED` on its L4 timing/power forecast, then closed for the campaign under `PLAN_v3` (`docs/notes/big-lever-experiments.md`; `research/INDEX.md` at git c4c4ab41). Defensive rescue search remained the T34 critical path.
