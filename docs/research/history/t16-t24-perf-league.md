# Colab GPU Throughput, Actor/Pool Optimization, and the T23 League Screen (`t16-t24-perf-league`, T16–T24)

## Question and Goal

Following the October 17, 2026 promotion of bare `b8-s3` (`Ego2sNet`, `1.64M` params), can Colab Pro+ GPUs (A100, H100, L4) or RunPod H100 pods train the M3C self-play and `N = 3` mixed-seat league recipes at least `4x` faster than the Mac MPS baseline (`5,459` self-play and `5,214` `N = 3` learner transitions/s at `256` transitions/update), what host/CPU bottlenecks limit `N = 3` static-pool throughput (`T16`, `T17`, `T17b`, `T18`, `T19`, `T22`, `T24`), and does an 8M-transition `N = 3` static-pool fine-tune of `b8-s3` (`T23`) improve paired H5000 mass over released `b8-s3`?

## Final Verdict and Key Numbers

All primary measurements and verdicts are preserved in [`docs/research/history/colab-ops.md`](colab-ops.md), [`research/t17b_20261017/results/`](../../../research/t17b_20261017/results/), and [`research/t23_r0_screen_20261017/RESULTS_T23.md`](../../../research/t23_r0_screen_20261017/RESULTS_T23.md):

1. **Throughput & Hardware Findings (`T16`–`T22`, `T24`):**
   - **Mac MPS Baselines:** `5,283` tr/s (`20.6` upd/s) hero-only, **`5,459` tr/s (`21.3` upd/s)** all-6 self-play ([`colab-ops.md`](colab-ops.md)), and **`5,214` tr/s (`20.4` upd/s)** `N = 3` static-pool league (`/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/t16-mac-n3-baseline/n3_w128_a2_mps_static_pool/throughput.json`). On Mac MPS (`B5`), `fp32 bs2` takes `47.81`–`50.53 ms`/update (`19.79`–`20.92` upd/s) while `bf16 bs2` regresses to `135.55`–`149.78 ms`/update (`6.68`–`7.38` upd/s), and `reduce-overhead` compile falls back to eager due to MPS gradient verification ([`research/t17b_20261017/results/b5_mps_raw_g0.json`](../../../research/t17b_20261017/results/b5_mps_raw_g0.json)).
   - **Colab A100 Self-Play (`smoke-02` → `smoke-03`):** Offloading `_sample_fast` to `3` nogil gather threads (`g3`) and switching from `--obs-pack` to raw mmap rings raised 128-world self-play on A100-40GB from **`11,500` tr/s (`~45` upd/s, `2.1x` Mac)** to **`15,927` tr/s (`62.2` upd/s, `3.0x` Mac)** at `a4` ([`colab-ops.md`](colab-ops.md)). Isolated learner benchmarks (`P0`) proved the 1.64M-parameter `Ego2sNet` update (`256` new + `768` replay + `256` anchor rows) caps at **`78` upd/s (`12.8 ms`/update)** on A100-40GB and **`82.7` upd/s (`11.9 ms` GPU leg)** on A100-80GB—just under the `84.375` upd/s (`21,600` tr/s) 4x bar. `bf16` reached `117` upd/s isolated but reversed to **`47.6` upd/s (`12,189` tr/s)** in-run with 4 CUDA actors.
   - **Actor Kernel & Frozen-Pool Optimizations (`Actor Plan`, `T22`, `T24`):**
     - In `a4` self-play (`4,601,856` transitions, `632.08 s` inclusive `env_s`), actor time split into **`40.6%` `v8` features (`55.78 µs`/tr)**, **`29.7%` ego raster (`40.83 µs`/tr)**, **`9.7%` GPU forward/sync (`13.36 µs`/tr)**, and **`19.9%` sim/other (`27.38 µs`/tr)** ([`research/actor_perf_20261017/smoke03_summary.json`](../../../research/actor_perf_20261017/smoke03_summary.json)).
     - `T22` cut 128-world `N = 3` CPU frozen-pool dispatch from **`41.109 ms` to `28.687 ms`/frame** (and non-B8 host pool work from **`14.458 ms` to `2.154 ms`/frame**, `-85.1%`), projecting **`13,578` tr/s (`a4`)** and **`14,833` tr/s (`a6`)** on A100 ([`research/t22_frozen_pool_20261017/a100_projection.json`](../../../research/t22_frozen_pool_20261017/a100_projection.json)).
     - `T24` reused retained-body release maps across the 6 actions and shared world membership between learner and frozen B8 seats, cutting `L = 1200` combined `v8` time from **`22.155 ms` to `16.280 ms`/frame (`-26.5%`)** and whole-actor long-body frame time from **`246.738 ms` to `234.482 ms`** ([`research/t24_v8_bodymap_20261017/before_23c1ba73.json`](../../../research/t24_v8_bodymap_20261017/before_23c1ba73.json), [`after_4b4723f.json`](../../../research/t24_v8_bodymap_20261017/after_4b4723f.json)).
     - On Colab **L4** (`62409ae` with T22+T24), `N = 3` achieved **`7,625` tr/s (`29.8` upd/s, `1.46x` Mac)** at `~2` units/hour (**`~3,800` tr/s per unit-hour** vs A100's `~2,100`), and the post-T23 plan moved online fine-tuning to **L4** ([`colab-ops.md`](colab-ops.md); `docs/research/t16_colab_smoke02_results_2026-10-17.md` and `docs/research/specs/after_t23_plan_2026-10-17.md`, removed in consolidation; see git c4c4ab41).
2. **T23 `R0` `N = 3` Static-Pool 8M Screen Verdict (`KILL`):**
   - Across `5` independent seeds (`7101`–`7105`, `8M` transitions each from `b8-s3`) evaluated on `640` paired H5000 worlds (`1,280` episodes), the `N = 3` league fine-tune regressed severely: **mean equal-mix `G = -80.8` mass vs `b8-s3`** (`one-sided 90% t limits [-96.1, -65.5]`, **all `5/5` seeds negative** from `-60` to `-111`; scripted `-101`, frozen `-61`, mixed `-80`), while episode survival stayed flat or higher (`0.76` vs `0.68` on frozen) ([`research/t23_r0_screen_20261017/RESULTS_T23.md`](../../../research/t23_r0_screen_20261017/RESULTS_T23.md), [`results/t23_verdict.json`](../../../research/t23_r0_screen_20261017/results/t23_verdict.json)).
   - Per the post-T23 plan (`after_t23_plan_2026-10-17.md`, `2026-10-18_overnight_a100.md`), the long league run (`Lane A`) was not bought. The leading but **untested** suspect was restarting B8's schedule (gamma `.99 -> .998`, eps `.05 -> .01`, v8 anchor weight `1.0` throughout) with fresh Adam and empty replay; a Q0 audit and a Q1 restart-vs-mature schedule factorial were planned to test it.

## What Was Learned

- **Normalize multi-actor RL throughput by optimizer updates/s (`256` transitions/update), never raw actor frames/s:** Adding actors without lifting the learner host-gather bottleneck only increases `policy_lag` (`4.4 → 9.0`).
- **Colab L4 delivered ~1.8x more `N = 3` transitions per compute unit than the projected A100:** measured L4 `~3.8k` tr/s per unit-hour vs A100 `~2.1k` (projected); the post-T23 plan cites `~3.4x` per unit and judged H100 not needed.
- **The T23 `N = 3` fine-tune of `b8-s3` traded mass for caution (`-80.8` mass, survival flat or better):** T23 bundled several changes; the post-T23 plan named the restarted schedule (B8 finished at gamma `.998`, eps `.01`, anchor `.1`; T23 restarted at gamma `.99`, eps `.05`, anchor `1.0`) with fresh Adam/empty replay as the leading **untested** suspect.

## Why It Closed / Current Status

**Closed (`T23 = KILL`; the long `Lane A` league run was not bought).** The engineering improvements from `T16`–`T24` (Python 3.12 `uv` bootstrap, nogil row-chunked gather threads, clean actor shutdown, Numba `v8` pool dispatch, and retained-body `v8` templates) remain active in the codebase, while new Colab jobs now run through `research/colab_cli/` (`AGENTS.md`).
