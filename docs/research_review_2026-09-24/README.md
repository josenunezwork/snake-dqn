# Astra review: turn demonstrated skills into a full-task learning program

Four GPT-6 Astra agents reviewed the learning evidence, evaluation design, research operations, and strategy, then challenged one another's recommendations. Root integrated this decision. This is a review of existing reports and selected source, not new scientific analysis: no experiments, checkpoint queries, raw trajectory census, or completed audit was rerun. Old outcomes and stopping rules remain unchanged.

**Conclusion:** we have made substantial learning progress, but the research program has not converted it into reliable full-task behavior. The strongest explanation is a combination of task/distribution mismatch, unresolved retention under TD learning, and a fragmented experimental process. No single optimizer, architecture, or simulator defect was established. Increasing width alone is poorly supported; substantially more informative experience remains an open question.

## Why progress has stalled

1. **The program keeps optimizing local questions without maintaining a path back to the desired agent.** First-food tasks and selected trap descriptions are useful diagnostics, but success there does not compose automatically into long-game growth and opponent competence. Existing good policies have often become reference material for the next diagnosis rather than maintained baselines for an integrated milestone. This is the team's strategic judgment, supported by the sequence of reports, not a measured causal effect of research organization.
2. **“Scale” has covered different quantities.** The 20k scaling cells used 79,872 accepted rows and 5.12 million replay draws each: about 64 draws per accepted row in aggregate, not uniform per-row exposure. Increasing TRAIN roots at the same accepted-row count changes the distribution; it does not increase transition volume. The wider network had about 3.8 times the parameters and about 1.8 times the training time. These experiments do not exhaust a larger, more diverse native-experience regime. They also do not prove excess replay caused failure.
3. **Retaining useful behavior during TD adaptation is a demonstrated gap with an unresolved mechanism.** Imitation-to-TD and several continuations lost competence. Fresh canonical Apex controls nevertheless learned. “RL cannot learn” and “the model is too small” are both unsupported summaries. Credit assignment, state/action exposure, objective conflict, and representation remain competing hypotheses.
4. **The headline has compressed learning, comparative benefit, reliability, and promotion into “pass/fail.”** Strict conjunctive gates correctly stopped overclaiming and remain binding. A failed conjunction is not a powered finding of no useful learning. Future studies should report effect sizes and uncertainty separately from reliability and the unchanged tournament gate. Three trained seeds are a screen; hundreds of worlds do not create hundreds of independently trained models.
5. **Engineering failures and stale orchestration have interrupted the science.** Missing initial descriptors, audit call-signature errors, undeclared checker conditions, and nondurable prefixes made some results unusable. Those failures do not explain away valid behavioral failures. Total planning/review overhead was not measured; nobody can honestly claim a percentage of effort was wasted. The concrete defects and remedies are in [operations](operations.md).

## Team disagreements and root decision

The first recommendation was another fresh width-by-breadth interaction replication. Astra judged its immediate decision value low for the full-game objective. That draft is deferred, not retroactively invalidated.

The team next proposed a deployment-gap evaluation before training. Root challenged this because CX already demonstrated a one-opponent retention gap. Repeating that screen would recreate the diagnostic loop. The final direction is **one integration-and-learning milestone**, reusing CW/CX and all compatible completed evaluations. At most one new baseline event is warranted, and only for a genuinely unanswered exact-serving/long-horizon mixed-opponent living-mass question. It must be part of the same frozen decision plan, not another prerequisite that spawns an indefinite chain.

The agents differed on the eventual training package. Strategy favors a much larger encounter-rich data stream plus solo retention, starting with all three CZ enemy-visible descendants of the competent CV cohort. Evaluation favors separating new experience from repeated fitting at matched optimizer dose. **Root prioritizes the task-distribution package as a utility screen against frozen parents, holding architecture fixed.** It answers whether the package is useful, not whether any single component caused a gain. The causal dose/data comparison is a later alternative, not another arm added now. Exact starting-state eligibility, transitions, updates, rehearsal, opponent mixture, physical caps, and budget still need a separate design review. The proposed ten-million-transition-per-seed ceiling is a planning candidate, not an admitted dose or a promise of throughput.

## The next milestone

1. **Make the baseline executable without changing it.** The proposed CZ cohort is chosen now, after observing past studies, for its existing enemy-input contract and retained absolute competence; it is not a previously designated reference or a proven winner. Bind all three CZ enemy-visible parents and their CV/CW lineage, exact input restrictions, mechanics, masks, action selection, and lifecycle. Reuse their completed results; do not describe CZ as identical to CW or erase its relative-study limitations. Confirm serving compatibility statically first; a wrapper that changes observations creates a new treatment. Include Apex in a direct comparison only under a defensible common contract. Otherwise retain separate native baselines. Do not select the favorable seed or imply the stock loader already supports every artifact policy.
2. **Repair only the demonstrated experiment infrastructure.** Add small schema/interface fixtures, durable completion records, and a single current dispatch record. Future recovery rules must distinguish checker defects from scientific failures and ambiguous numerical execution. No generic orchestration framework, no recovery of closed zero-recovery studies, and no replay of their completed work.
3. **Freeze one task-aligned comparison.** Use total-horizon living mass as the primary development endpoint under the current movement contract, with sustained food, survival, encounter exposure, boost use, and solo retention visible. Define independent development/confirmation boundaries. If a new baseline event is necessary, include it once alongside the training comparison. The treatment should add informative native experience with solo retention exposure; frozen-parent controls and explicit dose accounting support a package-utility claim, not an isolated data or optimization cause. Starting from full learner state versus weights with a new optimizer must be named honestly; no invented uninterrupted-resume claim.
4. **Make one decision at fixed endpoints.** Keep every prespecified seed, report practical gain and uncertainty, and distinguish useful gain, futility, and inconclusive evidence. A null result does not trigger another coefficient or architecture sweep. Publish the complete comparison and either adopt the supported development direction, redesign the identified blocker once, or stop that line. Promotion still requires the separate strict shared tournament gate.

This is a prospective program recommendation, not a frozen numerical protocol. No new numerical work was admitted during this review. Independent Astra learning and evaluation reviewers checked the final synthesis against the published lineage/results; a wording ambiguity about the failed CV conjunction was corrected. Publication and automation state were verified by root, outside those scientific reviews. Every future admission still needs independently reviewed implementation, finite physical caps, a new deadline fitting all remaining jobs/audit and 120 seconds of handoff, serialized Mac supervision, and the existing memory/thread limits. Longer and larger runs should be used when they answer the selected question; they are not substitutes for a clear comparison.
