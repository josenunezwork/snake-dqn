# Campaign success: a better Watch hero within the existing serving envelope

2026-10-19 · Skeptical research-lead advice · source snapshot `fe0fd6843320de263a67fa701ae227a461dc57f0`

**I would define this as a new product campaign: deliver a materially stronger, more persistent highlighted Watch hero on the owner's Mac, while preserving growth, responsiveness and existing modes. Explicitly permit hero-only lookahead to earn that success. Keep bare-network improvement as a separately scored scientific claim. Accept a Watch6-scoped enhancement only if other configurations retain their qualified B8 behavior.**

Advice only: these are prospective owner decisions, not an amendment, launch, release or spending authorization. Freeze the success contract before Round 0, including Path B's eligibility and selection rule; preserve failed distillation results even if assisted play succeeds.

The October goal was achieved: B8 beat frp3-s12+v8 without a veto and was released. [STATUS](../../STATUS.md) explicitly closes that campaign. Preserving its accomplishment does not require every subsequent product improvement to use the same architecture. Conversely, calling an action-changing search “verify-only” does not make its output a bare-network achievement. The proposed change deserves an explicit new claim: **assisted Watch hero**.

The product evidence favors focusing on that hero. [T13](../t13_product_roster_2026-10-17.md) found all-B8 improved whole-arena persistence while worsening the featured hero: twelve-snake hero deaths were 0.768 versus 0.368/1k in the FRP3 configuration. Those rosters differ in policies and veto scope; this is not an isolated skill comparison. [T13b](../t13b_six_snake_roster_2026-10-17_results.md) subsequently motivated mixed Watch6. Hold its FRP3+v8 opponents fixed: improving the exhibition by weakening opponents cannot establish a better hero.

I would require two complementary demonstrations. First, the exact delivered controller must beat bare B8-s3 on fresh, paired, terminal-hero H5000 worlds, equal-weighted across frozen/scripted/mixed opponents, with dead frames zero. As prospective development admission thresholds, carry the plan's +40 mean mass and positive one-sided LB90 onto the delivered controller, require scripted no-death improvement of at least 0.03, and retain all per-mix survival guards. These are proposed practical bars, not measured gains; the fresh Mac strict gate, independent audit and serving qualification still decide release. Second, fresh continuous-respawn mixed Watch6 comparisons must show fewer hero deaths with a paired 95% interval excluding zero and no material growth regression. I propose a paired relative hero-mass difference with lower 95% confidence bound ≥ −5%. Report length distributions and late/large-body deaths. Arena mass, kills and label accuracy remain diagnostics. Freeze banks, multiplicity handling and selection before outcomes; worlds do not become independent training seeds.

The research history lowers my confidence in distillation. [T3 diagnostics](../t3_diagnostics_2026-10-16.md) show LABELS put its argmax in the stored survivor set on 99.66% of sampled bank rows yet lost 75.145 scripted mass versus CTRL. The [retrospective](../redesign_campaign_retrospective_2026-10-17.md) records that T4's tighter labels fixed spillover without buying survival. Thus the plan's anchor is a hypothesis, not an established cure. T10 preserves dense teacher survival of 54/64 versus 38/64, but that is development evidence, not an asynchronous serving result. Its [explicit limitation](../t10_certificate_horizon_2026-10-17.md) also matters: these diagnostics establish no universal bare-network ceiling. Round 0 failure would reject this teacher/trigger proposal, not prove B8 unbeatable.

The serving contract should require:

1. **An executable action rule.** Specify what happens after failed verification, including replacement selection; verification alone cannot improve actions. Freeze available state, opponent prediction and RNG access. Qualify precisely those capabilities, deadlines and fallbacks, without credit from retrospective death windows or information unavailable in deployment. Snapshot consistently; bind results to session, frame, hero life and checkpoint. Cap assistance at one additional CPU thread. Discard stale results, bound outstanding work, and fall back to bare B8 without blocking a tick. A spare core and 12–30 ms are forecasts, not guarantees.

2. **Preserved product scope and timing.** The original Watch12 failure was repaired: [T12B](../t12_b8_serving_qualification_2026-10-17.md#t12b-check-4-result-and-evidence) passed. [T15](../t15_mixed_roster_qualification_2026-10-17.md#latency-and-cadence) qualified mixed Watch12 at 12 fps with 14.073 ms full-tick p95. The tower's projected 3–5 ms therefore does not justify surrendering Watch12. Retain the 24/12 fps processing ceilings with zero overruns in registered paced probes; measure actual delivered cadence separately. Compare full-tick tails, client responsiveness, memory and CPU under matched long-body play and ordinary contention. Watch6-only assistance is acceptable with explicit B8 fallback elsewhere; Play and larger rosters must remain usable. Qualify every changed path.

3. **Visible identity and reversible release.** Display assistance status, log interventions, deadline misses and fallback reasons, exercise reset/respawn/checkpoint changes, and verify rollback to B8. Establish separate monitoring baselines for each enabled roster; mixed Watch8/12 currently lack death baselines. Require owner release approval after qualification.

I would put deadline-constrained Path B feasibility before expensive label rounds: if it independently meets the product contract, distillation is optional further research. The plan's “0.6 of upside” is a judgment, not evidence. I would withdraw support if its gain disappears with real deadlines or valid state information, or requires degraded controls. A bare student meeting the same benefit bars would favor simpler serving. Successful Watch12 measurements would remove any justification for limiting the enhancement to Watch6.
