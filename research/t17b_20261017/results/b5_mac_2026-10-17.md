it is not a quiet-host claim.  Each eager cell used 40,000 rows/ring, 5 warm-up updates,
three 20-update rounds, and five separate serialized device-event diagnostic updates.
`host_gather_ms` is the fast learner's sampled host staging interval.  `device_ms` is a
separate serialized MPS event envelope after that gather, not total wall time minus host time.

All 16 matching `reduce-overhead` requests were separately attempted: four cells in each
of `b5_mps_raw_g0.json`, `b5_mps_raw_g3_compile.json`,
`b5_mps_packed_g0_compile.json`, and `b5_mps_packed_g3_compile.json`.  All are
`compile_execution: fallback_eager`: the learner's compile verification rejected MPS
gradient disagreement, rather than treating the platform as categorically unsupported.
The eager receipts above are the matched reference timings and are not compiled timings.

Commands used the explicit `--raw-rings` or `--obs-pack`, local
`--ring-dir research/t17b_20261017/results`, and `--gather-threads 0|3` options.  The JSON
receipts preserve each exact argv, runtime/platform metadata, timing status, and fallback
detail.  Mmap ring files were absent after every receipt completed.
