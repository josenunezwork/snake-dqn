# Round-30 Post-F4 Optimizer Certificate Diagnosis (R30-1), Rescue-Mass Ranking (R30-2), and G5 Calibration Hold

## Question and Goal

After the F4 (`Linear(256, 6)`, `1,542`-parameter) residual correction failed `Look R-A` (`H >= 1`), the Round-30 (`bl_round30`) package was registered to diagnose whether F4's failure stemmed from imperfect 2,000-step float32 Adam optimization or from an objective mismatch between unweighted choice classification and surviving continuation mass, before launching a single-factor F5 successor. Specifically, `R30-1` compared the F4 seed-`3403` 2,000-step float32 Adam solver (`lr = 1e-3`) against a 500-closure / `3,600 s` float64 L-BFGS solver equipped with a `Decimal(prec=50)` strong-convexity gradient enclosure certificate ($\|\nabla L(\Delta^*)\|_2 / \mu \le 10^{-5}$, where $\mu = 2\lambda_{\text{reg}} / 1536 = 2 \times 10^{-4} / 1536 \approx 1.3020833 \times 10^{-7}$ on unpenalized biases) on frozen parent split `t34-r30-parent-split-20261023-v1`, while `R30-2` evaluated a rescue-survivor mass-ranking objective ($\text{ratio} = \lambda_{\text{choice}} L_{\text{choice}} / \text{mean}(\text{adv}_{\text{norm}}) \ge 0.10$ across $\ge 32$ rescue parents) targeting $\ge 10\%$ validation survivor-reference regret reduction, rescue-top-1 retention $\ge 0.70$, and zero new deaths ($H = 0$). Companion workstreams `R32` (`research/r32_rehearsal`) and `R33` rehearsed the sequential-gate consumer pipeline on synthetic ledger payloads and closed out the non-consuming Mac qualification lanes.

## What Was Learned

1. **Optimization on the `65,825`-row / `1,542`-parameter linear head is fast (`19.674 s`), whereas pure-Python `Decimal(prec=50)` enclosure over all `65,825` rows is the sole bottleneck (`> 600 s`)**: `probe-03`'s per-phase telemetry proved that the `7,962 min` `G5` linear-scaling surrogate was an artifact of scaling fixed session/Cold-start overhead and pure-Python `Decimal(prec=50)` loops by `291` parents (`TIMING_PROBE04_CERTIFICATE_AMENDMENT_20261025.md`).
2. **Transport input upload caps must be separated from immutable output export caps**: `probe-01` failed pre-allocation because `max_export_bytes = 524,288` (`512 KiB`) was also applied to input uploads (`4.17 MiB` real train tensor payload). Separating `--max-upload-bytes` (`64 MiB`) from `--max-export-bytes` (`512 KiB`) resolved input staging cleanly while preserving output bounds (`docs/notes/r30-r32-f5-successors.md`).
3. **Timing probes require durable phase-checkpointed telemetry**: `probe-02` caught ` CERTIFICATE_DEADLINE` inside a blanket exception handler that only wrote `status = "INCOMPLETE"` without flushing completed phase wall times. Writing phase durations to disk after each phase (`probe-03`) isolated the exact bottleneck in one run without leaking scientific outcomes (`docs/notes/r30-r32-f5-successors.md`).
4. **Sequential consumer rehearsal (`R32`) validated end-to-end ledger skip and statistics before real data**: `R32` verified all `28/28` synthetic units on L4 and surfaced the missing `df=5` argument in `numpy.random.Generator.standard_t` (`statistics.py:349`) prior to any real-data consumer run (`research/r32_rehearsal/README.md`).

## Why It Closed / Current Status

**Closed on `HOLD` at Gate `G5` (2026-10-25)**. Under `TIMING_PROBE04_CERTIFICATE_AMENDMENT_20261025.md` (`d2134747...`), giving the `Decimal(prec=50)` certificate `1,200 s` required a `40 min` session timeout (`>= 2×` the `19.5 min` estimate), which collided with the hard `<= 30 min` canary/probe ceiling in `research/colab_cli/canary.py:213`. Per Fable ruling `JUDGMENT_R30_PROBE04_ADMISSION_ROUTE_CLOSED_20261025.md` (`d3bf41a9...`), the campaign refused to weaken the qualified transport supervisor or relax the `Decimal(prec=50)` mathematical certificate inside the timing-calibration lane, closed the calibration probe route as operationally unresolved, kept `R30-1` and `R30-2` on `HOLD` at `G5`, and unblocked independent pre-data preparation of the single-factor F5 successor (`docs/notes/r30-r32-f5-successors.md`).
