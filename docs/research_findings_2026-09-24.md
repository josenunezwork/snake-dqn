- [Fixed endpoints on H256](research/history/apex-small-task.md): nine new evaluations plus three reused references; no consistent factor benefit and no endpoint passed the full sustained-food/survival package.
- [Longer continuation and collection horizon](research/history/apex-small-task.md): all six adaptive endpoints failed required survival; new held-bank overlap invalidated the full scientific branch. This did not isolate updates alone.
- [Terminal masks](research/history/apex-small-task.md) and [pretrap choices](research/history/apex-small-task.md): audited selected-case descriptions, not a causal diagnosis or training improvement.
- [Native counterfactual probe](research/history/apex-small-task.md): required audit failed; native results remain explicitly provisional.
- [Space-ranking admission](research/history/apex-small-task.md): descriptor schema failure before games, model queries or training; scientific effects unknown and metadata audit pending.

## Publication boundary

The tracked reports, figures, tables, and representative records are published together. Local runtime and test changes are excluded from this documentation-only publication; source commit identities cited by reports may therefore remain local. Large external checkpoints, full raw trajectories and local experiment-driver directories remain outside Git, as in the earlier publication. Their paths and hashes remain in the reports; a link to a local artifact is not a remotely hosted artifact. No unpublished draft is presented as a completed finding.

The current proposed fresh scaling-interaction replication is a draft only. The user's broader Astra strategy review now precedes further admission. All existing failed/closed study criteria and records are preserved.
