## 1. Objective

This is a **new product campaign**. The October goal (beat frp3-s12+v8 without a veto) was met by B8
s3 and stays closed. Success = **a materially stronger, more persistent highlighted Watch hero than
bare B8 s3 (`3e649783…`) in the mixed Watch6 roster, on the owner's Mac, without losing growth,
responsiveness or existing modes.**

Two separately scored claims, both pre-registered here:

- **Claim A — bare student.** A distilled network served bare (no lookahead) meets the bars below.
- **Claim B — assisted hero ("Path B").** The exact delivered controller = B8-or-student + bounded,
  hero-only, asynchronous lookahead meets the same bars. Released only as a visibly labelled
  **assisted hero**; it is never reported as a bare-network result.

If both qualify and their gains are not resolved apart, **the bare student wins** (simpler serving).
A failed distillation is reported as failed even if Claim B succeeds.
