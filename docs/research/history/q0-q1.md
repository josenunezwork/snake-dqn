# Post-T23 Qualification & Schedule/Collection Factorial (Q0 / Q1, 2026-10-17 to 2026-10-18)

## Question and Goal

After the T23 R0 5-seed $\times$ 8M self-play fine-tuning screen lost $-80.78$ equal-mix `H5000` mass against frozen `b8-s3` (`0/5` positive seeds) despite neutral survival (`+0.008`), the `Q0`/`Q1` package (`research/q0q1_20261017`) was registered to answer two sequential questions before further training spend: first (`Q0a` & `Q0b`), whether T23's foraging-mass collapse occurred immediately at 2M–4M decisions, whether `b8-s3` initialized cleanly into the learner across all length/crowding strata, and whether Colab L4 and A100 runtimes met throughput, asynchronous lag, and bit-parity gates; second (`Q1`), whether a $2 \times 2$ schedule (`restart` vs `mature`: $\gamma=0.998, \epsilon=0.01, \text{anchor}=0.1$) $\times$ collection (`legacy` $N=3$ hero-shared vs `task-aligned-n1` $N=1$ opponent-frozen) factorial ($4\text{ arms} \times 5\text{ seeds} \times 8\text{M} = 160\text{M}$ decisions) could isolate and cure the post-`B8` fine-tuning regression within the registered Colab compute envelope.

## What Was Learned

1. **Aggressive restart schedules immediately degrade `b8-s3`'s foraging policy:** T23's R0 schedule ($\epsilon: 0.08 \to 0.02$, $\gamma: 0.99 \to 0.997$, decaying `v8` anchor, `smooth_l1_loss(beta=1.0)`) caused a 12–17% drop in food per alive frame within the first `2M` decisions across all opponent pools, proving that fine-tuning a mature checkpoint requires a low-perturbation `mature` schedule ($\gamma=0.998, \epsilon=0.01$, persistent anchor) rather than a cold-start schedule.
2. **At $N=1$ hero-only collection, L4 matches A100 throughput at ~38% of the unit burn:** Because $N=1$ collection is bottlenecked by CPU environment stepping and opponent inference rather than GPU learner backward passes, L4 (`~2,392 dec/s` at `2.0 u/h`) matched A100 (`~2,406 dec/s` at `5.3 u/h`) on throughput while cutting compute units by more than half.
3. **Colab queue workers sitting idle between retries dominate real compute burn:** Claim-to-terminal job timers (`13.40` GPU-h for `Q0`) hid `~76` GPU-h of idle/unclaimed notebook-worker runtime assignments across `r2`–`r6`, directly motivating the Mac-side CLI supervisor (`research/colab_cli`) that allocates per job and stops runtimes immediately on completion.

## Why It Closed

`Q1` closed **REFUSED / UNRUN** on 2026-10-18 under Amendment A5 (`f5b27165`) because allocation-inclusive upper-bound accounting (`105.63` GPU-h, `51.30` CPU-h, `362.55 u`) exceeded the final A5 caps and A5 explicitly barred any further cap raises ([Q1_A5_ADMISSION_20261018.md](../../../research/q0q1_20261017/Q1_A5_ADMISSION_20261018.md)). Following post-`Q1` advisor consultations, the 4-arm `Q1` factorial was replaced by the focused 2-arm mature-schedule study `S1` (`research/s1_mature_20261018`, comparing `mature + legacy` vs `mature + task-aligned-n1`) and the T34 ExIt-B8 campaign under the Mac-side Colab CLI supervisor.
