## Decision rule and multiplicity

Unit of inference is the paired world. Average the three mix means equally;
estimate variance within each disjoint mix, then use Welch-Satterthwaite degrees
of freedom for the pooled one-sided t interval. Freeze one family of two
one-sided superiority hypotheses: D+O minus D and D+O minus bare B8. Apply
Holm at family alpha 0.10: the smaller p must be <0.05 and the larger <0.10.
Report unadjusted LB90 and multiplicity-adjusted bounds/decisions. Exact zero
lower bounds fail.

GO requires both superiority hypotheses to reject and D+O minus B8 mean mass
gain >=40. The D contrast is mandatory: B8 superiority alone cannot attribute
anything to harvesting. D is not separately selected for advancement.

Every contract skill guard is vs bare B8 s3: survival guard A (pooled alive-frame
margin -0.05, each-mix -0.075 and the retained absolute floor), guard B (scripted
no-death point gain >=0.03), and delivered boost fraction in [0.005,0.10].
The analyzer records every clause and the exact mean, SE, bound and threshold.
Illegal action, non-finite Q or >90% one-action share in a full 100,000-decision
window is an unconditional fault. A failed boost bar is a failed delivered
controller bar, not a training tripwire.

Precedence: INVALID identity/accounting/parity/provenance failure;
INCOMPLETE missing/cap-expired/unclean evidence; KILL unconditional fault,
failed point bar, or clear inferiority (either mass contrast upper90 <=0,
pooled alive upper95 <=-0.05, or any mix alive upper95 <=-0.075);
GO only if all registered clauses pass; otherwise INCONCLUSIVE with no extension.
A valid failure does not establish a bare-network ceiling.

A typed study-arm unconditional fault is the sole early scientific stop: seal
its source/world/arm-bound fault artifact and emit KILL without inventing
missing trajectory metrics. Stop admitting further worlds and drain at most
the already admitted worker wave. Excluded-fixture faults remain mechanical
failures. Every supplied publication is identity-checked before a fault can
be admitted; this exception never permits an early efficacy look or retry.
