# Controlled Encounter (2026-09-21 — 2026-09-22)

## Question & Goal

Can a `raster31v2` convolutional network (`RasterDuelingNetwork`) solve a calibrated 4-frame (`H4`) 2-snake tactical encounter benchmark—requiring simultaneously high held-out threat survival ($\ge 95\%$ on `frontal`, `cross_left`, and `cross_right` encounters) and high food capture across 6 geometry families and 4 initial headings—via exact-horizon imitation learning (`H4` behavioral cloning, state-coverage aggregation, earlier-food tie-breaking, and 256-world empirical-teacher expansion) followed by native `PQN` $Q(\lambda)$ temporal-difference continuation?

## What Was Learned

1. **Label hygiene and earlier-food tie-breaking are decisive for short-horizon imitation**: Off-crop enemy respawns created clearance tie-break aliases (`2` groups), and unweighted 4-step food ties allowed stationary policies to procrastinate (`87` regretted choices in `79` fully covered train games). Weighting step rewards by `8/4/2/1` eliminated `100%` of procrastination loops and raised teacher food from `383 -> 480` (train) and `221 -> 256` (held).
2. **World diversity (`16 -> 64 -> 256` worlds) drives generalization**: Increasing gradient updates on a fixed world bank (`repeat64`) saturated, whereas expanding unique training worlds to `256` (`25,616` rows reconciled under a finite-bank empirical teacher) raised held action-set fit to `93.2–95.8%`, held food to `220–224`, and held survival to `89.6–93.8%`.
3. **Collector episode horizon controls root-state diversity during TD continuation**: Under a fixed update budget, `cap=256` starved early collector updates to `51–63` unique worlds (`~20–25%` of the 256-world bank) because surviving snakes stayed in post-encounter drift; cutting the collector cap to `16` (`SHORT64`) immediately exposed `206–207` unique worlds (`>80%`).
4. **Native `PQN` $Q(\lambda)$ continuation traded crossing survival for frontal survival**: every selected H16 crossing loss first diverged at step 2 or 4 and every frontal rescue at an odd step; forcing the parent action at that single step reversed all 44 selected cases. Terminal one-step TD residuals (`3.00–3.63`) were far above interior ones (`0.25–0.27`), and the final-mark train-agreement drift was direction-only (`934` lost vs `40` gained). The reports establish single-action sufficiency only, not an optimizer mechanism. Crossing NLL rehearsal, direction-margin anchors, a full 1,024-update cycle and an ambient-food bonus all failed to resolve it.

## Why It Closed / Current Status

- **Closed on 2026-09-22** after `controlled-encounter-native-full-cycle` and `controlled-encounter-ambient-objective` confirmed that native `PQN` $Q(\lambda)$ continuation could not preserve the 256-world imitation policy's crossing survival while improving frontal survival.
- The methodological findings—exact-horizon teacher tie-breaking, state-coverage aggregation, empirical-teacher alias reconciliation, and the diagnosis of `PQN` value/policy interference—fed directly into the subsequent `rl-repair-pqn` decoupling experiments and the October `ego2s-bv8` distilled-DQN (`B8`) campaign.
