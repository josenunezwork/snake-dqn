- Seed-1's action-collapse terminal retained raw-action counts and finite model
  telemetry, but not the opportunity context needed to explain whether a
  straight action was locally avoidable or useful. This is a telemetry gap, not
  evidence that the selected action was inherently wrong.
- The original arms had variable eligible-row counts per update. Their observed
  RSS figures motivate the profiling lane's variable-shape hypothesis, but the
  two runs do not establish a causal memory relationship.
- The operational follow-up changes padding, evidence thresholds, checkpoint
  retention, seeds, and wall budget together. It can test whether those safety
  controls operate as intended; it cannot attribute any outcome to one change
  or compare learning quality with original L1.

## Campaign scientific limits

(including the two already incomplete original L1 arms), at most **40 strict
evaluation calls**, and at most **4,800 scientific child seconds**. These
replace the earlier `4 arms / 20 calls / 3,000 seconds` planning proposal,
which was not the accepted campaign limit. Any later execution remains subject
to the existing hard deadline and numerical-launch cutoff. The optional padded
20,000-step preparation pair is a diagnostic and does not become a policy-study
arm merely because it uses the learner.
