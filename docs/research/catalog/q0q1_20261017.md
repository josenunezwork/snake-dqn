## Purpose

Per its README, Q0/Q1 asks one bounded question before adaptive league work: can a B8 weights-only fine-tune
preserve or improve fixed-opponent H5000 mass when its **schedule package** (`restart|mature`) and **collection
package** (`legacy|task-aligned-n1`) are varied independently (a 2x2 factorial, arms A-D, 5 seeds, B8 as shared
comparator)? Two gates precede Q1:

- **Q0a** - descriptive audit of saved T23/B8 artifacts (T23 2M/4M mass-loss audit; zero-update B8 initializer
  fidelity). Cannot reverse T23's 8M KILL.
- **Q0b** - hardware/capacity qualification (L4 and A100 timing packets, oracle and parity) for the exact Q1 config.

## Structure

**Docs and decision records (top level, `*.md`)** - README, PREREGISTRATION (frozen registration + amendments
A1-A5 + A2 attribution erratum), and the dated receipts in the table above.

**Owner admission / evidence JSON (top level)**
- `OWNER_ADMISSION_20261018.json`, `_A2` ... `_A5` - owner-approval bindings per amendment (built by `owner_admission.py`).
- `Q1_WORLD_BANK.json`, `Q1_SEED_AUDIT.json` - Q1 world allocation and pre-data collision audit (644 candidates).
- `CPU_EVAL_OVERHEAD_MAC_20261018.json` - Mac per-job staging/startup overhead measurement (excludes scored worlds).
- `qualification_template.json` - template for Q0b qualification evidence.

**Q0a audit code** - `audit_common.py` (fail-closed primitives), `audit_q0_bank.py` (Q0 worlds, disjoint from Q1),
`audit_t23_mass.py` + `mass_execution.py` (480-episode mass audit, parallel workers, durable partials/resume),
`audit_episode_metrics.py` (exact-event metrics over `run_simd_eval`), `audit_b8_init.py` + `init_fill_bank.py`
(initializer fidelity + supplemental fill worlds), `probe_open_geometry.py` (descriptive geometry probe),
`inventory_receipt.py` (Mac collision-inventory receipts consumed by Colab jobs).

**Q0b qualification code** - `q0b_measure.py` (4-cell CUDA capacity packet), `q0b_cuda_fixtures.py` (T22/T24
fixtures on CUDA), `q0b_queue.py` (Drive-queue prelude), `prepare_q0b_inputs.py`, `qualification.py` (fail-closed
packet + budget admission checks), `requalify_q0b.py` (Mac-side A3 requalification of preserved packets).

**Accounting / admission** - `q0_accounting.py` (claim-to-terminal debit accounting), `allocation_accounting.py`
(A5 sibling allocation-lifetime upper bound), `a4_compatibility.py` (A4 bridge for A3 parity evidence),
`owner_admission.py`.

**Q1 code (never executed for science)** - `q1_bank.py`, `q1_evaluate.py` (CPU shard evaluator, A-D + B8),
`q1_parity.py` (Mac/Colab 4-world parity, T23 comparator), `q1_analyze.py` (fail-closed endpoint analysis),
`make_q1_jobs.py` (manifest generator; refused under A3 and A5), `make_q1_mac_parity_reference.py`.

**Queue plumbing** - `queue_jobs.py` (build manifests, never publish), `run_queue_job.py` / `run_queue_job.sh`
(fail-closed Colab adapter), `run_host_contracts.sh` (host fixtures only; never dispatches).

**Manifests**
- `jobs/` (122 JSON) - historical DRAFT_PENDING_OWNER_REVIEW manifests: 2 Q0 audits, 20 trainers
  (`q1-train-{a..d}-{seed}`), 100 eval shards (`q1-eval-{a..d,b8}-{seed}-sNof4`). Never enqueued.
- `jobs_approved/` (16 JSON) - approved Q0/Q0b attempt manifests (b8-init r3-r6, t23-mass r2/r4/r5,

- `jobs_overnight/overnight-host-contracts-a100.json` - one host-contract manifest.

**Results** - `results/Q0_MASS_AUDIT.md`, `results/q0_mass_audit_receipt.json`. Other evidence (Q0b archives,
admission dir, geometry probe JSON) lives off-repo on Drive / in `snake-dqn-artifacts/q0q1-20261017/`.

an authorized Q1 run, which never happened). Queue-side: `run_queue_job.sh` -> `run_queue_job.py`. Exact flags
for the others: see each module's argparse (not summarized here).
