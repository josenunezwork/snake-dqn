# Native Solo Behavioral Cloning, Spacious Teacher, and Learner-State Aggregation (`solo-native-imitation`, Series `W`–`AM`)

## Question and Goal

Before multi-snake PQN self-play can succeed on `RasterDuelingNetwork` (`raster31v3`: tactical `(9, 31, 31)`, strategic `(3, 25, 25)`, scalars `(26,)`), can the unchanged convolutional network learn reliable 1-snake food-seeking and survival in the native `145 x 83` simulator (`56e0e92434ae510a84ebaf6f58cf41c3e4421b04`) via behavioral cloning (`BC`) of scripted teachers (`GreedyFood` and flood-fill `SpaceTeacher`) and DAgger-style learner-state aggregation across 64- to 256-frame horizons?

## Final Verdict and Key Numbers

All primary JSON analyses, receipts, and Drive archive manifests (`MOVED_TO_DRIVE.p01.json`..`p06.json`) for Series `W`–`AM` are preserved under [`/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/ongoing-research-20260913/`](/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/ongoing-research-20260913/) and summarized in [`docs/research_findings_2026-09-24.md`](../../research_findings_2026-09-24.md):

1. **Short-Horizon (64–128 Frame) Native Imitation Succeeded (`W`–`AB`):**
   - Training `RasterDuelingNetwork` on `16` worlds (`3,072` rows) in `X` ([`/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/ongoing-research-20260913/solo-native-food-diversity`](/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/ongoing-research-20260913/solo-native-food-diversity)) generalized across fresh 64-frame (`AA`) and 128-frame (`AB`) solo worlds with **`96/96` (`100%`) survival** and **`78.6%`–`81.9%`** of teacher food (`20.38`–`21.25` vs `25.94` at 128 frames) ([`docs/research_findings_2026-09-24.md`](../../research_findings_2026-09-24.md), [`/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/ongoing-research-20260913/MOVED_TO_DRIVE.p02.json`](/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/ongoing-research-20260913/MOVED_TO_DRIVE.p02.json)).
2. **Observation Sufficiency vs Distractor Channel Sensitivity (`Y`, `Z`, `AK`, `AM`):**
   - A deterministic geometry decoder recovered the exact teacher food action on **`100%`** of saved `raster31v3` observations (`9,216/9,216` in `Y` and `10,775/10,775` in `AK`), proving `raster31v3` is not missing food-direction information ([`docs/research_findings_2026-09-24.md`](../../research_findings_2026-09-24.md)).
   - However, zeroing non-food channels at inference (`Z`, [`/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/ongoing-research-20260913/solo-native-food-input-ablation`](/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/ongoing-research-20260913/solo-native-food-input-ablation)) jumped held-out accuracy from **`80.37%`–`81.80%` to `87.58%`–`88.53%`**, showing that `RasterDuelingNetwork` overfits to spurious correlations with moving self-body and boundary channels on world-aligned `raster31v3` grids.
3. **256-Frame Self-Trapping, `SpaceTeacher` Calibration, and Aggregation Plateau (`AD`–`AJ`, `AL`):**
   - By 256 frames (`AD`, length `~35`–`50`), the pure food-greedy teacher trapped itself on **`26/96`** lanes (`42.59` food) and students died on **`30`–`38/96`** lanes (`100` total self-deaths). Exact replay (`AE`) proved that **`100/100`** student deaths occurred at the exact first frame `F` where `legal & advisory_safe` became empty (`D == F`) ([`docs/research_findings_2026-09-24.md`](../../research_findings_2026-09-24.md)).
   - Adding the flood-fill free-space veto created `SpaceTeacher` (`AF`), which achieved **`96/96` (`100%`) survival** and **`46.375` food** (`+4.646` over old greedy, `95% CI [+0.691, +8.601]`).
   - Imitating `SpaceTeacher` via expert BC (`AG`) and two rounds of DAgger-style learner-state aggregation (`AI`, `AJ`) raised 256-frame survival over matched controls in every seed (**`55→70`, `66→73`, `58→71` of `96`** in `AI`, `+12.15 pp`, `95% CI [-1.44, +25.75 pp]`; **`61→71`, `65→72`, `63→65` of `96`** in `AJ`, `+6.60 pp`, `95% CI [-7.77, +20.96 pp]`), but plateaued at **`65`–`73 / 96` (`67.7%`–`76.0%`)** survival and **`20.0%`–`51.1%`** unseen veto-disagreement accuracy ([`docs/research_findings_2026-09-24.md`](../../research_findings_2026-09-24.md), [`/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/ongoing-research-20260913/HANDOFF.md`](/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/ongoing-research-20260913/HANDOFF.md)).

## What Was Learned

- **Every long-body self-collision is decided *before* the last safe move disappears (`D == F` on `180/180` replayed deaths in `AE` and `AH`):** At the frame of death, all legal actions are already traps; surviving requires taking spacious turns `1`–`12` steps earlier (`AH`, [`docs/research_findings_2026-09-24.md`](../../research_findings_2026-09-24.md)).
- **World-aligned `raster31v3` without explicit free-space scalars struggles to generalize rare flood-fill vetoes:** Even with DAgger-style state aggregation (`AI`, `AJ`) and auxiliary partial-view training (`AL`), `RasterDuelingNetwork` plateaued at `~84%–86%` unseen natural accuracy and `~20%–51%` unseen veto accuracy. This directly motivated the October redesign's switch to **heading-aligned egocentric rasters plus explicit `v8` free-space scalars (`ego2s-bv8`, `Ego2sNet`)** ([`docs/notes/redesign-and-b8.md`](../../notes/redesign-and-b8.md)).
