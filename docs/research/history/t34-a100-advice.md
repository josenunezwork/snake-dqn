# T34 Colab A100/H100 Consultations, Label Ordering Ruling, and Round-20 Parallel A100 Program

## Question and Goal

Given approximately `1,157` prepaid Colab Pro+ compute units (`~39 u/day` target through `2026-11-20`) and the T34 ExIt-B8 campaign's CPU-bound search teacher running at `54.4–57.2` worlds/hour per L4 lane (`~0.036 u/world`), the lineage addressed three connected questions: (1) whether switching from Colab L4 (`~1.7 u/h`, `12` vCPUs) to A100-40GB (`~5.4 u/h`) or A100-80GB (`~7.5 u/h`) runtimes—or porting the simulator, search algorithm, or student training loop to the GPU—would deliver a strictly gated better served snake than bare `b8-s3` sooner in calendar time; (2) whether Claim A Round 1 (`R1`) teacher-label collection on Colab could legally start after a published Round 0 `GO` in parallel with the Mac-bound Path B retention study (`SUCCESS_CONTRACT.md` §5 vs `DESIGN_V2_SHADOW.md`); and (3) after the `E1b` PyTorch device simulator ran at `0.217 steps/s` (`4.6 s/tick`), why `E1b` stalled and how to structure a parallel A100/H100 research program (`Round 20` / `PLAN_A100.md`) for batched GPU simulation, hazard/value prediction, and model-scale falsification (`1.64M` vs `~6.5M` vs `~25M`) without violating CPU exactness.

## Final Verdict and Key Numbers

### 1. Oct 20 A100 Consultation (`2026-10-20_a100`) — Unanimous `NO A100` for Host-Bound Teacher Work
- **Host CPU & GPU Share in Teacher Collection:** Both standard L4 and A100-40GB/80GB Colab runtimes expose `12` logical vCPUs (`6` physical cores; `53.0–56.9 GB` RAM on L4, `83.5 / 167.1 GB` on A100) (`docs/research/t16_colab_smoke02_results_2026-10-17.md`, `docs/research/advice/2026-10-20_a100/REVIEW_A_evidence.md`). On the qualified A5 v2 L4 layout (`12` single-thread CPU worker processes, `W=1`, fast O(bytes) digest), lanes achieved **`57.1734` and `54.3721` completed worlds/h** (`~0.036 u/world`, `16.3k–20.1k` row-steps/world, `8.50–8.88 MB/world` exported at `0.568–1.077 MB/s`) (`/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/t34-engine-fanout-20261020/manifests-final-3ebf3991/a5-l4-s0-output/exports/benchmark.json`, `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/t34-engine-fanout-20261020/manifests-final-3ebf3991/a5-l4-s1-output/exports/benchmark.json`).
- **Amdahl Ceiling on the Teacher:** In production A5 worker timers, `B8` forward + transfer accounted for only **`7.54%`** (`1,133 / 15,021 s` on lane 0) and **`7.81%`** (`1,172 / 15,008 s` on lane 1) of worker wall time (`/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/t34-engine-fanout-20261020/manifests-final-3ebf3991/a5-l4-s0-output/exports/benchmark.json`). On the 2-vCPU Colab diagnostic (`1,689` row-steps in `60.080 s`, `35.57 ms/row-step`), exclusive stage shares were digest **`35.4%`** (`12.60 ms`), `v8` hooks **`19.9%`** (`7.07 ms`), `B8`/opponent inference **`13.3%`** (`4.75 ms`), simulator **`12.3%`** (`4.36 ms`), raster **`7.0%`** (`2.49 ms`), and residual **`12.1%`** (`4.31 ms`) (`/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/t34-engine-fanout-20261020/manifests-final-3ebf3991/cpu-profile-output/exports/profile-cpu.json`, `docs/research/advice/2026-10-20_a100/REVIEW_A_evidence.md`). Even an infinitely fast GPU yields at most `1 / (1 - 0.133) = 1.15x` while costing `~3.14–3.51x` the units of an L4 (`5.37–5.40 u/h` vs `1.54–1.71 u/h`).
- **Student Fit & Distillation Loss Pathology:**
  - Single-thread Mac CPU probe of `Ego2sNet` (`1,638,263` params, `~22.5 MFLOP/forward`) measured **`1,060 rows/s`** (`966 ms/update` at `B=1,024`) for forward+backward+Adam and **`69,292 rows/s`** (`14.43 µs/row`) in the `t34_exit_b8_fit` data loader (`docs/research/advice/2026-10-20_a100/REVIEW_A_evidence.md`). Historical M2b student fits ran at **`23,286–29,327 rows/s`** (`research/redesign_m2_20261008/results_m2b/fits/r10-metrics.json`), and T4c 160-world H5000 bare-student evaluation took **`6.72 min`** (`2.519 s/world`) (`research/t3_screen_20261015/t4c_results/probes.jsonl`).
  - Analytical audit of `research/t34_exit_b8_fit/loss.py` proved that omitting the $\tau^2$ multiplier on $D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\text{softmax}(Q_{\text{prev}}/0.05) \parallel \text{softmax}(Q_s/0.05))$ while sharing row weights between KL and the margin-`0.1` hinge locks the stationary student gap $d = Q_{\text{old}} - Q_{\text{rescue}}$ to the **wrong action ordering**: for prior gaps $d_0 \in \{0.06, 0.10, 0.45\}$, the stationary student still favors the fatal action by **`+0.04686`, `+0.07956`, and `+0.14712`** (`docs/research/advice/2026-10-20_a100/REVIEW_A_evidence.md`).
- **Critical-Path Schedule Levers Identified by `REVIEW_C` and `astra_p4_critical_path.md`:**
  1. **Unpace the Path B bare comparator (`fps=0`):** Pacing bare `b8-s3` at 24 fps across the 64-world pre-check (`3.19 h` bare) and 176-pair efficacy study (`8.5 h` bare) wasted **`~11–12` Mac hours** in `time.sleep` (`docs/research/advice/2026-10-20_a100/astra_p4_critical_path.md`, `docs/research/specs/2026-10-20_t34_retention_amendment_bare_unpaced.md`).
  2. **Write the pre-data NO-GO branch plan (`Round 0-D`):** Because `teacher_v2` bisection recovered `39/75 = 52.0%` of all T3 changes (`39/42 = 92.9%` of single-deviation-capable rescues; `research/t34_shadow/RESULTS.md`), a pre-data fallback to dense `T3` search was written in `docs/research/specs/2026-10-20_t34_nogo_branch_plan.md`.
  3. **Three low-cost falsifier probes from the GPU experts:**
     - **Simulator (`gpu_expert_1`):** Thread-per-world GPU simulator (`~95–120 KB/world` at $L \le 1,600$) breaks even only at `~80,000` shadow worlds or multi-million dense searches; proposed a bounded GPU sim spike (`docs/research/specs/2026-10-21_gpu_sim_spike.md`).
     - **Search (`gpu_expert_2`):** Proposed testing a $k=8$ beam **two-deviation rescue search** on the `33/75` all-die T3 doom intervals (`research/t35_two_deviation/PREREGISTRATION.md`).
     - **Learning (`gpu_expert_3`):** Proposed training a `Linear(256, 12)` 6-action `H300` survival/mass **outcome head** on the 39 A5 v2 export worlds (`research/t35_outcome_head/PREREGISTRATION.md`).

### 3. Round 20 Judgment & Parallel A100/H100 Program (`2026-10-22_a100_program`)
- **Root Cause of `E1b` (`0.217 steps/s` = `4.6 s/tick`):** In `exp/bl-e1b/cuda-dtype-fix-20261021-01/l4-output/exports/decision-0000.json`, `device_loop_seconds` was **`8,294.06 s`** and `host_launch_cpu_seconds` was **`8,296.32 s`** over `1,800` world-steps (`4.6 s/tick`). Inspection of `research/gpu_sim_spike/device_sim.py:440, 839-843` proved `step()` unconditionally iterated `for col in range(n)` over `n = S * cap = 6 * 10,400 = 62,400` corpse-candidate slots per tick (`~1.2–1.9M` eager PyTorch ops/tick saturating 1 CPU core), compounded by a second wall in `research/gpu_sim_spike/vector_device.py:180-186` running `160` dense whole-board BFS passes (`~1–1.5 TB/tick` at `E=1,024`) (`docs/research/advice/2026-10-22_a100_program/JUDGMENT_round20.md:9-29`).
- **Three-Tier Prediction Doctrine & CPU Action-Tape Rescoring:**
  - Because `device_sim.py` uses counter RNG and cell-center geometry (`mass_integral_error` up to `-4.86`), **every consumed GPU-generated label must be rescored on the CPU reference simulator from its recorded action tape** (`cpu_rescored_label`, `0.305 s` per H300 branch) unless full-trajectory parity (`WP1c` Path A) passes (`docs/research/advice/2026-10-22_a100_program/JUDGMENT_round20.md:20-23, 100-105`, `PLAN_A100.md:39-42`).
  - Learned predictions (`WP3`) are restricted to three tiers: (i) ordering/scheduling exact rollout work (`>= 25%` throughput gain, `>= 95%` rescue retention); (ii) `H64`-exact-prefix leaf cutoff with exact fallback (only if **`<= 5%` lost rescues** and **`>= 95%` top-2 survivor containment** on `>= 256` world-disjoint audit roots); (iii) certifying a branch as safe (**never**) (`docs/research/advice/2026-10-22_a100_program/PLAN_A100.md:45-50`).
- **Model Scale & First 24h Experiments (`A0`–`A4`):**
  - `Ego2sNet` (`1,638,263` params) has **`89%`** of its parameters in two flatten layers (`Linear(4096->256) = 1,048,832` and `Linear(3200->128) = 409,728`) and only **`~60k`** params in its conv towers (`docs/research/advice/2026-10-22_a100_program/JUDGMENT_round20.md:34-37`).
  - Funded `A0` (device corpse-loop repair), `A1` (A100-40GB full-tick ruler at $E \in \{6, 256, 1024\}$: kill engine if **`< 2,000 steps/s`** at $E=1,024$; fund `WP1b` sparse flood if `2k–20k`; fund `WP2` label factory if **`>= 20,000 steps/s`**), `A2` (`1.64M` S vs `~6.5M` M scale + censored multi-horizon hazard falsifier on T5/T8 `19,976` states / `342` worlds + 39 A5 worlds; kill scale on old labels if `M - S` D300 AUC `< +0.02` and 50->100% slope `< +0.01`), `A3` (`P1` cached actor-enabled PPO learner ceiling `<= 1.6384 s/rollout`), and `A4` (Mac serving latency/identity assay for S/M/L shapes against `p95 <= 11.688 ms`) (`docs/research/advice/2026-10-22_a100_program/PLAN_A100.md:75-135`).

## What Was Learned

1. **Host-bound CPython/Numba pipelines cannot be accelerated by bigger GPUs:** When neural forward passes take `~8–13%` of step time and Colab A100 runtimes share the same `12`-vCPU host ceiling as L4 runtimes, three parallel L4 lanes (`~5.1 u/h` combined) dominate a single A100 (`~5.4–7.5 u/h`) on both wall-clock and unit cost.
2. **Audit the loss and the control loop before buying hardware:** The Oct 20 consultation caught two critical defects before compute was wasted—the missing $\tau^2$ factor in `t34_exit_b8_fit/loss.py` that algebraically prevented decisive rescue flips (leading to the split-stream Claim A loss amendment), and the `~11–12` Mac hours spent `time.sleep`-pacing the clock-independent bare comparator in Path B (`2026-10-20_t34_retention_amendment_bare_unpaced.md`).
3. **Eager Python loops inside PyTorch "GPU" simulators masquerade as device bottlenecks:** `E1b`'s `4.6 s/tick` had nothing to do with GPU compute; `62,400` Python loop iterations per tick (`~1.5M` eager tensor launches) saturated a single host core (`host_launch_cpu_seconds = 8,296.32 s` vs `device_loop_seconds = 8,294.06 s`).
4. **Learned surrogates may order or prune after an exact prefix, never certify survival:** Because rare self-enclosure traps occur at `~0.058–0.09` per `1,000` natural decisions and single-frame `H300` survival predictors plateau at `~0.74–0.77` AUC (`T8`, `T35`), any GPU-trained predictor or simulator variant must be gated by selection-conditioned audits (`<= 5%` lost rescues) and exact CPU action-tape rescoring.
