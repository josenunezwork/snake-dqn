## Power, bars, and limitations

`OC_V2.json` and `power_v2.py` fix the measured T23 source identity, SD extraction,
normal/Welch planning model, independent simulation seed and replicate count,
uncapped minimum allocation, immediately preceding balanced allocation,
strong-null checks, capped power and all assumptions. This is historical
planning, not an O2 outcome. Measured T23 paired-mass SDs are approximately
364.683 scripted, 262.732 frozen and 212.360 mixed. The same measured marginal
variances conservatively proxy each O2 contrast; O2 incremental variance and
cross-contrast covariance have not been measured. None is invented.

The planning alternative is true D+O−B8 = +40 and D+O−D = +10. Balanced worlds
are independent between mixes; pooled equal-mix variance is
`sum(s_mix^2/n_mix)/9`, with Welch-Satterthwaite df. The two one-sided p-values
use Holm thresholds .025 then .05. Exact-zero bounds fail. The smallest
balanced allocation whose dependence-robust Holm joint-power lower bound is
>=.80 is **5097 worlds (1699/mix)**; the immediately preceding 5094 worlds
have bound .799974, and N5097 has bound .800179. This
minimum concerns the two mass superiority tests under the stated variance
model. Holm's valid marginal-p assumption supplies strong family-wise false
rejection control <=.05, including either single-null configuration. Monte
Carlo checks and intervals are diagnostics, not a replacement for that bound.

**No finite N can provide .80 full-GO power at the true +40 boundary while the
contract's observed gain >=40 is retained.** Under symmetric continuous error,
that point cutoff alone has about .50 acceptance. V2 retains it, all survival
and boost clauses, fault precedence and GO scope. Thus this registration makes
no claim of .80 full-GO power. The budget-capped design below also falls short
of .80 for the two mass tests; this is the owner's specified >1000-world
fallback, not an adequate-power certification. Guard B's scripted no-death
point gain >=.03 and guard A's survival-band tests remain independent additional
requirements. Increasing N does not guarantee any controller meets those bars.

GO requires both Holm rejections, observed D+O−B8 mass gain >=40, survival guards
A/B vs bare B8, boost [0.005,0.10], and all integrity/accounting checks. Retained
precedence: INVALID identity/accounting/parity/provenance failure; INCOMPLETE
missing/cap-expired/unclean evidence; KILL unconditional fault, failed point
bar or clear inferiority; GO only if every clause passes; else INCONCLUSIVE.
A typed source/world/arm-bound unconditional study fault is the sole early
scientific stop; drain at most the already admitted worker wave. Excluded
fixture faults are mechanical. No early efficacy look or automatic retry.
A valid failure states this controller failed its registered bar.
