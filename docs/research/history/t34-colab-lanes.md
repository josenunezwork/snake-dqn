# T34 Parallel Colab Lanes, 1,160-Unit Compute Portfolio, and Speed-vs-Constraints Rulings

## Question and Goal

On 2026-10-20, following the owner's standing authorization to spend all ~1,154–1,160 prepaid Colab Pro+ units by ~2026-11-20 (~38–39 units/day) on parallel research lanes that produce concrete decisions—while preserving Mac-only strict gates, serving qualification, and the Path B real-time search retention critical path—three linked multi-panel advice and independent-judge rounds were conducted (`t34-compute-portfolio`, `t34-colab-lanes`, and `t34-speed-vs-constraints`). Their goal was to price the true L4 throughput of the `teacher_v2` shadow engine, allocate the ~920–1,030 uncommitted Colab units across decision-bearing Claim A (bare student) and Claim B (search-assisted hero) lanes, ratify the pre-data amendments needed to unblock Claim A distillation and the dense-teacher fallback (`Round 0-D`), and decide which runtime or governance restrictions could be relaxed or optimized in parallel without compromising the validity of a "better served snake than bare `b8-s3`" claim.

## Final Verdict and Key Numbers

1. **Engine Placement (`W1`) and True Teacher Cost:**
   - Before process fanout, `A5 #2` (`t34-exit-b8-v2-a5-s{0,1}-a3`, 2 workers × 5 numba threads, 256 resident roots) achieved only `~30–31` executed row-steps/s per L4 lane and `0/256` completed worlds in `1,500 s` ([`2026-10-20_compute_portfolio_FABLE_JUDGMENT.md`](../advice/2026-10-20_compute_portfolio_FABLE_JUDGMENT.md)).
   - After the `1.7139×` serial row-step speedup (`6.1605 → 3.5945 ms`, with zero diffs over `15,000` live/Grid/shadow frames) and switching to `12` single-thread processes per L4 with `1` resident world per process, production `A5` (`snake-dqn-artifacts/t34-engine-fanout-20261020/manifests-final-3ebf3991/a5-l4-s{0,1}-output/exports/benchmark.json`) reached **`57.2` and `54.4` completed worlds/lane-hour** (`377` and `432` active row-steps/s across 12 vCPUs, or `31–36` row-steps/s per L4 vCPU vs `208` row-steps/s on one Mac M5 Pro core, so `1` L4 vCPU `≈ 1/6` Mac core and `1` L4 observed at `1.54 u/h` costs **`~0.036 u/teacher world`**) ([`2026-10-20_colab_lanes/JUDGMENT.md`](../advice/2026-10-20_colab_lanes/JUDGMENT.md), [`2026-10-20_speed_vs_constraints/JUDGMENT.md`](../advice/2026-10-20_speed_vs_constraints/JUDGMENT.md)).
   - Post-fix exclusive CPU stage shares on the excluded Mac smoke (`snake-dqn-artifacts/t34-engine-fanout-20261020/cpu-profile-smoke-02/profile-cpu.json`, `6.022 s` wall) were **`35.5%` `full_digest` (`2.140 s`), `22.2%` `v8` hooks (`1.338 s`), `13.2%` neural inference (`0.793 s`), `9.5%` raster (`0.573 s`), and `5.7%` simulator step (`0.343 s`)**; on the 60 s x86 CPU-only Colab profile (`snake-dqn-artifacts/t34-engine-fanout-20261020/manifests-final-3ebf3991/cpu-profile-output/exports/profile-cpu.json`, `28` row-steps/s), shares were **`35.4%` digest, `19.9%` `v8`, `13.3%` inference, `12.3%` sim, and `7.0%` raster** ([`2026-10-20_speed_vs_constraints/JUDGMENT.md`](../advice/2026-10-20_speed_vs_constraints/JUDGMENT.md)).

2. **Claim A Distillation Blockers and Pre-Data Amendment (`L1`–`L3`):**
   - On the excluded 39-world `A5` diagnostic (`snake-dqn-artifacts/fable-panel-claima/design.md`), the ratified `research/t34_exit_b8_fit/loss.py` shared a single `sample_weight` across `KL(prev‖student, τ=0.05)`, the `0.1`-margin hinge, and `0.01·adv-MSE`. Because `KL` penalized any large logit shift on decisive rows (`0.550 / 0.792 / 0.298 / 2.126` counterexample), a free-Q probe flipped only **`0.4%` of `225` decisive rows** (`97.8%` stayed bare `b8-s3`), while the 1:1 `searched:unsearched` epoch sampler yielded only `~1` optimizer step/epoch on 39 worlds (`~12` steps/epoch, `~60` steps per 5-epoch fit on 800 worlds), and default world splitting left `0` held-out decisive rows ([`2026-10-20_compute_portfolio_FABLE_JUDGMENT.md`](../advice/2026-10-20_compute_portfolio_FABLE_JUDGMENT.md), [`2026-10-20_colab_lanes/JUDGMENT.md`](../advice/2026-10-20_colab_lanes/JUDGMENT.md)).
   - The approved Claim A amendment (`t34-claim-a-amendment-20261021` @ `71de7645`) separated the objective into two normalized streams with `all_die_delay` excluded from both (`KL(prev‖θ) + 0.01·adv-MSE` on shadow-confirm anchor rows only; `τ·CE(a*) + 0.1-margin hinge` on decisive rows only), fixed batch size at `1,024` (`128` decisive + `896` anchor), froze the update dose `U* ∈ {512, 1024, 2048, 4096}` via `L1` (`D0`, `≤12 u`) on excluded `A5 v2` data, and sized `L2` (`R1`, `≤60 u`) at `800` training + `200` whole-world held-out worlds with a hard precondition of `≥60` decisive-bearing held-out worlds and `≥300` held-out decisive rows ([`CLAIMA_AMENDMENT_JUDGMENT.md`](../advice/2026-10-20_colab_lanes/CLAIMA_AMENDMENT_JUDGMENT.md)).

3. **Gated Colab Portfolio (`L0–L9 + Reserve`) and Exclusions:**
   - Out of `1,153.84 u` available at `2026-10-20 19:18 PT`, the adopted lane ceilings were: **`L0` existing deciders** (`≤43 u` Round 0 remainder, `≤5 u` fit timing, `≤42.5 u` unpaced Path B arm at `81c54731`), **`L1` `D0` projection diagnostics** (`12 u`), **`L2` sparse `R1` labels** (`60 u`), **`L3` `D1` 2×2 projection** (`120 u`), **`L4` on-policy `R2` + conditional `R3`** (`150 u`: `R2 ≤ 80 u`, `R3 ≤ 70 u`), **`L5` coverage learning curve `800 → 2,400 → 7,200` worlds** (`280 u`: step 1 `≤100 u`, step 2 `≤180 u`), **`L6` full-arena tower ablation** (`80 u`), **`L7` re-scoped `Round 0-D` (`120 u` hard total + `≤10 u` `A5-D`) + yield-sized dense `R1` (`200 u`)**, **`L8` deadline-delivery successor** (`100 u`, gated on Path B late/stale attribution), **`L9` finalist replication (`128` paired worlds/mix) + failure census** (`100 u`; census-only `≤15 u`), and **`Reserve`** (`100 u`) ([`2026-10-20_colab_lanes/JUDGMENT.md`](../advice/2026-10-20_colab_lanes/JUDGMENT.md), [`ROUND0D_JUDGMENT_STAGE1.md`](../advice/2026-10-20_colab_lanes/ROUND0D_JUDGMENT_STAGE1.md)).
   - Explicitly excluded from funding: two-deviation beam search (`research/t35_two_deviation/RESULT_EXTEND.md` pooled `KILL`, `0` novel rescued worlds), auxiliary outcome-head training (`research/t35_outcome_head/RESULT.md` `KILL`, AUC `0.747`, `46.2%`), `A100/H100/G4` runtimes for host-bound search (`G4` at `8.7 u/h` vs L4 at `1.7 u/h` requires `5.12×` throughput for unit parity with only `4×` vCPUs), 5-seed recipe-population studies for fixed-checkpoint claims, and PQN/PPO/league restarts ([`2026-10-20_colab_lanes/JUDGMENT.md`](../advice/2026-10-20_colab_lanes/JUDGMENT.md), [`2026-10-20_speed_vs_constraints/JUDGMENT.md`](../advice/2026-10-20_speed_vs_constraints/JUDGMENT.md)).

## What Was Learned

- **Paced Mac evaluation is wall-clock bound, not engine-compute bound:** At `24 fps`, a paced Watch6 session sleeps ~75% of every tick; speeding up `GridBatchSim` or `full_digest` saves Colab units and offline label-collection hours, but cannot shorten a single-session paced Mac run. The highest-leverage calendar optimization for future Mac paced runs is concurrent multi-session pacing (`S4`: `N ≈ 4` sessions × 2 threads on the 18-core M5 Pro, cutting ~18–19 h to ~5–6 h once qualified by a 1-vs-N contention probe), alongside the already-ratified unpacing of the bare `b8-s3` comparator (`operator-02` at `81c54731`, saving ~8.7–10 Mac hours) ([`2026-10-20_speed_vs_constraints/JUDGMENT.md`](../advice/2026-10-20_speed_vs_constraints/JUDGMENT.md)).
- **`full_digest` is controller logic, not merely an audit receipt:** `RollingShadow.catch_up` and `confirm` (`research/t34_shadow/shadow.py`) and `teacher_v2.py` use per-step digests to invalidate stale predictions and clear pending labels/interventions; naively subsampling the hash changes the controller. Replacing recursive Python traversal with flat canonical serialization (`t34-shadow-full-digest/v4`, `S1`) and routing non-root shadow/branch rows through `v8_nb` (`nogil`, `S2`) targets the `35.5% + 22.2% = 57.7%` post-fix CPU bottleneck (`1.36×` to `1.61×` conditional whole-step gain) while preserving exact transition identity ([`2026-10-20_speed_vs_constraints/JUDGMENT.md`](../advice/2026-10-20_speed_vs_constraints/JUDGMENT.md)).
- **Distillation failure was structural, not a label-volume deficit:** Collecting thousands of additional teacher worlds under the frozen loss would have guaranteed `FIT_STOPPED` because `KL(prev‖student)` and the decisive hinge shared one row weight and conflicted on rescue rows. Separating anchor and decisive streams and freezing the update dose on excluded data (`D0`) was an essential prerequisite before scaling `R1` or `L5` ([`2026-10-20_colab_lanes/JUDGMENT.md`](../advice/2026-10-20_colab_lanes/JUDGMENT.md), [`CLAIMA_AMENDMENT_JUDGMENT.md`](../advice/2026-10-20_colab_lanes/CLAIMA_AMENDMENT_JUDGMENT.md)).
- **Credit targets must remain gated on scientific GO decisions:** The judges established that the ~38 u/day spend rate is achievable only if `Round 0` (or `Round 0-D`) and `L3` pass; if premises fail, reporting unspent credits rather than padding killed studies or adding unplanned seeds is mandatory ([`2026-10-20_colab_lanes/JUDGMENT.md`](../advice/2026-10-20_colab_lanes/JUDGMENT.md)).
