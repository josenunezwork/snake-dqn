## Implementation / execution steps

1. Strict mode requires immutable candidate/incumbent/opponent hashes, clean/source-hashed inputs, the named deployment profile, all required mixes, balanced precomputed roster rotations and a valid serving readiness receipt. Generic/scripted comparisons remain diagnostics.
2. Replace baseline-only pilot sizing with paired delta variance and declared power/MDE. Require at least 40 untouched final worlds and any larger pilot-derived count. Forty is a floor, not a power guarantee.
3. Use the decision rule in README: per-mix paired superiority with multiplicity handling, at least two successful mixes, and a genuine scripted noninferiority bound against a predeclared absolute margin. Preserve per-world data and do not use the descriptive combined interval as authority.
4. Keep seed sets disjoint after model selection. Null/unimplemented required probes block readiness; calibrate behavioral bands before final data, not from final outcomes.
5. Remove champion authority from --copy-best-to diagnostics. Do not auto-promote after evaluation. An explicit later promotion operation must recheck the exact passing receipt/hash and preserve the incumbent; publishing remains outside these packages.

## Acceptance evidence

- Two-world or arbitrary-scripted baselines cannot obtain strict authority. Duplicate hashes/seeds, missing mixes, unbalanced rosters, changed snapshots and insufficient pilot-sized n fail.
- Constant-baseline/variable-delta pilot counterexample is handled correctly. Superiority/multiplicity/noninferiority boundaries have analytical oracles.
- Failed/legacy/missing-serving receipts cannot authorize a champion update. Diagnostic comparisons still run with their authority clearly labeled.
