# Big-Lever Served Search Ceiling (`L0` / `L2'`) and Root-Rescue Follow-Ups (`M0` / `B2` / `C1` / `X5`) (`bl-served-search-l2`)

## Question and Goal

What is the decision-quality ceiling of dense test-time rollout search over the served bare `b8-s3` network (`Ego2sNet`) when free-space `v8` safety is already satisfied (`L0` / `L2'`), why does dense 1-ply `6 x H300` rollout search regress early-game survival on the 160-world H5000 bank (`M0`), and can either survival-gated root search (`B2`), root-rescued states distilled into a bare student (`C1`), or 1-ply action-prefix imitation (`X5`) beat bare `b8-s3` without a serving-time veto?

## Final Verdict and Key Numbers

1. **`L0` / `L0'` Saved-Smoke Bookkeeping (`amendment08-l0-r4`):**
   - Across `80` LAD and `80` N1 saved episodes from `t34-pathb-smoke-20261021`, `34` death intervals were rescue-capable (`t_D - t_F > 1`) and `41` were delay-only (`t_D - t_F <= 1`) ([`/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/big-lever/exp/bl-l2/amendment08-l0-r4/l0_prime_receipt.json`](/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/big-lever/exp/bl-l2/amendment08-l0-r4/l0_prime_receipt.json)).
   - Under `L0'` (`D300` = `min(t_F, t_D - 300)`), **`0 / 62`** complete own-deadline rows fell inside the `300`-step deadline, while `D300` remained reachable from saved checkpoints on `29/34`, `27/34`, `21/34`, and `17/34` rescue-capable episodes under `1, 5, 8, 12 s` wall caps ([`research/bl_served_search/AMENDMENT_08.md`](../../../research/bl_served_search/AMENDMENT_08.md)).
2. **Colab L4 Scheduling Stop (`STOP_ON_SCHEDULING`) and Mac `L2'` Execution (`KILL`):**
   - On Colab L4 (`12` vCPUs), the two-worker CPU ruler `bl-l2-ruler-l4-01` averaged `52.509 s`/world (`70.0 min` for `160` worlds before 3x safety), and the native CUDA canary `bl-l2-ruler-l4-native-canary-01` took `1,974.99 s` across `2` worlds (`987.49 s`/world) because per-step Python/GPU dispatch overhead inside H300 rollouts projected **`2,633.3 min`** (`> 22 h` cap), triggering `STOP_ON_SCHEDULING` ([`research/bl_served_search/SCHEDULING_STOP_STATUS_20261022.md`](../../../research/bl_served_search/SCHEDULING_STOP_STATUS_20261022.md)).
   - Executed on the 18-core Mac with 12 workers in **`65.9 min`** under [`AMENDMENT_MAC_L2.md`](../../../research/bl_served_search/AMENDMENT_MAC_L2.md), dense `L2'` search (`L >= 600`, stride `5`, `6 x H300` rollouts, `16,580` branch states, `26.8%` disagreement with `b8-s3`) **failed**: paired mean mass delta **`-42.68`** (`95% CI [-158.95, +73.59]`, `506.03` vs `548.72`), endpoint survival **`64.38%` vs `73.75%` (`-9.38 pp`)**, and deaths **`57` vs `42`** ([`docs/research/results/L2prime_mac_analysis_20261022.json`](../results/L2prime_mac_analysis_20261022.json)).
   - On the `68` worlds where both arms reached `L >= 600`, `L2'` improved mass by **`+44.54`** (`946.47` vs `901.93`), proving that dense H300 search helped late-game mass but suffered severe early-game (`L < 600`) mortality ([`docs/research/results/L2prime_mac_analysis_20261022.json`](../results/L2prime_mac_analysis_20261022.json)).
3. **`M0` Root-Cause & Counterfactual Rescue Audit:**
   - Why did `L2'` die `26` times at `L < 600` (including `18` worlds where `b8-s3` survived) if `L2'` only branches at `L >= 600`? Because `GridBatchSim` advances a single per-world RNG for food respawns and random opponent moves; once `L2'` branched once at an early `L >= 600` spike (`380` early branch frames across those `18` worlds), the world trajectory diverged from `b8-s3` and later died after boosting back below `600` ([`research/bl_served_search/M0_HANDOFF.md`](../../../research/bl_served_search/M0_HANDOFF.md), [`research/bl_served_search/M0_VERIFICATION_20261022.json`](../../../research/bl_served_search/M0_VERIFICATION_20261022.json)).
   - Across `b8-s3`'s `42` deaths, **`33 / 42` (`78.6%`)** had a legal alternative action within `5` frames before death that survived the full `H300` rollout while `b8-s3`'s chosen action died inside `H300` (`42.9%` at `k = 1` frame before death) ([`research/bl_served_search/M0_HANDOFF.md`](../../../research/bl_served_search/M0_HANDOFF.md)).
4. **`B2`, `C1`, and `X5` Follow-Up Outcomes:**
   - **`B2` (`B2-cons`):** Gating H300 rollouts to fire *only* when `b8-s3`'s chosen action dies within `150` frames in its own H300 rollout (`0.54%` override rate, `3.67` overrides/episode) scored **`+118.67` paired mass** (`95% CI [+22.58, +214.76]`) and **`+10.62 pp` survival** (`84.38%` vs `73.75%`) on the 160-world screen ([`research/bl_served_search/b2/ROUND16_HANDOFF.md`](../../../research/bl_served_search/b2/ROUND16_HANDOFF.md)), and diagnosed the `GridSimCarryRuntime.restore_config` bug where restoring a snapshot without restoring `_config` turned off opponent `v8` vetoes inside rollouts ([`research/bl_served_search/b2/B2_RESTORE_CONFIG_ERRATUM_20261022.md`](../../../research/bl_served_search/b2/B2_RESTORE_CONFIG_ERRATUM_20261022.md)). (On fresh confirmation banks in `B2-confirm`, `B2-cons` later regressed at `-61.71` / `-40.54` mass; see [`docs/notes/big-lever-experiments.md`](../../notes/big-lever-experiments.md).)
   - **`C1` (`C1-200`, `C1-400`):** Fine-tuning `b8-s3` on `479` root-rescue states (`3,832` 8-way dihedral rows) reached `97.7%` rescue-action accuracy on the mined states but failed on the 160-world H5000 bank (`-3.41` and `-21.48` mass vs `b8-s3`) ([`research/bl_served_search/c1/HANDOFF.md`](../../../research/bl_served_search/c1/HANDOFF.md)).
   - **`X5`:** Confirmed bare `b8-s3` satisfies the 24-fps Mac latency budget (`p95 = 3.21 ms`), while 1-ply prefix imitation lost `-20.7` mass vs `b8-s3` ([`research/bl_x5/README.md`](../../../research/bl_x5/README.md), [`docs/notes/big-lever-experiments.md`](../../notes/big-lever-experiments.md)).

## What Was Learned

- **Unconditional rollout value maximization perturbs trajectories and hurts survival:** Overriding `b8-s3` on `26.8%` of `L >= 600` frames chases noisy H300 value differences and increases deaths (`57` vs `42`).
- **Snapshot-based rollout engines must restore opponent wrapper configurations (`_config`):** `GridSimCarryRuntime.snapshot()` must restore both dynamic state and `_config` so opponent `v8` vetoes remain active during counterfactual rollouts ([`B2_RESTORE_CONFIG_ERRATUM_20261022.md`](../../../research/bl_served_search/b2/B2_RESTORE_CONFIG_ERRATUM_20261022.md)).
- **Small offline rescue patches (`C1`) do not generalize across the full state distribution:** Memorizing `479` pre-death rescue states (`97.7%` train accuracy) perturbs ordinary actions enough to offset any rescue gain (`-3.41` to `-21.48` mass).

## Why It Closed / Current Status

**Closed (`L2'`, `C1`, and `X5` = `KILL`; Colab L4 `L2'` = `STOP_ON_SCHEDULING`).** The findings from `L2'`, `M0`, and `B2` directly informed the later `exact-stop-l2-prime` controller that was confirmed and promoted for Watch replay serving (`watch-replay-bank-live-132`; see [`docs/STATUS.md`](../../STATUS.md) and [`docs/notes/big-lever-experiments.md`](../../notes/big-lever-experiments.md)).
