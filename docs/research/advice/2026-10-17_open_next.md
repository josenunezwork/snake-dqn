# Advice: what I would do next for a bare network that survives like the lookahead (2026-10-17)

Advisory note (cloud advisor), requested by the local session at the owner's request. Not gate
evidence. Inputs: `ego_raster.py` / `ego_raster_b.py` (observation geometry), `train_m3c.py`
(N_STEP = 5), T1, T3 ceiling, T5/T5b, T6, the T8 spec, `game_config.py` (arena 1450 x 830 px).

## The diagnosis I would bet on

The arena is 145 x 83 cells, about 12,000 cells. B8 sees full resolution only in the 31 x 31
local crop, 15 cells from its head. The global view is 37 x 37 blocks of 8 x 8 cells holding
segment *counts*. A one-cell-wide body wall across a block and a loose coil in the same block
produce similar counts, so the global view cannot tell a sealed pocket from open ground. The
only exact topology the network receives is the handful of region scalars and the 24-step
reach flood inside the local window.

At L >= 800 the hero's own body is 1,000 cells, about 8% of the arena, laid out as a path
that partitions the arena into pockets of tens to hundreds of cells. The fatal commitment
happens a median 88 decisions before death, when the hero enters a pocket that will close.
That pocket is almost never inside the 31 x 31 window. The lookahead wins not because it
is smarter but because simulation reads exact topology for free. The 0.73-0.77 probes, the
restart null, the label caution and the Huber tax are all the same fact: the signal is
not in the observation, so every loss lands as a global prior.

If T8's raster probe comes back at or above 0.85 I am wrong and the problem is the trunk; the
rest of this note still applies with step 2 dropped. I expect about 0.77.

## What I would do, in order

**0. Divergence study (one to two Mac-hours, now, CPU, alongside T8).** T1 retained the
surviving and fatal continuations for 46 late deaths. Trace both for the 100 decisions after
the LAD: size of the connected free region the head is in, distance from the head to the
hero's own tail, hero cells and opponent cells within radius 15 and 30, and whether the fatal
branch is following food into a pocket. I expect the fatal branch to enter a region that is
bounded mostly by the hero's own body, and the surviving branch to stay in the largest
component. This tells us which topology quantity to compute before building any of it.

**1. Give the network exact topology at full arena scale (the main bet).** The featurizer
already computes exact BFS quantities for v8 and the region scalars; this extends that
pattern rather than adding a rule. Two layers, cheapest first:

- Per-action reachability profile, 12-20 scalars: free cells the hero reaches within 16, 32,
  64, 128 and 256 steps under the time-aware, tail-vacating, arrival-race flood (opponents
  as competing sources, 1 cell per frame); the size of the component the action's next cell
  belongs to once segments with TTL <= 64 and <= 128 have vacated; the bottleneck width
  between that component and the largest component; whether the own tail is reachable
  before any opponent. These are the quantities a 300-step rollout integrates implicitly,
  made deterministic and policy-agnostic. Numba floods over 12,000 cells per agent per
  decision are within the current actor budget, which already runs three region floods.
- If the profile alone does not separate (T5-style probe first), a full-resolution global
  plane at 1 cell per pixel, 145 x 83, three channels: passability TTL, hero arrival time,
  earliest opponent arrival time, binned to uint8, fed through a strided conv tower. This
  preserves thin walls, which 8 x 8 counting destroys. Cost is about nine times the current
  raster bytes; MPS absorbs it, the actors must be measured.

Gate: rerun the T5 GBM with the new features on the restored bank; a jump from 0.75 toward
0.85 earns training, no jump and the bet is dead in a day.

**2. Learn the lookahead directly, as a hazard head with Monte Carlo targets.** Five-step
TD with gamma 0.998 has to carry a -3 death back through 100 bootstrapped states the trunk
aliases; the signal leaks. A hazard head predicting "death within 300 transitions" per
action, trained with Monte Carlo targets from the actors' own trajectories, gets a direct,
non-bootstrapped label at every state within 300 steps of every death: roughly 1.3 million
positive frames per run against about 4,500 deaths. It is the distilled lookahead, computed
from inputs that can now carry it (step 1). Alone it is an auxiliary loss shaping the trunk.

**3. Move the signal into Q with learned potential shaping (training-time help, disclosed).**
Add Phi(s) = -kappa * max_a hazard(s, a) as a potential-based term, gamma*Phi(s') - Phi(s),
to the training reward. Potential-based shaping leaves the optimal policy unchanged in
theory; its purpose is to deliver the death penalty at the commitment decision rather than
100 steps later. Refit the hazard head on a frozen snapshot every few hundred thousand
updates and hold it fixed between refits, so Phi is stationary while it shapes. The served
artifact stays a bare Q network; the hazard head is dropped or kept as a diagnostic.

**4. Then the usual screen, nothing exotic.** M6 CTRL recipe plus steps 1-3, warm-started
from a B8 final (new input branch initialised to zero contribution, so the student's mass
advantage is not thrown away), five seeds, 96 scripted worlds, the T4/T6 screen protocol
and tripwires, Tier 1 comparator. If it moves scripted survival by more than +0.05 with
mass within the futility bound, pre-register Phase R. RunPod does not help; the arm is
learner-bound and pods are actor-bound.

## What I would stop doing

No further loss, label, reward, restart or density arms on the current observation; six have
returned null for the same reason. No strategy-level nudges (shrinking, wall-hugging):
they trade the mass clause for survival the lookahead shows is available without that
trade. No ensemble as the primary line; keep it as a cheap serving stopgap only if Phase R
passes marginally.

## Questioning the framing

- The target "as well as the lookahead" (54/64) is the oracle under B8's own continuation.
  A bare network that closes half the gap, to about 0.70, would clear Tier 1 comfortably and
  likely Tier 2. I would pre-register for that, not for the oracle.
- B8 is a distillation student; its mass edge came from the teacher, and so may its blind
  spot. Warm-starting is right for compute, but one cold-start seed with the new inputs is
  worth running as a control for inherited bias.
- The metric is fine. Alive-frame survival against the served agent on fixed worlds is the
  honest question, and the guard amendment already separates it from the champion line.
- The game rewards growth with an unbounded body in a bounded arena; late-game sealing is
  not a bug in the policy but the natural end state. The owner could consider whether
  Watch-mode play should ever run beyond the length where the arena itself becomes the
  enemy; that is a product question, not a research one, and I would not spend arms on it.

## Expected cost

Divergence study and feature probes: one Mac-day. Featurizer and hazard head
implementation: one to two days of the local session's time. One five-seed screen: about
one Mac-day on the learner with the actors at current throughput. Decision inside a week,
without touching the running Phase R or T8.
