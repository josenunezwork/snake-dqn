## Transport prerequisite discovered during integration

`native_compile.require_for_a100_canary` before validation/dispatch. It requires
a **same full commit** compiler-only PASS/release receipt; a compile proof
executed inside that canary cannot satisfy its own pre-allocation gate.

needs a prospective operational amendment before dispatch. The concrete proposal
is L4 compile 5/10 minutes + A100 canary 10/20 + main 105/210, preserving total
120/240 and every science parameter. Native integration parity remains inside
the A100 canary. No transport guard was weakened and no amended cap was adopted.
