# Strategy: measure the deployment gap before buying another local answer

This is a read-only synthesis of reports published at `66baaaac50e2fd2dd735dec1b60eddd7dbbfc69e`, not a new experiment, code audit, admission, or training authorization. References below give report line ranges. Local runtime changes and artifact wrappers were not inspected. The failed native-counterfactual audit contributes no scientific evidence here.

**Recommendation:** defer the fresh width-by-breadth interaction replication. Design one ten-working-day integration-and-training milestone: can sustained, diverse encounter experience improve retained mass while preserving established solo competence? Reuse existing CW/CX evidence; do not insert another solo/one-opponent evaluation prerequisite. This memo proposes the campaign but does not admit or launch it.

## What has actually progressed

**Facts.** Fresh canonical Apex TD controls learned the small food task in all three seeds; imitation warm starts regressed under the same continuation ([canonical retention](../experiments/apex_canonical_retention_2026-09-22/README.md), lines 3–24). The later width/breadth study learned strong fresh H64 food behavior, despite failing its scaling and historical-retention conjunctions ([scaling](../experiments/apex_scaling_width_breadth_2026-09-23/README.md), lines 48–81).

The raster branch also contains substantial successful behavior. BO's learned shared-head candidates passed every frozen H512 criterion and survived all 96 lanes in all three seeds; their parents survived only 30, 21, and 21 ([BO](../experiments/solo_food_h512_confirmation_2026-09-20/README.md), lines 56–80). CW's three fixed native-PQN length-access policies passed their fresh H768 food/survival package ([CW](../experiments/solo_food_pqn_h768_length_confirmation_2026-09-21/README.md), lines 3–47). CW is evaluation, not fresh training; it does not retroactively prove CV's failed relative hypothesis.

Those results refute “nothing learns.” They do not establish competitive play. CW's mean mass integrals ranged from 12.5 to 61.0 despite passing food/survival ([CW](../experiments/solo_food_pqn_h768_length_confirmation_2026-09-21/README.md), lines 56–75). CX already showed useful one-opponent behavior and adequate exposure, but all three policies lost paired retention ([CX](../experiments/solo_food_pqn_one_opponent_2026-09-21/README.md), lines 3–40). The longer enemy-visible PQN continuation delivered no reliable incremental benefit and explicitly closed unchanged continuation ([enemy dose](../experiments/solo_food_pqn_enemy_dose_2026-09-21/README.md), lines 38–98).

## Why the research feels stalled

**Inference:** the portfolio has accumulated local skill demonstrations without a stable bridge back to the product objective. Short food tasks are useful unit tests for learning; they are weak proxies for sustained mass, enemy handling, and robust multi-agent play. Opponent encounter, body growth, and retention cannot be inferred from first-food success.

**Fact:** each scaling cell used 79,872 accepted H64 rows but 5.12 million replay draws, approximately 64 draws per accepted row. Width doubled training time; random play already survived every fresh H64 case ([scaling](../experiments/apex_scaling_width_breadth_2026-09-23/README.md), lines 23–58). This is not evidence that broad, long-trajectory data scaling was exhausted. It is also not evidence that less replay would repair learning: changing reuse changes optimization and target age.

**Judgment:** another interaction replication has low immediate decision value. H256 showed no complete sustained-food/survival pass and no reliable factor effect ([H256](../experiments/apex_scaling_h256_2026-09-24/README.md), lines 5–49). Even a clean interaction replication would leave the competitive-play gap unresolved. Cheap compute alone does not justify another branch; engineering and interpretation also consume the research budget.

Do not reopen architecture folklore. The historical “CNN lost” claim was retracted because those CNNs were never trained; newer raster/PQN results are separate evidence. The blueprint also explicitly stages cardinal-grid play before continuous coiling ([blueprint](../ml_redesign_blueprint_2026-07.md), lines 56–58, 88–94). **Judgment:** keep the current movement contract for this milestone. A smooth-coiling product would require a separately specified dynamics project, not a wider network.

## One finite milestone: integrate and train on the missing task

**Provisional design ceilings, not admitted budgets:** ten working days, 24 serialized numerical hours across qualification/training/evaluation, at most ten million fresh valid hero transitions per lineage, three lineages, and one final comparison. Qualified throughput and resource fit must establish feasibility before admission; reduce scope prospectively if necessary, never after seeing learning outcomes.

1. **Days 1–3: establish the contract.** Use all three fixed CZ `enemy_visible` mark-4608 parents, the existing enemy-aware descendants of CV, rather than choosing a favorable seed or inventing new input surgery. All had absolute solo/S2 competence ([CZ](../experiments/solo_food_pqn_enemy_access_2026-09-21/README.md), lines 3–37). Preserve network width, native PQN loss/reward, masks, restricted-input transform, model/Adam state, and termination semantics. Package one reusable training/serving adapter. Static identity and interface admission, plus a bounded implementation smoke, are prerequisites; perfect baseline gameplay is not. A contract mismatch ends training admission and produces an integration blocker.
2. **Days 4–8: one campaign.** Propose a fixed training mixture of 20% solo, 40% two-snake, and 40% six-snake episodes, with fresh procedural roots, a frozen scripted opponent pool, and maximum collection horizon 1,024. Keep one exact-coverage SGD epoch; do not add a replay-ratio intervention. Restore each parent separately with fresh, prespecified runtime seeds. Fix dose checkpoints before execution; intermediate marks describe learning and never replace the final endpoint. This changes the task-distribution/horizon package and dose together: it estimates package utility, not the isolated cause of improvement. It does not reopen the rejected unchanged S2 continuation.
3. **Days 9–10: one final decision.** Compare every final endpoint with its own frozen parent on a sealed fresh bank under the same exact native contract. Use long solo retention and two predeclared opponent mixtures, 3,000 frames with prefixes, total-horizon living mass primary. Include compatible scripted anchors; do not require an unqualified cross-engine Apex comparison. Cap evaluation and qualification at 800 hero episodes and 2.4 million simulator frames. Existing CW/CX records supply motivation, not substituted comparison rows. Report every lineage, world-paired intervals, exposure, food, survival, boost, and failures.

**Proposed practical rule, to freeze before admission:** each lineage must improve mean mass by at least 10% over its parent in both opponent mixtures, with a positive paired 95% lower bound; retain at least 95% of parent solo mass and food, and lose no more than two percentage points of solo alive-time fraction. Require encounter exposure under a prespecified definition. These are product tolerances, not empirically validated power claims; the evaluation specialist must assess precision before freezing them.

**Diagnostic branch and stop:** failure to execute exact contracts is engineering work only. Adequate exposure plus flat opponent mass ends this training package; inspect saved records once to choose objective versus data/representation work. Better opponent mass with failed solo retention points to skill preservation. Passing the package permits design of the shared tournament gate, not promotion. Missing exposure, identity drift, leakage, exhausted budget, or an incomplete matrix is inconclusive and stops. No additional learner arm, held-bank tuning, favorable-seed continuation, or automatic extension.

## Compute and dissent

Existing PQN capacity measurements show roughly 1,284 valid hero transitions/second in one specific S2 configuration ([capacity](../experiments/pqn_mac_capacity_2026-09-21/README.md), lines 3–36). This makes a materially larger fresh-data budget worth qualifying, not guaranteed feasible in six-snake play. Allocate compute to new long trajectories and three retained lineages before more width; do not turn spare RAM or elapsed time into an uncapped search.

Questions for the team: Can the exact competent wrappers be served without silent input changes? Is retained mass truly the desired product objective, or does the user require observable aggression/coiling? Does the next proposed study change a deployment decision? What result would make us stop a branch permanently? These decisions matter more than another favorable local interaction.
