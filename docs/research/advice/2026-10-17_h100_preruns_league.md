# H100 pre-runs: the minimum useful league — 2026-10-17

**Recommendation: do not spend a long Colab session on the current all-snakes mode and call it a league.** First implement and qualify three learner seats plus three frozen seats, actor-owned GPU-resident BV8 opponents, and a small deterministic PFSP ledger. Then measure that exact workload, including a diverse late pool, and require a short paired learning probe to show improvement against frozen references without collapse. Start with one active learner at a time, no hyperparameter PBT, fp32, and the adopted K2 recipe at **256 ingested replay transitions per update**. Five independent replicas remain the scientific unit for the later campaign.

This is **advice and proposed preregistration content**, not an executed experiment, launch receipt, or modification of the Lane A protocol. All new numerical cutoffs below are prospective engineering or spending decisions; they are not calibrated power claims. Freeze implementation, expanded arguments, seed audit, manifests, analysis and GO/KILL/EXTEND rules before running. ## Evidence that changes the decision

- **The comparator is released B8-s3, bare**, SHA `3e64978337b67b7acaf4daa93bdefa827d8296bcb0f63413886a1aaad12eb4f8`. Its original claim is more mass at equal survival against FRP+v8, not a safety advantage. Tier 2 passed by only 0.034 at SE 0.023. Mixed Watch now uses B8 hero versus FRP+v8 opponents. Sources: `docs/STATUS.md:7-39`; `docs/research/b8_s3_release_2026-10-17.md:12-40,54-59`. Training against oneself is a new distribution, not evidence of beating this comparator.
- **A100 is no longer explained by the old host-only diagnosis.** Smoke-03 reports fp32 K2 a3/a4 at 58.9/62.2 updates/s; isolated raw/g3 fp32 K2 is about 78. K4 doubles transitions/update and roughly halves updates/s. BF16 reaches 117 alone but only 47.6 in-run, unexplained. These are reasons to test H100 at the same recipe, not adopt BF16 or K4. The missing lifted lag field made comparison rows INVALID despite underlying telemetry; preserve that qualification boundary. Source: `docs/research/t16_colab_smoke02_results_2026-10-17.md:170-200`.
- **All-six learning hides pool costs.** At N learner seats, a fully alive world produces N learner decisions and 6 total decisions. N=3 has one pool decision per learner decision; N=1 exploiters have five. Different frozen weights require distinct forwards. CPU features, GPU context contention, cache misses and action transfers all remain. Sources: `docs/research/specs/lane_a_league_design_2026-10-17.md:71-97`; `docs/research/specs/actor_throughput_plan_2026-10-17.md:43-99,124-139`. The latter's ~340 ms CPU CNN batch at 768 rows argues against moving every BV8 opponent to CPU as the default.
- **Old advice is not a configuration authority.** The broad 8–16 concurrent policies/PBT suggestion and “nearly free” inference claim in `docs/research/advice/2026-10-17_new_lane.md:39-58` are narrowed by the measured learner ceiling and Lane A's serial design. Its no-PBT boundary is at `docs/research/specs/lane_a_league_design_2026-10-17.md:33-61`. The retrospective closes the old late-death reward/label/restart route; league gains must be demonstrated on the new opponent distribution, not promised as a cure for length-1,000 deaths (`docs/research/redesign_campaign_retrospective_2026-10-17.md:14-56`).

The relevant lesson from [AlphaStar](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-019-1724-z) is to retain diverse responses and expose weaknesses, not merely increase population count; its league showed substantial non-transitivity. [OpenAI Five](https://cdn.openai.com/dota-2.pdf) retained historical opponents alongside current self-play. Neither establishes a transferable win-rate threshold or convergence guarantee for six-player general-sum Snake. The thresholds here are local choices, and “beats pool” below means contextual full-horizon mass preference, not Nash exploitability.

### L4: frozen-pool competence and gross regression, on Mac

Predeclare **four ≤60-minute evaluation steps**: L4a and L4b separately for each replica. Combine only after all four finish. That is only a reference, not a forecast for this adapter or the current three-worker development cap. L0 must project each actual step below 50 minutes, leaving analysis/cleanup reserve; otherwise do not launch or truncate coverage.

**L4a, contextual pool probe:** per replica compare (M3M,B8), (M3M,M2M), and (C3M,B8). For each ordered pair use 16 fresh seed-pairs in each of two fixed four-opponent backgrounds: all B8 bare and all FRP+v8. Put the two compared policies in the remaining seats and swap seats in a matched second world. Full H5000 mass including dead frames defines a preference 1/0.5/0. One seed plus swap is one cluster; two contexts remain separate. This is 2 replicas ×3 comparisons ×16 pairs ×2 backgrounds ×2 orders =**384 episodes**. It tests a declared protected subset of the pool, not mastery of every one of 16 entries.

Use raw empirical preferences for this screen, not the smoothed training PFSP probabilities. Bootstrap matched seed-pair clusters 10,000 times with a fixed analysis seed, averaging replica effects equally and reporting each replica; at n=2 replicas these are **conditional development intervals**, not population-level inference. For the M-versus-C contrast, compare their paired preference scores against B8 on the same worlds, never direct training rewards.

**L4b, historical guard probe:** 16 fresh terminal-hero H5000 worlds per scripted/frozen/mixed roster, for M/C/B8/champion+v8: **192 episodes per replica, 384 total**. Record full-horizon mass, alive-frame fraction, no-death episode fraction and greedy behavior. No substituted all-candidate roster. With these small counts, guard estimates are screening alarms, not strict passes.
