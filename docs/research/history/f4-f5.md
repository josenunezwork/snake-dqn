# Frozen-B8 Residual Dose Study & Harm-Weighted Successor (F3 / F4 / F5, 2026-10-23 to 2026-10-26)

## Question and Goal

Following the discovery that exact `H300` rollout search could rescue late-game `b8-s3` self-trapping deaths (`T3-dense`), the `bl_residual` program (`F3` build $\to$ `F4` dose study $\to$ `F5` harm-targeted successor) asked whether fitting a zero-initialized `Linear(256, 6)` residual on cached 256-D `b8-s3` trunk features (`Adam` `fp32`, $\text{lr}=0.001$, `2,000` updates, seed `3403`, batch `256` = `128` doom + `128` ordinary rows) across nested rescue-bearing life doses (`D256` = `192` fit lives, `D1024` = `960` fit lives, `D4096` = `4,032` fit lives, plus a shared `64`-life fold-0 reserve) could increase life-macro rescue retention while maintaining zero natural-archive harm ($H = 0$) and beating bare `b8-s3` in paired `H5000` gameplay. After `F4` passed both retention bars at Look `R-A` (`+0.0662` mean gain, `LB90 = +0.0485`) but triggered terminal `STOP_FUNDING` on the `D1024` natural-archive harm screen ($H \ge 1$), a once-only post-mortem export and exploratory successor `F5` (`W`: $\times 4$ survival-pair weight on ordinary `B8`-survives vs alternative-dies pairs) were launched to test whether upweighting ordinary harm pairs on the frozen 960-life corpus could eliminate $H$ without losing rescue retention.

## What Was Learned

1. **Scaling rescue-life dose (`D256 -> D1024`) improves held-out rescue retention (`+6.62 pp`, `LB90 +4.85 pp`) but increases natural-archive action flips (`26 -> 48` of `560`) and confirmed exact deaths (`C: 1 -> 3`, `H: 1 -> 5`):** On frozen 256-D `B8` trunk features, pushing rescue-state margins harder inevitably perturbs nearby ordinary states where `B8` was already safe.
2. **Frozen 256-D `B8` trunk features ($\phi_{\text{B8}}$) do not linearly or non-linearly separate rescue states from ordinary states (`0/432` in Sandbox `B-3`, kNN ceiling `~0.15–0.17`):** No reweighting (`W`), KL penalty (`P`), or decoupled head on frozen $\phi_{\text{B8}}$ can achieve $\ge 70\%$ rescue retention at $\le 1\%$ ordinary flip rate; improving bare-network survival requires updating the spatial trunk (GPU-resident training) or running test-time rollout search.
3. **Long Colab timing/collection jobs require per-unit idempotent exports and primitive-accurate cost equations:** Treating a multi-action `rollout_all` bundle (`21.38 s`) as a per-action cost over-projected archive wall time by $\sim 5\text{–}6\times$ (`88.97 h`), and only per-unit immutable exports with SHA/completion sidecars (`f5_timing_resume`) survived `RPC_EXEC_POLL_TIMEOUT` interruptions without losing completed fixtures.

## Why It Closed

- **`F4`** closed on 2026-10-25 with terminal **`STOP_FUNDING`** at Look `R-A` because `D1024` failed the zero-harm gate ($H = 5 \ge 1$), sealing `D4096` as `NOT_RUN_BY_RULE` under rule `B6` ([JUDGMENT_RA_STOP_FUNDING_f9c08082_20261025.md](../../../research/bl_residual/ledger/F4_R_A_20261025/JUDGMENT_RA_STOP_FUNDING_f9c08082_20261025.md)).
- **`F5`** closed on 2026-10-26 with **`F5_CLOSED_NO_FIT`** by owner decision after A100 Sandbox `B`/`B-2`/`B-3` (`732` configurations total) demonstrated that frozen-`B8` feature residuals hit a hard `~4.5%` retention ceiling at $\le 1\%$ ordinary flip rate, redirecting campaign compute to Mac served search (`Claim B`) and GPU-resident full-network training (`Sandbox A`) ([F5_CLOSED_NO_FIT.md](../../../research/bl_residual/ledger/F5_CLOSURE_20261026/F5_CLOSED_NO_FIT.md)).
