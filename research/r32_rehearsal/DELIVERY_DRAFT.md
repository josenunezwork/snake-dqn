# R32 fixed-D4096 delivery contingency — draft only

**Status: DRAFT_ONLY — NOT A REGISTRATION, ADMISSION, DISPATCH, OR RELEASE DECISION.**

## Outcome and audience

This charter prepares exactly one possible future route: the actual final,
immutable F4 D4096 fused checkpoint versus bare B8 s3. It lets Claude assemble a
complete future packet for Astra if, and only if, the F4 registered result supplies
the qualifying `TOP_PASSED_DOSE_FAILED` flag. Astra must judge a new pre-data
registration that funds the fresh route. The charter grants no compute, Mac slot,
bank, command, serving action, or owner release approval.

The audience is the future registration author, Codex-V auditor, Claude operator,
and Astra judge. They need the F4 registration, the T34 success contract, and P7
statistics rules. This document neither reads nor changes F4 outcomes.

**R32-4 cap and kill rule.** The adopted preparation allowance is 0.5 Sol-day
plus 0.25 Codex-V-day, at most 0.125 Mac core-hour, 0 units, and no gameplay
allocation. Zero checkpoint substitutions and zero skipped required bars are
tolerated. An absent qualifying flag, any top-controller guard failure, or no
funded fresh-bank route closes the contingency.

## Required future ordering

The future registration must make the following order explicit. A later stage
cannot be treated as passed because F4 skipped it after a dose failure.

1. Bind the governing F4 disposition and immutable D4096 identity; preserve the
   terminal F4 label and its conditional Watch outcome.
2. Freeze selection, family, and attempt accounting. The family must include this
   one candidate only; no post-result candidate expansion, checkpoint search, or
   alpha recycling is allowed.
3. Decide whether a fresh development confirmation is necessary. If it is,
   allocate collision-audited paired worlds and price it prospectively. If it is
   not, explain why it is not required without treating F4 development evidence
   as strict evidence.
4. Run every outstanding product requirement on its own fresh, registered paired
   sessions. An F4 Watch6 skip is `NOT_RUN_BY_RULE`, not a product pass.
5. Run a separately registered fresh Mac strict gate on untouched strict banks.
6. Run Mac serving qualification for the delivered bare candidate, including the
   success-contract scope and paced timing requirements.
7. Only after all registered evidence and independent review may a release
   recommendation be considered; the owner alone may approve a release.

No automatic replacement two-arm screen follows. The illustrative 128 worlds per
mix, two-arm arithmetic is not a chosen design or strict-gate size; the future
registration selects N and any development confirmation from prospective evidence.

## Required bars in the future registration

The registration must state its exact inferential details before data and retain
the campaign's then-binding rules. At minimum it must include:

- paired-world, mix-stratified fixed-controller inference versus B8 s3 with equal
  mix weight; P7's minimum 16 worlds/mix is a floor, not selected power;
- full-horizon H5000 mass gain of at least +40 with one-sided LB90 > 0;
- survival guard A (survival band v2) and survival guard B (scripted no-death
  gain at least +0.03), each versus B8 s3;
- delivered boost share in `[0.5%, 10%]`;
- fresh paired continuous-respawn Watch6 product sessions with the contract's
  paired 95% death and mass requirements;
- a fresh Mac strict gate on untouched strict banks; and
- Mac serving qualification: Watch6 scope, preservation of Watch8/Watch12 and
  Play, timing/cadence/client-latency requirements, rollback identity, and the
  owner's release approval boundary.

The registration must name decision-label precedence, missing-unit behavior,
source/checkpoint/bank hashes, collision audit, and a prospective multiplicity
plan. No result in this draft supplies those missing registration details.

`delivery.py` encodes the common ordered subset of this table as
`classify(registered_flag, top_guards_pass, funded_registration,
checkpoint_matches, product_complete, strict_pass, serving_pass,
owner_release)`. It is pure and draft-only: it reads no artifacts and cannot
dispatch, admit, pin, serve, or release a candidate. The registration still owns
the complete decision analyzer and all source-identity validation.

Every classifier control must be a built-in boolean; `"false"`, `0`, missing
evidence, and a truthy object are rejected with `TypeError` rather than silently
coerced. In particular, `product_complete=True` means that every required fresh
product bar is both complete **and passed**; a completed product run that fails a
bar remains `False`.
