# Early Solo & Ambient-Food PQN Series (2026-09-13 to 2026-09-21)

## Question and Goal

Following the failure of six-snake `raster31v3` PQN runs to acquire reliable foraging—even when half of the hero's $\epsilon$-greedy exploratory steps were replaced by `scripted:greedy_food`—this lineage stripped away multi-agent confounds by building an opt-in single-snake (`S1`) lifecycle and source-aware evaluation harness (`sole_snake_death_or_frame_cap_v1`, `solo-watch-diagnostic-v1`). Its goal was to determine whether native PQN (`RasterDuelingNetwork` on `raster31v3`) can learn reliable ambient-food foraging and survival from scratch in a solo arena, isolate why initial solo food-contact gains failed to replicate across seeds, and trace the subsequent solo-food progression through behavior-cloning warm starts, long-horizon solo survival (`H128`–`H768`), one-opponent transfer, and Mac M5 Pro PQN throughput sizing.

## Final Verdict and Key Numbers

The early solo and ambient-food PQN line closed without promoting a checkpoint ([SOLO_FOOD_SERIES.md](../../experiments/SOLO_FOOD_SERIES.md)). Key quantitative results and their primary artifact/kept sources:

- **Boost-trail self-eating artifact (`solo_food_survival` & `solo_food_source_trace`):** In the first 50k-step `S1` run on worlds `2026091611–18`, seed `2026091601` increased total food contacts from `2.500` to `1,100.875` (mean mass `1.201625 -> 1.119000`, peak length `11.625 -> 10.750`) while seed `2026091602` rose from `0.125` to `14.375` (mean mass `1.005375 -> 1.976500`, peak length `10.125 -> 24.375`) ([SOLO_FOOD_SERIES.md](../../experiments/SOLO_FOOD_SERIES.md)). ## What Was Learned

1. **Unattributed food counts in `S1` were dominated by boost-trail self-eating:** Without source attribution (`ambient` vs `own_boost_trail`), a snake spinning in boost circles can register $>1,100$ contacts/episode by re-consuming its own shed tail while losing net mass. Source-aware ambient food accounting is mandatory.
2. **Cold-start PQN foraging failure is an action-advantage lock-in problem, not sensory blindness or rollout starvation:** Even zero-food seeds (`1901`) experience $\sim 14\text{–}15$ random ambient food contacts per 1k training steps and generate positive one-step TD residuals on adjacent food, yet small food-induced $Q$ shifts fail to flip the argmax of a locked-in turn/circle policy.
3. **Supervised teacher warm-starts solve solo foraging through `H768`, but neither solo PQN tweaks nor 1-opponent transfer yield seed-uniform paired gains:** While BC warm-started PQN (`CW`) and shared supervised safety heads (`BO`) reach strong solo food and survival, every multi-seed paired RL improvement rule failed in at least one seed, and introducing a single opponent shifted mortality to head-on/body collisions (`70/76` deaths) that solo policies could not handle.

## Why It Closed

The lineage closed at the **2026-09-21 research stopping point** ([SOLO_FOOD_SERIES.md](../../experiments/SOLO_FOOD_SERIES.md)) after ~95 solo-food studies established that (a) solo foraging and self-avoidance were solvable via supervised imitation and anchored warm-starts, (b) unanchored cold-start PQN tweaks (`eps_end`, $\lambda$, loss shape, reward coefficient) were seed-fragile, and (c) solo policies failed paired retention as soon as a single opponent was introduced—motivating the October pivot to cell-exact `GridBatchSim`, `ego2s` rasters, and distilled multi-snake DQN (`B8`).
