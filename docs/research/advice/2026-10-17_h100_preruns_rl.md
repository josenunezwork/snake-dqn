# H100 preruns: learning recipe and experimental design — 2026-10-17

**Recommendation:** qualify one B8-initialized, fp32, bs2 learning recipe before buying a long run. Screen bf16 next; screen bs4/sqrt only if H100 measurements make it economically plausible. Select the smallest actor count that supplies the learner without degrading freshness. A fast all-six-learner smoke is infrastructure evidence, not evidence for a three-learner/frozen-opponent league. The decisive learning screen is five independent fine-tunes at 8M transitions, followed by paired fresh-world evaluation against released B8. A 2M screen can reject gross instability; it cannot reliably select the eventual winner.

It is stronger in mass than the former served agent at equal survival, not established safer. Its Tier 2 margin was only 0.034 at SE 0.023 (`docs/research/b8_s3_release_2026-10-17.md:3-25,30-40,54-59`). Current mixed Watch is B8 hero versus FRP+v8; the release's older all-B8-default wording is superseded (`docs/STATUS.md:5-39`).
- Smoke-03 isolated A100 fp32 bs2 reached ~78 updates/s; full-run a4 reached 62.2. Bf16 reached 117 alone but 47.6 in-run; bs4 delivered 30.5 updates/s at 512 transitions/update. The final results append supersedes the earlier host-only diagnosis. The comparator still marked rows invalid because lag was not lifted into the result row, so import and audit original receipts before using them in a launch projection (`docs/research/t16_colab_smoke02_results_2026-10-17.md:170-200`). The actor plan uses different measured windows (~15.46k rather than 15.93k a4 transitions/s); do not average those windows into a new estimate (`docs/research/specs/actor_throughput_plan_2026-10-17.md:28-41,65-88`).
- Learning-curve history directly cautions against short-run ranking. LABELS scripted survival changed sign at 6M and returned negative at 8M; CTRL4M was 57.33 mass and 0.14375 episode survival below CTRL8M. These are five-seed, 32-world comparisons, not a measured correlation with 20M league outcomes (`docs/research/t4_update_every_and_curve_2026-10-15.md:36-54,74-101`). No validated short-probe predictor of beating B8 at 20M is established here.
- Reward shaping, Huber, rollout labels, return labels and restart exposure did not solve the late-death problem. Do not spend this screen budget reopening them, adding recurrence/R2D2, changing replay capacity, or running a broad learning-rate search (`docs/research/redesign_campaign_retrospective_2026-10-17.md:14-37,41-56`). The new mechanism to test is opponent-distribution exposure, with recipe/numerics qualification around it.
- Mixed N=3 learner/frozen-pool training and BV8 opponents in arbitrary evaluation slots are not implemented. The current switches are hero-only or `--all-snakes-learner`; Lane A's five replicas, serial exploiters and PFSP remain proposals (`docs/research/specs/lane_a_league_design_2026-10-17.md:3-5,29-48,63-87,140-148,233-245`). Experience-replay ratio is an algorithmic variable, not merely a utilization knob; see the primary [Fedus et al. replay study](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/fedus20a.html). Few-run RL conclusions need uncertainty and replication; see [Agarwal et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.13264). Those papers motivate controls; none validates the local margins or predicts this game's winner.

## 4. Rank 1: cheap probes that actually inform learning

### Saved-state numeric sentinel (necessary, not sufficient)

Use a SHA-bound development corpus of **4,096 real B8 observations/transitions**, stratified by short/long body, crowded/open, boost availability and terminal/trapped cases. It must exclude strict worlds and label-training banks used for measuring held-out retention. Capture legal masks, B8 fp32 Q, TD targets and frozen anchor labels; exact Q ties are a separate stratum. Use the same sample order, target updates, RNG and initial weights for 200 fp32/bf16 optimizer steps. Existing synthetic drift is a plumbing check; it does not supply this real-state corpus (`research/perf_learner_20261013/bench_learner.py:1-39,625-665`).

Proposed R1 numeric GO: no nonfinite parameters/targets/loss, no skipped/failed optimizer updates; fixed-input p99 Q error normalized by `1+max(abs(Q_fp32))` ≤0.01; masked-greedy disagreement ≤1% overall and zero on states whose fp32 top-two gap exceeds twice the largest actionwise Q error for that state. Separate forward-only error from the 200-step trajectory divergence; apply the error/disagreement thresholds at both checkpoints. Report exact/near ties and reference fp32 repeat drift. These are conservative screening tolerances, not bit-identity or scientific noninferiority. Failure keeps fp32; do not relax a threshold after inspecting errors.

For all arms, at initialization and 0.5M/2M save held-out masked action agreement with B8, decisive v8 agreement, Q scale, TD residual distribution, anchor loss, gradient norm/clipping frequency, parameter displacement and greedy boost by length. The current BV8 path does **not** compute the legacy `holdout_agreement` metric (`train_m3c.py:3606-3609`), so add an offline held-out BV8 probe. Training anchor agreement is not held-out evidence, and a decreasing TD loss can reflect changing targets or a collapsing policy.

Proposed early KILL: nonfinite update; masked action invalidity; on the fixed corpus p99 |Q| >5× its own B8 baseline (floor denominator at 1); or >5 percentage-point loss of decisive v8-label agreement at **both** 0.5M and 2M. The Q threshold is a new hard screen rule: current trainer code only appends a Q flag (`train_m3c.py:3610-3615`). Treat one label-agreement breach as diagnostic until the second planned point; never stop on ordinary fluctuating reward. Include Lane A's proposed tripwires explicitly: >90% one-action share over a full 100k action window; greedy boost >2% overall or >1% below length 700 per 100k telemetry window; and, after 2M, greedy boost <0.05% for three consecutive sufficiently supported windows (at least 1,000 boost-eligible decisions/window). Preserve their all-decision versus eligible-decision denominators; insufficient support is not zero. The low-boost floor needs added support-count instrumentation. Keep these rules separate from the offline checks (`docs/research/specs/lane_a_league_design_2026-10-17.md:183-196`). No 2M GO authorizes a long run. All clean arms continue to their preregistered 8M endpoint.

### Fixed-world gameplay is the strongest available short probe

At 2M, 4M and 8M, evaluate the **same declared checkpoints**, never the best-looking intermediate, with terminal hero H5000 and fresh paired worlds. Only 8M decides efficacy; earlier points describe whether mass and survival recover after the warm start. Primary is full-horizon mass integral, dead frames zero. Report alive-frame fraction and no-death episode survival separately. Use fixed opponents and replace only the hero, so the candidate cannot appear improved merely because every opponent changed with it. Historical T4 curves support this design, not a promise that it predicts 20M (`t4_update_every_and_curve_2026-10-15.md:39-54,78-101`).

Prefer persistent mass gain against B8 with preserved survival, across all mixes and most training seeds, to lower TD loss, high v8 agreement, higher kill count, or short-horizon win rate. Record 8M minus 4M effects but do not require monotonicity: the history already rejects that assumption. H10000 with H5000 prefix is a useful later endurance diagnostic, not a cheap replacement for H5000 replication. Strict H10000 evaluation already exists in T7 (`research/redesign_t7_phase_r_20261016/PREREGISTRATION_T7.md:63-68`); a new short-screen wrapper must bind its own profiles and worlds rather than invoke T7's frozen producer.

## 6. Ranks 3–5: replication, thresholds and power

### Five replicas, two different comparisons

Allocate five fresh training-seed IDs **after a collision audit**, not convenient reuse of 0–4 or 10–14. Pair recipe arms by seed ID, B8 initializer, initial opponent pool and evaluation bank; retain separate RNG/replay/optimizer states for each arm. This estimates fine-tuning variability conditional on the one B8 initializer. Five actor processes, six snakes, five checkpoints from one run, or five PFSP descendants are not five independent replicas (`lane_a_league_design_2026-10-17.md:29-40,175-177`).

Use three exploratory seeds only for sentinel failures or profiling. They cannot select a numerically different long-run recipe. Before observing any selection results, declare five seeds for each arm that reaches gameplay screening. For an affordable reduced **recipe** screen, use per replica **64 scripted / 32 frozen / 32 mixed H5000 worlds**, distinct between replicas, paired across R0/R1/R2/B8. This is 640 worlds/hero and 1,920 episodes for R0/R1/B8; an additional R2 adds 640 episodes. Shared B8/R0 outputs may be reused only within this single predeclared factorial screen and identical banks. Add matching historical FRP/champion hero records if assessing their guards; do not substitute B8 for those references.

This reduced screen is not Lane A L2: Lane A specifies 96/32/32 historical worlds plus 64 product worlds per roster, five M/C pairs and its own product thresholds. If adaptive league is the long-run intervention, run that **exact separate L2** and its matched static-pool control before promoting the league mechanism. The R0/R1/R2 test chooses its learning implementation, not permission to waive L2 (`lane_a_league_design_2026-10-17.md:133-144,173-188`).

### Proposed fixed decision rules for recipe screen

For each seed average the three mix means equally, regardless of world counts. Define `G` as candidate−B8 mass, `D` as alternate recipe−R0 mass. Compute mean and one-sided 90% Student-t limits across the **five seed effects**, df=4; retain paired-world variances and a hierarchical paired-bootstrap sensitivity. These are development-screen intervals, not strict HK results or corrected familywise confidence. All point survival guards below are screening guards, not proofs of noninferiority.

1. **INVALID/INCOMPLETE:** missing rows/hash/seed identity, cap expiry, unclean process exit or sample-count mismatch. No scientific GO/KILL label for infrastructure failures. At most the governance-permitted mechanical recovery on the same intent; never automatically rerun a poor outcome.
2. **KILL:** any registered numerical/behavioral tripwire; or complete 8M data have pooled alive-frame difference vs B8 below −.03 in any mix, any seed scripted difference below −.15, or pooled no-death episode survival below .50 in any mix. For R0, also KILL long-run efficacy if upper90(G)≤0. For R1/R2, KILL the arm if upper90(D)<−10 mass units. These decisions reject investment, not prove a universal mechanism failure.
3. **GO R0** for long-run planning only if lower90(G)>0, mean G≥20, scripted mass point≥0, ≥4/5 seed mass effects >0, and all guards pass. These echo the proposed B8-comparator strict target without conferring strict status (`lane_a_league_design_2026-10-17.md:152-165`). A static-roster GO does not qualify PFSP.
4. **GO R1/R2** only if R0 has GO, the candidate also meets the R0-vs-B8 GO conditions, lower90(D)>−10, and the candidate's alive-frame and episode-survival point differences vs R0 are each ≥−.02 on every mix. Also require its rank-2 end-to-end speed gain (15%/20%). The 10-mass and 2-point margins are proposed economic screening tolerances, not acceptable strict regressions. Report every seed. If both alternatives qualify, prefer R1 because it preserves optimizer cadence; select R2 only if D/s is at least 10% above qualifying R1. Do not combine the arms.
5. **EXTEND** once if complete valid data satisfy neither KILL nor GO: freeze checkpoints and add 64/32/32 new worlds/seed to the same mixes; recompute combined results under unchanged rules. This addresses world noise only. If seed heterogeneity dominates, or it still does not pass, report INCONCLUSIVE and retain the already-qualified R0; if R0 is inconclusive, defer the long run. No extra training, third look, best-checkpoint swap or seed replacement. Intervals after the adaptive extension remain descriptive; strict confirmation must use fresh worlds.

This is deliberately conservative about spending hundreds of Colab units: failure to establish an advantage can defer the run even when improvement remains plausible. Ordinary 2M/4M mass/survival fluctuations cannot invoke the 8M rules early.

### Honest power arithmetic

For paired mass SD `s` across independent world pairs, a one-sided 10% test with 80% power needs approximately `n = (1.282 + .842)^2 * (s/delta)^2 = 4.51*(s/delta)^2`, before small-sample t and between-training-seed variance. Thus **illustrative**, not measured B8, SDs 100 and 200 with a 20-mass effect need about 113 and 451 world pairs respectively. Thirty-two pairs detect effects of roughly .375s, not small improvements. Use observed development paired variances to size strict evaluation; never reuse final worlds to estimate power.

Across five training-seed effects, lower90 is `mean − 1.533*s_seed/sqrt(5)`; its half-width is .686*s_seed. A rough 80%-power threshold is 1.06*s_seed (optimistic small-sample approximation). If seed SD is 20 mass, even five seeds struggle with a 20-mass effect; adding worlds does not eliminate that variance floor. With three seeds the analogous rough effect is 1.57*s_seed. More seeds are preferable to ever more worlds when between-seed variance dominates, but adding seeds after seeing a borderline result requires a new independent registration.

For paired episode survival let q be the fraction of candidate/reference pairs with discordant alive/dead endpoints. Approximate variance is `(q − delta^2)/n`. If q=.20, detecting a .05 difference at the same alpha/power needs about **361** pairs (using q conservatively); at n=32 the rough detectable difference is .168. Repeated frames from one terminal episode do not increase n. An exact deterministic A/A comparison has zero paired difference and does **not** estimate candidate−B8 variance. Use historical paired development deltas as planning inputs, with sensitivity ranges, or a new non-authoritative candidate development pilot. Distinguish implementation A/A identity from statistical power.

Before 20M, report a power feasibility table for mass, alive-frame and episode survival separately, by mix and training seed. Do not multiply all frames/seats/worlds together to claim narrow CIs. The narrow B8 Tier 2 release margin and T7's 192 scripted worlds/seed are evidence that survival precision is not free (`b8_s3_release_2026-10-17.md:54-59`; `research/redesign_t7_phase_r_20261016/PREREGISTRATION_T7.md:39-59,94-100`). No ranking of five short runs guarantees success; retain an independent long-run and strict gate.
