## Plan and placeholder

Plan: `research/redesign_t4_20261015/plans/t4_pod_plan.json`.
Five 8M fine-tunes pair seeds 10–14 with B8_s0–4 finals. All five checkpoints and
**every file** in the derived prefer-nonboost v2 directory (the shard and manifest)
are SHA-256 pinned in `extra_inputs`. Both bank files map beneath `/r/in/t4-bank-v2`.
The original bank and the derived files were not modified.

The recipe retains M6 CTRL's mass reward, fixed gamma 0.998, final epsilon,
25% mixed opponents, v8margin anchor, bv8 observations, numba, fast learner,
batch scale 2, packed observations, 128 worlds and 2M checkpoint interval.
Huber delta is explicitly 1.0. The final contiguous block of each `extra` is:

```text
--label-bank /r/in/t4-bank-v2 --label-bank-frac 0.5
--onpolicy-labels --label-frac 0.5
--anchor-hold 0 --anchor-floor-at 1 --anchor-floor 0.1
```

`placeholder_arm_flags` documents that block; the runner consumes each run's
`extra`, so replace all five blocks together after T3 reports. These flags are
PLACEHOLDERS, not a selected or preregistered T4 arm. See the
[T3 pre-registration](specs/t3_screen_prereg_2026-10-15.md) and
[T4 bank tooling](t4_prep_tooling_2026-10-15.md).

One RTX 4090 is pinned by the runner's pod body, with no fallback option in the
commands below. Five runs start together. Minimum host: 32 vCPU, 64 GB RAM;
4.3 vCPU and 9 GB RAM per run, plus the runner's 8 GB reserve. This allows the
M6 packed baseline (~6–7 GB), third actor, bank and loading headroom. The bank's
resident arrays total 203,548,709 bytes (~0.204 GB) per learner, with transient
loading copies. VRAM allowance is 2.7 GB/run (13.5 GB total); packed rings are
2.6 GB/run. Image is torch 2.5.1 CUDA 12.4 devel, matching M6; neither learner nor
actors use compile. The 30,000-transition pod preflight remains configured but
was not executed. The five-run cap prevents adaptive overfill.

## Wall time, cost and 4× verdict

Source: [M6 plan](../../research/redesign_m6_20261014/plans/m6_pod_plan.json),
its attempt-3 notes: ~4.1k transitions/s/run, actor-bound, combined 24.6k/s before
CUDA-graph OOM failures; compile bought no throughput. The final M6 plan removed
compile. [M6 result](m6_result_2026-10-14.md) records failed pod attempts and Mac
fallback. These are historical projections, not a measured five-run T4 wave.

| Projection | Time / cost |
|---|---:|
| 8M / 4,100 per run | 32.52 min |
| Five concurrent runs on one pod, compute only | 32.52 min |
| Illustrative setup/preflight/transfers allowance (unmeasured) | 10–20 min |
| Whole pod with that allowance | 42.52–52.52 min |
| Compute-only at $0.89/h; at $0.95/h ceiling | $0.48; $0.52 |
| Whole pod with allowance at $0.95/h ceiling | $0.67–$0.83 |
| Hard two-hour lifetime at $0.95/h ceiling | $1.90 |

The allowance is a scenario, not measured overhead; 2.28 GB of proposed inputs,
stock waits, slow hosts and T4 bank loss can add time. Capacity polling adds
elapsed time even when it costs nothing. No retries or launch were performed.
The $10 step budget is a ceiling, not permission to spend.

Mac baseline from the spec: 27–43 minutes/run in **two lanes**, so five runs take
three waves, **81–129 minutes**. Against 32.52 pod minutes before overhead,
speed-up is only **2.49–3.97×**; with the illustrative overhead it is **1.54–3.03×**.
Even the optimistic 24.6k/s aggregate extrapolation (27.10 compute minutes for
40M) does not establish 4× once two-lane Mac timing and operational overhead are
accounted for. The old M6 4.55× aggregate comparison to one Mac run is not this
step's speed-up.

The plan conservatively sets `mac_rate = 2 × 8M / (27 × 60) = 9,876.54/s` and
`g2_factor = 4`: runner G2 requires 39,506.17/s aggregate and a whole-pod budget
of **1,012.5 s (16.875 min)**. Its continuous-work accounting is stricter than the
three-wave 81-minute Mac schedule (whose 4× budget is 20.25 minutes). Both fail
the measured-rate projection. G2 runs about 30 minutes after training starts;
it cannot rescue an unsupported prelaunch speed claim for this small batch.
The runner checks completion before G2, so a queue finishing before that check
can complete without a runtime G2 verdict; prelaunch policy review is necessary.
**Do not launch under the current 4× rule without new qualifying evidence or an
explicit step-specific waiver, plus spending/upload authorization.**
