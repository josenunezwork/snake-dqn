## Outside-counter matching

The separate part is
`/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/t4c-data-20261016/outside-bank`.
Its inputs are CTRL training seeds 10–14 and their same-init B8 finals (B8 seeds 0–4),
replayed on the saved 96 scripted round-62 probe worlds. CTRL parity is checked against
its saved probe receipt by the diagnostic. A B8 replay has no saved B8 round-62 source row;
its own receipt therefore claims only internal exact mass/alive-frame/world-set accounting,
never saved-row parity. Both use the same saved CTRL roster, rowwise inference, advisory
mask, no veto, and the H5000 Grid evaluator.

Matching uses length buckets 700–999 and ≥1000 and **raw B8 −max Q** on both the stored
decisive rows and probe observations. Nine cutpoints are computed once from all 10,885
stored decisive B8 scores. Ties enter the upper decile. This data-matching score is separate
from Part A's explicitly CTRL-based score and cutpoints. The final bank must match every
one of the 20 decisive length × B8-decile counts exactly, giving 10,885 outside rows.
No unsupported-stratum extrapolation or replacement is permitted.

Outside labels are B8's own advisory-mask argmax with the existing normal-action fallback
if no advisory action is available. Their surviving label is a singleton containing that
action, `weight=1`, `row_weight=0.5`, and `row_family=2`. The launch arm uses its own margin
scale 1 × 0.0632, independently of the primary bank's 4.75 × 0.0632 margin.

The collector counts decisions separately for each living hero. The terminal evaluation
never respawns heroes. For observed deaths, eligibility requires `death_decision - decision
>=300`; this excludes the final 300 decisions before the hero death. For right-censored
survivors, the final 300 decisions of the H5000 trajectory are excluded to retain at least
300 decisions of observed future. This is a statement about observed continuation only;
it makes no assertion about deaths beyond the replay horizon. “Any death” here refers to
any terminal death in that hero's observed trajectory, not deaths of unrelated opponent
snakes. The replay checks that no hero returns from death.

Canonical exact-observation fingerprints exclude every v2 bank observation and deduplicate
all selected outside rows across sources. Bounded seeded per-stratum reservoirs retain
packed candidate observations, rather than storing every replay raster. Final seeded
sampling is without replacement. Candidate payloads, replay inputs, their receipts, and
the final shard have SHA-256 bindings in the final manifest. Changed source artifacts,
missing rows or insufficient stratum support fail preparation without publishing a final
manifest. Existing nonempty outputs are refused; resume reuses completed immutable parts
and constructs any new part at a fresh path.
