## Purpose

Redesign milestone 2 (2026-10-08): distil the frp3-s12 (+v8 veto) teacher into an ego-raster
("ego2s") student network via DAgger, then test with a pre-registered non-inferiority (NI) check
whether the student **without veto** is non-inferior to frp3-s12 **without veto** on dev H5000 mass
(margin −15, pooled one-sided 90% Student-t LB, 48 worlds × 3 mixes = 144 paired worlds).
Development check, not gate evidence.

* **M2** (`ego2s` draft observation, data rounds 0–2) — [PREREGISTRATION.md](../../../research/redesign_m2_20261008/PREREGISTRATION.md).
* **M2b** (`ego2s-b` observation closing the teacher-feature gap, data rounds 9–12, fresh NI worlds) —
  [PREREGISTRATION_M2B.md](../../../research/redesign_m2_20261008/PREREGISTRATION_M2B.md). Owner decision "Option 1".
* Plus pre-M3 follow-ups on the M2b student: serving latency (item 1) and boost under-use diagnosis (item 3).
