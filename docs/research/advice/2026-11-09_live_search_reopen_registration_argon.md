### 1.4 Engineering Justification of Each Threshold from Repository Evidence
1. **Why main-thread synchronous search is capped at `~3.9 ms` (`dto_split` / worker offload required):**
   - In T15 qualification ([t15_mixed_roster_qualification_2026-10-17.md](file:///Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn/docs/research/t15_mixed_roster_qualification_2026-10-17.md#L103-L116)), bare mixed-Watch6 `GameState.update` `p95 = 7.792 ms` (`p50 = 6.027 ms`), and the governing bar ([qualify.py](file:///Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn/research/watch_replay/qualify.py#L73)) is `BAR_UPDATE_P95_MS = 11.688 ms` (`1.5 × 7.792 ms`). Thus search **must** run off the main thread in `W <= 4` worker processes ([SUCCESS_CONTRACT.md](file:///Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn/docs/research/final_run_20261019/SUCCESS_CONTRACT.md#L119-L123) §4 Amendment), where the per-frame wall budget is `BAR_PACED_BUDGET_MS = 1000 / 24 = 41.67 ms`.
2. **Why single-core step speedup is bounded at `~1.4–1.5×` (citing the `v6` kernel result and its retired `0.22 ms` bar):**
   - Stock native-`v8` rollout steps take **`1.892–2.105 ms/step`** ([ROADMAP.md](file:///Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn/docs/research/ROADMAP.md#L58-L59)).
   - The `v6` fast kernel (`argon-v6-kernel-1103` @ `32b638a2`, [CAMPAIGN_LOG.md](file:///Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn/docs/research/final_run_20261019/CAMPAIGN_LOG.md#L2130)) accelerated the `v8` flood kernel `2.7–3.3×` (`0.40 -> 0.11–0.15 ms/step`), yet full $E = 1$ step time fell only **`1.18×` (`1.98 -> 1.65–1.74 ms/step`)** because `ego_raster` non-`v8` (`~0.8 ms`), neural forwards (`~0.5 ms`), and `GridBatchSim` (`~0.2 ms`) dominate. That is why the `0.22 ms/step` bar was retired on `2026-11-09` ([STATUS.md](file:///Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn/docs/STATUS.md#L174)).
   -Fusing the 5 opponent `vector61` forwards into one `(5, 61)` batch + pre-allocated `v8` buffers + omitting `session.live.snapshot()` (`capture_obs=False` as in [`open_stop.py`](file:///Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn/research/watch_replay/open_stop.py#L161)) can plausibly yield **`~1.4–1.5×`** total single-core step speedup (`~1.25–1.35 ms/step`), never `5–10×`.
3. **Why `W <= 4` parallelism + multi-frame amortization yields at most `~2.0–2.2×` effective latency reduction without stale-state collapse (`3.0×` = `125.0 ms` threshold):**
   - On `92.1–94.3%` of `exact-stop-l1` roots (`1.057–1.079` branches/root) and `99.4%` of `open-stop-h32-nb` roots (`0.61%` root override rate), Branch 1 (`bare`) certifies on the first pass, which is a **serial** rollout of $H$ steps that cannot be split across cores.
   - Closeout Part B Regime 2 ([REPORT.md](file:///Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/t34-sol/argon-closeout-audit/REPORT.md#L100-L102)) proved that a **3-tick (`125 ms`) async delay** from root capture $w_{t-3}$ to action delivery at $t$ loses **`87.1–93.5%` of rescues** because the live world diverges over 3 ticks.
   - However, a **1-tick speculative pre-start** — where Worker 0 starts rolling out from the predicted next state $\hat{w}_{t+1} = f(w_t, a_{\text{bare}, t})$ at the start of frame $t$ while Workers `1..3` evaluate alternate branches $a \neq a_{\text{bare}, t+1}$ in parallel — provides a **2-frame (`83.33 ms`) causal execution window** with `0` stale-world divergence whenever frame $t$ executes $a_{\text{bare}, t}$ (`> 99%` of ticks).
   - Combining `~1.5×` step speedup with `2.0×` speculative 2-frame pipelining + `W = 4` branch parallelism yields **`1.5 × 2.0 = 3.0×` (`125.0 ms` at `W = 1`)** for `p95` and **`2.0×` (`83.33 ms`)** for the mean.
4. **Why `> 10.0×` (`> 416.7 ms` `p95` or `> 250.0 ms` `mean`) is impossible for live engineering (Branch (b)):**
   - Even if all `W = 4` worker processes ran at `100%` parallel efficiency (`4.0×`) with a `1.5×` faster step (`4.0 × 1.5 = 6.0×` maximum theoretical throughput multiplier), a `W = 1` mean cost $> 250.0\text{ ms/tick}$ ($> 6.0 \times 41.67\text{ ms}$) or `p95` $> 416.7\text{ ms}$ ($> 10.0 \times 41.67\text{ ms}$) exceeds total 4-worker capacity at `24 fps`. Similarly, if `open-stop-h32-nb` alone has `p95 > 125.0 ms`, even the 32-step every-tick shield cannot finish within the 2-frame (`83.33 ms`) speculative window after `1.5×` step optimization.

### 1.5 Gray-Zone Resolution Rule (`M0-Gray-Split`, `0 u`, `<= 15 min` Mac)
Because `exact-stop-l1-os` runs $H = 32$ `open-stop` on `80%` of ticks (`~60 ms` expected at `W = 1`) and $H = \min(L, 1200)$ `exact-stop` on `20%` of ticks (`~1.5–2.5 s` expected at $L \ge 800$, `W = 1`), the measurement may land in **Branch (c)** where `S_os,p95 <= 125.0 ms` (`open-stop` fits) but the `20%` scaled-$H$ check ticks push $S_{\text{p95}}$ into `(125.0, 416.7] ms` or via-check-tick spikes.
- **One Extra Measurement (`M0-Gray-Split`):** On `32` cached $L \ge 800$ roots from fresh dev namespace `t34-live-l1os-dev-20261109-v1` plus the `24` product-bank death roots from `t34-sol/bank-v2-census/census.json` (`watch-replay-bank-live-132-l1` product files, `0` sealed exports touched), test a **4-frame amortized check-tick controller (`W = 4`, `K = 8`)**:
  - Workers `0..1` run 1-frame-speculative `open-stop-h32-nb` ($H = 32$) on every tick.
  - Workers `2..3` run the every-5th-tick check search truncated to **$H_{\text{check}} = 160$ with terminal [`evaluate_terminal_openness`](file:///Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn/research/watch_replay/open_stop.py#L97)** (`v8_best_safe_area >= 160` and `max_pocket_r >= L`), amortized across the `4` inter-check ticks (`40` rollout steps/frame $\approx 54\text{ ms}$ total over `4` frames = `13.5 ms/frame`) by advancing along the realized hero trajectory (`RH-DAG` suffix check; aborting if the live trajectory deviates from the checked prefix).
- **Fixed Resolution Rule:**
  - Route to **Branch (a)** iff: (1) per-frame worker completion `p95 <= 41.67 ms` across all ticks at $L \ge 800$ (`>= 95%` on-time delivery), **AND** (2) $H_{\text{check}} = 160$ + terminal openness matches or rescues **`>= 20 / 24` (`83.3%`)** of the Bank-v2 census death roots.
  - Otherwise, route immediately to **Branch (b)**.
