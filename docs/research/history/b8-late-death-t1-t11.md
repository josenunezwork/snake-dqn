# B8 Late-Game Self-Trapping Diagnosis & T1–T11 / T12–T13b Series (2026-10-14 to 2026-10-17)

## Question and Goal

In the M4 Phase R gate, `B8` (`Ego2sNet` on `ego2s-bv8` without a runtime veto) beat the served `frp3-s12 + v8` incumbent by $+128.7$ `H5000` mass (`LB +101.5`) and passed Tier 1 scripted survival (`-0.003 >= -0.03`), yet failed the single-seed Tier 2 scripted survival guard (`c6_guard_scripted_survival`) against `champion_a5 + v8` on seed 0 (`-0.156` vs `-0.05` floor). This lineage investigated why `B8` suffered elevated late-game self-collisions at length $L \ge 800$, whether exact rollout teachers (`T3`), learner/loss/margin/return modifications (`T2`, `T3`, `T4a`–`T4f`, `T6`), or static and dynamic geometric/certificate inputs (`T5`, `T5b`, `T8`, `T9`, `T10`, `T11`) could distill that survival headroom into a single bare network, and how to qualify `b8-s3` for serving (`T7`, `T12`–`T13b`) under a statistically well-powered survival guard (`survival_band_v2`).

## Final Verdict and Key Numbers

1. **`B8` Death Census & Two-Expert Panel (`PANEL_DECISION_MEMO.md`, `research/b8_death_census_20261014/README.md`):**
   - Across 160 scripted `H5000` episodes (`5` seeds $\times 32$ worlds), `B8` recorded `75` deaths (`0.531` episode survival, `0.818` alive-frame fraction, `570.7` mean mass) vs `48` deaths (`0.700` survival, `0.903` alive-frame, `197.4` mass) for `champion_a5+v8` and `67` deaths (`0.581` survival, `0.831` alive-frame, `437.4` mass) for `frp3-s12+v8` ([PANEL_DECISION_MEMO.md](../panel_2026-10-15/PANEL_DECISION_MEMO.md)).
   - **Late-length exposure confound:** `49/75` (`65.3%`) of `B8`'s deaths occurred at $L \ge 800$, where `B8` spent **`42.0%`** of its `659,595` alive decisions (`277k` frames, hazard `0.177` deaths/1k decisions) compared to **`5.6%`** (`40k` frames, `0` deaths) for `champion_a5+v8`. At $L < 800$, `B8`'s hazard (`0.068/1k`) matched `champion_a5+v8` (`0.067/1k`). Of `B8`'s 75 deaths, `67` (`89.3%`) were self-collisions and `64/75` (`85.3%`) died inside an enclosure where all 3 directions had `v8 < 1.0` (median trap onset `31` decisions before death; `41/64` already unavoidable 1 step after onset) ([PANEL_DECISION_MEMO.md](../panel_2026-10-15/PANEL_DECISION_MEMO.md)).
   - **Statistical reform (`survival_band_v2`):** Because a 32-world per-seed difference had $\text{SE} \approx 0.075$ on alive-frame fraction (`~22%` per-seed false-positive failure rate at zero true delta, `~71%` across 5 seeds), the panel ratified `survival_band_v2` (Tier 1 vs served agent pooled $\Delta \text{surv5} \ge -0.03$, single-seed floor $-0.15$, absolute episode survival $\ge 0.50$). Under `T5b`'s power calculation (`38.81%` Tier 1 guard-failure risk at $N=96$), `T7` ran one-look Phase R at $N=192$ scripted / $96$ frozen / $96$ mixed worlds, where `b8-s3` passed **`STRICT_PASS`** with **`+138.70`** pooled `H5000` mass (`95% CI [+112.97, +164.32]`, scripted $\Delta \text{surv5} = -0.0081 \ge -0.03$) ([PANEL_DECISION_MEMO.md](../panel_2026-10-15/PANEL_DECISION_MEMO.md), [2026-10-16_strategy.md](../advice/2026-10-16_strategy.md), `docs/research/t7_phase_r_result_2026-10-16.md`).

2. **Exact Rollout Headroom vs Student Distillation (`T1`, `T3`, `T4a`–`T4f`):**
   - **`T1` Commitment (`research/t1_commitment_20261015/README.md`, `docs/research/t1_commitment_study_2026-10-15.md`):** Backward exact replay of the 49 late deaths ($L \ge 800$) showed **`46/49`** had at least one surviving action within $K \le 300$ decisions (`17/49` at $K \le 50$, `25/49` at $K \le 100$, `39/49` at $K \le 200$), with median last-avoidable-decision horizon $H_{\text{LAD}} = 100$ decisions (`p75 = 175`, `p90 = 250`), median $Q(s, a_{\text{fatal}}) - \max_{a \in S} Q(s, a) = 0.45$ $Q$-units (`25/46` $< 0.5$), `29/46` having a non-boost survivor, and `17/46` requiring boost ($a \in \{3,4,5\}$) to outrun a closing loop.
   - **`T3` Teacher Ceiling vs Student Screen (`2026-10-15_t3_launch_review.md`, `research/t3_diagnostics_20261016/`, [`m3-m7-redesign.md`](m3-m7-redesign.md)):** On 64 scripted worlds, the exact $6 \times H300$ rollout shadow (`T3-dense` at $L \ge 700$) achieved **`+0.250` episode survival** (`54/64 = 0.844` vs `B8` `38/64 = 0.594`, $\Delta \text{surv5} = +0.132$) and **`+92.2` mass** (`697.8` vs `605.6`), overriding `B8` on only `75` of `~300,000` decisions (`19` boost overrides). However, distilling a `20,000`-state rollout bank (`4,061` decisive rows) into `B8` over 8M steps failed (`LABELS` `+0.025` survival / `-72` mass; `LABELS_KEEPV8` `+0.021`; `LABELS_KEEPV8_LO` `-0.005` / `-88` mass) because one-sided surviving-set margins caused `67%` action disagreement on ordinary `CTRL` states (`75%` boost-mode flips).
   - **`T4a`–`T4f` Two-Sided Margin & Return Regression (`2026-10-16_return_arm_review.md`):** `T4a` (`TWO_SIDED` with 1:1 counter-rows and margin `0.3` on bank v2) learned the bank (`93%` decisive / `99.9%` counter agreement) and cut growth loss from `-460` to `-135` length units, but round-65 survival delta was `-0.027` (`-24 / -41 / -15` mass vs `CTRL` $\to$ **KILL**). `T4e` (`RETURN`) and `T4f` (`RETURN_C`) halted on the boost tripwire (`34%` greedy boost at `0.8M` steps, return loss `~200`) because 1-step-deviation $H300$ rollouts ($a$ at $t=0$, then `B8`) gave all surviving actions nearly identical discounted return under telescoping PBRS while pricing 1-frame boost mass cost at zero ([2026-10-16_return_arm_review.md](../advice/2026-10-16_return_arm_review.md)).

3. **Representational Separability, Divergence Horizon, and `LOCAL30` (`T5`–`T11`):**
   - **`T5` / `T5b` / `T8` (`2026-10-16_inputs_design.md`, `2026-10-17_clever_ideas.md`):** On `19,976` restored T4e states (`342` worlds, 5-fold grouped by world), adding 24 static geometry/flood/articulation features (`G1`–`G3`) increased `D300` death-prediction OOF AUC by only **`+0.0088`** (`0.7798` vs `0.7710`, `< +0.05` bar), `B8`'s 256-D `fuse` layer stayed at **`AUC ~0.77 < 0.85`** across logistic/MLP/GBM probes (`T5b`), dynamic arrival-race scalars (`T8a`) added **`+0.014`** AUC (`0.785`), and a 3-layer raw-raster `D300` CNN (`T8b`) reached **`0.742`** AUC (`docs/research/t5_separability_2026-10-16.md`, `docs/research/t5b_risk_and_fuse_2026-10-16.md`, `docs/research/t8_probes_2026-10-17.md`).
   - **`T9` Divergence & `T10` Certificate Horizon (`2026-10-17_clever_ideas.md`, `research/t10_certificate_horizon_20261017/README.md`):** Step-by-step replay of all 46 `T1` late LADs from $t=0$ to $100$ (`T9`, `research/t9_divergence_20261017/`, [`m3-m7-redesign.md`](m3-m7-redesign.md)) showed that both fatal and surviving trajectories sit inside `~90%` own-body pockets for the first ~50 steps; arrival-race area separates surviving from fatal branches only at offset **`49–65`** (`20/26 = 77%` alive pairs, median gap `+1,086` cells at $t=65$). Consistently, `T10a` (`research/t10_certificate_horizon_20261017/README.md`, `docs/research/t10_certificate_horizon_2026-10-17.md`) showed optimistic tail reachability held on `99.970%` of `T3-dense` decisions vs `99.92%` of `B8` decisions (`+0.05 pp`), and `T10b` showed GBM AUC decays smoothly from **`~0.92` at `D30`** (`~0.88` at `D60`, `~0.83` at `D100`, `~0.79` at `D200`) to **`~0.75` at `D300`** across all feature sets alike.
   - **`T11` `LOCAL30` (`research/t11_local30_20261017/README.md`, `research/t11_screen_20261017/README.md`):** Appending 30 $H=30$ dynamic arrival-race scalars (`ego2s-bv8-local30`, 90 scalars total; `92.3%` $D \le 30$ coverage, `0.916` $D30$ GBM AUC) was **KILLED before gameplay** when seed 13 exceeded the `1.0%` $L < 700$ greedy boost ceiling (`1.07%`, `219/20,480` states) at `300,160` transitions (`docs/research/t11_screen_result_2026-10-17.md`).

4. **Serving Qualification & 6-Snake Watch Roster (`T12`–`T13b`):**
   - Eliminating per-tick deep-copy and GC stalls in `T12b` lowered 6-snake `B8` step latency from `p95 = 45.73 ms` (`T12` FAIL) to **`p95 = 16.43 ms <= 40.0 ms`** (`p50 = 12.15 ms`, `p99 = 19.65 ms`, `SERVING_PASS`), and `b8-s3` shipped in `T12c` (`docs/research/t12_b8_serving_qualification_2026-10-17.md`, `docs/research/b8_s3_release_2026-10-17.md`).
   - On 32 18,000-frame Watch sessions (`T13` / `T13b`), 6-snake homogeneous `B8` (`6x B8`) increased deaths/1k frames from `0.151` (`frp3-s12+v8`) to `0.453` (`mean max length 553.8`), whereas a 6-snake mixed roster (`1 B8 + 2 frp3-s12+v8 + 2 champion_a5+v8 + 1 greedy_food`) reduced deaths/1k to **`0.250`** and raised mean max length to **`729.6`** (`+123.6` over `6x B8`), selecting `PLAN_MIXED_PENDING_ROSTER_QUALIFICATION` ([`b8-release-serving.md`](b8-release-serving.md), `docs/research/t13b_six_snake_roster_2026-10-17_results.md`).

## What Was Learned

1. **Late-game self-trapping (`L >= 800`) is an emergent 50–100 step trajectory-packing problem, not a static 1-step sensory blind spot:** At the Last Avoidable Decision (`median H_LAD = 100`), both the fatal and surviving branches sit inside `~90%` own-body boundaries and retain tail reachability (`99.92%`); the fatal enclosure only seals 50–65 decisions later after subsequent foraging turns.
2. **Exact multi-step rollout search (`T3-dense`) solves late self-trapping (`+0.250` survival, `+92.2` mass) by intervening on only ~0.025% of decisions:** Because true trap-avoidance overrides are extremely sparse (`75 / ~300,000` decisions), naive auxiliary margin or 1-step-deviation return losses over-perturb ordinary states (`67%` policy disagreement or `34%` boost collapse).
3. **1-step-deviation rollouts under telescoping PBRS cannot price multi-step boost or coiling:** Rolling out $a$ for 1 step and following `B8` for 299 steps collapses return differences across safe actions to zero while underpricing boost mass burn.

## Why It Closed

The `T1`–`T11` single-state probe and auxiliary fine-tuning line closed on 2026-10-17 after `T9`–`T11` proved that static/1-step features cannot predict `D300` self-sealing without multi-step search and that 90-scalar `LOCAL30` fine-tuning tripped early boost drift (`docs/research/t11_screen_result_2026-10-17.md`). Meanwhile, `b8-s3` passed the higher-powered `T7` Phase R gate (`STRICT_PASS`, `+138.70` mass) and `T12b` serving qualification (`p95 = 16.43 ms`) and was released on 2026-10-17, shifting the campaign to on-policy league/scale training (`T21`–`T23`, `Q0`/`Q1`) and the T34 ExIt-B8 / search-assisted Watch line.
