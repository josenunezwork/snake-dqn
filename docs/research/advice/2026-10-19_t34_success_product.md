# Campaign success: a better Watch hero with honest boundaries

2026-10-19 · Product owner's advocate · Source snapshot `fe0fd6843320de263a67fa701ae227a461dc57f0`.

**Define success as a visibly better featured hero in the released mixed Watch6 experience, without sacrificing responsiveness or trust. Allow a qualified, explicitly labelled lookahead-assisted hero to satisfy that product objective. Record stronger bare-network performance separately. Accept a Watch6-only enhancement while preserving B8 for existing larger rosters and Play.** Publish this amendment before Round 0; assistance cannot become a consolation definition after distillation fails.

This is advice, not an amendment, registration, release or spending authorization. Numerical acceptance margins below are prospective proposals to freeze before outcomes.

[STATUS](../../STATUS.md) records the October no-veto goal as met. The product now owes viewers continuity, growth and understandable behavior. Bare inference still offers simpler operations and broader deployment; prefer it when benefits are comparable. But mechanism purity alone should not disqualify a better experience after the original research goal has been achieved.

There is reason to investigate assistance, not confidence to ship it. [T3 dense](../t3_ceiling_test_2026-10-15.md#dense-ceiling-addendum-declared-before-running) raised pooled scripted survival from 38/64 to 54/64 and mass from 608 to 700, changing 75 actions. That reused development bank covered two B8 seeds, not the live mixed roster. [T3 distillation](../t3_screen_result_2026-10-15.md) subsequently lost roughly 81–88 scripted mass versus CTRL. The [funded plan](../final_run_20261019/FINAL_PLAN.md) assigns most upside to Path B, but its 12–30 ms estimate and expected-value allocation are projections. Teacher efficacy, imitation accuracy and delivered product value are different claims.

[T13](../t13_product_roster_2026-10-17.md) explains why hero experience must be measured directly: all-B8 Watch12 increased arena mass while hero mass fell from FRP3's 239.73 to 121.51. Opponents and veto scope differed. [T13b](../t13b_six_snake_roster_2026-10-17_results.md) found mixed Watch6 hero deaths of 0.296/1k versus all-B8's 0.392, with mass 316.76 versus 236.91. Changing the exhibition already helped without changing B8. Therefore hold opponents, population, speed and respawns fixed when judging this campaign.

I would require the following contract:

1. **Earn both skill and product evidence.** The exact delivered controller must pass fresh Mac strict mass-superiority and survival gates against released B8 s3, followed by independent serving qualification and owner release approval. Separately, on fresh paired continuous mixed Watch6 episodes, target at least 20% fewer hero deaths/1k, with a paired 95% interval excluding zero, and no loss of full-horizon hero mass or median/p95 length beyond a predeclared 5% noninferiority margin. Freeze sample size and interval rules beforehand. Report uninterrupted life duration, late self-collisions and both-alive growth; arena mass, kills and boost cannot compensate for a worse hero. Keep respawn metrics separate from terminal-hero strict scores.

2. **Preserve supported experiences.** T12's original Watch12 failure was repaired: [T12B](../t12_b8_serving_qualification_2026-10-17.md#t12b-check-4-result-and-evidence) passed at the lower ceiling; [T15](../t15_mixed_roster_qualification_2026-10-17.md#latency-and-cadence) qualified mixed Watch6/8/12 at settings 24/12/12. The tower's projected 3–5 ms does not prove Watch12 infeasible. Qualify each advertised candidate roster; retain visibly identified B8 elsewhere. Watch assistance confers no qualification for Play's multiple AI opponents or human interaction. Prefer the plan's tower-free primary unless the tower earns its cost.

3. **Budget delivered responsiveness.** Preserve current speed ceilings initially. Require zero processing-budget overruns in the registered paced qualification and no more than 5% deterioration in delivered cadence or 10% in p95 browser frame-arrival and Play input-to-paint latency versus matched B8. Measure p99 tails, long bodies, resets and ordinary Mac contention too. T15's mixed Watch6 achieved 9.436 fps at the 12 setting; network/rendering were excluded. Neither a fast forward pass nor a spare-core estimate certifies smooth play.

4. **Qualify the real asynchronous policy.** Specify what happens after failed verification: rejecting an action does not identify its replacement. Budget verification, expansion, copying and dispatch together. Use one bounded worker and at most one outstanding request; never wait in the game loop. Bind results to session, hero life, state and action deadline; discard stale results and immediately use the qualified bare fallback. Include misses and fallbacks in efficacy estimates. Round 0's synchronous lookahead credit cannot stand in for this test. Resolve this feasibility before committing later rounds on the strength of Path B's expected value.

5. **Make identity truthful.** Label assisted Watch, expose interventions and fallback, and distinguish the network's proposed action from the executed action. Assistance follows the fixed hero, not inspector selection. Bind checkpoint, controller version, roster and assistance status to telemetry and any affected scores. Preserve an exercised B8 rollback; monitoring remains separate by roster and size.

I would withdraw support if realistic deadlines erase the gains, or blinded matched viewing sessions prefer B8 despite better survival because growth becomes stagnant or motion stalls. I would expand beyond Watch6 only after the same quality/latency contract passes there; usage evidence that larger rosters matter strengthens that priority. A bare finalist meeting comparable product margins wins on simplicity. Round 0 failure rejects this teacher and budget, not all possible improvements to B8.
