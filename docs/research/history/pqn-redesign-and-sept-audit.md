# July–September 2026 PQN Redesign Blueprint, State-System Audit, and RL Portfolio Reviews

## Question and Goal

Following the June 2026 promotion of the `vector61` Apex free-space champion (`champion_a5_freespace_20260621.pth`), the project asked whether the 61-feature feed-forward Apex DQN could be replaced by a dual-scale ego-raster representation (`raster31v2`, later the experimental `raster31v3`) trained by synchronous Parallelized Q-Network (`PQN`, $Q(\lambda)$) learning on the vectorized `BatchSim` simulator. Between July and September 2026, this lineage drafted the redesign blueprint, ran a PQN sampler diagnostic (`pqn_sampler_2026-09-06`), conducted a state-representation and system audit (`state_system_audit_2026-09-06`), executed the RL contract repair (`rl_repair_execution_2026-09-08`), specified and screened opt-in per-environment autoreset (`B0–B5`), tested whether a hidden remote enemy heading was decision-relevant (`H1-A`), and repaired the trainer/Watch decision-phase contract (`watch_pre_move_v1`).

## Final Verdict and Key Numbers

1. **Historical CNN Retraction and Blueprint Baseline (2026-07-05):**
   - Git archaeology in [`docs/ml_redesign_blueprint_2026-07.md`](../../ml_redesign_blueprint_2026-07.md) found the claim that "CNN variants were evaluated and lost every paired benchmark" false: the CNN code (written 2026-06-21, deleted 2026-06-28) never entered git, zero CNN checkpoints existed, and all 10 overnight benchmark arms were feedforward. The July gate ranked mean mass over alive frames only, against 5 clones of one checkpoint, and analyzed paired seeds unpaired.
   - The blueprint's `raster31v2` contract: 31×31 tactical raster (cell = 1 segment, 10 px), 25×25 strategic raster (cell = 5 segments, 50 px, 3 density channels), and 26 ego-frame scalars; planned network ~1.3M params with a dueling `V(1) + A(6)` head.

2. **September 6 PQN Sampler Diagnostic and State-System Audit:**
   - Sampler ([`docs/pqn_sampler_results_2026-09-06.md`](../../pqn_sampler_results_2026-09-06.md)): `epoch` used `100.0%` of eligible rows (`2,503,528` draws, `11,237` optimizer steps) vs `68.132%` for `legacy` (`2,903,004` draws, `12,024` steps). Paired per-seed `epoch - legacy` deltas were `-3.3868` (401), `-4.7433` (502), `-0.9776` (603), `+2.0003` (704), `+0.2602` (805); mean `-1.3694`, 95% CI `[-4.748, +2.009]`. Secondary: legacy minus initial `+1.022` (`[-1.945, 3.990]`), epoch minus initial `-0.347` (`[-1.635, 0.941]`). The production sampler was unchanged.
   - Audit ([`docs/state_and_system_review_2026-09-06.md`](../../state_and_system_review_2026-09-06.md), [`docs/experiments/state_system_audit_2026-09-06/README.md`](../../experiments/state_system_audit_2026-09-06/README.md)): decision to keep the `vector61` Apex incumbent frozen and continue the ego-raster + `BatchSim` direction only after repairing versioned contracts; evidence covers hidden boost/hunger aliases, action-mask counterexamples, raster paint-order and alias probes, serving identity/mask mismatches, PQN reset/policy-assignment/RNG probes, stale Apex replay-slot priorities, and evaluation engine/metric differences. Its [`vector/actor_vs_full_obs.md`](../../experiments/state_system_audit_2026-09-06/vector/actor_vs_full_obs.md) KS check found observation drift between `free_space_v2.yaml` at scale 0.2 and scale 1.0 (max KS `0.943`, `44/61` dims flagged).

3. **RL Repair Execution (`C0`–`H0`, `G0-R3`, `X0`, 2026-09-08):**
   - Accepted packages and `G0-R3` qualification of `7cb66a9` as listed above (`strict_authority: false`, `release_action_authorized: false`).
   - `X0` retained five `wall_stop` arms (screen-1..5: `416,687`, `404,516`, `431,780`, `424,379`, `455,227` last recorded hero steps of `500,000` requested; frozen per-arm wall cap `435.61 s`). With zero complete arms or evaluations, the five-seed interval is null. In screen 1 the first 73 updates ran at `1,513.5` useful hero transitions per update-wall-second and later updates at `859.7`; the short calibration sampled only the early all-hero phase.

4. **Per-Env Autoreset (`A2/A3`, `B0–B5`, 2026-09-09 to 2026-09-12):**
   - `A2/A3`: both calibration arms completed on `7cb66a9`; the predeclared process-wall formula set an `822 s` future cap for 500,000 useful hero transitions (timing evidence only; not retrofitted to `X0`).
   - `B1–B4`: accepted on `9265eb59` (serving suite `108` passed; B4 oracles `9` passed; full non-slow suite `2,602` passed, 6 skipped, 3 slow deselected on `9245c003`). Runtime defaults remain `batch_barrier_v1 + continuous_env_rng_v1`; `per_env_autoreset_v1` is opt-in.
   - `B5 v2`: pair 1 `batch_barrier_v1` reached `200,115` useful steps (`197` updates, `221.62 s`, peak RSS `3.06 GB`); `per_env_autoreset_v1` reached `200,376` (`173` updates, `226.95 s` worker, peak RSS `2.71 GB`), but the parent recorded `child_exit_unconfirmed` (child return code unavailable). The pair was not accepted, so the paired median is null and the predeclared 15% advance criterion was never evaluable. The post-experiment monitor repair (`b51b1588`) passed `43` focused tests and does not authorize a retry.

5. **Observation-Value Gate (`H1-A`) and Decision-Phase Repair (2026-09-12):**
   - `H1-A`: the supervising parent completed in `26.07 s` (peak RSS `243.05 MiB`); supplemental evidence recorded `51` next-observation digest differences across `113` matched-action comparisons spanning `20` of the `36` pairs (confirming controlled aliasing), but zero regret and no required action change. No remote-heading tokens, GRU/history model, or network change is supported.
   - Decision phase: the repair makes `watch_pre_move_v1` (available only with `corrected-v3` and `raster31v3`) select actions from the real prepared simulator state via `BatchSim.step_with_policy()`; `pre_transition_v1` remains the default and old checkpoints keep their legacy interpretation.
   - `R1–R12` research lanes found that `train_pqn.py --no-self-play` still co-adapts six live heroes, so a fixed-opponent control should use five scripted `random_safe` foes with forced hero slot 0. `VC1` (a disposable full-trunk visible-food direction classifier on 215 examples from 36 retained `H1-A` snapshots, 161 train / 54 held-out) completed one initialization with train accuracy/macro-F1 `1.0/1.0` and held-out `0.5926/0.5299`; the second initialization stopped at 56 updates under the cap, so the two-seed fit gate was not met. `LP1` (a two-arm 50,000-useful-transition learning probe) was not launched.

## What Was Learned and Why It Closed

- **Why it closed:** The July–September 2026 work built and engineering-qualified the repaired raster/PQN stack (`raster31v3`, opt-in `per_env_autoreset_v1`, opt-in `watch_pre_move_v1`, E2 paired statistics with Holm correction) without producing a learning result: the sampler screen, `X0`, and `B5 v2` were all inconclusive, `H1-A` found no decision-relevant remote-heading aliasing, and `LP1` was deferred while a successor policy-quality study was separately frozen. The follow-on September 13 campaign is recorded in [`four-hour-2026-09-13.md`](four-hour-2026-09-13.md).
- **Current status:** Closed historical lineage. The raster PQN path remains an unpromoted redesign experiment; the blueprint is superseded as an active plan (2026-10-08).
