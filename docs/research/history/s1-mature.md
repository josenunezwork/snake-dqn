# S1 Mature-Schedule League vs Hero Fine-Tuning (`s1-mature`)

## Question and Goal

After T23's KILL (`-80.8` equal-mix mass vs bare B8), can either mature fine-tuning collection package improve the released bare B8 initializer, and is terminal-hero `N1` collection better than static-pool `N3` under the same mature schedule? Both arms start from identical B8 online/target bytes with fresh Adam and empty replay, fix `gamma = .998`, `epsilon = .01`, `anchor = .1` from decision zero, and train exactly `8,000,000` learner decisions per run across `5` paired seed blocks on Colab L4 (`N3`: 6 actors / 3 learner seats, Q1's static-pool package; `N1`: 3 actors / 1 seat, Q1 arm D's terminal-hero collection). Endpoint: terminal-hero H5000 full-horizon mass integral vs bare B8 ([`PREREGISTRATION.md`](../../../research/s1_mature_20261018/PREREGISTRATION.md)). The preregistration states the study cannot identify schedule, collection, optimizer-reset or interaction causality.

## Final Verdict and Key Numbers

1. **Scientific Verdict (`KILL` for both `N3` and `N1`):**
   - Both mature-schedule arms (`gamma = .998`, `epsilon = .01`, `anchor = .1`) lost far less than T23's `-80.8`, but the preregistration explicitly states S1 cannot identify schedule, collection, optimizer-reset or interaction causality ([`PREREGISTRATION.md`](../../../research/s1_mature_20261018/PREREGISTRATION.md)).
   - However, neither mature-schedule arm improved upon bare `b8-s3`: the two arms scored **`-12.5` and `-14.9` pooled mass vs B8** (`KILL` both arms; audit PASS), failing the pre-registered GO rule (all KILL guards pass, `LB90(G) > 0`, mean `G >= 20`, plus scripted-mass conditions) ([`docs/research/final_run_20261019/CAMPAIGN_LOG.md`](../final_run_20261019/CAMPAIGN_LOG.md) "Measured results so far", [`docs/TIMELINE.md`](../../TIMELINE.md), [`PREREGISTRATION.md`](../../../research/s1_mature_20261018/PREREGISTRATION.md)).
2. **Exact Barrier Accounting & Preflight Measurements (`research/s1_mature_20261018/*.json`):**
   - **Barrier overshoot root cause and fix (`TRAIN_PREFLIGHT.json` → `TRAIN_PREFLIGHT_BARRIER_FIX.json`):** In the initial implementation, `train_m3c.py` continued ingesting ordinary actor frames while a final full-state barrier was pending, so the 2-actor / 8-world / 3-seat CPU fixture reached `12,048` decisions at the `12,000` target (`observed_overshoot = 48 > registered bound 47`) ([`research/s1_mature_20261018/TRAIN_PREFLIGHT.json`](../../../research/s1_mature_20261018/TRAIN_PREFLIGHT.json)). Halting ingestion while a barrier is pending and returning drained packets to learner-local per-actor FIFOs produced **`0` overshoot** on the same 3-seat fixture (`actual_learner_decisions = 12,000`, `emitted_replay_rows = 11,760`, `updates = 45`, `pending_update_debt = 240`, `deferred_decisions = 48`; [`research/s1_mature_20261018/TRAIN_PREFLIGHT_BARRIER_FIX.json`](../../../research/s1_mature_20261018/TRAIN_PREFLIGHT_BARRIER_FIX.json)).
   - **Full-state replay checkpoint size (`CHECKPOINT_SIZE_MEASUREMENT.json`):** At full capacity (`500,000` replay rows + `300,000` on-policy labels, `12,449 B` per raw observation row), exact saved ring/replay/label array capacities are **`10,103,294,896 B` (`N3`)** and **`9,481,840,816 B` (`N1`)**, real `torch.save` serializer measurements of production-shaped arrays are **`11,178,149,683 B`** and **`10,490,632,471 B`**, total serialized checkpoint planning upper allowances are **`22,680,004,768 B` (`21.12 GiB`)** and **`20,268,551,032 B` (`18.88 GiB`)**, four snapshot archives per job are `91,631,413,568 B` / `81,889,140,476 B`, and `10` jobs would require **`867,602,770,220 B`** before final archives ([`research/s1_mature_20261018/CHECKPOINT_SIZE_MEASUREMENT.json`](../../../research/s1_mature_20261018/CHECKPOINT_SIZE_MEASUREMENT.json)).
   - **Final-only export footprint (`FINAL_ARTIFACT_SIZE_FIXTURE.json`, `TRAIN_PREFLIGHT_FINAL_ONLY.json`):** In the 12,000-decision Mac CPU fixture (not an 8M L4 measurement), exporting only final weights (`final.pth = 6,566,741 B`, `target.pth = 6,562,677 B`) gave a compressed archive + transport index of **`12,180,957 B` (`11.62 MiB`)** (`64 MiB` enforced per-attempt cap, `67,108,864 B`; [`research/s1_mature_20261018/FINAL_ARTIFACT_SIZE_FIXTURE.json`](../../../research/s1_mature_20261018/FINAL_ARTIFACT_SIZE_FIXTURE.json)).
   - **Mac evaluation timing (`EVAL_PREFLIGHT_FINAL.json`):** Evaluating `10` disjoint H5000 worlds with the B8 checkpoint (`3e649783...`) summed to **`67.21 s`** of per-episode time with 2 one-thread workers (max `8.31 s`) and **`63.68 s`** serially (max `7.90 s`), with identical ordered result bytes; command wall time was `154 s` ([`research/s1_mature_20261018/EVAL_PREFLIGHT_FINAL.json`](../../../research/s1_mature_20261018/EVAL_PREFLIGHT_FINAL.json)).

## What Was Learned

- **Mature-schedule online fine-tuning from bare B8 did not beat B8:** With fresh Adam and empty replay, both packages lost mass (`-12.5` / `-14.9` vs T23's `-80.8`); per the preregistration, failure closes these recipes within this package, not all RL, and does not attribute T23's loss to any single cause.
- **Full-replay snapshots (`~10.5–11.2 GB` serialized per snapshot) made export liability the blocker:** S1 switched to uninterrupted runs (estimate/timeout `30/60` min `N3`, `68/136` min `N1`) that export only terminal online/target weights (`~12.2 MB` fixture archive).

## Why It Closed / Current Status

**Closed (`KILL` both arms, recorded in the 2026-10-19 campaign log).** The T34 compute-portfolio advice later cited T23's `-80.8` and S1's `-12.5/-14.9` as evidence favoring search over more ordinary training ([`docs/research/final_run_20261019/CAMPAIGN_LOG.md`](../final_run_20261019/CAMPAIGN_LOG.md); `docs/research/advice/2026-10-20_compute_portfolio_astra_gpuops.md` (removed in consolidation; see git c4c4ab41)).
