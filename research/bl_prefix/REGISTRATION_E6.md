## Counterfactual protocol and observable

Per root run R, F and bare B8 greedy for 64 decisions, then bare B8 greedy to
original frame 5000 (terminal hero, dead mass zero), under TWO shared future
RNG streams. Opponents recompute actions and react each step. Restore the same
root for every continuation; future RNG streams are independent from selection
and shared across controllers, not recorded opponent action tapes. Secondary:
each controller greedy through H5000 under those same streams, descriptive only.

Score = remaining per-frame alive logical mass sum /5000, never /remaining
horizon or /alive frames. Average the two RNG rows within source world before
inference; no pseudoreplication. Report early and ordinary strata separately,
equal-weight the three mix means within each stratum; EPD is equal-weight early
and ordinary means. One-sided 90% Student-t bounds use stratified world-cluster
variance and Welch-Satterthwaite df (t.isf(.1,df)). R and F are separate fixed
controllers, not a training-recipe inference. Ordinary guard is its point mean.
