## Recorded process lessons

- **2026-10-03 PT — Launch conditions and capacity:** require an open Mac lid and check pod capacity before planning large runs. (src: docs/experiments/apex_veto_v8_2026-10-03/README.md:137; docs/research/runpod_x86_consistency_2026-10-03.md:119-120)
- **2026-10-12 PT — Discriminating comparisons:** score development candidates without the v8 veto. (src: docs/research/m3c_m3d_learnings_2026-10-12.md:34-36)
- **2026-10-12 PT — Execution and governance:** a study that never runs a fit does not count as progress, and governance must be capped relative to the experiment. (src: docs/research/m3c_m3d_learnings_2026-10-12.md:72-78)
- **2026-10-14 PT — Evaluation noise:** use at least 96 scripted worlds in future screens. (src: docs/research/m6_result_2026-10-14.md:23-27)
- **2026-10-14 PT — Pod-runner hardening:** retain failed-run logs, preflight the trainer, poll stock at one-minute intervals and sweep stray pods. (src: docs/research/m6_result_2026-10-14.md:27-29)
- **2026-10-17 PT — Shared-directory preparation:** pre-create shared Colab directories before launching workers. (src: research/t23_r0_screen_20261017/RESULTS_T23.md:14-15)
- **2026-10-17 PT — REST admission:** filter RunPod data-center candidates against the REST enum. (src: docs/codex-skills/snake-dqn-runpod/SKILL.md:99-101)
- **2026-10-17 PT — Evidence boundaries:** smoke parity records do not count as qualification evidence, and a short startup without warmed latency samples is not a healthy monitoring window. (src: docs/research/t12_b8_serving_qualification_2026-10-17.md:132-133; docs/research/b8_s3_release_2026-10-17.md:182-185)
- **2026-10-18 PT — Failure reporting:** do not enable `--showlocals` for shared-memory actor tests. (src: docs/testing_openmp_macos.md:51-56)
- **2026-10-19 PT — Advisor persistence:** default to two advisors, persist each answer on arrival and expand only for a named unresolved question. (src: docs/research/advice/2026-10-19_rules_audit_fable_judgment.md:29)
- **2026-10-19 PT — Audit boundaries:** the rules audit identified post-data relaxation of survival margins or tripwires, moving strict or serving qualification off the Mac, and removing the owner’s push instruction without asking as dangerous changes. (src: docs/research/advice/2026-10-19_rules_audit_fable_judgment.md:45)
- **2026-10-20 PT — Whole-session sizing:** timeouts must cover workload, setup and export. (src: docs/research/final_run_20261019/HANDOFF_2026-10-20.md:134)
- **2026-10-21 PT — Publish before analysis:** every registered analysis must check that the local ledger equals the published ledger before consuming the look. (src: docs/research/final_run_20261019/CAMPAIGN_LOG.md:99; big-lever/round-12/JUDGMENT.md:150-152)
- **2026-10-21 PT — Colab family preflight:** before science dispatch, a new manifest family needs a target-platform canary exercising real transport bytes and file counts, a CUDA decision, producer sidecars, export recovery and verified release. (src: big-lever/round-12/JUDGMENT.md:119-125)
- **2026-10-21 PT — Measure before plan:** rate, latency, power and transport premises require receipts from the target platform and exact code path; dependent builds cannot rely on unmeasured premises. (src: big-lever/round-12/JUDGMENT.md:126-131)
- **2026-10-21 PT — Execution attestation:** speed and identity claims require executed-path counters, including native versus fallback activity. (src: big-lever/round-12/JUDGMENT.md:132-135)
- **2026-10-21 PT — Shared validation:** pre-allocation validation must use the supervisor’s accounting validator and required-export inventory check. (src: big-lever/round-12/JUDGMENT.md:136-139)
- **2026-10-21 PT — Failure classification:** distinguish child exit, foreign generation and RPC timeout in supervisor receipts. (src: big-lever/round-12/JUDGMENT.md:140-142)
- **2026-10-21 PT — Power before building:** a screen, bank or evaluation requires a signed power-and-size sheet before builder assignment. (src: big-lever/round-12/JUDGMENT.md:143-145)
