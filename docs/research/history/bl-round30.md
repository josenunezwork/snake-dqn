## Question and Goal

After the F4 residual (`1,542` trainable parameters) ended in terminal `STOP_FUNDING` at Look R-A (retention passed, D1024 harm screen failed with `H >= 1`, exact `H` not exported), the Round-30 (`bl_round30`) package asked whether F4's shortfall came from imperfect optimization or from an objective mismatch, before any single-factor successor. Astra's 2026-10-23 ruling (`ADOPT_DIAGNOSIS_FIRST`) ordered:

- `R30-0`: corpus admission and consumer rehearsal (`<= 0.25` aggregate Mac core-hour, `0` Colab units).

- `R30-2` (conditional): rescue-survivor mass-ranking fit vs matched control, triggered only if aggregate choice loss / available advantage `>= 0.10` across `>= 32` ultimate training parents with `>= 2` surviving alternatives; its single look required `>= 10%` survivor-reference regret reduction, rescue retention `>= 0.70` and `>= control`, and successor `H = 0`.
- `R30-3` (continuation diagnostic): deferred and unfunded.

Companion workstreams `R32` (`research/r32_rehearsal`, synthetic sequential-consumer rehearsal) and `R33` (`research/r33_ops`, ops closeout) ran alongside.

## What Was Learned

1. **The certificate, not the optimizer, was the measured blocker:** probe-03's phase telemetry showed Adam and closures projecting to minutes at most, while the `Decimal50` gradient enclosure hit its `600 s` cap; the `7,962 min` G5 figure was an unseparated-cost screening surrogate, not a forecast.
2. **Upload and output caps must be separate:** probe-01's refusal came from the 512-KiB output cap also capping uploads; probe-02 used a 64-MiB transport bound with a separate 524,288-byte timing-output cap.
3. **Timing probes need durable phase telemetry:** probe-02's blanket exception handler erased the failure cause (still UNKNOWN); probe-03's durable redacted per-phase records identified `CERTIFICATE_DEADLINE` in one run.
4. **Synthetic consumer rehearsal caught a real bug:** `R32` surfaced the missing `df` argument in `standard_t` (fixed with `heavy_tail_df=5`) before any real-data consumer run ([`research/r32_rehearsal/README.md`](../../../research/r32_rehearsal/README.md)).
