# After T23: preserve B8's competence, then earn an adaptive league

2026-10-17 · Multi-agent / game-AI advice · Evidence snapshot: `56d59d27`

**I would first test whether B8 can improve under a mature fine-tuning schedule on the actual fixed-opponent task. I would not extend T23, start a large population, or wait for an H100.** Queue a small causal experiment before the league: mature versus restarted schedules, crossed with task-aligned single-learner collection versus T23's three-learner collection. If ordinary fixed-opponent learning wins, take that simpler candidate to the Mac gate. If it preserves competence but stalls, test a bounded adaptive opponent curriculum against its matched static control. Separately, a capped training-time search experiment can determine whether there is a productive teacher worth distilling into a bare network.

This is advice for a **new registration**, not a registration, execution receipt, or authorization to launch. Every new threshold and budget below is a proposed choice to freeze before data. This task changes only this document; no training, pods, or tmux operations are part of it. The current objective is **higher fixed-opponent H5000 mass integral than released B8, with survival guards and a Mac strict pass**. It supersedes the older Lane A proposal's product-roster deaths/median-length primary for this proposal only; it does not amend that document or any hash-bound record.

## 1. What T23 changes

The [T23 result](../../../research/t23_r0_screen_20261017/RESULTS_T23.md) and [verdict](../../../research/t23_r0_screen_20261017/results/t23_verdict.md) are decisive about the tested recipe:

| Quantity | T23 result |
|---|---:|
| Equal-mix mass difference versus B8 | −80.7846 |
| One-sided 90% t lower / upper limits, five training seeds | −96.059 / −65.5103 |
| Positive training seeds | 0/5 |
| Scripted / frozen / mixed mass differences | −101.317 / −61.185 / −79.851 |
| Frozen no-death episode survival, candidate / B8 | 0.7625 / 0.68125 |

The agent became less productive while survival was broadly maintained or improved. It did not merely fail to reach significance. However, “more cautious” is a behavioral interpretation: food acquisition, boost eligibility, space use and reward decomposition are needed to identify how it lost mass. Scripted alive-frame fraction also fell slightly in aggregate, and two seeds lost about five to six percentage points there. Do not describe every survival cell as improved.

T23 did **not** test adaptive league training. The current [pool module](../../../research/redesign_m3c_20261008/league_pool.py) explicitly contains no PFSP or opponent publication. Three seats shared the changing learner and three sampled immutable opponents. That tests a joint change in collection distribution and fine-tuning procedure. It neither establishes that a changing league will help nor proves that all self-play is exhausted.

There is a concrete reset hypothesis. The [R0 advice](2026-10-17_h100_preruns_rl.md) and [trainer schedules](../../../research/redesign_m3c_20261008/train_m3c.py) show that weights-only fine-tuning starts gamma at 0.99, epsilon at 0.05, and anchor weight at 1; by 8M they are 0.998, 0.01 and still 1. The released B8 finished at 20M with gamma 0.998, epsilon 0.01 and anchor 0.1. Its original `B8_s3/summary.json` and final `log.jsonl` row were checked against the released checkpoint SHA. Thus T23 restarted a shorter-horizon, more exploratory, much more strongly anchored curriculum around already-trained weights. Fresh Adam and empty replay are additional changes from continuation. On-policy v8 labels transform the **current learner's** choices; they are not an immutable B8 behavior-preservation teacher.

That is a plausible mechanism, **not an attribution established by T23**. Gamma also enters the mass reward term, so resetting it changes more than the nominal discount horizon. The experiment below deliberately tests the schedule package as a package. It will not claim that anchor weight alone caused the regression.

The [retrospective](../redesign_campaign_retrospective_2026-10-17.md) remains binding context: repeated late-death reward/loss/label/restart arms failed. Those results do not establish a universal representational ceiling for food competition, interception, or short-horizon route choice. Equally, they are a strong reason not to sell another survival-label loss as a new league mechanism.

With none, close this same-observation fine-tuning route for this budget. Do not queue a sequence of LR/anchor sweeps until something passes.

These four-arm development intervals are **selection screens**, not familywise-controlled discovery claims. Report all arms and contrasts. A winning arm must clear an independent fresh-bank Mac procedure; do not present the largest development interval as a strict confidence guarantee. ## 5. Q2: a minimal adaptive league, only after competence is preserved

I would begin with **main plus frozen history**, not eight simultaneous learners, PBT, two exploiter species and a large payoff matrix. The hypothesis is that exposure to new responses improves the fixed-opponent objective without inducing a passive equilibrium. AlphaStar supplies precedent for population diversity and exploiters, but its two-player results do not supply a convergence theorem for a six-player general-sum arena. [Primary AlphaStar paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-019-1724-z).

For each of the five selected Q1 finals, create paired static/adaptive children. Both are weights-only restarts with fresh Adam/replay and the **same selected schedule package restarted at t=0**; this deliberately matches the tested fine-tuning operation. Current T21 full-state support exists; using it for a true continuation would require workload-specific optimizer, replay, target, actor/n-step, sampler and RNG restore validation. No cross-replica exchange of checkpoints or payoff data.

Each child gets 8M decisions. Preserve the selected N and episode semantics. Both children use **75% selected static-anchor worlds /25% history worlds**, drawn at segment/world creation and kept fixed for that segment. The static child always uses the fixed parent in its history slice; only the adaptive child updates that slice. In history worlds, learner seats keep their own individual rewards; all frozen seats use one sampled historical-main identity. No team return or kill redistribution. At t=0 history consists of the parent's fixed checkpoint. Publish the main at 2M/4M/6M for the next interval; history sampling is uniform, retaining the parent plus all three snapshots. No performance-based admission, eviction or PFSP yet. The two children therefore have the same opponent distribution before the first publication. Their contrast tests replacing frozen-parent exposure with changing history; it does not confound adaptation with adding a new 25% opponent family.

Evaluate the 8M children on fresh 64/32/32 banks per replica, candidate/static/B8: **1,920 H5000 episodes**. Use Q1 guards and efficacy rules. Additionally require the adaptive−static equal-mix contrast's lower90 bound > 0 and ≥ 4/5 positive paired seed contrasts. No extension. Outcomes have different consequences:

- Adaptive beats B8 and static: history curriculum earns Mac preparation; another 20M is not mandatory.
- Both beat B8 but adaptive does not beat static: advance the static recipe; league overhead did not earn its place.
- Adaptive beats a failing static child but still loses to B8: no success; close this branch.
- Neither wins: stop; don't automatically add PFSP or increase population size.

**Only after that history GO**, a next registered increment may spend 10M total on five independent, 2M, N=1 main-exploiter searches against each replica's frozen main. Initialize exploiters from B8; freeze schedule/recipe and use 80% all-current-main backgrounds /20% anchor backgrounds. The same stability tripwires apply. An exploiter's purpose is to expose a reproducible weakness, not to become the served hero.

For every exploiter, compare it with the current main as the hero in each of three immutable five-opponent backgrounds: all-current-main, all-B8, and all-FRP+v8. Use 32 paired worlds/context, fresh training-only seeds, giving 5×3×32×2=**960 episodes**. Require the lower90 paired-world bootstrap bound on its mass advantage over the current main to be > 0 in at least one declared context, with survival floors met in all sampled contexts. This establishes a profitable response, not yet an opponent that harms the main.

Also play the frozen main as hero against one exploiter plus four frozen-main opponents, paired against one B8 plus the same four frozen-main opponents, on 32 fresh worlds per replica. This adds **320 episodes**, for a total **1,280**. Keep the changed opponent in the same preregistered seat. Require main mass to fall by at least 20 and the one-sided 90% paired-world upper bound on that change to be < 0. Admit an exploiter witness only when both tests pass. These are adaptive training-selection rules, not scientific significance or Nash exploitability. A failed bounded exploiter search is not evidence of unexploitable play.

If no witness qualifies, stop the exploiter increment. If one does, preregister a subsequent matched add-witness versus history-only experiment; don't silently add it to an already-scored run. A future PFSP sampler should keep the 75% anchor allocation fixed, adapt only the 25%, use smoothed context-specific mass preference `(wins+.5*ties+1)/(n+2)`, and blend 50% uniform with 50% normalized `(1−p)^2` difficulty weights within that slice. With at most four dynamic identities, this gives each support; cap any one at 50% of the dynamic slice when at least two exist, and allocate the whole slice to its sole member otherwise. Renormalize with deterministic capped redistribution. Use training-only panels at fixed barriers. No gate scores, global Elo ranking, or fastest-worker publication race may drive sampling.

A snake can starve rivals while losing absolute mass, or exploit a weak player while losing against another background. Hence the primary remains the candidate's **absolute paired mass against the same fixed opponents**, not win rate, kill count, arena-wide mass, or an Elo number. Keep protected anchors and context-specific response tables to expose cycling. This narrow design also avoids the old Lane A worst case of approximately 139,000 evaluation episodes before serving: a full all-pairs ladder is not necessary to answer this first question.

## 6. Q3/Q4: search and opponent modeling that can lead to a bare agent

Search is valuable here as a **training teacher with a measured transfer test**. It must not become a hidden serving veto. Expert Iteration provides a precedent for separating planning from policy generalization; it does not guarantee that B8's observations can represent this planner. [Anthony et al., primary paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2017/hash/d8e1344e27a5b08cdfd5d027d9b8d6de-Abstract.html).

**Q3 teacher pilot, fixed before gameplay:** B8 acts normally except every 16th hero decision while length < 700. At those decisions enumerate all legal first actions, roll each for 64 frames in the exact simulator with B8 thereafter and the actual fixed opponents, using common random numbers across alternatives. Score the undiscounted 64-frame hero mass integral with dead frames zero; no binary survival label, kill bonus, geometry rule or Q-unit mixing. Choose the highest score; ties retain B8's action if tied, then the registered action order. Restore all simulator, opponent carry and RNG state after each hypothetical branch. The planner may use privileged full simulator state during training; the student may not.

Test this one teacher versus B8 on **32 scripted /16 frozen /16 mixed** fresh H5000 paired worlds:128 episodes, one teacher, no horizon/trigger sweep. Require equal-mix mean gain ≥ 20 and a one-sided 90% paired-world bootstrap lower bound > 0, scripted mass ≥ 0, alive-fraction differences ≥ −0.03 in every mix, and teacher no-death fraction ≥ 0.50 in each mix. Here there is one fixed teacher, not five training seeds, so the uncertainty is conditional on this teacher. This small test only licenses data collection. Any valid failure ends this proposal; a12 CPU-runtime-hour cap or unfinished coverage means INCOMPLETE. Benchmark an excluded development world first and decline the full pilot if it cannot fit with 20% reserve.

This differs from the closed late-death label work: ordinary on-policy states, length < 700, near-term **mass** optimization, and a demonstrated full-episode productivity gain before distillation. If it merely trades mass for survival, stop. It is not a back door to re-run LOCAL30 or promise to solve the L≈1,000 tail.

**Learnability gate before Q4's five-seed screen:** collect at most 50,000 teacher-labeled decisions from at least 256 new training worlds, equal world allocations across the three mix families up to deterministic rounding, plus an independent 128-world validation bank. Split by complete world, never adjacent states. First fit the unchanged B8 network to teacher action preferences; evaluate on held-out states where teacher and B8 disagree and the 64-step average-mass advantage is ≥ 5. Require at least 1,000 such validation rows spanning at least 64 worlds and ≥ 70% top-action agreement on them. If support is insufficient or the criterion fails, do not infer a network improvement from a low overall imitation loss. Close unchanged-input transfer for this planner.

For a passing pilot, Q4 uses five independently initialized student/control fine-tunes from B8, three fixed data-aggregation rounds, maximum 50,000 new labels/round across training, and 50,000 optimizer steps per run total. Both branches collect on their own trajectories with the same fixed roster mixture. Student targets are the teacher's legal-action preferences; control targets are frozen B8's legal-action preferences with the same query schedule. Use masked cross-entropy on the six Q outputs, temperature 1; retain 50% ordinary B8 imitation rows in every batch. Freeze LR `1e-5`, batch 256, optimizer-step allocations 16,000/17,000/17,000 for the three rounds, and all masks before data. No online TD or reward modification in this transfer test; it is explicitly a different supervised recipe. Teacher queries are capped and charged, not called free.

Only the final bare checkpoints decide, on fresh 64/32/32 H5000 worlds per seed against B8 and their matched imitation controls: **1,920 episodes**. Require Q1 efficacy/guards and lower90(student−control) > 0. The teacher may win while the student fails; that is a distillation gap, not authorization to serve the teacher. If action/Q scale calibration breaks required inspector or serving contracts, repair and requalify under a new registered step rather than equate classification logits with calibrated returns.

**Opponent modeling:** first use the known frozen policies in the training simulator to generate counterfactual data. Do not feed a checkpoint ID into the served network as if it were an observation. If a later representation experiment is justified, predict opponents' next 1/4/8-step motion from observable positions/headings and test on held-out policy identities and worlds; compare against the existing enemy-next feature. Prefer an auxiliary head that can be removed at serving. Predictive accuracy alone does not admit a new policy: it must improve the same paired gameplay endpoint. Exact-policy search is an upper-bound teacher for this fixed-opponent task, not evidence of human-opponent generalization.

## 8. Queue contract and the final Mac decision

The Drive queue should schedule **experiments with dependencies and caps**, not arbitrary commands until credits run out. Each manifest binds study/arm/replica, source/environment/config hashes, input/checkpoint/wrapper hashes, schedule and pool versions, seed banks, exact endpoint, hardware eligibility, deadlines, unit reservation and acceptance rules. Distinguish actual decisions D, emitted replay rows R, completed updates U, pending update debt, pool decisions and world frames. Preserve R/U=256 after accounting for startup/tails; bs2 does not mean 256 SGD samples.

Pre-create the shared Drive directory tree from one coordinator. T23 produced duplicate `evaluation` folders from concurrent creation; string-identical paths are not sufficient identity. Use immutable directory/file IDs and unique job/attempt paths. Drive FUSE is storage, not a transactional lock service: use one assigning coordinator, or a separately verified lease service. Lease expiry must not authorize a second active scientific trainer. Duplicate deliveries may publish one validated result only; conflicting hashes quarantine the job.

Run hot logs/rings/checkpoints on local storage, copy incrementally to durable storage, verify hashes, then publish a completion receipt last. A queue status or a clean trainer exit is not scientific completion. The aggregator requires every expected row, clean process exits and valid identities before analysis. Never substitute the fastest surviving replicas. On interruption, preserve the attempt; exact recovery requires full validated state, otherwise mark INCOMPLETE and register any new attempt explicitly. No silent weights-only resume, automatic retry after KILL, unbounded adaptive search, or default serving switch.

An advanced recipe still needs a **new B8-comparator Mac protocol**, not a replay of T7 against the previous incumbent. Use the current sequential Phase R default, with pinned rule/plan, predeclared looks, spending boundaries, operating-characteristic checks and audited receipts. The [sequential amendment](../governance_amendment_sequential_phase_r_2026-10-07.md) explicitly separates Phase R selection from the fresh strict gate; a development GO does not promote. Freeze the selected recipe, five final checkpoints, selected-checkpoint rule and all new banks before the appropriate stage. Do not edit T7's bound bytes or reuse its worlds.

Preserve the [T7 rule structure](../../../research/redesign_t7_phase_r_20261016/rule_spec_t7.py) with B8 as the primary comparator: equal-mix H5000 mass lower bound > 0 and point ≥ 20, scripted mass ≥ 0, ≥ 4/5 positive seed effects, exact prefix controls and H10000 mass point ≥ 20, feasibility cap 600, alive-fraction floors −0.03 per mix and −0.15 per-seed scripted, and no-death episode floor 0.50 per mix. Retain historical FRP survival checks and the champion guard, and require Tier 2 scripted no-death difference versus champion+v8 ≥ −0.05. The sequential look-specific boundaries come from the validated new plan, not naive repeated 90% intervals. Opt out of optional G=50 Phase R futility in this proposal so it cannot automatically discard a potentially meaningful 20–49 mass gain; retain feasibility and the later strict requirements. Changed rules/populations require the amendment's operating-characteristic validation before ratification.

After fresh strict skill evidence, run the required identity/parity/latency/refusal/rollback serving qualification for the selected **single bare checkpoint**. Existing v8-derived input features are allowed by B8's release contract; veto wrappers, search, ensemble selection and opponent-ID oracles are absent from serving. Product-roster measurements are useful secondary confirmation and disclosure, not replacements for the fixed-opponent endpoint. Keep B8 served unless that complete chain passes and release is authorized.

The first useful queue is therefore **audit → exact-workload qualification → schedule×collection factorial → one evidence-selected continuation**. T23 argues for preserving and measuring competence before increasing opponent difficulty. The credit budget is ample for these tests; the scarce resource is a clean answer about what actually makes the bare policy stronger.
