B8 round-62 probe states, length >=700, at least 300 decisions from any death,
matched 1:1 to decisive rows by length bucket and -max Q decile. Targets are B8's
own actions, single-action hinge, weight 0.5 and separate margin scale 1.0
(`--label-bank-outside-margin-scale 1`), giving 0.06319992542266846 Q.
Data provenance and exact matching conventions are recorded in the data receipt.
