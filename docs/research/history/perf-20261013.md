# CPU, Simulator & Learner Performance Optimization (`perf-20261013` / `simd-vector61` / `perf-sim` / `S0`–`S2`)

## Question & Goal

Can bit-exact simulator geometry optimizations (`perf-sim`), Numba-vectorized `vector61`/`ego2s` feature and opponent-veto kernels (`simd-vector61`, `actor_perf_20261017`, `t34_speed_s2`), and learner batch-scaling/compilation (`perf_*_20261013`) deliver `1.5–5.5x` wall-clock speedups across live `GameState` evaluation, `GridBatchSim` actor collection, `bv8` learner updates, and T34 teacher rollouts without altering policy decisions or evaluation parity?

## Final Verdict & Key Numbers

### 1. Batched `vector61` Featurizer & Policy (`simd-vector61`, 2026-09-26 to 2026-10-03)
- **Parity & Speedup:** `Vector61Featurizer` (`src/simd_env/vector61_featurizer.py`) and `Vector61SimdPolicy` (`src/simd_env/vector61_policy.py`) matched the live profiled Watch evaluator (`tournament_eval.rollout`) decision for decision across `~66,000` vector decisions (about `28,000` in committed tests + `38,000` in probes, `0` divergences) and **`48/48` `H5000` evaluation records** (`3.65x` aggregate wall speedup: `367.5 s` SIMD vs `1,341.1 s` live) ([`docs/research/simd_vector61_plan_2026-09-26.md`](../simd_vector61_plan_2026-09-26.md)).
- **Veto Parity:** the `v5` boost-aware veto matched `12/12` sampled `H5000` B records (SIMD batch `126.3 s` vs live sum `251.3 s`). The `v7`/`v8` hooks passed constructed-state unit parity (`tests/test_simd_vector61_v7v8_veto.py`); their `H5000` record-level check (`research/simd_parity_v7v8_h5000_20261003/h5000_check.py`) was prepared but not run in this source ([`docs/research/simd_vector61_plan_2026-09-26.md`](../simd_vector61_plan_2026-09-26.md)).

### 2. Live Simulator Geometry Optimization (`perf-sim`, 2026-10-06 to 2026-10-07)
- **Strict Gate & Actor Throughput (`git 68c0efe`):** On the M5 Pro Mac, the per-snake geometry cache (`src/game/snake_geometry.py`), integer squared-radius checks, and flood-fill reuse increased strict-world `H5000` evaluation (`frp3-s12 + v8`) from `111 -> 233` episodes/h/core (**`2.10x`**; `2.42x`, `92 -> 224`, on an earlier perf-sim commit), FRP-v3 gate-world actor throughput from `145 -> 317` rows/s (**`2.19x`**), and default Apex arena throughput from `2,031 -> 2,634` rows/s (`1.30x`) (`docs/research/perf_plan_2026-10-07.md`, removed in consolidation; see git c4c4ab41; [`docs/notes/gpu-engine-and-speed.md`](../../notes/gpu-engine-and-speed.md)).
- **Identity Proof:** All identical across `42` strict-world `H5000` episodes (`210k` frames; per-frame world digests and every forward's float32 input/output bytes, `730k` rows), `15` FRP-v3 gate-world actor episodes (`55.9k` buffer rows), and `23` default-arena actor episodes (`9.9k` rows) (`research/perf_sim_20261007/results/identity_summary.json` per the perf plan).

### 3. Quiet-Host Learner, Numba Actor & Batch-Scale Pass (`perf_*_20261013`, 2026-10-13 to 2026-10-14)
- **M5 Pro Learner (`bench_learner`, `bv8`):** Baseline `35.0 ms/update` (`3,658 tr/s`) improved to **`30.4 ms/update`** (**`1.15x`**, `4,212 tr/s`) with `--learner-fast` (`t1`), and to **`44.7 ms / 256 tr`** (**`1.57x`**, `5,730 tr/s`) with `t1 + --batch-scale 2`. `torch.compile` was not used on MPS (wrong gradients in torch 2.9.1 / MPS Inductor conv-bias gradient errors; auto-fallback to eager; `research/perf_compile_20261013/README.md`) ([`research/perf_quiet_20261013/RESULTS.md`](../../../research/perf_quiet_20261013/RESULTS.md)).
- **M5 Pro Actor (`bench_actor`, B8 flags):** Numba `vector61` (`--v61-backend numba`) sped up short-body actors **`1.9–2.1x`** (`4,210 -> 7,845 steps/s` at `E=64`, `5,337 -> 11,300 steps/s` at `E=256`) and long-body actors (`--min-init-len 400 --max-init-len 1200`) **`5.3–5.5x`** (`1,184 -> 6,264 steps/s` at `E=64`, `1,336 -> 7,324 steps/s` at `E=128`) ([`research/perf_quiet_20261013/RESULTS.md`](../../../research/perf_quiet_20261013/RESULTS.md)).
- **M5 Pro End-to-End Trainer (`bench_e2e` & 3M-Frame A/B):** Learner-bound at `3,726–3,986 tr/s` across `64×2`..`256×4`, rising to **`5,364 tr/s`** at `128×2 + --batch-scale 2`. In a single-seed (seed 0) 20M-transition B8 A/B with a no-veto 32-worlds × 3-mixes dev probe (`/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/perf-ab-k2-20261014/`), `K2` (`--batch-scale 2`) reached **`537.5` final mass** (`+102.6` vs `frp3-s12+v8`, SE 27; survival frozen/scripted/mixed `0.69/0.59/0.81`) in **`1.49 h`** vs `K1`'s `527.3` (`+92.4`, SE 28; `0.81/0.62/0.75`) in `2.10 h` (K2 − K1 = `+10`, well inside one SE; **`1.41x` faster wall**) ([`research/perf_quiet_20261013/RESULTS.md`](../../../research/perf_quiet_20261013/RESULTS.md)).
- **RunPod RTX 4090 (x86 EPYC, Torch 2.5.1+cu124):** Learner baseline `14.9 ms/update` (`8,603 tr/s`) reached **`8.4 ms/update`** (**`1.74x`**, **`15,170 tr/s`**; **`22,212 tr/s`** with `k2`) under `t1 + --compile-learner reduce-overhead`, while EPYC actor cores were `~2.4x` slower per actor than the M5 Pro (B8-recipe single actor at 64 worlds: `3,338` vs Mac `7,845`; long bodies `2,614` vs `6,264`) ([`research/perf_quiet_20261013/RESULTS.md`](../../../research/perf_quiet_20261013/RESULTS.md), `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/perf-cuda-compile-20261014-try1/out/`).
- **Multi-Seed Stacked Learner (`perf_multiseed_20261013`):** On MPS, `vmap` stacking of `K in {1,2,4}` `Ego2sNet` learners was slower than sequential updates (`0.45x`–`0.86x`); a hand-grouped `groups=K` forward was `1.16–1.42x` faster than in-process sequential updates at `K = 2, 4` but only about `3–16%` faster than `K` separate processes, so integration was **`NO-GO`** (revisit only if, among other conditions, grouped reaches `>= 1.5x` `procs`) ([`research/perf_multiseed_20261013/DESIGN.md`](../../../research/perf_multiseed_20261013/DESIGN.md)).

### 5. T34 Speed `S0` Baseline & `S2` Native Numba `v8` Opponent Veto (2026-10-20 to 2026-10-21)
- **`S0` Contended M5 Pro Baseline (`REFERENCE_MAC.json` sha `2561df19...`):** `103.1 s` per 6-world `H300` round (`209` worlds/h, `155` active row-steps/s, `16,021` row-steps/round); pooled worker shares were **`v8` `56.8%`**, inference `13.5%`, digest `11.9%`, other `5.7%`, raster `4.6%`, sim `3.4%` (`long-frozen` `v8` `56.5%`, `long-mixed` `v8` `67.0%`) -> D1: digest + v8 `>= 40%` on all three long-body mixes, so S1 and S2 continue ([`research/speed_s0_baseline/RESULT_MAC_CONTENDED_01.md`](../../../research/speed_s0_baseline/RESULT_MAC_CONTENDED_01.md)).
- **`S2` Native Numba `v8` Hook (`fcb0b16a`, `s2-matched-mac-contended-01/matched-s0.json` sha `cf8320ec...`):** The opt-in native `v8` veto (`research/t34_speed_s2/native_v8.py`) cut median 6-world round wall time from **`98.534 s` to `51.878 s`** (**`1.8994x` throughput, `-47.35%` wall**; `long-mixed` `70.810 -> 27.615 s` = `2.56x`; `long-frozen` `11.476 -> 7.719 s` = `1.49x`), with **`144/144` retained files byte-identical** to `REFERENCE_MAC.json`, `2,995/2,995` stock-audited native rows exact (`s2-respawn-audit-01/respawn-audit.json` sha `2ac1eee5...`), and `33` tests passing in `18.78 s` -> **`D3 PASS`** ([`research/t34_speed_s2/RESULT_D3_MAC_CONTENDED_01.md`](../../../research/t34_speed_s2/RESULT_D3_MAC_CONTENDED_01.md)).
- **Mac vs L4 `S0` Export Comparison (`L4_MISMATCH.md` sha `4870327f...`):** Saved Mac and Colab L4 `S0` exports differed only in stored `labels[*].q[*]`: `4,443 / 10,800` Q scalars (`1,120 / 1,800` labels, max absolute `7.62939453125e-6`); all `1,800` root action entries and every other field agreed, with smallest stored top-two Mac margin `0.000289` (303 FP32 ULP). Cross-platform byte identity remains failed; the exact backend cause is unproven (`REVIEW_20261021.md`, removed in consolidation; see git c4c4ab41; [`research/t34_speed_s2/RESULT_D3_MAC_CONTENDED_01.md`](../../../research/t34_speed_s2/RESULT_D3_MAC_CONTENDED_01.md)).

## What Was Learned & Current Status

1. **Long-body Python `v8` veto dominated CPU wall time:** In T34 `H300` teacher rollouts (`S0`), the opponent `v8` veto hook took `56.5%` (long-frozen) and `67.0%` (long-mixed) of worker wall time. Numba backends delivered **`1.9–5.5x`** single-actor speedups for `vector61` (`--v61-backend numba`) and **`1.8994x`** whole-workload throughput for the native `v8` veto with byte-identical S0 exports.
2. **The MPS learner is latency/launch-bound, not compute-bound:** No MPS multi-seed arm exceeded `1.5 TFLOP/s` of useful work, `vmap` stacking lost to layout copies, and `torch.compile` was unusable on MPS (wrong gradients), whereas doubling batch size (`--batch-scale 2` + `--learner-fast`) raised throughput **`1.57x`** (`3,658 -> 5,730 tr/s`) with no evidence of quality loss in the single-seed A/B (`+10`, inside one SE). On CUDA (`RTX 4090`), `torch.compile(mode="reduce-overhead")` delivered **`1.74x`** (`15,170 tr/s`).
3. **Status:** **CLOSED.** `perf-sim` was merged (`68c0efe`); `--learner-fast`, `--batch-scale 2`, `--v61-backend numba` and `--obs-pack` were adopted as production flags; the `S2` native `v8` veto is an opt-in flag with default execution unchanged; the multi-seed stacked learner was not integrated.
