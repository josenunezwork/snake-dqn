# Evaluation, distributions, and decision value

The research has demonstrated learning; it has not demonstrated a reliably better full-game agent. My diagnosis is that narrow success packages, adaptive follow-ups, and incomplete distribution infrastructure now limit decision value alongside genuine behavioral weaknesses. This is a prospective design recommendation, not a reclassification of any sealed result. No raw trajectories, checkpoints, new numerical analyses, or provisional counterfactual results were used.

## What the evidence actually establishes

The smallest Apex scaling condition collected 292/301/293 foods on fresh H64 roots, versus teacher 304, random 14, and its own initial 1/2/31. That is substantial food learning. The same study's historical compatibility package failed and no width/breadth contrast passed. Both statements matter ([H64 report](../experiments/apex_scaling_width_breadth_2026-09-23/README.md), lines 50–81). H256 endpoints likewise collected 830–1,007 foods against teacher 1,046, but none passed the complete package ([H256 factorial report](../experiments/apex_scaling_h256_2026-09-24/README.md), lines 11–45). “No reliable scaling benefit” answers the frozen comparison; it does not mean learning was absent or every promising direction has been excluded.

A conjunction of positive gains in every training seed, nonregression on several metrics, and perfect survival is a demanding reliability criterion. It is not an estimate of expected benefit or a powered equivalence test. Three trained seeds remain three algorithm replications, however many roots each sees; world pairing improves environmental precision without eliminating training variability. The H256 practical margins were borrowed, not validated minimum detectable effects (same report, lines 9–13). Conversely, the gates did identify real limitations: effect directions reverse between seeds, old-bank retention fails, and continuation deaths remain. This is not merely an unfortunate threshold crossing.

The earlier PQN line shows the same distinction. BR improved mean food over control in all three lineages while its declared fit and reliability conjunction failed ([BR](../experiments/solo_food_own_policy_coverage_2026-09-20/README.md), lines 3–9, 46–77). Later CW passed its full fresh H768 solo package, yet CX lost paired solo retention with one opponent ([CW](../experiments/solo_food_pqn_h768_length_confirmation_2026-09-21/README.md), lines 3–6; [CX](../experiments/solo_food_pqn_one_opponent_2026-09-21/README.md), lines 25–49). Local progress and missing competitive transfer coexist.

## Distribution and selection are the central boundaries

H64/H256 random play survived every calibration game. Survival there cannot establish learned survival; food acquisition changes body growth and therefore exposure to self-trapping. A weak food collector can survive by avoiding the challenge. Future reports should retain unconditional total-horizon performance, then add prespecified growth/exposure diagnostics without presenting outcome-conditioned survivor subsets as causal comparisons. The audited terminal/pretrap results describe selected deaths, not preventability or a training mechanism ([reference H256](../experiments/apex_reference_h256_2026-09-23/README.md), lines 17–29; [pretrap](../experiments/apex_pretrap_choice_2026-09-23/README.md), lines 14–16).

The fresh H64 bank was generated after endpoints were sealed, a useful protection. Its state-overlap check covered bound replays, not all historical knowledge. The subsequent H256 factorial evaluation reused an observed bank, and its reference arm was chosen after results for cost and simplicity. That is useful development evidence, not independent confirmation ([H64](../experiments/apex_scaling_width_breadth_2026-09-23/README.md), lines 29–33, 94–96; [H256 factorial](../experiments/apex_scaling_h256_2026-09-24/README.md), line 13).

Root IDs alone were insufficient: the continuation's proposed holdout matched 48 old initial geometries despite zero matching IDs. A later exclusion attempt encountered descriptors with no initial food coordinate; later-event `second_*` fields cannot repair that identity ([continuation](../experiments/apex_horizon_continuation_2026-09-23/README.md), lines 103–120; [space ranking](../experiments/apex_space_ranking_2026-09-23/README.md), lines 3–15). Preserve both closed outcomes. Prospectively, build a versioned descriptor registry with explicit missingness, initial geometry, generator lineage, split role, and exposure history. If historical exclusion cannot be established, state its bounded scope or use a demonstrably separate generator region; never silently impute novelty. Qualify schema and partition mechanics on disposable fixtures before expensive training, while keeping confirmation outcomes concealed.

Matching initial roots does not force identical later food: controllers consume native RNG differently. Estimate policy performance under a common initial-state/generator distribution, and label that estimand honestly. Teacher ratios are useful anchors, not universal ceilings: teacher behavior, horizon, mechanics, and food process define the comparison. Qualification that selects only teacher-solvable cases also conditions the task distribution and must be declared.

## A prospective hierarchy

1. **Learning progress:** publish fixed-dose curves and effect sizes for all seeds, with uncertainty over training seeds and clustered world families; separate fresh starts from inherited lineages. Designate one primary behavioral endpoint and treat fit, food suffixes, and agreement as secondary diagnostics.
2. **Reliability:** separately estimate failure frequency, training-seed dispersion, and performance across prespecified strata. Keep genuine safety/compatibility requirements, but distinguish sampled zero failures from proof of zero population risk. Report every existing gate unchanged alongside these new prospective estimands.
3. **Transfer and promotion:** evaluate frozen policies first on calibrated solo and opponent distributions, then use the existing tournament authority. Total-horizon mass matters because food can be spent on boost: CW's high-food seeds had widely different recorded mass integrals ([CW](../experiments/solo_food_pqn_h768_length_confirmation_2026-09-21/README.md), lines 58–61). Solo food cannot substitute for competitive living mass.

Repeated tiny studies are useful when each eliminates a meaningful decision branch. Their costs rise when every miss spawns another outcome-informed diagnostic on the same cohort. Reusing an observed bank is efficient development, but repeated designs can adapt to it without gradient leakage. Maintain a development suite, reserve one concealed confirmation campaign, and retire a confirmation bank to development once it informs the next design.

## One decisive next campaign

Prioritize a **fixed-policy deployment-gap benchmark** before training or the proposed fresh-H256 width-by-breadth interaction replication. Ask: *How much competitive living-mass capability do the already competent solo policies retain under a declared multiopponent deployment distribution?* Reuse CW's completed solo and CX's completed one-opponent evidence; do not repeat those assessments.

First perform static admission of all three fixed CV/CW endpoints: authenticate the restricted input transform, wrapper, masks, checkpoint loader, mechanics, and evaluation metric. CW explicitly retained its special pipeline ([CW](../experiments/solo_food_pqn_h768_length_confirmation_2026-09-21/README.md), lines 10–20); stock tournament compatibility is not established. If comparable serving cannot be specified faithfully, stop the design with zero new queries. Do not improvise a bridge or compare results from incompatible engines.

If admitted, freeze one six-slot native profile, opponent mixture, horizon, fresh world-family split, and resource ceiling before model outcomes. Evaluate every fixed endpoint against the incumbent and scripted anchors under that common profile. Reuse compatible old controls wherever identities permit; new controls are justified only by the new distribution. Primary output is paired total-horizon mass deficit or gain; food, survival time, encounter exposure, and existing horizon prefixes explain that gap. Prespecify a practically meaningful deficit and interval precision from the intended product requirement. An upper confidence bound below the negative deficit margin identifies a meaningful gap; an interval spanning that margin is inconclusive. Neither outcome retrospectively changes solo success or authorizes promotion.

All three saved policies are required; they are fixed-policy screening evidence, not a newly replicated recipe. Limit this to one baseline event resolving serving and competitive mass. CX already establishes opponent retention loss; require no chain of additional diagnoses before training. Then freeze one controlled comparison aimed at the uncovered gap: refreshed native experience versus replay of fixed experience at matched optimizer dose, with complete continuation state, target-update clocks, and mixture changes specified. Set fixed endpoints, precision, futility and retention rules before training. A later dose-by-experience factorial is contingent, not another automatic campaign. Three fresh seeds can screen; recipe confirmation needs uncertainty over training seeds and a precision-based replication budget. Equal updates are not equal compute: wider models cost roughly twice as much ([H64](../experiments/apex_scaling_width_breadth_2026-09-23/README.md), lines 23–39). These recommendations authorize no numerical execution here.

The strongest counterargument is that the descriptive positive width-by-breadth interaction could replicate cheaply on fresh H256 worlds, yielding cleaner evidence than a new distribution. That is valid if capacity interaction is the actual decision. But three seeds, H64-trained policies, random-survival saturation, and weak H256 factor consistency make it a lower priority for the broader full-game objective. Strict old gates correctly prevented overclaiming; the remedy is a sharper prospective question and informative distribution, not relaxed historical criteria.
