# 2026-09-13 Four-Hour Improvement Program and Same-Day Follow-Up Studies

## Question and Goal

On 2026-09-13, following the September 6–12 state-system audit and `watch_pre_move_v1` / `raster31v3` decision-phase repair ([`docs/pqn_decision_phase_2026-09-12.md`](../../pqn_decision_phase_2026-09-12.md)), a bounded four-hour RL campaign (`2026-09-13T02:47:41Z` to `06:47:41Z`) and its immediate same-day single-factor follow-up studies tested whether the repaired synchronous `raster31v3` PQN stack could learn repeatable full-horizon mass growth against fixed `random_safe` and `greedy_food` scripted opponents (`5,000` frames, post-alive logical mass with dead frames scored as `0`) and challenge the incumbent `vector61` Apex champion. Across the base four-hour window and subsequent matched-seed interventions—covering extended training curves (`50k -> 150k`), living-mass reward overlays (`beta = 0.003`), randomized initial cardinal headings, MPS throughput qualification, lower Adam learning rate (`1e-4` vs `5e-4`), common fresh-world retention across 12 checkpoints, canonical Watch scripted anchors, higher exploration floors (`eps_end = 0.20` vs `0.02`), and valid-hero food-contact telemetry—the program sought to isolate why PQN policies failed to sustain growth or collapsed into deterministic turning loops.

## Final Verdict and Key Numbers

- **Program Verdict:** **No PQN candidate advanced to the promotion gate; `vector61` Apex remained the incumbent.** Extending the unchanged `raster31v3` PQN recipe from `50k` to `150k` transitions reduced full-horizon mass on both seeds in the four-hour curve (`-1.143075` and `-6.426900` vs `50k`; `-0.492600` and `-0.642600` vs `initial`, `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/four-hour-20260913/next-learning-evaluation/v2/learning-curve/execution/terminal.json`), and every single-factor intervention tested later that day—living-mass reward `beta = 0.003` (`+10.40505` / `+0.56000` on seed `1317`, then `-0.58615` / `-0.20370` on seed `1318`, `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/mass-objective-20260913/decision.json`), randomized initial cardinal headings (`+7.28540` / `+7.98765` on seed `1321`, then `-0.36315` / `-0.38565` on seed `1322`, `docs/experiments/direction_coverage_2026-09-13/README.md` (removed in consolidation; see git c4c4ab41)), Adam `lr = 1e-4` (`+1.31100` / `+1.57175` on seed `1341`, then `-13.53755` / `-5.05230` on seed `1342`, `docs/experiments/learning_rate_2026-09-13/README.md` (removed in consolidation; see git c4c4ab41)), and `eps_end = 0.20` (`8/12` conditions, `-0.5610` in seed `1502` random-safe, `docs/experiments/exploration_floor_2026-09-13/README.md` (removed in consolidation; see git c4c4ab41))—failed two-seed replication across both opponent mixes.
- **Looping and Asymmetry Diagnosis:** The `80,000`-frame `O1` trajectory trace (`/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/four-hour-20260913/research/objective-trace-results.md`) proved that the four-hour 50k checkpoints chose relative right on **`30,128 / 30,171` (`99.86%`)** valid decisions (`99.933%` seed 1, `99.720%` seed 2), dropping food collection from `543` to `5` and boost from `1,269` to `0` while entering exact period-4 head-position loops (`4–25` cells visited). However, re-evaluating 12 checkpoints on 8 common fresh worlds (`common_world_retention`, `docs/experiments/common_world_retention_2026-09-13/README.md` (removed in consolidation; see git c4c4ab41)) showed that late collapse is **heterogeneous** across cohorts/seeds rather than universal (seed `1342` `50k -> 150k` gained `+4.6574` in random-safe to `10.4106` and `+1.8489` in greedy-food to `4.7766`, and seed `1317` control in `mass_objective` favored left turns without a period-4 loop).
- **Retained Engineering Gains:** The campaign permanently landed packed `uint32` `np.maximum.at` tactical raster painting (`-11.206%` CPU / `-23.165%` MPS update wall, `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/four-hour-20260913/profiling/raster-optimization/performance-results.md`), MPS update qualification (`2.361×` and `2.534×` over CPU2, `docs/experiments/compute_probe_2026-09-13/README.md` (removed in consolidation; see git c4c4ab41)), `--simd-body-storage-policy` capacity handling in [`src/scripts/tournament_eval.py`](../../../src/scripts/tournament_eval.py), `--archive-checkpoints` immutable checkpoint manifests and `--living-mass-reward-coefficient` in [`src/scripts/train_pqn.py`](../../../src/scripts/train_pqn.py), `CanonicalWatchScriptedPolicy` in [`src/training/rollout_policies.py`](../../../src/training/rollout_policies.py), and `hero_food_contact_events` telemetry in [`src/training/pqn_trainer.py`](../../../src/training/pqn_trainer.py).

## What Was Learned

1. **Single-seed descriptive gains in 6-snake PQN frequently reverse on a second independent training seed:** Four consecutive interventions (`mass_objective`, `direction_coverage`, `learning_rate`, `exploration_floor`) looked strongly positive on their first training seed or on `greedy_food` alone, then reversed on the second training seed or `random_safe`. Two-seed multi-mix gates prevented false promotions in every case.
2. **Objective alignment and minimum-mass survival loops:** Under `gamma = 0.997` and sparse event rewards, surviving in a tight 4-cell loop avoids collision penalties while sacrificing food growth; conversely, adding an unconditional living-mass bonus (`beta = 0.003`) can still reward surviving at `mass = 1` (as observed in `mass_objective` seed `2026091318`, where all random-safe rows had mean alive mass `1.0`).
3. **Training/evaluation opponent clock parity matters:** `common_world_retention` identified that historical training opponent wrappers indexed frames differently from production Watch evaluation (`prepared frame 1 -> anchor frame 0`), motivating `CanonicalWatchScriptedPolicy` (`canonical_anchor`) and ultimately the stripped-down solo food-seeking diagnostic series to isolate basic food acquisition from multi-snake confounds.
