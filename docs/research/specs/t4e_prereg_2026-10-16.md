The M6 CTRL recipe is retained exactly, including its fast learner and batch
scale, full on-policy v8 anchor, constant lambda 0.1, and no margin bank:

```text
--demo-data redesign-m2b-20261008/data
--transitions 8000000 --device mps --actors 2 --worlds 128
--segment-frames 5000 --reward mass --mass-kappa 1.0 --kill-scale 0
--gamma-start 0.998 --gamma-end 0.998 --gamma-hold 0 --gamma-anneal-end 1
--eps-end-at 1 --mixed-frac 0.25 --anchor-mode v8margin
--anchor-hold 0 --anchor-floor-at 1 --anchor-floor 0.1
--onpolicy-labels --label-frac 1
--obs-version bv8 --v61-backend numba --learner-fast --batch-scale 2 --obs-pack
--checkpoint-every 2000000 --tight-telemetry --td-huber-delta 1.0
--return-bank BANK --return-frac 0.5 --return-weight 1.0 --return-huber-delta 100
```

Other defaults, including frozen fraction 0.25 and death value -3, are those of
M6 CTRL. No reward overlay, margin bank, snapshot restart, learner compilation,
or update-every change is introduced. `BANK` is the completed, validated training
return bank, excluding every holdout world. Its final path and hashes will be
bound in the launch-input receipt after collection.

**Sampling:** `F=0.5` yields `round(128 * batch_scale(2) * 0.5) = 128`
additional return branch rows per update. The full on-policy anchor is retained:
its configured batch is 256 rows with M6's batch scale 2, subject to existing
label-buffer warmup/validity rules. Return rows do not replace anchor or TD rows.
Sampling is with replacement, as in the validated T4d tooling.

**Separate Huber deltas:** TD remains at 1.0; return regression uses 100.
Fatal and surviving branch returns differ by approximately Phi(L)+3, around
100 Q. Delta 1 would fit a median and erase the magnitude information this arm
is intended to supply (the T2 finding). Delta 100 retains the quadratic region
over those errors. The new flag defaults to the TD delta, preserving existing
behavior when omitted; the six-mode, eight-update default trace is byte-identical
to the pre-change tensors, RNG states, and stable telemetry. Active fast/reference
return tests cover both default and explicit delta 100, TD isolation, sampling
RNG order, and non-finite loss halting. The implementation precedes this document
in commit `a5ade83` (integration/pushed head `b72b759`).

The subsequently merged advisory `../advice/2026-10-16_return_arm_review.md`
suggests gradient-calibrated weight and additional thresholds. Those are advice,
not amendments to this requested spec: **W remains 1.0**, with no calibration,
weight search, or new decision threshold in this arm. Preserve the existing Q,
loss, action-collapse and training-halt telemetry to report the resulting behavior.

## Collection, matching, and holdout

Collection is CPU-only with at most 12 single-thread workers on AC power, in the
new task-owned tmux session `t4e-collect`. Fresh namespace allocation and its seed
audit precede every played batch. Audit repository/artifact evidence and generated
NI/distillation/evaluation exclusions, explicitly including rounds 68 and 69.
Never reuse the T3/T4 label worlds or the T4e evaluation worlds. The five immutable
B8 finals supply both played and continuation policies, evenly across balanced
200-world cycles (40 per final). Preserve the historical collection mix cycle:
27 scripted, 9 mixed, 4 frozen per final. Thus non-frozen worlds are 75% scripted /
25% mixed, with the same additional 10% frozen coverage as before.

Capture eligible prepared states every five decisions, length >=700, with enough
remaining H5000 horizon to support D=300. All advisory-legal actions are actually
rolled for D=300 or until death. A **decisive** state requires the played B8
advisory-masked greedy action to die and at least one legal alternative to survive
the branch horizon. Other death-window states do not count toward the target.
Retain every legal-action outcome for a selected state, including its fatal row;
never infer an unrolled action's outcome. The effective state weight is 1.0 at
death distance <100, and 0.3 at distance 100–299.

Outside states require >=300 observed future decisions, and >=300 decisions to
any observed death. Exclude the last 300 censored decisions of a surviving world.
They have weight 0.5. Match exactly 1:1 to the newly retained training decisive
states by the established T4c length buckets and fixed historical decisive
`-max(Q_B8)` decile cuts. Historical quotas do not substitute for this new bank's
quotas. Exclude observations already in the reference bank or selected decisive
set, enforce unique observations, and do not substitute across strata. Collect
compact outside candidates first, then deterministically select by source-plan
and decision order, replay selected worlds, verify state identity, and roll all
legal branches. Continue fresh balanced cycles until the targets and exact
matching are satisfied; no partial bank is eligible for launch.

Hold out **10% of collection worlds before play**, selected deterministically by
hash within each complete 200-world cycle. Every state and branch from a held-out
world remains outside the training bank. World groups are indivisible, so report
both the exact world fraction and the realized decisive-state fraction (near 10%,
not artificially forced to exactly 10% by splitting worlds). Holdout assignment,
selection order, and stop decisions cannot depend on worker completion order.
Target **>=8,000 training decisive states**, plus their equal number of outside
states, with the held-out decisive states additional to that minimum. Retain the
held-out all-action outcomes for the Q-gap readout.

Bank records follow T4d: forced first action then frozen B8 greedy continuation,
T, died, raw discounted mass sum S, L0, terminal live length, and survivor
terminal observation/mask. With gamma=0.998 and Phi(L)=L/10:

```text
G = kappa (1-gamma) S - Phi(L0)
    + gamma^T Phi(L_T) [survivor] - 3 gamma^(T-1) [died]
y = G + gamma^T Q_target(s_T, argmax_mask Q_online(s_T)) [survivor]
```

The bootstrap is detached. These are expert-rollout targets ("a then B8"), not
returns under the changing learner. Raw S is gamma-dependent; gamma must match
the bank, while kappa may be recomputed. Publish actual state/branch counts,
per-family/per-final/per-mix counts, holdout worlds and fraction, exact matching
receipt, shard hashes, checkpoint identities, seed audits, and measured wall time.
