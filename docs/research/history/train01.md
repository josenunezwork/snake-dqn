# GPU-Resident Long-Horizon PPO on A100 (`train01`, Packets 05–16)

## Question and Goal

Can a fully trainable B8-s3-initialized actor (ego2s, frozen-B8 KL anchor, separate clocked PopArt critic prepared on complete natural lives) be trained with PPO on the GPU-resident simulator-D task (E1024 resident worlds, T256 rollouts, natural H5000 lives, equal `scripted`/`frozen`/`mixed` mixes) to the finite `384`-update / `100,663,296`-actor-row endpoint under the Resume-by-Default contract, and does any checkpoint show a material H5000 mass gain over bare `b8-s3` (T34 bar `>= +40` equal-mix pooled mass) worth one strict Mac crossing? Recipe and boundaries: [`TRAIN01.md`](../../../research/sandbox_a_resident/TRAIN01.md).

## Final Verdict and Key Numbers

1. **Closeout (2026-10-29 Astra advice; recorded closed 2026-10-30):** Source: [`docs/STATUS.md`](../../STATUS.md) (TRAIN-01 bullet under closed Oct 29–31 verdicts), the 2026-10-29/30 row of [`CAMPAIGN_LOG.md`](../final_run_20261019/CAMPAIGN_LOG.md), and `snake-dqn-artifacts/t34-sol/train01-closeout/SUMMARY.md`.
   - The development proxy is H5000, **96 paired worlds per mix**, deterministic masked argmax vs cached bare `b8-s3`, on one repeated development bank (not independent confirmations).
   - Endpoint (update `384`): equal-mix paired mass **`+8.09`** (`CI95 [-18.66, +34.85]`; scripted `+26.32`, frozen `+2.85`, mixed `-4.90`).
   - Update-`320` peak: **`+14.23`** (`[-11.86, +40.32]`; scripted/frozen/mixed `+41.16/+4.60/-3.06`). Earlier proxies: `64` `-0.14`, `128` `-7.99`, `192` `-2.10`, `256` `-3.81`; diagnostic update 16 `-10.91`.
   - Both are far below the **`>= +40`** material-gain bar; no checkpoint was nominated for a strict Mac gate. The intervals are descriptive (no adjustment for repeated monitoring or checkpoint selection).
2. **Engineering facts preserved from the removed `TRAIN01_06`, `TRAIN01_07` and `TRAIN01_10` notes** (see git c4c4ab41):

## What Was Learned

- **Optimization ran, but no material gain appeared:** the late peak is mostly scripted; there is no broad learning slope, and mixed-mix mass is negative at both 320 and 384 (closeout SUMMARY). This is insufficient evidence to advance, not a proof of zero benefit.
- **Low-temperature calibration exposes float32 categorical underflow:** at `T ≈ 0.00844` (`1/T = 118.487`), a B8 advantage gap of `≈ 0.877` pushes a legal action's log-probability below `-103.97`, where float32 `exp()` underflows and PyTorch's categorical KL returns `+inf`; computing legal KL/entropy with float64 `log_softmax` fixed it ([`TRAIN01_12.md`](../../../research/sandbox_a_resident/TRAIN01_12.md), [`TRAIN01_15.md`](../../../research/sandbox_a_resident/TRAIN01_15.md)).
- **NumPy slice views pin parent batches:** copy observation slices before storing them in long-lived buffers (`TRAIN01_06`).
- **Resume snapshots must fit staging transport:** the `1.33 GB` full-state snapshot breached the `300 s` upload cap; a lossless whole-file `xz` derivative brought it to `8.8 MB` ([`TRAIN01_14.md`](../../../research/sandbox_a_resident/TRAIN01_14.md)).

## Why It Closed / Current Status

**Closed (2026-10-29 closeout advice; STATUS records closure 2026-10-30) with zero strict-gate nominations.** Both completion allowances were used; there is no continuation under a new label. The endpoint actor and full state are retained for provenance only. The closeout named the H5000 C/T natural-support successor as the next GPU priority instead of another PPO trajectory (`t34-sol/train01-closeout/SUMMARY.md`).
