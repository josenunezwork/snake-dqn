# Early Solo & Ambient-Food PQN Series (2026-09-13 to 2026-09-21)

Source records cited below as `docs/experiments/...` were removed in consolidation; see git c4c4ab41.

## Question and Goal

Following a six-snake (`E16/S6/T16`, `raster31v3`) PQN screen in which replacing half of the hero's actual $\epsilon$-exploration choices with `scripted:greedy_food` did not produce a repeatable advantage, this lineage isolated learning in an opt-in single-snake (`S1`) lifecycle with a diagnostic-only evaluator (`sole_snake_death_or_frame_cap_v1`, `solo-watch-diagnostic-v1`). Its goal was to determine whether native PQN on `raster31v3` can learn reliable ambient-food collection from scratch in a solo arena, isolate why initial solo food-contact gains failed to replicate across seeds, and trace the subsequent solo-food progression through behavior-cloning warm starts, long-horizon solo survival (`H128`–`H768`), one-opponent transfer, and Mac M5 Pro PQN throughput sizing.

## Final Verdict and Key Numbers

The early solo and ambient-food PQN line closed without promoting a checkpoint ([SOLO_FOOD_SERIES.md](../../experiments/SOLO_FOOD_SERIES.md)). Key quantitative results and their sources:

- **Scripted exploration advice (`scripted_advice_2026-09-13`):** Under `E16/S6/T16` (one hero vs five fixed `random_safe` opponents; epsilon `1.0` to `.02` over 30,000 useful hero transitions), substituting `scripted:greedy_food` on half of actual exploration events gave treatment-minus-control mass deltas of `-2.23530` (`2026091331`) and `-1.59725` (`2026091332`) in the random-safe mix, and `+0.28000` and `+0.06110` in the greedy-food mix. Both seeds failed the predeclared rule; the four worlds per mix are paired diagnostics and no confidence interval was computed (`docs/experiments/scripted_advice_2026-09-13/README.md`, removed in consolidation; see git c4c4ab41).
- **Boost-trail self-eating artifact (`solo_food_survival` & `solo_food_source_trace`):** In the first 50k-step `S1` run on worlds `2026091611–18`, seed `2026091601` increased mean food from `2.500` to `1,100.875` (mass integral `3.087625 -> 4.812475`) while seed `2026091602` rose from `0.125` to `14.375` (mass integral `1.124800 -> 14.437075`) (`docs/experiments/solo_food_survival_2026-09-13/README.md`, removed in consolidation; see git c4c4ab41). Exact source-trace attribution showed `8,679 / 8,807` (`98.5466%`) of `2026091601`'s final contacts were its own boost trail (`128` ambient, mean `16.000`), whereas `2026091602` had `115` ambient (mean `14.375`) and `0` own-trail contacts (`docs/experiments/solo_food_source_trace_2026-09-13/README.md`, removed in consolidation; see git c4c4ab41).
- **Replication failure & action-insensitivity (`solo_ambient_replication` & `solo_food_response`):** On fresh seeds `2026091701–02` and worlds `2026091711–18`, `2026091701` reached `83.625` ambient contacts at 50k (`44.500` own-trail, mass integral `4.028375`, `4/4` conditions passed), but `2026091702` reached `2.500` ambient at 30k and fell to `0.000` contacts at 50k (survival `1.000`, `1/4` passed) (`docs/experiments/solo_ambient_replication_2026-09-13/README.md`, removed in consolidation; see git c4c4ab41). Moving a single donor pellet to adjacent left/straight/right cells across 7 initial Watch states (`168` raw-Q rows) changed every raw-Q vector, with all `42` condition-vector pairs distinct at each checkpoint, yet `0/7` worlds changed greedy action at the final checkpoints (`1702` selected left in all `21` variants) (`docs/experiments/solo_food_response_2026-09-13/README.md`, removed in consolidation; see git c4c4ab41).
- **Ambient reward shaping, 200k budget curve (C2), and exploration floor:**
  - At 50k steps (`solo_ambient_objective`, seeds `2026091801–02`), `ambient_food_reward_coefficient` `0.1` passed only `7/16` descriptive conditions (`2/8` on `1801` with ambient `5.25 -> 0`, `5/8` on `1802` with ambient `4.25 -> 1.25`) (`docs/experiments/solo_ambient_objective_2026-09-13/README.md`, removed in consolidation; see git c4c4ab41).
  - Extending to 200k steps across 3 seeds (`solo_budget_curve` C2, seeds `2026091901–03`, `1,200,657` useful hero transitions) passed `9/24` control conditions, `18/24` ambient conditions, and `9/12` ambient-over-control increments (no advancement): ambient 200k food means were `0` (`1901`), `22.625` (`1902`), and `110.75` (`1903`) (`docs/experiments/solo_budget_curve_2026-09-13/README.md`, removed in consolidation; see git c4c4ab41).
  - Probing the three C2 ambient 200k checkpoints on 24 controlled adjacent-food transitions (`solo_food_signal`) found `24/24` positive native one-step TD residuals per endpoint, but 32-update Adam clone fits decoded only `7/12` (`1901`), `9/12` (`1902`), and `11/12` (`1903`) held-out directions vs the `12/12` bar (`docs/experiments/solo_food_signal_2026-09-13/README.md`, removed in consolidation; see git c4c4ab41).
  - Telemetry analysis (`solo_training_exposure`) showed failed seed `1901` ambient still recorded `2,756` training ambient contacts (`13.765/1k` overall; `15.017/1k` in 175k–200k) vs `3,558` (`17.783/1k`) on `1902` and `3,443` (`17.213/1k`) on `1903` (`docs/experiments/solo_training_exposure_2026-09-13/README.md`, removed in consolidation; see git c4c4ab41). Native zero-SGD capture (`solo_native_pressure`, `8,192` transitions/checkpoint) recorded `6`, `176`, and `243` ambient contacts and overlay credit in `18`, `1,044`, and `1,595` earlier neither-food rows on `1901–03` (`docs/experiments/solo_native_pressure_2026-09-13/README.md`, removed in consolidation; see git c4c4ab41).
  - Raising `eps_end` from `0.02` to `0.10` (`solo_exploration_floor`) increased recorded training ambient contacts (`2,756 -> 3,731`, `3,558 -> 4,203`, `3,443 -> 3,955` vs the C2 ambient arm), but final greedy Watch food was `1.375, 5.000, 2.375` (`16/24` learning, `6/12` C2 nonregression) (`docs/experiments/solo_exploration_floor_2026-09-13/README.md`, removed in consolidation; see git c4c4ab41).
- **BC warm start, long-horizon solo survival, and 1-opponent ceiling ([SOLO_FOOD_SERIES.md](../../experiments/SOLO_FOOD_SERIES.md)):**
  - An observation probe recovered `100%` of `12,288` saved greedy-food teacher actions, and behavior cloning passed the short food microbenchmark `3/3` seeds vs native PQN `1/3` ([SOLO_FOOD_SERIES.md](../../experiments/SOLO_FOOD_SERIES.md)).
  - Warm-started native PQN progressed to `CW` at `H768`, where all three fixed CV finals passed all 37 food-and-survival conditions (mean food `122.51, 116.85, 135.34`; endpoint survivors `353, 364, 383` of `384`), and supervised shared safety heads (`BO`) passed fresh `H512` worlds with `96/96` survivors each (mean food `77.01, 77.73, 77.93`) (`docs/experiments/solo_learning_status_2026-09-20.md`, removed in consolidation; see git c4c4ab41). However, each paired treatment-beats-control rule failed in at least one seed, and adding a single `GreedyFood` opponent (`CX`) broke paired retention in every policy; the `CY` census found `70` of `76` S2 deaths were head collisions ([SOLO_FOOD_SERIES.md](../../experiments/SOLO_FOOD_SERIES.md); `docs/experiments/solo_food_learning_progress_2026-09-21.md`, removed in consolidation; see git c4c4ab41).
- **Mac M5 Pro PQN capacity (`pqn_mac_capacity_2026-09-21`):** Across 7 `(envs, threads)` configurations on the 18-core M5 Pro (`64 GiB`), `E128/T4` processed **`1,284` valid hero transitions/s** (`1,297` and `1,270` reps; `38.0%` faster than `E16/T2` at `930`/s); maximum monitored process-tree RSS was `0.913 GiB` (`docs/experiments/pqn_mac_capacity_2026-09-21/README.md`, removed in consolidation; see git c4c4ab41).

## What Was Learned

1. **Unattributed food counts in `S1` were dominated by boost-trail self-eating:** Without source attribution (`ambient` vs own boost trail), a snake that spends much of its time boosting and re-consuming its own trail can register `1,100.875` mean contacts per world while its mass integral rises only modestly. Source-aware ambient food accounting is mandatory.
2. **Cold-start PQN foraging failure was not explained by missing exposure or an absent food reward path:** The failed seed (`1901`) still saw roughly 12–15 ambient contacts per 1k training steps, controlled adjacent food produced positive one-step TD residuals, and food relocation changed raw $Q$ values, yet final greedy actions did not switch toward food. The sources leave the cause unresolved (epsilon, coverage, forgetting, or other factors).
3. **Supervised/anchored warm starts reached strong solo food and survival through `H768`, but neither solo PQN tweaks nor 1-opponent transfer yielded seed-uniform paired gains:** Every paired treatment-vs-control rule failed in at least one seed, and introducing a single opponent produced deaths dominated by head collisions (`70` of `76`) that broke paired retention.

## Why It Closed

The lineage closed at the **2026-09-21 research stopping point** (`docs/experiments/research_stopping_point_2026-09-21.md`, removed in consolidation; see git c4c4ab41; the record notes work was explicitly resumed later that day) after the solo-food series established that (a) solo food-seeking and survival were reachable via supervised imitation and anchored warm starts, (b) native PQN variants (`eps_end`, $\lambda$, loss shape, reward coefficient) were seed-fragile under paired rules, and (c) solo policies failed paired retention as soon as a single opponent was introduced.
