## Arm and fixed recipe

One new arm, **TWO_SIDED**, five seeds 10–14 initialized respectively from
`redesign-m4b-mac-20261013/B8_s0..4/final.pth`, for 8,000,000 transitions.
These are fine-tunes from weights, not exact optimizer/replay resumes.
**CTRL** reuses `redesign-m6-mac-20261014/CTRL_s10..14/final.pth`; no CTRL retraining.
All artifact paths below are under `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/`.
The five existing CTRL summaries have no halt, 8,000,128 transitions and actor exits
`[0, 0]`; their saved M3-C flags match the common recipe. An unset TD Huber delta
is the same 1.0 loss. Input checkpoint, control, margin and bank hashes are frozen
in `research/t4a_two_sided_20261016/launch_inputs.json`.

Common M6 recipe:

```text
--demo-data redesign-m2b-20261008/data
--transitions 8000000 --device mps --actors 2 --worlds 128
--segment-frames 5000 --reward mass --mass-kappa 1.0 --kill-scale 0
--gamma-start 0.998 --gamma-end 0.998 --gamma-hold 0 --gamma-anneal-end 1

--checkpoint-every 2000000 --tight-telemetry --td-huber-delta 1.0
```

TWO_SIDED adds:

```text
--onpolicy-labels --label-frac 0.5
--label-bank t4-prep-20261015/bank-v2 --label-bank-frac 0.5
--label-bank-counter-mode single-action --label-bank-counter-ratio 1
--label-bank-margin-scale 4.75
```

Lambda is 0.1 throughout. Half the configured anchor batch is the ordinary
on-policy v8 anchor, and half is bank v2. Existing on-policy buffer warmup and
ring-validity rules still apply: early batches can have fewer ordinary rows.
The 128 bank rows in a full batch are 64 decisive plus 64 counters, sampled
with replacement. The stored bank has 10,885 decisive and 5,442 counter rows.
The existing base margin is 0.06319992542266846; the bank margin on both sides is
**0.30019964575767517** (scale 4.75). The ordinary v8 margin is unchanged.

Decisive rows use the weighted surviving-set hinge with bank v2's prefer-nonboost
labels. Counter rows use the weighted single-action DQfD hinge toward B8's stored
action, over all six actions. Counter identity is B8 survival in
`surviving_original`, not membership in the relabeled preferred set: 35 counters
would otherwise be misclassified. No cross-entropy or return-regression target
is introduced. The served artifact remains a bare network.

## Counter matching decision

Matching is assessed before training from the immutable bank on length, frame,
and `-max(Q_B8)` across all six actions. Full quantiles, identities and diagnostic
rules are recorded in `../t4a_counter_matching_2026-10-16.json`.
The material-mismatch screen is absolute standardized mean difference >0.25 or
absolute median difference >0.25 decisive IQR on any variable. This is a
descriptive design threshold, not a statistical hypothesis test or proof of
identical distributions. The observed standardized differences are approximately
0.1000, 0.1110 and 0.0249 respectively; the median checks also pass. Thus the
conditional inverse-density matching option is not needed or enabled for this
arm. Quantile/tail differences remain reported; no Q-based resampling is added.

## Evaluation and decision

Fresh development round **65**, seed audit before evaluation. Each checkpoint
gets **96 scripted, 32 frozen and 32 mixed H5000 worlds**, no veto, rowwise ego2s
forward, advisory mask, numba vector61, fast anchors (the T2 probe path).
CTRL and incumbent frp3-s12 + v8 are probed on the same worlds. The registration
does not authorize reuse of cached results from other rounds. The seed audit
also reserves fresh round **66** for the single allowed extension and records
the checked NI, distillation, T2/T3, rollout-label and observed artifact scope.
Refresh this audit at evaluation launch; registration alone cannot exclude
future use by another task.

Primary is mean paired scripted episode-survival delta versus CTRL across the
five corresponding training seeds, as in T3. Apply KILL for any halted run
(nonzero exit), otherwise:

- **GO:** primary >= +0.06, mass delta versus CTRL >= -30 on every mix, and
  neither frozen nor mixed episode-survival delta below -0.05.
- **KILL:** primary <= 0.
- **EXTEND:** otherwise, once only; 96 additional scripted worlds at round 66
  for TWO_SIDED and CTRL. Re-decide using 192 scripted worlds and the unchanged
  frozen/mixed results; stop. A remaining intermediate result is inconclusive,
  with no second extension.

GO means a later 20M retrain with the bank, then a fresh-bank Phase R under a
guard rule fixed before it. It promotes nothing and is not automatic launch
authorization.

## Registered discriminability readout

Report `bank_agree_decisive` and `bank_agree_counter` per 100k transitions and
at 8M for every seed, plus the five-seed means. The 8M values are the final
100k telemetry window at/just beyond 8M (allow normal actor packet overshoot),
not a selected best checkpoint. Agreement uses raw six-action argmax: surviving
preferred-set membership for decisive rows, equality with the stored B8 action
for counters. Side denominators exclude the other side; missing sides are not
reported as zero agreement.

For the predeclared readout use the five-seed mean and display each seed:
both >0.9 supports the inputs carrying the distinction and labels being
learnable; decisive <0.8 while counter holds (>0.9) points to an input limit
and makes input changes the next research direction. Other combinations are
inconclusive. This diagnostic does not replace the gameplay GO/KILL rule and
is not a proof of representational impossibility.

Retain existing `bank_agree`, bank loss/rows, TD/anchor/Q, greedy boost and
tightness telemetry. Secondary reports follow T3: alive-frame survival, mass
versus incumbent by mix, death lengths, final greedy boost rate, 2M and 8M bank
agreement, and a post-hoc re-roll of 200 bank states under each final. The re-roll
is descriptive and cannot change the decision rule.

## Post-hoc diagnostics (declared 2026-10-16 before any TWO_SIDED result; diagnostic only)
From the cloud advisor's note `docs/research/advice/2026-10-16_t3_diag_note.md`; they never change
the GO/KILL/EXTEND decision.
1. Spillover: argmax-change rate TWO_SIDED vs CTRL on (a) decisive bank rows, (b) counter bank rows,
   (c) CTRL-trajectory states outside the bank, matched on length bucket and -max Q decile; per
   decile, cross-boost share separately.
2. Growth curve: mean alive length at frames 1000-5000 vs CTRL on shared survivors.
Interpretation, written before the data: counter agree > 0.9 AND (c) low AND decisive agree > 0.9
-> inputs carry the distinction, expert iteration next; decisive high but (c) high -> the loss
still generalises, tighten margin/counter matching before return regression; decisive agree stuck
below ~0.8 while counter agree holds -> states ambiguous at the input, move to inputs.
