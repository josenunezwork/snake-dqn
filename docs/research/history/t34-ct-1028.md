# T34 Counterfactual Learnability + Dynamic-Topology Probe (`t34-ct-1028` / `t34-cf-1028`)

## Question & Goal

When natural 6-snake deaths (`L >= 600`) on `CTDeviceSim` (`t34-cf-v1-terminal-hero-skill-instep-refill`) are rewound by 8 lookbacks `{15, 35, 65, 100, 140, 185, 235, 285}` across `1,024` worlds (`820` train / `204` held-out assessment) and all legal root actions are scored with `H = 300` `4 + 4` Common-Random-Number (CRN) suffixes (`<= 1,280` death roots + `<= 1,280` `(partition, mix, length_bin)`-matched ordinary roots + `50,000` unsearched anchors), are the deaths counterfactually rescuable (`>= 25%`), can the B8 student distill those rescue actions (`full_bv8` retention `>= 30%` and `>= +15 pp` over `frozen_phi` with `<= 1.0%` anchor flips), and do `(4, 83, 145) + 6` dynamic-topology approximations (`full_bv8_plus_topology`) add `>= +15 pp` retention over `full_bv8` (`D100–D300` `AUC >= 0.83`)?

## What Was Learned & Current Status

1. **Unattended multi-stage GPU jobs require process-launch heartbeats and an external `SIGKILL` watchdog helper:** Cold CUDA/Numba initialization and startup parity checks took `355.51 s` on A100 before `ct_run.py` emitted its first stage event, and CPython 3.12's `faulthandler.dump_traceback_later(exit=True)` can deadlock in `dump_frame` when walking active threads. Starting a stdlib `StatusHeartbeat` before heavy imports (max observed status gap `17.3 s` on A100) and pairing `faulthandler` with `ct_watchdog_guard.py` (`0.25 s` post-dump `SIGKILL` grace) addressed both failure modes in qualification.
2. **Lossless domain-specific compression (`v2`) cuts 50k-anchor topology exports by `>28x`:** Deriving `wall_occupancy` from `own_ttl`/`enemy_ttl` and applying delta + byte-shuffle + `zlib` packing shrank projected `stage-0001.pt` from `1,449.4–3,110.3 MiB` to `51.27–87.72 MiB`, inside the `256 MiB` per-stage export cap.
3. **Status:** **PREPARED (`packet-ct-1028-14/reseal-04/transport-01`, `sandbox-ct-cf-20261028-14-r4t1` and `-a100-fallback`, `allocations=0`).** 22 packet handoff/erratum markdown files are kept (listed below); the other 6 lineage notes (`CT_1028_08_PREPARATION.md`, `CT_1028_12_HOLD.md`, `CT_1028_14_MAC_CONTINUATION.md`, `CT_1028_14_RESEAL_04_HOST_ERRATUM.md`, `CT_1028_14_RESEAL_04_MAIN033_ERRATUM.md`, `CT_STARTUP_1028_09.md`) were removed in consolidation (see git c4c4ab41).
