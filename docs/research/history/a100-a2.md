# Big-Lever A2 Historical-Label Model-Scale and Hazard-Target Falsifier on A100

## Question & Goal

On the historical T5/T8 (`19,976` states / `103,264` legal branches / `342` parent worlds) and excluded-A5 (`39` worlds / `296` searched rows / `225` decisive rows in `21` worlds) datasets across nested `25% / 50% / 100%` parent-world doses, does scaling the student encoder from `S-random` (`1.64M` params) to `M-random` (`6.68M` params) improve D300 death-prediction AUC (`>= +0.02` with paired world-bootstrap 95% LB `> 0` or `50% -> 100%` slope `>= 0.01`), and does a 5-interval censored discrete-hazard head (`{30, 60, 100, 200, 300}`) reduce D300 Brier score by `>= 10%` relative to a single-horizon D300 head?

## Final Verdict & Key Numbers

- **Scientific Verdict:** **`scale = NOT_CLOSED_EXPLORATORY`**, **`target = KEEP_SINGLE_HORIZON`** ([`research/a100_a2/README.md`](../../../research/a100_a2/README.md), [`docs/research/final_run_20261019/CAMPAIGN_LOG.md`](../final_run_20261019/CAMPAIGN_LOG.md)).
- **T5 D300 AUC at 100% Parent-World Dose:**
  - Frozen-B8 256-D linear probe (`c0`, initialized from `3e649783...`): **`0.767`** (best overall).
  - B8-initialized `1.64M` encoder (`s_b8`): **`0.719`**.
  - `6.68M`-parameter (`6,680,134` params) wider/deeper random-init encoder (`m_random`): **`0.711`**.
  - `1.64M`-parameter random-init encoder (`s_random`): **`0.706`**.
  - Increasing parent-world data volume (`25% -> 50% -> 100%`) added **`+0.02` to `+0.05` AUC**, keeping the `50% -> 100%` slope active (`scale = NOT_CLOSED_EXPLORATORY`), whereas scaling model parameters from `1.64M` (`s_random = 0.706`) to `6.68M` (`m_random = 0.711`) yielded no meaningful gain ([`research/a100_a2/README.md`](../../../research/a100_a2/README.md)).
- **Target Comparison (Single-Horizon D300 vs 5-Interval Censored Hazard Head):** The `{30, 60, 100, 200, 300}` discrete-hazard head failed to achieve the pre-registered `>= 10%` relative D300 Brier improvement over the single-horizon D300 head (`target = KEEP_SINGLE_HORIZON`; [`research/a100_a2/README.md`](../../../research/a100_a2/README.md), [`research/a100_a2/PREREGISTRATION.md`](../../../research/a100_a2/PREREGISTRATION.md)).

## What Was Learned

1. **Representation & Data Volume Dominate Encoder Scale on Historical Labels:** A linear probe on frozen B8 features (`0.767` D300 AUC) beat end-to-end training of both `1.64M` (`0.719` B8-init, `0.706` random-init) and `6.68M` (`0.711` random-init) encoders on 20k states, while doubling parent-world volume consistently added `+0.02` to `+0.05` AUC.
2. **Single-Horizon D300 Suffices:** Censored multi-interval hazard supervision (`30, 60, 100, 200, 300`) did not beat direct single-horizon D300 BCE on Brier score.
3. **Reusable Transport Hardening:** Pack-02 through Pack-06 produced two lasting operational fixes: `tools.ops.import_preflight` (catching lazy transitive imports like `src.core.config_loader -> pydantic` in a clean `python -I` venv before Colab allocation) and state-level observation deduplication before upload staging.

## Why It Closed / Current Status

Closed after completing its single registered look on 2026-10-22 (`scale = NOT_CLOSED_EXPLORATORY`, `target = KEEP_SINGLE_HORIZON`), retaining single-horizon D300 targets and frozen-B8 feature representations for downstream F2/F4 residual studies.
