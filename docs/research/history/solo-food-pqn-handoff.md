# Solo-Food PQN Warmstart, Imitation-to-RL Handoff, Horizon Extension (H64–H768), and One-Opponent Studies (AN–DA and Enemy-Dose)

## Question and Goal

Following the supervised solo food-seeking studies (`AA`–`AM`), this 37-study lineage (`AN`–`AO`–`AP`–`AQ`/`AR`/`AS`, `BV`–`DA`, and the uncoded `enemy_dose` continuation, executed `2026-09-14` to `2026-09-21` on the native `BatchSim` / `FoodGeometryNetwork` / `raster31v3` stack at commit `56e0e92434ae510a84ebaf6f58cf41c3e4421b04`) investigated how to hand off a behavior-cloned (`BC`) food-seeking policy to synchronous Parallel Q-Network (`PQN`, $Q(\lambda)$) updates without catastrophic forgetting, and how far the resulting native PQN learner could scale across episode horizons (`H64 -> H128 -> H256 -> H384 -> H512 -> H768`), observation channels (food-only $\to$ own-body TTL $\to$ explicit body length $\to$ enemy tactical channels 1–3), and opponent presence (`S1` solo $\to$ `S2` one `GreedyFood` opponent). Every study enforced three-seed unpooled replication (`8`-world `96`-lane or `32`-world `384`-lane fresh evaluation banks), serialized Mac CPU/MPS resource guards, and independent hash/array audits.

## Final Verdict and Key Numbers

1. **Imitation-to-PQN Handoff (`AN`–`CC`)**:
   - Unanchored native PQN updates (`AO`, `BV`) immediately degraded `AN`'s `BC500` food-routing skill (`11.44–11.74 -> 0.88–7.74` food in `32` updates) because policy-time $Q(\lambda)$ target residuals on already-greedy actions eroded action margins (`BW`: `90.5–94.8` greedy vs `14.6–19.0` non-greedy eroding scalar mass in update 1) (Sources: `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/ongoing-research-20260913/solo-food-pqn-warmstart/analysis/analysis.json`, `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/ongoing-research-20260913/solo-food-pqn-margin-pressure-r1/analysis/analysis.json`, [`docs/experiments/solo_food_pqn_margin_pressure_2026-09-20/margin-pressure.png`](../../experiments/solo_food_pqn_margin_pressure_2026-09-20/margin-pressure.png)).
   - Combining a persistent forward Q-head scale $\tau = 0.1$ (`BX`) with an initial `CE=1` teacher-action anchor annealed over updates `9–32` (`BY`, `98,210` transitions) retained `H64` food-seeking across `32` unanchored updates in **`3 / 3` seeds** (`11.78 / 11.64 / 11.42` food, `96 / 95 / 95` survivors), and continuing unchanged native PQN (`food01 = 0.1`, `CE=0`) to update `1024` (`CC`) recovered from a mark-128 dip (`8.38–9.59`) to **`11.73 / 11.74 / 12.15` food** and **`96 / 94 / 95` survivors** (`3/3` recovery over mark 64) (Sources: `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/ongoing-research-20260913/solo-food-pqn-anchor-release/analysis/analysis.json`, `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/ongoing-research-20260913/solo-food-pqn-training-dose/analysis/analysis.json`, [`docs/experiments/solo_food_pqn_training_dose_2026-09-20/learning-curves.png`](../../experiments/solo_food_pqn_training_dose_2026-09-20/learning-curves.png)).
2. **Solo Horizon Scaling from `H128` to `H768` (`CD`–`CW`)**:
   - **Food-only limit (`H128` pass at mark `2048`, `H256` self-trap failure)**: At mark `2048` (`CG`), food-only PQN passed all `17/17` `H128` checks in `3/3` seeds (`357–370 / 384` survivors, `22.80–24.09` food), but at `H256` (`CI`/`CJ`) all `6` policies failed endpoint survival (`258–310 / 384 < 348`), with **`607 / 607` deaths** replaying as `DEATH_SELF` under empty advisory-safe fallback (Sources: `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/ongoing-research-20260913/solo-food-pqn-training-horizon-r1/analysis/analysis.json`, `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/ongoing-research-20260913/solo-food-pqn-h256-diagnostic/analysis/analysis.json`, `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/ongoing-research-20260913/solo-food-pqn-h256-deaths/evidence/report.json`).
   - **Own-body TTL + half-MSE (`H256` & `H384` `3/3` pass)**: Because terminal states were only `0.08–0.10%` of rollout rows (`CM`) and `100%` clipped by Smooth-L1 (`Huber`), switching to unclipped `half_mse` ($0.5 \cdot \text{MSE}$) on own-body + food channels (`CO` at mark `3072`, `CR` `train_h256` control at mark `3584`) achieved **`3 / 3` absolute passes at `H256`** (`368 / 376 / 374` survivors in `CO`) and **`3 / 3` all-22-condition passes at `H384`** (`348 / 366 / 381` survivors and `64.03 / 57.71 / 64.10` food in `CR`, beating the scripted `GreedyFood` teacher's `272 / 384` survivors) (Sources: `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/ongoing-research-20260913/solo-food-pqn-loss-shape/analysis/analysis.json`, `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/ongoing-research-20260913/solo-food-pqn-h384-training/analysis/analysis.json`, [`docs/experiments/solo_food_pqn_h384_training_2026-09-20/behavioral-learning-curves.png`](../../experiments/solo_food_pqn_h384_training_2026-09-20/behavioral-learning-curves.png)).
   - **Explicit body-length scalars (`H512` & `H768` `3/3` pass)**: At `H512` (`CS`/`CT`/`CU`), `CR` body-aware controls had `98` deaths (`337 / 343 / 374` survivors) where advisory-safe actions first became empty at offset `0` (`63/98` had $\ge 2$ safe directions at offset `-1`). Exposing `length / 150` and `log1p(length) / log1p(150)` (`CV`, mark `4096`) passed all `29/29` `H512` conditions in **`3 / 3` seeds** (`380 / 380 / 384` survivors, `84.68 / 80.33 / 92.81` food), and on a fresh `32`-world `H768` bank (`CW`) passed all **`37 / 37` conditions and `72 / 72` cells in `3 / 3` seeds**: **`353 / 364 / 383` survivors** (survival fraction `0.979 / 0.990 / 0.998`) and **`122.51 / 116.85 / 135.34` food** (vs `GreedyFood` teacher's `94.52` food and `127 / 384` survivors) (Sources: `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/ongoing-research-20260913/solo-food-pqn-length-access/analysis/analysis.json`, `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/ongoing-research-20260913/solo-food-pqn-h768-length-confirmation/analysis/analysis.json`, [`docs/experiments/solo_food_pqn_h768_length_confirmation_2026-09-21/behavioral-horizons.png`](../../experiments/solo_food_pqn_h768_length_confirmation_2026-09-21/behavioral-horizons.png)).
3. **Transition to One Scripted Opponent (`CX`–`DA`, enemy-dose)**:

## What Was Learned and Why It Closed

- **Why BC warmstarts collapse under naive Q-learning and how to prevent it**: Uncalibrated BC logits suffer severe action-margin erosion on greedy actions during early TD updates (`AO`, `BV`, `BW`). Scaling Q outputs by $\tau = 0.1$ (`BX`), maintaining a temporary `CE=1` teacher anchor during critic warm-up (`AP`, `BY`), keeping ambient food reward small relative to terminal penalties (`CB`: `0.1` vs `1.0`), and training past the transient post-handoff dip (`CC`: `1,024` updates) reliably preserve and extend BC skills.
- **Stepwise feature and loss requirements for long-horizon survival**:
  - Food-only observations suffice through `H128` (`CG`) but self-trap by `H256` (`CI`/`CJ`).
  - Adding own-body TTL plus unclipped `half_mse` loss (so rare `-10` terminal residuals are not clipped to `-1` by Smooth-L1) solves `H256` and `H384` (`CO`, `CR`).
  - Adding explicit normalized/log body-length scalars (`CV`, `CW`) solves `H512` and `H768` (`353–383 / 384` survivors, outlasting the scripted teacher by `~3×`).
- **Why the solo-food PQN line handed off to targeted multi-snake/redesign studies**: By `DA` and the enemy-dose continuation (`2026-09-21`), solo survival and food collection were solved through `H768`, and `S2` opponent head-collision learning was shown to be bottlenecked by rare encounter density (`~20` deaths per `131k` ambient steps) rather than solo food/body representation, motivating targeted encounter and multi-snake curricula.
