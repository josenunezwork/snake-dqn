# Solo-Food Safety Residual, Gate, and Free-Space Wrapper Series (Studies AT–BU)

## Question and Goal

Conducted between 2026-09-19 and 2026-09-20 across 28 sequential governed studies (Studies AT through BU) under `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/ongoing-research-20260913/`, this lineage asked how a frozen `raster31v3` solo food-seeking parent (`FoodGeometryNetwork` from Study AN, seeds `2026093401–2026093403`) could be augmented with an additive body/wall/reachability safety residual so that it avoids late self-enclosure deaths (`H128`, `H256`, and `H512` solo rollouts, 96 lanes across 8 worlds per bank) without degrading ordinary food collection, and—once the safety head succeeded—whether the remaining long-horizon zero-food stalls could be eliminated by fine-tuning the food parent on own-policy states, wider world banks, or CoordConv-style coordinate planes.

## Final Verdict and Key Numbers

1. **Breakthrough on Solo Self-Trap Prevention (Studies BC → BM → BN → BO):**
   - **Label + architecture + loss triad:**
     1. *Parent-consistent safety oracle (`BC`, `BD r1`, `BE`):* Removing `SpaceTeacher` food-ranking disagreements (>50% of disagreements in `solo-food-reachability-label-geometry/analysis/report.json`) yielded a privileged oracle that achieved **`96/96` H128 survivors in `3/3` seeds** (`solo-food-parent-safety-confirmation/analysis/analysis.json`).
     2. *Shared per-action `3→16→1` safety head (`BH`, `BI`):* A 2D CNN residual overfit singleton rasters (`21.43%–31.82%` held fit in `solo-food-parent-safety-learning-r2/analysis/analysis.json`), whereas a shared `Linear(3,16)`–ReLU–`Linear(16,1)` head over `[deficit[i], threshold/cap, boost]` had only `138–146` distinct feature nodes across `3,072` rows (`solo-food-shared-feature-feasibility/analysis/analysis.json`).
     3. *Parent-action ranking hinge + 1,500 updates (`BI`, `BJ`, `BK`, `BL`, `BM`):* Replacing the mathematically infeasible zero-KL / zero-square preservation penalties (`X` in `solo-food-shared-feature-feasibility/analysis/analysis.json`) with a capped-margin ranking hinge and 1,500 Adam updates achieved **`97.22% (140/144), 97.78% (44/45), 98.57% (69/70)` held intervention accuracy** and **`99.93%–100%` non-intervention parent preservation** across both BK/BL and fresh-init replication BM (`solo-food-ranking-budget-generalization/held-analysis/analysis.json`, `solo-food-shared-head-fresh-init-confirmation/analysis/analysis.json`).
   - **Zero-shot horizon scaling (`BN`, `BO`):** Without retraining, the fixed BM-1500 shared-head policies achieved **`96/96` (100%) survival across all 3 seeds at both H256 and H512** (`0` self-deaths vs parent `66, 75, 75` H512 self-deaths and `30, 21, 21 / 96` H512 survivors), raising H512 mean food from `55.08, 52.75, 54.57` to **`77.01, 77.73, 77.93`** and H512 mass integral from `23.29, 21.41, 22.30` to **`41.16, 41.24, 41.90`** (`solo-food-longer-solo-confirmation-r1/analysis/analysis.json`, `solo-food-h512-confirmation/analysis/analysis.json`).
2. **Negative Results on Own-Policy Food Fine-Tuning (Studies BP → BU):**
   - **Stall diagnosis (`BP`, `BQ`):** All zero-food lanes (`1` per seed in BO H512: lanes `66, 10, 19`) were exact inherited parent loops where the snake remained roomy (`0` safety-head overrides) while `GreedyFood` disagreed on `384–511 / 512` frames (`solo-food-stall-census/analysis/analysis.json`, `solo-food-stall-state-diagnostic/analysis/analysis.json`).
   - **Own-policy coverage (`BR`), world breadth (`BT`), and coordinate routing (`BU`):** Fine-tuning the `FoodGeometryNetwork` parent for 500 updates on `1,536` own-policy rows plateaued at **`86.65%–89.13%` held accuracy** (`< 95%` bar) across 8-world (`BR`), 16-world (`BT`: `88.25%–88.77%`), and CoordConv (`BU`: `88.36%–88.54%`) variants (`solo-food-own-policy-coverage-r1/analysis/analysis.json`, `solo-food-world-breadth-r1/analysis/analysis.json`, `solo-food-coordinate-routing/analysis-r2/analysis.json`).

## What Was Learned

1. **Why CNN safety residuals failed to generalize (AT–BF):** Supervised on sparse safety overrides, the spatial CNN residual saw nearly disjoint train/held body-visible inputs (`0, 0, 1` exact overlapping rows in AX) and stayed below the `75%` held-fit bar (e.g. `21–32%` in BF r2), while BG found no exact-view label contradictions. Restricting the safety residual to a low-dimensional per-action invariant (`[deficit[i], threshold/cap, boost]`) forces structural generalization across all body geometries (`97.2%–98.6%` held fit).
2. **Distribution-preservation penalties vs action-ranking hinges (AW, BH–BK):** On a low-dimensional shared feature key, requiring zero KL divergence or zero residual on ordinary rows creates exact two-edge negative cycles with intervention ranking constraints when the same reachability deficit triple appears in both ordinary and intervention states (`BI`). A parent-action ranking hinge (`U+R-action`) eliminates the cycle while preserving `99.93%–100%` of ordinary parent actions.
3. **Why 500-update supervised food fine-tuning stalled at ~87–89% held accuracy (BR–BU):** The remaining `~11–13%` held food-teacher errors were predominantly strictly worse immediate food-distance moves rather than equal-distance ties (`BS`), and neither 16-world breadth (`BT`) nor ego-relative coordinate planes (`BU`) reduced them by the required `20%`.
