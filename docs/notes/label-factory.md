## What F0 produces

- F0 (the hindsight label factory) plays untouched bare B8 lives, reads the bare H300 outcome from the natural trajectory, and branches only alternative actions and selected bare audits to create development data without a gate, fit, timing qualification or serving change. (src: research/bl_labels/README.md:3-7)
- The owner's 2026-10-23 PT work order and round-24 judgment authorized labels for `b8-s3`, checkpoint SHA `3e649783…12eb4f8`. (src: research/bl_labels/INTENT.md:3-10)
- The factory uses the existing exact simulator, sparse full-state snapshots, exact clones, full digests, native opponent v8 and T3 exact-stop; opponents react on every counterfactual transition, with no tape reuse after divergence or synthetic world edits. (src: research/bl_labels/INTENT.md:12-15)
- Watch6 means bare B8 against five FRP3-s12+v8 opponents with continuous respawn, while S-gate worlds preserve the frozen, mixed and scripted gate populations. (src: research/bl_labels/README.md:23-29)
- For fatal action index `e` and exclusive endpoint `f=e+1`, doom roots lie in `[f−300,f)`, with 60 stride roots unioned with the last 30 roots normally yielding 84 distinct roots under a cap of 90. (src: research/bl_labels/README.md:31-34)
- Eight ordinary roots are sampled separately by bottom-hash from the same life's known-safe natural H300 roots. (src: research/bl_labels/README.md:34-37)
- Exact outcomes use fixed-H300 mass with zero mass after death, retain bare among surviving ties, and choose the longest delay when every branch dies. (src: research/bl_labels/README.md:37-39)
- Skipped actions remain `NOT_EVALUATED` with null outcomes, and short surviving suffixes are `UNKNOWN`, never negative. (src: research/bl_labels/README.md:39; research/bl_labels/INTENT.md:31)
- Snapshots occur every 20 decisions in a ring covering at least 420 decisions plus a death snapshot, and every restored root must match its live full digest without changing the continuing natural stream. (src: research/bl_labels/INTENT.md:24-27; research/bl_labels/README.md:41-45)
- Independent bare rerolls audit 10% of doom roots for exact death decision and final length, while last-30 roots and a 10% stride sample receive complete legal outcomes. (src: research/bl_labels/INTENT.md:32-36)
- H1440 continuation audits cover 10% of rescues, and more than 10% sign reversals cause the aggregator to derive tranche-wide `delay` labels while preserving immutable raw outcomes. (src: research/bl_labels/README.md:57-61)
- Each death-life exports immediately as `lives/<parent>--life-NNNNNN.json`, with SHA sidecar and completion marker last; terminal session exports include the six-seat action tape, per-decision Q/legal/length, digests, audit-root metadata and timing buckets. (src: research/bl_labels/README.md:47-53)
- Completed sessions skip their parent during resume, while partial parents replay deterministic natural discovery and skip verified lives; this is completed-unit resume rather than interrupted-trajectory continuation. (src: research/bl_labels/README.md:134-139)
- The independent Mac replay audit uses all six recorded actions rather than x86 policy decisions, classifies Mac-side ties at four float32 ulps, checks simulator digests and requires 64 distinct roots for `PASS`. (src: research/bl_labels/README.md:141-143)
- Verdict precedence is `INVALID`, `INCOMPLETE`, `INSUFFICIENT`, `KILL`, then canary readiness. (src: research/bl_labels/INTENT.md:47-54)
- An unexplained digest or bare-audit mismatch makes the tranche `INVALID`, a completed zero-rescue canary is `INSUFFICIENT`, and fewer than eight rescue-bearing lives per Mac-worker-hour equivalent is `KILL`. (src: research/bl_labels/INTENT.md:47-54)
- Colab worker-hours cannot replace the Mac denominator without a measured Mac ruler or separately measured conversion. (src: research/bl_labels/README.md:156-158)
