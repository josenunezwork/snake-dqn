# Apex vector61 Serving-Time Free-Space Veto Progression (v1–v8) and Early October Diagnostics

## Question and Goal

At horizon `H5000` (`promotion-v2-watch-rect`), `85` of `96` recorded deaths of the June 2026 `vector61` incumbent (`champion_a5_freespace_20260621.pth`, sha256 `43d4e2c5…`) in the September 2026 strict pilot were self-collisions, leaving the hero alive for only `29%–37%` of the horizon despite already computing 3 per-direction flood-fill free-space features (cap `160`) in its observation vector. Between September 26 and October 3, 2026, this lineage asked how much `H5000` mass-integral and survival performance could be recovered purely through deterministic serving-time safety wrappers around the frozen `champion_a5` checkpoint without retraining the policy, progressing through eight veto variants (`v2` speed-preserving one-step free-space veto, `v3` tail-aware one-step veto, `v4` depth-8 lookahead veto, `v5` two-step boost-landing veto, `v6` opponent-head + no-spacious fallback veto, `v7` tail-aware space-preference re-ranking, and `v8` space-preference + opponent-head veto), paired with point-of-no-return trap-horizon diagnostics, `v5`/`v8` death censuses, `vector61` SIMD H5000 parity checks, and serving-time search feasibility benchmarks.

2. **October 3 `v8` Death Census and Serving-Time Search Feasibility Findings:**
   - Across `180` SIMD `H5000` episodes (`60` worlds/mix, `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/death-census-v8-20261003/run-v1/merged/summary.json`, sha256 `a85296e5b877609d…`), `champion_a5 + v8(8)` survived the full `5,000` frames in `61.7%` of episodes (`111/180`; mean survival fraction `0.847`, mean mass integral `337.4`, survivors' final mass `825`). Deaths were `29.4%` self (`53/180`, fatal lengths `235–881`, median `517`, median death frame `3,088`), `7.2%` enemy-body encirclement (`13/180`), `1.1%` wall (`2/180`), and `0.6%` head-on (`1/180`).
   - All `69` deaths occurred inside `v8`'s `no_spacious` regime (median `28` consecutive `no_spacious` decisions before self deaths). Counterfactual closure checks (`closure_check.py`, `length_aware.py`) proved that `68%` (`36/53`) of remaining self deaths were closed by other snakes moving (`16/27` exact count-PNR closures had a static escape one frame earlier) or threshold boundaries (`11/27`), while increasing the one-step cap from `160` to full `length` fired in only `4/69` deaths. All remaining deaths combined cost at most `+60` (hold) to `+91` (grow) pooled mass.
   - Micro-benchmarks in [`docs/research/serving_time_search_feasibility_2026-10-03.md`](../serving_time_search_feasibility_2026-10-03.md) showed that `v8` costs `0.51 ms` SIMD / `0.65 ms` live per decision, whereas a 40-frame hero-only rollout check (`v9` prototype) costs `3–6 ms` typical / `17–37 ms` worst-case, and also uncovered a `GameState` `copy.deepcopy` bug where `AISnake._get_frame` closures remained bound to the parent world unless rebound.

## What Was Learned and Why It Closed

- **What was learned:**
  1. Serving-time free-space shielding and tail-aware space-preference re-ranking (`v2 -> v5 -> v7 -> v8`) lifted the frozen `champion_a5` checkpoint's `H5000` mass integral nearly `6–7×` (`~49–62` to `~337–369`) and survival fraction from `~0.25–0.32` to `~0.83–0.92` while modifying only `0.20%–0.29%` of decisions at `0.65 ms`/decision.
  2. The `v8` death census proved that static single-snake veto wrappers had reached diminishing returns on `vector61`: head-on deaths (`0.6%`) and two-step boost traps were solved, and `~75%` of the remaining death headroom came from dynamic multi-frame opponent encirclement and base policy food-intake rate (`0.19` pellets/frame).
- **Why it closed and current status:** After `frp3-s12 + v8` was promoted on `2026-10-06` ([`docs/research/history/frp.md`](frp.md)), the `vector61` line was frozen (`2026-10-08`), and the `v8` free-space features (`30` scalar channels) were embedded directly into the `ego2s-bv8` observation contract so the `Ego2sNet` CNN (`b8-s3`, released `2026-10-17`) could internalize `v8` safety without a serving-time veto. The `v8`, `v7`, `v5`, and `v2` wrappers remain in the codebase as rollback variants (`SNAKE_SERVE_CHECKPOINT=champion`, `SNAKE_SERVE_VETO_VARIANT=v8|v7|v5|v2`).
