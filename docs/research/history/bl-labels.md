## What Was Learned

1. **Snapshot-ring + Q-ordered exact-stop makes counterfactual H300 labeling practical on CPU:** Natural play (`47.2%`) and doom counterfactual branches (`33.5%`) dominate runtime, while 20-step ring snapshots keep restore/replay overhead to `1.2%` (`147.7 s` out of `12,403 s` in Canary A) with `100%` digest and bare-reroll fidelity (`1,012` restores, `82` bare rerolls, `0` mismatches).
2. **Per-worker slowdown bars penalize E-core scheduling; aggregate throughput + windowed medians are the right Mac guards:** Running 12 workers (`W12`) on Apple Silicon uses efficiency cores, raising per-worker seconds per life while delivering `1.36x` total wall throughput with `0` thermal events. Replacing the single-life `1.30x` slowdown check (which had a `~10%` false-trip rate per life and self-armed on resume) with a per-width `4 * width` rolling median (`SLOWDOWN_GUARD_HANDOFF_20261023.md`) and a `>= 30 s` / 7-observation `(PID, birth_tick)` persistence filter for foreign `nice < 15` processes eliminated spurious preemptions.
3. **Completed-unit union ledgers decouple operational interruptions from scientific integrity:** Because every life and session exports atomically with SHA-256 and `.complete` sidecars and partial parents deterministically replay natural discovery while skipping verified life exports, `D256` (`8` attempts) and `D1024` (`2` attempts) resumed cleanly across host checks, width transitions, and registered gate windows without recomputing or invalidating a single life.

## Why It Closed / Current Status

**CLOSED (D256 & D1024 Complete; D4096 Sealed `NOT_RUN_BY_RULE`).** The F0 label factory delivered its two admitted production tranches (`D256` with `1,566` units across `864` parents; `D1024` with `3,865` units across `2,160` fold `1–4` parents) into the F4 and R30–R32 student-fitting pipelines (`r30-r32-f5-successors`). When D1024 R-A triggered `STOP_FUNDING` on 2026-10-25, `D4096` was permanently sealed `NOT_RUN_BY_RULE` per condition B6, and both the October L4 label lane (`C6` sidecar timeout failure) and the GPU label route (`W12_BELOW_4`) were closed.
