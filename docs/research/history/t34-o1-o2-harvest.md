# T34 Offense-vs-Defense Audit, O1 Offensive Harvest, O2 200-World Bank, and Student Fit

## Question and Goal

Initiated on 2026-10-20/21 during the T34 ExIt-B8 campaign, this lineage tested the hypothesis that the largest remaining mass upside over bare `b8-s3` lay in offensive play (killing opponents and harvesting corpse drops) rather than defensive rescue search (`Path B` / `teacher_v2`). It proceeded in three stages: (1) a five-persona Astra audit and Fable judgment (`docs/research/advice/2026-10-21_offense_vs_defense/JUDGMENT.md`) verifying the simulator's mechanics-v2 corpse economy and measuring `b8-s3`'s actual kill and food-source shares; (2) **O1** (`research/t34_o1_harvest/`), a 48-world L4 counterfactual branch census testing whether single-action H500 deviations at safe states capture ≥`+5` death-drop-attributed branch mass; and (3) **O2** (`research/t34_o2_harvest/`), a closed-loop H5000 terminal-hero comparison of bare `b8-s3` (`B`), rescue-only search (`D`), and rescue plus offensive corpse harvesting (`D+O`) across `N = 1,260` fresh paired worlds (`420` per mix).

## Final Verdict and Key Numbers

1. **Simulator Mechanics v2 & B8 Food Economy (`docs/research/advice/2026-10-21_offense_vs_defense/JUDGMENT.md`, `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/astra-offense/measure/run02/receipt.json`):**
   - Across 36 terminal-hero H5000 worlds (`12` per mix, `164,514` acted frames), bare `b8-s3` recorded **`332` credited kills** (`18.08/game` scripted, `4.08` frozen, `5.50` mixed) and **`0.1252%` boost share** (`206` frames, `-40` net mass on boost frames).
   - Gross mass intake was **`50.53%` ambient (`20,952`), `39.71%` death drops (`16,467`), and `9.76%` boost trails (`4,049`, at least `4,018` from opponents)** (`59.84%` death-drop share in scripted, `29.63%` in frozen, `26.76%` in mixed).
   - Under mechanics v2 (`src/core/mechanics_constants.py`), corpse and trail pellets share a **board-wide FIFO cap of `150` pellets** (`0.5 × max_food`) and consumption is capped at **1 pellet/tick**, so all 9 observed deaths at length `>= 800` (mean length `1,018`, max `1,454`) retained exactly **`150` pellets** on the board.
2. **O1 Counterfactual Harvest Census (`GO_TO_O2`, `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/t34-o1-20261021/LOOK_O1.json`):**
   - Across `48/48` fresh H5000 worlds (`16` per mix, `309` roots, `1,854` H500 branches, `917,051` branch steps; `3/3` H5000 observer-parity worlds matched bit-for-bit), **`32/48` worlds (`66.7%`, bar `>= 8`)** and **`20/32` frozen+mixed worlds (`62.5%`, bar `>= 4`)** contained at least one surviving first-action branch with `delta_A500 >= +5.0` and positive incremental death-drop intake (`12/16` scripted, `11/16` frozen, `9/16` mixed; `96/309` qualifying roots with mean `delta_A500 = +19.56`, max `+96.18`, mean `+29.27` extra death-drop pellets).
3. **O2 1,260-World Closed-Loop Comparison (`INCONCLUSIVE`, `/Users/josenunez/Projects/ml/snake-dqn-artifacts/t34-o2-20261021/LOOK_O2_V2.json`):**
   - Across all `1,260` fresh paired H5000 worlds (`420` scripted, `420` frozen, `420` mixed; `3,780` episodes across 10 L4 chunks `v3-chunk-00..09`):
     - **Mean H5000 mass integral:** Bare `B` = **`629.40`**, Rescue-only `D` = **`686.02`** (`+56.62` over `B`), Rescue+Harvest `D+O` = **`689.38`**.
     - **Co-primary contrast 1 (`D+O - B`):** **`+59.98` mass** (`SE = 4.51`, two-sided `90% CI [+52.56, +67.40]`, Holm-adjusted `LB90 = +51.63 > 0`, `p = 2.66e-39`) — **PASS** (`>= +40` bar).
     - **Co-primary contrast 2 (`D+O - D`, pure offensive harvest attribution):** **`+3.36` mass** (`SE = 4.39`, two-sided `90% CI [-3.86, +10.58]`, Holm-adjusted `LB90 = -3.86 <= 0`, `p = 0.2219`) — **FAIL to clear `LB90 > 0`** (`UB90 = +10.58 > 0`, so classified `INCONCLUSIVE` rather than `KILL`).
     - **Per-mix breakdown (`B` / `D` / `D+O`, `D+O - D`):**
       - *Scripted (`n=420`):* `677.96` / `772.97` / `781.90` (`D+O - D = +8.93`, `90% CI [-3.41, +21.26]`); no-death rate rose from `58.33%` (`B`) to `75.71%` (`D`) and `76.67%` (`D+O`, `+0.1833 >= +0.03` vs `B`).

     - **Survival & boost guards:** Pooled alive fraction was `0.8588` (`B`), `0.9032` (`D`), `0.9052` (`D+O`, `+0.0465` vs `B`); delivered boost share was `0.130%` (`B`), `0.123%` (`D`), `0.145%` (`D+O`) — all guards **PASS**.

## What Was Learned

1. **Defensive rescue (`D`) is a 17× larger closed-loop lever than opportunistic corpse harvesting (`O`):** In the exact same 1,260 paired worlds, defensive doom-only search (`D`) added **`+56.62` H5000 mass** (`+94.99` in scripted, `+36.48` in frozen, `+38.37` in mixed) by cutting hero deaths from `34.1%` to `19.6%`, whereas adding up to 8 H500 offensive harvest searches per world (`D+O - D`, firing `5,152` times across `1,260` worlds) added only **`+3.36` mass** (`-6.07` in frozen) because local H500 corpse detours displace future ambient/corpse trajectories and only ~54% of local H500 harvest advantages persist to H5000.
2. **Distilling sparse offensive harvest branches into bare `b8-s3` degrades gameplay (`p3_o2_student`):** Just like defensive rescue distillation (`Claim A`), supervised fine-tuning on the `1,984` `delta_A500 >= 20` O2 harvest roots failed to improve held-out top-1 agreement (`25.30% → 24.49%`) and hurt closed-loop H5000 mass (`-61.26`).

## Why It Closed and Current Status

**CLOSED (2026-10-23).** Per `JUDGMENT.md` §3 (`O2 advances only if (D+O - D) has LB90 > 0`), O2's `+3.36` (`LB90 = -3.86`) closed the offensive corpse-harvesting line and confirmed that defensive rescue search (`exact-stop-l2-prime`, `+56.62` in O2 and later `+63.74%` mass / `-0.1250` deaths/1k in Watch6 V3) is the decisive T34 mechanism.
