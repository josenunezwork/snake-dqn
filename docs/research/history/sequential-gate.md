# Sequential Strict Gate, Phase R Protocol, and Two-Tier Gate Calibration

## Question and Goal

What evaluation horizon (`H3000`, `H5000`, `H10000`), opponent mixes (`frozen`, `scripted`, `mixed`), sample sizes (`N`), and group-sequential stopping and survival-band rules (`strict-sequential-obf-bonferroni-v1`, paired survival noninferiority, and `sequential-phase-r-obf-hk-v1`) are required so that promotion and Phase R screening gates detect genuine `~3–20%` (`10–30+` mass) improvements at high statistical power while cutting evaluation episodes by 27–75% under null or strong-signal regimes and controlling familywise Type I error at `<= 0.05`?

## Final Verdict and Key Numbers

- **Verdict**: **ADOPTED AND ACTIVE**. The calibrated two-tier evaluation hierarchy, 4-look Lan–DeMets O'Brien–Fleming sequential strict gate (`strict-sequential-obf-bonferroni-v1`), paired survival band v2 (`pooled_ni_continue` / `paired_ni_at_stop`), and sequential Phase R engine (`sequential-phase-r-obf-hk-v1`) replaced fixed-sample promotion gates and uncalibrated 16-world reference bands across the repository.
- **Two-Tier Gate Calibration (`gate_calibration_20260926`)**:
  - Across `144` saved `H5000` episodes (`records_sha256_digest = 672a630aa52b5300af8e4a7bf2689ad23e3d02e9cb263b4682648a6d8a460396`), exact single-death decomposition `mass_integral = survival_fraction * mean_mass_alive` held with `0.0` max error, and death timing explained `R^2 = 0.862–0.959` of within-arm mass variance and `R^2 = 0.85–0.90` of paired mass deltas (`research/gate_calibration_20260926/README.md`).
  - Self-collision accounted for `85/96` (`89%`) of incumbent Apex deaths (`40/48` dev, `45/48` pilot) (`research/gate_calibration_20260926/README.md`).
  - Per-world mass pairing correlation between divergent policies (`6102` vs Apex) was near zero (`r = -0.01` frozen, `+0.11` mixed, `-0.22` scripted; variance ratios `0.99`, `1.09`, `0.95`), whereas anchoring the `10%` MDE on a noisy 16-world scripted draw (`38.8` vs `92.2` pilot, Welch `t = 1.77`) inflated required `N` from `3,047` (pooled mean `65.5`) to `8,676`; at `20%` MDE of pooled means (`59.2` frozen, `65.5` scripted, `72.0` mixed), required `N` is `212` frozen, `141` mixed, and `762` scripted (`research/gate_calibration_20260926/README.md`).
  - Repeating a naive fixed one-sided `(1 - 0.10/3)` t futility bound across 4 looks (`32, 128, 384, 768`) inflated cumulative false stops of truly equal candidates to `~27%`, whereas Lan–DeMets O'Brien–Fleming futility spending (`alpha = 0.10/3` per mix; z bounds `10.36, 5.08, 2.79, 1.85`) bounded cumulative false stops to `0.096 <= 0.10` (`research/gate_calibration_20260926/README.md`).
- **4-Look Sequential Strict Gate (`sequential_gate_validation_20261002`)**:
  - Simulated 4 looks at `61, 122, 183, 243` worlds/mix (`N_max = 243`, paired SD `140`, MDE `30`, `delta_NI = 3.5`) with one-sided OBF efficacy boundaries at `0.05/3` (`z = 4.637, 3.183, 2.539, 2.174`; nominal `p = 1.77e-6, 7.28e-4, 5.57e-3, 1.485e-2`) and scripted NI OBF boundaries at `0.05` (`z = 3.741, 2.534, 2.011, 1.721`) (`research/sequential_gate_validation_20261002/simulate.py`, `src/evaluation/sequential_gate.py`).
  - Vectorized simulation matched `src.evaluation.sequential_gate.run_sequential_gate` and `eval_stats.strict_promotion_decision` on `1,250/1,250` fresh replicates (`0` mismatches) (`research/sequential_gate_validation_20261002/simulate.py`).
  - Over `200,000` replicates, global-null `(0, 0, 0)` `P(PASS)` was `0.00058` (`95%` Wilson `[0.0005, 0.0007]`) with familywise false rejection `0.0492` (`0.0270` with futility honored); under between-mix correlation `0.5`, global-null `P(PASS)` was `0.0053` and FWER `0.0438`; under LFC `(130, 0, 0)`, `P(PASS)` was `0.0182`; under NI-null `(130, -3.5, 130)`, `P(PASS)` was `0.0500` (`research/sequential_gate_validation_20261002/simulate.py`).
  - Expected total paired worlds across all 3 mixes (vs `729` fixed) dropped to `532` (`-27.0%`, `89.1%` `STOP_FUTILE`) at `delta = 0`, `597` (`-18.1%`, power `0.9434` vs `0.9628` fixed Holm and `0.9481` fixed Bonferroni) at `delta = +30`, `369` (`-49.4%`, power `1.0000`) at `delta = +60`, and `183` (`-74.8%`, `61.1` worlds/mix, power `1.0000`) at `delta = +130` (`research/sequential_gate_validation_20261002/simulate.py`).
  - Severe negative skewness (`-2.83` in `skew_probe.py`) inflated per-mix any-look efficacy from `0.0167` to `0.0397` (fixed-N `0.0323`) and NI from `0.05` to `0.0877` (fixed-N `0.0720`), motivating the mandatory pre-final resampling skew check (`<= 0.020` efficacy per mix, `<= 0.06` scripted NI) frozen into `sequential_runner.py` (`research/sequential_gate_validation_20261002/skew_probe.py`, `research/sequential_strict_template/README.md`).
- **Paired Survival Bands (`paired_band_validation_20261003` & `survival_band_v2_20261006`)**:
  - In `paired_survival_20261003.json` (`sha256: 7fc6101c...a016`), a 16-world incumbent calibration reference had SD `~0.06` (`3x` the `0.02` margin), causing the old un-paired rule (`block_at_stop`) to pass a no-regression candidate only `9.2–23.1%` of the time while letting a true `-0.05` one-mix survival regression pass `6.6–32.2%` of the time jointly (`research/paired_band_validation_20261003/README.md`).
  - Switching to paired noninferiority (`paired_ni_at_stop`, `M = 0.05, alpha = 0.05, pointwise`, floor `0.30`) capped the joint gate error for a `-0.05` one-mix survival regression at `0.020–0.053` across all `36` pool $\times$ `theta` $\times$ mix cells while raising no-regression pass rates at `n = 249` from `23.1%` (`22.8%`) to `91.4%` (`92.8%`) and `+0.05` survival-improvement pass rates at `n = 63` from `35.4%` (`40.9%`) to `92.7%` (`94.5%`) (`research/paired_band_validation_20261003/README.md`).
  - `survival_band_v2_20261006` (`outputs/oc.json`, `outputs/design_plan_pooled.json`) extended this to `sequential-strict-template/v2-pooled` (`pooled_ni_continue` with `rci_obf`, pooled margin `0.05`, floor `0.30`, per-mix catastrophic margin `0.075` or `0.10`), allowing an efficacy-qualified look whose survival band is noisy at look 1 to continue (`CONTINUE_BANDS`) rather than falsely failing early (`research/sequential_strict_template/README.md`, `research/survival_band_v2_20261006/outputs/oc.json`).
- **Sequential Phase R (`sequential_phase_r`)**:
  - `src/evaluation/sequential_phase_r.py` and `research/sequential_phase_r/` (`oc_results_20261007.json`, `variance_pool_20261007.json`) provided the multi-look OBF Phase R screen with create-only `plan.json` / `looks/look-<k>.json` receipts, shard-start `look_gate` bindings, and `python -I` stdlib auditing (`research/sequential_phase_r/audit.py`, `src/evaluation/sequential_phase_r.py`).

## What Was Learned

1. **Trajectory chaos destroys per-world mass correlation, but pairing eliminates reference-bank noise and enables caching**: Once a candidate takes a single different action from the incumbent, multi-snake trajectories diverge (`r = -0.22..+0.11` on mass), so sample size `N` must be sized near unpaired variance; however, pairing on shared world seeds allows caching the deterministic incumbent arm and prevents noisy 16-world calibration draws from corrupting survival bands (`research/gate_calibration_20260926/README.md`, `research/paired_band_validation_20261003/README.md`).
2. **Group-sequential O'Brien–Fleming boundaries save 27–75% of evaluation compute with negligible power loss**: Spending efficacy (`0.05/3`) and NI (`0.05`) via Lan–DeMets OBF with conditional-power futility (`CP < 0.10` under MDE `30` in `>= 2` mixes) cuts expected episodes by `27%` under the null and `49–75%` under medium-to-large effects (`+60` to `+130` mass) at `< 0.5 pp` sequential power cost relative to fixed Bonferroni (`research/sequential_gate_validation_20261002/simulate.py`).
3. **Survival guards must be paired and continuation-capable (`v2-pooled`)**: Evaluating candidate survival against an independent 16-world calibration mean had a `32%` false-pass rate on `-0.05` regressions and a `77–91%` false-reject rate on non-regressing candidates; paired survival noninferiority with `CONTINUE_BANDS` (`v2-pooled`) solves both defects (`research/paired_band_validation_20261003/README.md`, `research/sequential_strict_template/README.md`).
